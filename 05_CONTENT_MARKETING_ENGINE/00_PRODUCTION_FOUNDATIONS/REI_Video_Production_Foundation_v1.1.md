# REI Method — Video Production Foundation
**Approved Master v1.1 | Singapore Real Estate Insider | Coach Edmund Tan | July 2026**

Fixed structural foundation for all video ad creation: Reels (120s–180s), VSL (4–6 min), YouTube Long Form (45 min). Every future session builds on this. Do not deviate without updating this master.

---

## Section 1 — The One Principle Behind Every Format

Every winning video ad runs on the same three-part spine regardless of length:

| HOOK | STORY | OFFER |
|------|-------|-------|
| Stop the scroll or open a loop. | Mirror, Lie, Hard Truth, Reframe, Method, Proof. | Promise the opposite of the hook pain. One action. |

Source validated: 1CC Live Top Performing Ads Breakdown (Caleb Sim). Length changes. Structure does not.

---

## Section 2 — The Building Blocks

MUST-HAVES appear in every video. The Lie is optional but highest conversion lift when it lands.

### Hook — pick ONE per video

| HOOK TYPE | WHAT IT DOES | EXAMPLE (REI) |
|-----------|--------------|---------------|
| Call-Out | Names exactly who it is for. | If you own a condo but have no clear next move — this is for you. |
| Open Loop | Starts a story they must resolve. | Here is how the property that made us comfortable almost cost us 10 years. |
| Hard Truth Hook | Identity-level statement. Stings. | One good property is not a property strategy. |
| Contrarian | Says the opposite of expected advice. | Upgrading is not always the right move. Most agents will not say that. |

### Story Blocks — in this order

| BLOCK | ONE JOB | RULE |
|-------|---------|------|
| MUST — Mirror | Make them feel seen | 2–3 parallel you-lines. Real scenes not feelings. |
| The Lie (optional) | Name the story they tell themselves | One line in their own voice. Highest-converting optional line. |
| MUST — Hard Truth | The blunt sentence they cannot argue with | One sentence. No softening. |
| MUST — Reframe | Show the wrong way then flip it | Wrong way first. Then the right way. Gap = big idea. |
| MUST — Method | Name your system | 2–4 numbered steps. Named system is easier to trust. |
| MUST — Proof | Show it works | ONE type per video. Real names and numbers only. |

## Section 3 — Proof Types (Pick ONE Per Video)

| PROOF TYPE | HOW TO USE IT | USE WHEN |
|------------|---------------|----------|
| Track Record | Your numbers, your results. Repeat same system. | Warm audience that already believes the problem exists. |
| Someone Else Win | Most unlikely person gets the result. | Cold audience. Kills the you-are-special objection. |
| Your Own Story | You struggled then figured it out. | They think your success was luck. Repetition kills that belief. |
| Warning Story | A real person who did not fix this. Show the cost. | Audience at decision point. Fence-sitters who need a push. |

- Real details only. Real names and numbers. Never invented.
- The method is the hero, not you.

## Format 1 — 120 to 180 Second Reel Ad

**PURPOSE:** Cold and warm audience. Scroll-stopping problem-aware content. Drives to VSL or booking page.
**PLATFORM:** Instagram Reels, Facebook Reels, TikTok. Vertical 9:16 only.

### Fixed Structure — 120s Reel (APPROVED MASTER)

| TIMING | BLOCK | ONE JOB | WHAT TO SAY / WRITE | SCORE FOCUS |
|--------|-------|---------|---------------------|-------------|
| 0–3s | HOOK | Stop the scroll | Call-Out, Hard Truth, or Open Loop. One sentence. First cut by 1.2s. | Hook Power (20pts) |
| 3–20s | MIRROR | Make them feel seen | 2–3 parallel you-lines. Real scene. Slip The Lie inside if clean. | Self-Recognition (20pts) |
| 20–35s | THE LIE | Name self-deception | Optional. One line in their own voice. | Self-Recognition (20pts) |
| 35–50s | HARD TRUTH | Create productive tension | One blunt sentence. No softening. No preamble. | Belief Shift (20pts) |
| 50–80s | REFRAME | Flip the wrong belief | Wrong way first. Then flip. This gap is your big idea. | Belief Shift (20pts) |
| 80–105s | METHOD + PROOF | Make it real | Name your REI framework. ONE proof type with real numbers. | Trust/Authority (10pts) |
| 105–120s | OFFER / CTA | Invite next step | Promise OPPOSITE of hook pain. Book a free strategy session. | Action Pull (10pts) |

### Scoring Rubric — 120s / 180s Reel

| DIMENSION | MAX | WHAT EARNS FULL MARKS |
|-----------|-----|----------------------|
| Hook Power | /20 | Stops scroll in 3s. Identity-level sting. Viewer reaction: this is me. |
| Self-Recognition (Mirror) | /20 | Parallel you-lines describe their actual week. The Lie present when clean. |
| Retention Flow | /15 | No dead air. Each block earns the next 5 seconds. |
| Belief Shift (Reframe) | /20 | Old belief named. New belief obvious. Gap surprising but undeniable. |
| Trust / Authority | /10 | Proof has specific numbers. Method has name and clear steps. |
| Action Pull (CTA) | /10 | CTA mirrors hook pain. One specific next step. |
| Simplicity | /5 | One idea. Viewer can explain it in one sentence. |
| **TOTAL** | **100** | **Score below 70 = rewrite. Not film. Not deploy.** |

## Format 2 — 4 to 6 Minute VSL Ad

**PURPOSE:** Warm or retargeting audience. Deeper belief work before booking.
**PLATFORM:** Facebook/Instagram feed, YouTube pre-roll, landing page embed.

### Fixed Structure — 6-Minute VSL (APPROVED MASTER)

| TIMING | BLOCK | ONE JOB | WHAT TO SAY / WRITE | SCORE FOCUS |
|--------|-------|---------|---------------------|-------------|
| 0–15s | HOOK / DIAGNOSIS | Stop and diagnose | Name the exact person and situation. No warmup. | Hook Power (20pts) |
| 15–60s | MIRROR (45s) | Deep feel-seen moment | 4–5 parallel lines. Build the week they are living. Bury The Lie. | Self-Recognition (20pts) |
| 60–90s | THE LIE (30s) | Name self-deception | Its own block in VSL. Then: And I understand why you believe that. | Self-Recognition (20pts) |
| 90–120s | HARD TRUTH (30s) | Create tension | One blunt statement. Then: Here is what most advisors will not say. | Belief Shift (20pts) |
| 120–180s | REFRAME (60s) | Flip the wrong belief | State wrong model. Flip it. Name the gap. Most craft goes here. | Belief Shift (20pts) |
| 180–270s | METHOD (90s) | Make solution concrete | Name REI Method. Break into 3 clear steps with specifics. | Trust/Authority (10pts) |
| 270–330s | PROOF (60s) | Prove it works | ONE proof type. Real names. Real outcomes. | Trust/Authority (10pts) |
| 330–360s | OFFER / CTA (30s) | Invite next step | Book a free strategy session. One action. No over-explaining. | Action Pull (10pts) |

### Scoring Rubric — 6-Min VSL

| DIMENSION | MAX | WHAT EARNS FULL MARKS |
|-----------|-----|----------------------|
| Hook Power | /20 | Stops and holds in 15s. Viewer: How does he know this about me? |
| Self-Recognition (Mirror) | /20 | 45-second mirror feels like viewer's own monologue. The Lie acknowledged with empathy. |
| Retention Flow | /15 | Zero dead air. No self-congratulatory detours. |
| Belief Shift (Reframe) | /20 | Old model explicitly named. New model surprising but obvious. |
| Trust / Authority | /10 | Method has name and steps. Proof has specific numbers. |
| Action Pull (CTA) | /10 | CTA mirrors hook pain. One action. No benefit list. |
| Simplicity | /5 | Six minutes, one idea. |
| **TOTAL** | **100** | **Score below 70 = rewrite. Not film. Not deploy.** |

## Format 3 — YouTube 45-Minute Long Form Series

**PURPOSE:** Authority positioning, YouTube SEO, pipeline warmup. Not direct conversion.
**PLATFORM:** YouTube search and browse. Horizontal 16:9. Thumbnail carries 50% of the hook.

### Fixed Structure — 45-Minute YouTube Episode

| TIMING | BLOCK | ONE JOB | WHAT TO SAY / WRITE | SCORE FOCUS |
|--------|-------|---------|---------------------|-------------|
| 0–2 min | HOOK + PROMISE | Win the click and first 2 min | Call out who this is for. State one outcome. Thumbnail = Call-Out visual. | Hook Power (20pts) |
| 2–8 min | MIRROR (Deep) | Establish deep resonance | Full exploration using real examples from consults. No theory yet. | Self-Recognition (20pts) |
| 8–15 min | LIE + HARD TRUTH | Challenge the wrong model | Unpack The Lie with empathy. Then the Hard Truth. | Belief Shift (20pts) |
| 15–25 min | REFRAME + METHOD | Deliver the big idea | Full REI Method walkthrough. Diagrams, worked examples. Real value. | Trust/Authority (10pts) |
| 25–35 min | PROOF (Extended) | Prove with real stories | 2–3 case situations. Real numbers. Same system pattern. | Trust/Authority (10pts) |
| 35–42 min | IMPLEMENTATION DEPTH | Make them feel capable | Walk through how they would apply this. Pure value, not a pitch. | Retention Flow (15pts) |
| 42–45 min | SOFT CLOSE + NEXT STEP | Move the ready ones forward | ONE CTA: book a strategy session. No hard sell. | Action Pull (10pts) |

### YouTube-Specific Rules

- Thumbnail = 50% of the hook. Bold text, one problem, your face.
- Title mirrors the Mirror block, not the Method.
- First 2 minutes are the whole game: no channel intros before the hook lands.
- One episode = one problem group. Never mix two groups.
- End screens link to the matching Reel ad for that problem group.
- Chapter timestamps are mandatory.

## Section 5 — Structural Validation

Framework validated against 1CC Live Top Performing Ads Breakdown (Caleb Sim). Cross-checked against VSL research. Key confirmations:

- Hook must create identity-level self-recognition, not just attention.
- The Lie is the highest-leverage optional line — when it lands, it nearly doubles self-recognition.
- Reframe is where most craft belongs. It is not a transition — it is the big idea.
- Proof works best when it is specific (real names, real numbers) and tied to the method, not just the result.
- CTA must mirror the hook pain, not summarise the video.

## Section 6 — The 3-Step Sequence (Applied to REI)

**Step 1 — Mirror the exact situation**
Do not describe feelings. Describe the week. Real scenes from real consults. Viewer reaction: how does he know this?

**Step 2 — Name the wrong model, then flip it**
State the conventional wisdom they follow. Show why it keeps them stuck. Offer the REI reframe. Keep it one idea. Do not lecture — illuminate.

**Step 3 — Prove it and invite one action**
One proof type. Method tied to the proof. One CTA. No list of benefits. No multiple offers.

## Section 7 — Hook System (Five Types, Eight Per Group Minimum)

Updated from 3 types to 5. For every problem group produce a minimum 8 hooks across the types below. Keep body locked — only hook swaps for testing.

| HOOK TYPE | FORMULA | EXAMPLE (REI METHOD) |
|-----------|---------|---------------------|
| Hard Truth — 3 per group | [Wrong belief] is not [what they think]. | One property is not a strategy. |
| Question — 2 per group | [Situation] — but [uncomfortable gap]? | Your condo went up. But did your options go up too? |
| Contrarian — 2 per group | Say the opposite of what they expect from an advisor. | Upgrading is not always the right call. |
| Open Loop — 1 per group | Start mid-scene or mid-consequence. They must resolve it. | Here is what happened when my client found out their gain was locked. |
| Wildcard (Warning / Number / Pattern Interrupt / Authority) — 1+ | Choose the type that fits the group's deepest fear or proof angle. | After 200 consults, one mistake keeps showing up. |

**Production Rule:** Build H1–H8 before filming. H9+ expansion hooks added only after Phase 1 data shows a winning type. Body remains unchanged throughout. Test all 8 before any creative decision.

## Section 10 — 3-Phase Production Plan

### Phase 1 — Create (Scripts Before Filming)
- 10 master body scripts — one per core problem group
- 8 hooks per group minimum (3 Hard Truth, 2 Question, 2 Contrarian, 1 Wildcard)
- All 10 groups scripted before any filming begins

### Phase 2 — Film (Top 5 Groups First)
Start with the 5 highest-resonance problem groups:
- Group 2 — One Property Trap
- Group 3 — Paper Gain, No Options
- Group 6 — Forever Home Trap
- Group 5 — Asset Rich, Option Poor
- Group 4 — Comfort Trap / False Safety

### Phase 3 — Scale (Data-Driven Only)
- Keep winning body scripts. Rewrite only if 50% watch rate is below 20%.
- Rotate hooks. Only top 2 per group advance.
- Add 180s only for groups where 120s wins on both retention and lead quality.
- YouTube long form follows Reel winners — film the topic proven in short form first.

Three additions that close the gap between creative strategy and execution.

### 12A — Booking Page Brief (Post-Click Alignment)

**Universal CTA across all formats: "Book a free strategy session." Do not change this per video.**

The CTA text is fixed. What must be aligned is the BOOKING PAGE that the viewer lands on after clicking.

#### The Problem
If a viewer watches the Forever Home Trap video and lands on a generic "Let's Talk Property" booking page, the emotional thread breaks at the click. The feeling built by the video disappears before they fill in the form.

#### The Fix — One Page, Universal Tension Headline

| ELEMENT | BRIEF |
|---------|-------|
| Headline | Your property should be creating your next move. Let's figure out what that is. |
| Sub-headline | Most owners we speak to are not stuck because of the market. They are stuck because no one has reviewed their position with them honestly. |
| What happens in the session (3 points) | 1. Review your current property position. 2. Map what your next move could unlock. 3. Give you a clear recommendation — not a sales pitch. |
| Social proof line | Over 200 property owners have gone through this session. Most left knowing exactly what to do next for the first time in years. |
| CTA button text | Book My Strategy Session |
| What NOT to put on the page | Project listings. Launch promotions. Generic agent bio. Anything that looks like a sales brochure. |

#### When to Build Group-Specific Pages
Only if one problem group converts at 3x the others after Phase 1 data. At that point, build one dedicated landing page for that group only. Not before.

---

### 12B — Retargeting Script Template

**Build RT scripts only for top 3 performing groups after Phase 1. Do not produce RT scripts upfront.**

#### What Retargeting Is
Retargeting targets viewers who already watched 50%+ of your original video. They know the Mirror. They felt it. They just did not click. The RT video skips the Mirror entirely and opens on the tension.

#### RT Script Structure — 45 to 60 Seconds

| TIMING | BLOCK | WHAT TO SAY / WRITE |
|--------|-------|---------------------|
| 0–5s | HARD TRUTH HOOK | Open directly on the hard truth or The Lie. No warm-up. They already know you. |
| 5–20s | PROOF | One proof statement with real numbers. Make it the most specific thing in the video. |
| 20–40s | METHOD REMINDER | Name the REI Method again. Two sentences max. They have seen it — just remind them it exists. |
| 40–55s | SHARPER CTA | Add mild urgency or social proof. "Over 200 sessions done. Most people wait too long." Then: Book your strategy session today. |

#### Decision Rule — When to Build an RT Script

| SIGNAL | THRESHOLD | ACTION |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| 50% Watch Rate | >20% | Group is resonating. Eligible for RT. |
| CTR to booking page | >1.5% | Offer is working. RT will compound it. |
| Lead quality from this group | Consult-quality leads | Confirm before spending on RT. |
| Both thresholds met | Build RT script | Use same group, same angle, same proof type. |

#### Asset Code for RT
`LL_RT_[GROUP]_[ANGLE]_[HOOKTYPE]_60_V01`

Example: `LL_RT_ONEPROPERTYTRAP_FIRSTWINSTUCK_HARDTRUTH_60_V01`

### 12C — Content Flow Decision Table

**Reels validate. VSL deepens. YouTube amplifies. YouTube clips refresh Reels. This is the content flywheel.**

#### Production Sequence — Correct Order

| STAGE | FORMAT | PRODUCES | DECISION GATE |
|-------|--------|----------|---------------|
| Stage 1 | Reel 120s | Validates which problem groups resonate | Run all 10 groups. Data decides who goes to Stage 2. |
| Stage 2 | VSL 6-min | Deepens conversion for winning groups | Build VSL only for groups with 50% watch >20% AND consult-quality leads. |
| Stage 3 | YouTube 45-min | Establishes authority on proven topics | Build YouTube only for groups with consistent consult-quality leads for 4+ weeks. |
| Stage 4 | Reel clips from YouTube | Refreshes Reel testing pool with proven content | Extract 3–5 clips per YouTube episode at the Hard Truth, Reframe, and Method moments. |

#### Upgrade Decision Rules

| UPGRADE | REQUIRES | DO NOT UPGRADE UNTIL |
|---------|----------|----------------------|
| Reel → VSL | 50% watch rate >20% + lead quality confirmed | 2+ consults from this group in 4 weeks |
| VSL → YouTube 45-min | Consistent consult-quality leads from this group | 4+ weeks of data, booking rate >15% |
| YouTube → Clip Reels | YouTube episode published | Extract clips at 0:30 (hook), 8:00 (Hard Truth), 20:00 (Reframe), 30:00 (proof) |
| Reel → 180s version | 120s winner confirmed on retention AND lead quality | Do not upgrade to 180s on watch rate alone |

#### YouTube Clip Extraction Guide

When a YouTube episode is published, extract clips at these four moments for Reel repurposing:

| TIMESTAMP | BLOCK | WHY IT WORKS AS A REEL HOOK |
|-----------|-------|------------------------------|
| 0:30–2:00 | Hook + Promise | Already proven hook. Trim to 60s for Reel. |
| 8:00–10:00 | The Lie + Hard Truth | Highest emotional tension moment. Opens cold perfectly. |
| 18:00–22:00 | Reframe + Method | Delivers the big idea. Works as standalone Reel for warmer audiences. |
| 28:00–32:00 | Proof moment | Social proof clip. Use for retargeting and warm audience Reels. |
