## IDEAL PROSPECT SNAPSHOT

### Primary Prospect — The Parent
- **Age:** 48–65 | **Status:** Married, working-age children (child aged 23–32)
- **Citizenship:** Singapore Citizens (primary); PRs eligible
- **Income:** Combined $10,000–$20,000/month (may be lower if semi-retired)
- **Financial Position:** Owns 1 property (own stay), idle capital $150,000–$500,000+ in FD/savings/CPF OA
- **Mindset Triggers:** "We want to help our child but don't know how", "FD about to mature", "what do we do with idle capital?"
- **Belief Blocks:** "ABSD will make it too expensive", "Our child isn't ready", "We might need the money"

### Secondary Prospect — The Child
- **Age:** 23–32 | **Income:** $3,500–$7,000/month | **Property Status:** None or HDB
- **Mindset:** "I want to build wealth but don't know where to start"
- **Belief Blocks:** "Can't afford a condo on my income", "Should buy HDB first"

## CAMPAIGN RULES

1. Both generations must eventually be present — parent-only session cannot produce a complete Family Legacy strategy
2. Lead with the parent's aspiration, not the child's need — trigger is the parent's desire to leave something
3. Never pitch a specific property in marketing — campaign is about strategy and structure
4. Idle capital framing: compare to FD, not stocks — audience is risk-aware, not risk-tolerant
5. BNTTS framing for the child must be introduced early — child sees investment property as a vehicle, not future home
6. Do not rush to co-purchase recommendation — some parents are better served by idle capital deployment alone
