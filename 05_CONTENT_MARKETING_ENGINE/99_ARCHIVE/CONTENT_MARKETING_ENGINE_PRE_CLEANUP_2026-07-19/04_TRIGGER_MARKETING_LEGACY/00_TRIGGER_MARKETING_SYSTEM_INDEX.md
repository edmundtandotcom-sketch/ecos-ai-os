## Governing Rule

Marketing triggers attract prospects into MAS conversations.

They should be used for:

- YouTube hooks
- short-form hooks
- Meta ad hooks
- caption structures
- WhatsApp broadcast CTAs
- campaign CTAs
- curiosity openers
- future regret angles
- contrast angles
- educational content angles

## Relationship To Advisory Trigger Bank

The Advisory Trigger Question Bank is used inside conversations.

The Marketing Trigger Bank is used to attract prospects into conversations.

Do not confuse the two.

Advisory bank location:

`00_AI_OPERATING_SYSTEM/03_CLIENT_ADVISORY_OS/04_TRIGGER_QUESTIONS/`

Marketing bank location:

`00_AI_OPERATING_SYSTEM/05_CONTENT_MARKETING_ENGINE/04_TRIGGER_MARKETING/`
