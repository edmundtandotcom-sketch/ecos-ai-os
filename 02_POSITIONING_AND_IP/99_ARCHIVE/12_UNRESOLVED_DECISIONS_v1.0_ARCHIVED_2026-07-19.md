## U-1 — Session and deliverable naming (rename vs keep constitution names)

**Status: DECIDED — 2026-07-16**

**Decision required:** whether to adopt the rename proposed in `90_SOURCE_MARKDOWN\02_SECOND_LADDER_NEW_EXECUTION.md` — "Second Property Strategy Session™" replacing "Next Move Strategy Session™", and "Second Property Route Map™" replacing "Second Property Ladder Blueprint™" — or keep the names currently locked at constitution level.

**Why it matters:** the constitution names were only recently locked (Decision 055, 2026-07-11) and are referenced across the AI OS. A rename inside the Phase 1 marketing package that isn't reflected upstream creates a two-name problem across marketing assets, the CRM and public-facing copy.

**Options considered:** (A) keep constitution names; (B) adopt the rename inside this package only and flag the conflict; (C) adopt the rename and bump the constitution immediately.

**Edmund's ruling:** Adopt the rename (Option B). "Second Property Strategy Session™" and "Second Property Route Map™" are now the names used going forward within this Phase 1 package (see `10_MARKETING_FOUNDATION_v1.0.md`, Section 1).

**Follow-up action item (not yet done):** the AI OS constitution (`00_AI_OPERATING_SYSTEM\CLAUDE.md`, currently v4.3) still states "Next Move Strategy Session™" as the locked public name (§2). This is now a live naming conflict between an approved constitution and this now-APPROVED Phase 1 master. A separate constitution version bump (v4.4) is required in a future session to carry the rename upstream — Edmund's ruling here does not itself edit the constitution. Until that bump happens, treat the constitution as showing a superseded name for this specific detail only.

**Affected assets:** any future landing page, ad, CRM field or script referencing the session or deliverable name; the constitution itself (§2).

**Status: DECIDED — 2026-07-16**
