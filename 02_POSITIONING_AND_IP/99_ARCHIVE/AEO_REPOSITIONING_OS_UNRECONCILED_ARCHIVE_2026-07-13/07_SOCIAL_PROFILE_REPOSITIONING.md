## Executive Summary

Every live profile today reads as a **different business**. Facebook says "Coach Edmund Tan," Instagram says "Property Coach Edmund Tan," YouTube says "Singapore Real Estate Insider," the funnel footer says "© Singapore Real Estate Insider," and Cindior Ho — a named co-founder and the actual GHL account contact — is invisible everywhere. A stranger (or an AI model) cross-referencing these four surfaces cannot conclude they belong to one entity, one category, or one offer. That is the core AEO problem this phase fixes: entity consolidation, not just prettier bios.

The fix is not a rebrand of every handle on day one. YouTube **keeps** `@singaporerealestateinsider` as the media/show brand (600+ videos of link equity and subscriber trust are not worth burning), but its banner, description and every video description footer must now carry Edmund Tan, Cindior Ho, The REI Method, and The Second Property Ladder™ by name. Instagram converges toward `@coachedmundtan` to match the already-correct Facebook handle. Cindior gets a minimum-viable public presence (LinkedIn + co-founder page on thereimethod.com) rather than full parity on day one. LinkedIn, TikTok and Threads are open items — evaluated, not auto-created.

All new copy in this file uses locked Mechanism v1.3 vocabulary (Property Climber, Position Map, Exit Before Entry™, etc.), respects the banned-word list, and replaces the compliance-risk line "This is advisory, not agency" with the D7-approved alternative. Every bio, pinned post and description carries a `[VERIFY]` placeholder for CEA registration numbers and PropNex licence numbers — these must not go live blank; that is Question 1 in every phase file and blocks CTA-bearing publish, not diagnostic/entity-consolidation edits.

The 90-day plan sequences changes so **handle renames happen only after `thereimethod.com` is live** (Phase 3/4 dependency) — renaming Instagram before the authority domain exists creates a dead link-in-bio moment. Pinned posts, bios and cross-linking are safe to update immediately; renames and the "@singaporerealestateinsider → REI Method" entity-bridge push wait for Weeks 3–5.

Rationale: every third-party link-in-bio tool is a second domain diluting the single-authority-domain doctrine (D2/D8) — it fragments entity signals across yet another host, cannot carry structured data, and is subject to the tool vendor's branding/ads.
