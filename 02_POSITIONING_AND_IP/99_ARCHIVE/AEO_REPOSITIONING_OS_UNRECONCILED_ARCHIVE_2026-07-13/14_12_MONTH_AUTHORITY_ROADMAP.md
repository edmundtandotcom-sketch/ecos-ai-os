## EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Doc 13 covers the first 90 days in weekly granularity. This document extends that into a 12-month arc at quarterly and monthly resolution, because the category-ownership bet (Master Brief §1: "The Second Property Ladder" has ~zero search demand today) only pays off over a multi-quarter horizon — Month 1's AI-visibility scores are expected to be almost entirely 0, and the entire measurement system exists to prove the climb from there, not to show instant results.

The year is sequenced in four quarters: **Q1** builds the foundation (entity, compliance, first 90 pages, diagnostic live) — this is doc 13 verbatim. **Q2** scales demand-capture content to ~114 cumulative pages, builds review velocity on GBP, and deepens the YouTube-to-article integration. **Q3** is where the deferred, higher-risk moves happen — the Legacy Launch/New Launch Ladder™ rename pass under SPL, and a deliberate backlink/PR push — because by Q3 the entity and content foundation is durable enough to survive a rename without fragmenting signal again. **Q4** is an audit-and-decide quarter: a full authority audit, a review of whether the category term is gaining real AI-engine citation, and a scale decision (double down, adjust vocabulary strategy, or hold cadence) for Year 2.

Page-count cadence, monthly themes, and running totals below are taken directly from doc 04 §5 (30/30/30 pages in Months 1–3, then ~8/month) — this reaches **162 cumulative pages by Month 12**, comfortably past a 150-page bar. The risk register closes the document: eight named risks, each with an early-warning signal and a mitigation, because a roadmap this dependent on unverified figures, an unbuilt product, and a brand-new domain's ranking lag needs its risks named as explicitly as its milestones.

## QUARTERLY REPOSITIONING REVIEW AGENDA

Run in the final week of each quarter (Weeks 13, 26, 39, 52), ~2 hours, both founders present.

1. **Entry/exit criteria check** — walk the quarter's exit criteria above line by line; do not advance to the next quarter's higher-risk moves (especially Q3's rename pass) until every checked item is genuinely true, not "mostly true."
2. **Compliance re-confirmation** — re-confirm CEA/PropNex status even if resolved earlier; regulatory advertising guidance can change (D7), and this is the checkpoint to catch it.
3. **Cooling-measures/regulatory landscape scan** — has anything changed (ABSD rates, MOP, TDSR/MSR, loan tenure caps) that invalidates a live page's figures ahead of its scheduled refresh? Escalate off-cycle if yes.
4. **Proof pipeline health** — how many of the 7 proof categories are fully populated vs. still gaps; re-prioritise sourcing for the quarter ahead.
5. **AI-visibility trend read** — not just the raw scores but the *shape* of the trend: is citation appearing first on category-defined terms or demand-capture terms? This tells you whether the two-layer strategy (doc 04's central tension) is actually working as designed or whether one layer is carrying all the weight.
6. **Capacity reality check** — is the 3–5h/week founder budget still holding, or has client work encroached? Adjust the next quarter's page-cadence ambition honestly rather than let the backlog silently grow.
7. **Backlog grooming** — retire or reprioritise any doc-13/14 action that slipped and is no longer relevant; do not let a stale action item linger past one full quarter.
