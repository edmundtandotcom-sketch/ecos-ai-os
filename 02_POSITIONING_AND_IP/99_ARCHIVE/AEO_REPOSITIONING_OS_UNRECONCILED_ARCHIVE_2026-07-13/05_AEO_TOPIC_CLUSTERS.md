# 05 — AEO Topic Clusters
**Owner:** Sonnet agent (Phase 5) · **Domain:** all pages below live on `thereimethod.com` (D2) · **Status:** DRAFT for orchestrator QA

## Executive Summary

Ten clusters map what Property Climbers actually type into Google and ask ChatGPT/Perplexity across the climb: choosing who to trust, the HDB-to-condo upgrade, second-property/investment mechanics, head-to-head comparisons, timing/policy, mistakes, the Second Property Ladder™ framework itself, genuinely distinct local/estate content, long-term/legacy planning, and the New Launch/Legacy Launch bridge. Every cluster routes to the same three funnel objects: the **Which Step diagnostic**, a **calculator/tool**, and the **Position Map session** — never a generic "book a call." The "best property advisor" intent is handled as a how-to-choose guide, not a self-proclaimed "best" claim, per CEA guardrails (D7). Slugs use five consistent patterns — `/guides/`, `/compare/`, `/mistakes/`, `/tools/`, `/case-studies/` — so this document merges cleanly with doc 04's sitemap. FAQ questions are written as literal AI/search phrasing, which means a handful legitimately contain the word "agent" (people search that way); our own titles, slugs, and hooks never do. All regulatory figures (ABSD, MOP, SSD, CPF, TDSR/MSR, loan tenure caps) are date-stamped July 2026 and flagged [VERIFY] — none should ship without a compliance/records check. Volume deliverables below (100 questions, 50 pages, 50 supporting pages, 50 video titles, 50 hooks) are deduped against the clusters, not additive to them.
