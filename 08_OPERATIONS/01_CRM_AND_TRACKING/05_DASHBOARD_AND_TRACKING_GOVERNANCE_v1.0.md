# Dashboard and Tracking Governance
Version: v1.0
Status: APPROVED
Date: 2026-07-20

Dashboards support decisions; they do not redefine Marketing, Sales or Advisory doctrine.

## Every dashboard requires

- decision or question served;
- metric definitions and owner;
- live source and refresh cadence;
- reporting window and filters;
- acceptable data latency;
- missing-data behavior;
- privacy level;
- last QA date.

## Ownership

- Marketing defines acquisition questions and campaign decisions.
- Sales defines progression and conversion questions.
- Client Advisory defines advisory-delivery quality questions.
- Operations implements instrumentation, transformations, dashboards and alerts.

Use aggregate or de-identified views in the general AI OS. Named drill-downs remain inside authorized private systems.
