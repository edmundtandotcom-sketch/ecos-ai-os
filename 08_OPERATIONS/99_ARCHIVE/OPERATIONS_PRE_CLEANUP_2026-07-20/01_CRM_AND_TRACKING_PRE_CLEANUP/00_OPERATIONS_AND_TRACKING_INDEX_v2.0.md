## 1. Operating Role

Operations and Tracking is the measurement and execution-control layer for Client Edition.

It owns:
- CRM and pipeline rules;
- lead quality and booking status;
- dashboard and ad performance tracking;
- automation script ownership;
- weekly / monthly reporting rhythm;
- follow-up cadence and accountability;
- nurture, reactivation, trigger monitoring, and database movement;
- operational handoff from campaign execution back into business decisions.

It does not own:
- campaign strategy or creative drafts;
- advisory frameworks;
- buyer psychology source files;
- raw research references;
- old scripts that no longer control current dashboards.

## 4. Folder Boundary

### Operations owns

- dashboards;
- ad result trackers;
- CRM pipeline SOPs;
- lead quality sheets;
- booking status;
- follow-up rhythm;
- nurture and reactivation rules;
- database movement and trigger monitoring;
- automation scripts currently used to pull or process data;
- reporting templates.

### Content Marketing owns

- campaign packs;
- ad copy;
- image/video creative concepts;
- campaign-specific landing pages;
- campaign-specific outputs and launch handoffs.

### Knowledge Vault owns

- reusable learnings;
- market references;
- buyer language;
- case proof;
- transcript and consult insight.

### Client OS owns

- advisory logic;
- client diagnosis;
- conversation conversion;
- ProDeck and consult delivery.

## 5. Clean Saving Rule

When unsure where to save something:

- If it helps run the business, measure performance, manage CRM movement, or reactivate leads, save it here.
- If it is for a specific marketing campaign, save it inside that campaign pack.
- If it is only useful as reference, save it in Knowledge Vault.
- If it is outdated, save it in Archive.

## 6. Current Decision

Operations is not a dumping ground for every spreadsheet.

Only files that are actively used to measure leads, bookings, follow-up, nurture movement, campaigns, or business performance should stay active.
