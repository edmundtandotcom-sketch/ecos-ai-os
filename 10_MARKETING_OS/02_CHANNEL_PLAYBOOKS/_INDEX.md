The seventeen retained channel playbooks are useful operating drafts from the pre-cleanup Marketing OS. They remain DRAFT until their platform-dependent guidance is verified against current live platform behavior.

## Use boundary

- Playbooks govern acquisition/distribution use of a channel.
- Reusable content-production doctrine belongs in Content Marketing Engine.
- Active execution belongs in Active Campaigns.
- Sales follow-up, nurture and pipeline progression belong in Sales OS.
- GHL implementation belongs in Operations.

Files such as `CRM_NURTURE.md`, email, SMS or WhatsApp playbooks may inform acquisition handoffs but do not govern Sales follow-up or CRM automation.

Before use, record verification date, campaign, applicable audience, compliance limits and owner.
