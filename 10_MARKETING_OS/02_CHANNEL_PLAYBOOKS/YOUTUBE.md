## Strategic role
Client Advisory primary long-form authority channel — seven business roles per the Authority Engine: positioning, pre-consult education, objection removal, evergreen search, trust transfer, content mother-asset, demand qualification. Agent Edition: the engine documents non-property adaptation rules (§31) but no Market Maker YouTube episode was found in inventory — treat as unconfirmed for this engine.

## Best use cases
Flagship authority episodes on Legacy Launch pillar topics (Appreciation Is Not Progression, Idle Million, One Property Trap, Decoupling Doubt, etc.); repurposing mother-asset for reels/shorts/carousels/email/WhatsApp; evergreen search-intent capture; pre-roll ads feeding GOOGLE_ADS.md.

## Required inputs
Guided Direction Intake completed (Mode A quick / B full blueprint / C AI-recommended) or an approved Episode Direction Board; topic qualifying for chosen length (≥4 of: high anxiety, high decision value, high misunderstanding, multiple wrong beliefs/objections, strong search/evergreen potential, strong authority payoff, real examples, clip-worthy moments, direct relevance to a next step); 3+ title angles, 3+ thumbnail angles; playlist assignment; CTA plan; clip-extraction map.

## Process
Intake → Direction Board → episode-type selection (12 types) → duration decision (8–12 / 20–30 / 45–60 min) → script via the 27-field Long-Form Output Template → 100-point QC checklist (below 70 = do not film) → production brief → film → repurposing map (minimum from a flagship episode: 5–10 reels/shorts, 3–5 image angles, 1 carousel, 1 broadcast, 1 retargeting clip, 1 next-video angle).

## Outputs
Episode Direction Board, script, title/thumbnail options, description, chapter outline, repurposing map — saved to the campaign folder under `05_CONTENT_MARKETING_ENGINE/`. Marketing OS Framework/Creative Library reference, do not duplicate.

## KPIs
Define: view-through rate, average view duration, CTA click-through, subscriber growth, clip-derived asset count, pre-consult mentions during the actual consult. **No live baseline exists** — brief §5's Tier-2 aggregate covers paid leads only, not organic YouTube. YouTube currently has **zero measured baseline of any tier**.

## Common failure modes
Filming before the Direction Board is approved (blank-page scripting); choosing a length the topic doesn't earn (padding); no clip-extraction plan built into the script; constant micro-CTA interruption instead of the 5-layer strategic CTA system; treating title/thumbnail packaging as an afterthought.
