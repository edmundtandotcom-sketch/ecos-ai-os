# Data Source and Evidence Rules
Version: v1.0
Status: APPROVED
Date: 2026-07-20

Every evidence claim must be labeled as one of:
- live system observation;
- dated campaign result;
- verified external fact;
- internal interpretation;
- hypothesis.

Record source, retrieval date, reporting window, transformation and known gaps. Live systems outrank dated summaries for current state. Client-identifiable rows and transcripts remain private. Only de-identified, reviewed patterns may enter Marketing OS or Knowledge Vault.

If evidence conflicts with a governing document, preserve both, stop execution and route the contradiction to Command Center.
