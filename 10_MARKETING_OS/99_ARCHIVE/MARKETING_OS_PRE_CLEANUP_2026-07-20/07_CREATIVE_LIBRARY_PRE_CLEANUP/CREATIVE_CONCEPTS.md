## Purpose
A fit-selection catalogue of creative concept types — not a performance ranking. Nothing here is proven for REI. Use this to choose a concept format that fits the message and awareness stage, not because it "performed" — it has not been tested locally. Every entry is Tier 4 (observed market usage elsewhere) or Tier 5 (hypothesis / rubric only).

No local performance history exists for any concept in this file (see `10_PERFORMANCE/HISTORICAL_BASELINE.md` — the only Tier 2 data on file is channel/campaign-level CPL, not creative-concept-level).

## Concept Taxonomy

### 1. WhatsApp-message / chat-screenshot
**Fit:** Problem-aware to solution-aware. Mimics a real conversation a Property Climber would have with an advisor or a friend — lowers guard, feels native to how Singaporeans actually discuss property.
**Best use:** TOF-to-MOF reel, retargeting after a soft-CTA ("comment REVIEW").
**Failure modes:** Feels staged if the copy is too polished; breaks trust if used for a hard-sell CTA; platform may flag as deceptive UI if it mimics native chrome too closely.
**Production needs:** Screenshot template, 2 "voices" (advisor + owner), captioned for sound-off viewing.
**Evidence:** Tier 4/5 — common format in guru swipe files (see `06_KNOWLEDGE_VAULT/04_MARKETING_REFERENCES/`); no REI instance built yet.
**Existing-ad fit:** None of the 16 Legacy Launch ads are titled in a way that suggests this format.

### 2. Calculator / number-reveal
**Fit:** Interest-to-desire. Makes an abstract NAV/ABSD/CPF concept concrete with a specific number ("$X unlocked", "$X ABSD gap").
**Best use:** Diagnostic-quiz-adjacent content, retargeting owners who engaged with the Scorecard/diagnostic funnel.
**Failure modes:** Any dollar figure not sourced becomes a fabricated claim — every number must trace to a real, cited case or be marked illustrative; CEA/PDPA claim risk if presented as guaranteed outcome (see `01_BUSINESS_AND_BRAND/COMPLIANCE.md`).
**Production needs:** On-screen number animation or static reveal card; a verified source figure per number used.
**Evidence:** Tier 4/5 — matches "The 90% Filter" and "Financial Structuring" title patterns (see below); no confirmed script content reviewed.
**Existing-ad fit:** CR-20260710-LL-TBD-VID-009 ("The 90% Filter"), CR-...-013 ("Financial Structuring") — titles suggest this concept; not confirmed without script review.

### 3. News-editorial
**Fit:** Awareness-stage, broad reach. Borrows the authority frame of a news segment or headline ("Breaking: ...") to earn attention and credibility.
**Best use:** Cold traffic TOF, market-timing or policy-change angles (ABSD changes, cooling measures).
**Failure modes:** Can misrepresent REI as a news outlet if not disclosed as sponsored/advisory content; requires genuine, current market fact — never invent a "breaking" claim.
**Production needs:** Lower-third graphics, presenter delivery in a news register, a real, dated market fact.
**Evidence:** Tier 4/5 — observed in competitor ad libraries (`03_MARKET_INTELLIGENCE/AD_LIBRARY.md`); not built by REI.
**Existing-ad fit:** None of the 16 titles suggest this format.

### 4. Split-screen comparison
**Fit:** Interest-to-desire. Contrasts two paths (Agent vs Business, Taker vs Maker, Owner vs Strategist) side by side — strong for reframe-led messaging.
**Best use:** Flagship reframe ads for either engine (Agent Edition Angle 1 "Agent vs Business" is built this way — see `04_AGENT_EDITION_OS/02_MARKETING/MAS_AdAngle_Master_MarketMaker_v2.0_DRAFT.md` §3 Angle 1 visual spec).
**Failure modes:** Both sides must be fair contrasts, not straw-manned, or it reads as manipulative; needs a strong single-word anchor (e.g. "BUILT") to land in 1–2 seconds.
**Production needs:** Two-panel edit or literal split frame, one anchor word/graphic.
**Evidence:** Tier 4 (confirmed used in Agent Edition Angle 1 spec) / Tier 5 for Client engine variants.
**Existing-ad fit:** CR-...-005 ("Theory Vs Practical Agent"), CR-...-006 ("NAV vs Profit Thinking"), CR-...-014 ("Comfort Vs Demand") — "Vs" titling strongly suggests split-screen or direct-contrast structure; not confirmed without script review.

### 5. Myth-vs-reality
**Fit:** Problem-aware. Directly names and corrects a belief block (e.g. "You need to sell to buy again" → decoupling). Matches the MAS Trigger Bank "Myth-bust" post template.
**Best use:** Educational carousel/post, warm audience re-engagement.
**Failure modes:** The "myth" must be a belief REI has actually encountered (TRAPS bank / VoC evidence), not an invented strawman.
**Production needs:** Two-line myth/reality card or 20–30s reel; cite the correcting fact.
**Evidence:** Tier 4 — a live example exists in `05_CONTENT_MARKETING_ENGINE/04_TRIGGER_MARKETING/MAS_TRIGGER_MARKETING_BANK_v1.0.md` §3 "Post Type: Myth-bust" (decoupling myth). That template itself is Tier 4 (approved trigger copy, no attached performance data).
**Existing-ad fit:** CR-...-008 ("Wrong Name Wrong Zone") and CR-...-003 ("The Wrong Checklist") titles suggest myth-correction framing; not confirmed without script review.

### 6. Talking-head / direct-to-camera
**Fit:** All stages. Founder-authority format; builds trust fastest when the founder is the proof (see `02_POSITIONING_AND_IP/Founder_Case_Masters.md`, [VERIFY]-wrapped).
**Best use:** Retargeting, YouTube Authority Engine content, any angle needing founder credibility.
**Failure modes:** Weak without a specific story or number; generic "hi I'm Edmund" openers underperform against pattern-interrupt hooks per Reel Rulebook (`00_FOUNDATIONS/LAYER_3A_REEL_SCRIPT_RULEBOOK_v1.1.md`).
**Production needs:** On-location or studio shoot, teleprompter script (see `02_REEL_SCRIPTS/LL_TOF_REEL_SCRIPTS_TELEPROMPTER_v1.0.md` for the house format).
**Evidence:** Tier 5 — all 10 existing LL reel scripts in this format are rubric-scored only, zero market data.
**Existing-ad fit:** CR-...-007 ("The Obligation Agent"), CR-...-011 ("Who Buys It From You") plausibly founder-POV direct address given title phrasing; not confirmed.

### 7. Whiteboard / explainer
**Fit:** Interest-to-desire. Breaks down a mechanism (decoupling, ABSD, the 5 Moves) step by step — matches the "safe order" USP (mechanism delivered, not advertised).
**Best use:** MOF nurture content, consult-prep material, YouTube long-form companion to a short ad.
**Failure modes:** Can run long / lose retention on paid social if not cut to platform length; risks stating a platform-specific claim as fact without verification (Rule §2.9).
**Production needs:** Whiteboard or motion-graphic build, scripted breakdown matching an approved framework (`05_FRAMEWORK_LIBRARY/`).
**Evidence:** Tier 5.
**Existing-ad fit:** CR-...-013 ("Financial Structuring") title fit; not confirmed.

### 8. Case-study / before-after
**Fit:** Desire-to-trigger. Uses a proof point to make the mechanism concrete ("Couple A did this in 2021. Today they have two condos.") — matches Trigger Bank "Post Type: Story".
**Best use:** BOF retargeting, consult-booking push.
**Failure modes:** PII RULE — never use real names/phones/photos of actual clients without explicit consent on file; the Trigger Bank template already uses placeholder names ("Mr and Mrs Tan") for this reason — follow that pattern. Every figure must be sourced or explicitly marked illustrative.
**Production needs:** Verified case detail or an explicitly labelled illustrative composite; approval from Proof Registry (`07_BRAND_AND_PROOF_BANK/02_PROOF_REGISTRY.md`) before claiming a real outcome.
**Evidence:** Tier 4 (template proven as approved copy) / Tier 5 (no attached ad performance).
**Existing-ad fit:** CR-...-015 ("Same Project Different Outcome"), CR-...-012 ("The Unit We Rejected") — outcome-contrast titling fits this concept; not confirmed.

### 9. Text-on-screen / notes-style post
**Fit:** All stages, especially scroll-stopping TOF. Mimics a personal "notes app" reflection — low production cost, high perceived authenticity.
**Best use:** Daily/organic content cadence, testing new angles cheaply before video production.
**Failure modes:** Overused format across the category — needs a genuinely sharp line to stand out; easy to default to generic motivational filler (prohibited under Tight Ship style).
**Production needs:** Minimal — text template, brand type treatment.
**Evidence:** Tier 5. Matches ANG-001 "Idle Million" best-format list ("notes-style post") in `05_CONTENT_MARKETING_ENGINE/01_CAMPAIGN_LEGACY_LAUNCH/01_ACTIVE_FOUNDATION/02_LL_ANGLE_MESSAGE_MATRIX_ACTIVE_v1.0.md`.
**Existing-ad fit:** Not applicable to the 16 finished video ads (this is a static/organic format).

### 10. Showflat / on-location walk-and-talk
**Fit:** Problem-aware, credibility-building. Physically situates the advisor at a real project — grounds abstract strategy talk in a concrete decision point.
**Best use:** Comparison angles ("this unit vs that unit"), objection-handling content.
**Failure modes:** Risk of reading as a project-selling ad, which breaks the Legacy Launch campaign boundary rule ("this is not a project-selling campaign" — `01_LL_CAMPAIGN_BRIEF_ACTIVE_v1.0.md` §1). Must stay framed as advisory review, not project pitch.
**Production needs:** Location access, on-site filming.
**Evidence:** Tier 5.
**Existing-ad fit:** CR-20260710-LL-TBD-VID-001 ("Showflat Loop") — clear title match; content not reviewed to confirm framing stays advisory (not project-selling).

### 11. Comparison chart / report-style image
**Fit:** Interest stage. Visualises "appreciation vs progression," "NAV vs profit," or similar gap — matches ANG-002's best-format list.
**Best use:** Static feed ad, carousel first frame.
**Failure modes:** Chart must use real or clearly-illustrative data; a fabricated-looking chart with no source undermines advisor credibility.
**Production needs:** Design template, one clean data point or framework diagram.
**Evidence:** Tier 5. Matches ANG-002 "Appreciation Is Not Progression" best-format ("strategic gap chart," "report-style image") in the LL Angle Matrix.
**Existing-ad fit:** CR-...-006 ("NAV vs Profit Thinking") plausible fit; not confirmed.
