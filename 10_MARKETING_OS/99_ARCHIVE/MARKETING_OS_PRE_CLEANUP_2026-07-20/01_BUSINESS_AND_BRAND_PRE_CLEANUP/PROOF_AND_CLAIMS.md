Purpose: the rules that decide **what REI may claim in marketing, and with what proof.** No claim ships without a citable source and an evidence tier.

## 1. No-fabrication rules (non-negotiable)
- No fabricated results, testimonials, statistics, market claims, credentials, or deadlines.
- Every figure states its **source file** and **evidence tier** (see `00_COMMAND_CENTER\SOURCE_OF_TRUTH_MAP.md`).
- Real past figures = proof. **Future/projected figures are illustrative** — conservative/base/upside, caveated.
- "Never make numbers up." Use `[FILL IN]` when a real figure is not on hand (`06_COMMUNICATION_STYLE.md` §3).
- Every case **proves the ORDER, never the pick.** A case that reads "we chose a winning property" is off-category and barred, no matter the numbers (`Case_Study_Bank` §The Rule).

## 2. Evidence-tier gate for claims
| To claim… | You need… |
|---|---|
| A commercial result (revenue/client/CAC) | Tier 1 — **currently NONE exists.** Do not make these claims. |
| A performance metric (CTR/CPL/bookings) | Tier 2 — archived Codex aggregates only (PII-free, through 2026-06-24). |
| An external stat | Tier 3 with a cited source. |
| "Competitors do X" | Tier 4 — observed usage; visibility ≠ proof. |
| A creative/copy "winner" | Tier 1/2 data. Rubric scores (Tier 5) may NOT be called winners (C12). |

## 4. Case-study claim rules (from the Case Study Bank)
- Real clients only; **written PDPA consent on file before drafting**; anonymised by default, named only with explicit written consent.
- Figures rounded but verifiable — every figure traceable to a document in the client file.
- No forecasted returns, no performance promises, no "guaranteed" anything.
- Property names appear only as historical fact of what a client did — never as a recommendation.
- Every published case passes **CEA advertising-compliance review** and carries the standard disclaimer line (see `COMPLIANCE.md`).
- **Case Type 5 (structure/two-name) is the highest-risk proof** — legal/compliance review before ANY use; out of cold ads by default.

## 5. Founder cases (interim proof)
Until the client bank fills, the founders' own climb is used as category-true proof — "We built it before we advised it." Every founder figure remains `[VERIFY]` (§3) and every structure story passes compliance review before public use.
