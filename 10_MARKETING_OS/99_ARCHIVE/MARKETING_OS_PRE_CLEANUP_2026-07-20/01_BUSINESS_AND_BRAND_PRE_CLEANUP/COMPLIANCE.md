Purpose: one place for the advertising, data, and structure-compliance rules a marketer must obey. Three regimes: **CEA** (advertising conduct), **PDPA** (personal data/consent), **IRAS** (tax-structure representations).

## 1. CEA — advertising & advisory conduct
Found in: `Case_Study_Bank_7_Proof_Categories.md` (§Writing Rules, §QC, §Compliance–Case Type 5); `06_COMMUNICATION_STYLE.md` (§8 forbidden language).
- Every published case study passes **CEA advertising-compliance review** and carries the **standard disclaimer line** (draft and approve the line once, apply to every published case — **still open**).
- No forecasted returns, no performance promises, no "guaranteed" anything.
- Property names appear only as historical fact of what a client did — never as a recommendation.
- Forbidden claim language (never use): guaranteed profit · sure make money · best buy · confirm good investment · once-in-a-lifetime · last chance · must enter now · no-brainer · property guru · top agent · number one agent · "trusted advisor" without proof. (`06_COMMUNICATION_STYLE.md` §8)
- Identity: REI markets as a **property advisory practice, not an agency**; staff are advisors/coaches, not "agents" (client-facing); PropNex is the regulatory wrapper (`00_AI_OPERATING_SYSTEM\CLAUDE.md` §10).

## 2. PDPA — personal data & consent
Found in: `Case_Study_Bank_7_Proof_Categories.md` (§Writing Rules, §Harvest Engine); `Which_Step_Are_You_On_Diagnostic.md` (§8 GHL build spec, §VERIFY BEFORE LAUNCH).
- **Written PDPA consent on file before any case study is drafted.** Anonymised by default; named only with explicit written consent.
- Consent status is a tracked CRM field (none / verbal / written-anonymised / written-named).
- Lead-capture forms (e.g. the diagnostic contact slide) require **PDPA-compliant consent copy** — confirm before launch.
- **Marketing OS PII rule:** archived lead exports contain real names/phones/emails. **Never copy PII into the Marketing OS** — aggregates only, source-cited (build brief §2.10).

## 3. IRAS — tax-structure representations (highest risk)
Found in: `Case_Study_Bank_7_Proof_Categories.md` (§Compliance–Case Type 5); `Which_Step_Are_You_On_Diagnostic.md` (§VERIFY: ABSD rates, §9 ABSD central).
- **Case Type 5 (structure / two-name) stories are the highest-risk proof.** Feature ONLY structures lawful in substance and form. **Never frame any arrangement as a stamp-duty workaround** — IRAS has pursued contrived 99-to-1 arrangements; CEA holds marketing to account.
- Every Case Type 5 story passes **legal/compliance review before ANY deployment** and stays **out of cold ads by default.**
- Any asset referencing **ABSD / tax exposure** (e.g. diagnostic C6, Result 3) must **re-check current ABSD rates and wording against IRAS before going live.** Never assert tax figures from memory.

## 4. Platform policy
Meta / Google / YouTube / GHL ad-policy and character/format limits are **not asserted from memory.** Every platform-specific asset is labelled **"DRAFT — pending platform verification"** until checked against the live platform (build brief §2.9; AI OS constitution §11).

## 5. Marketing compliance gates (apply before publish)
1. Claim passes the evidence-tier gate (`PROOF_AND_CLAIMS.md` §2) and carries no un-cleared `[VERIFY]` figure.
2. PDPA consent on file for any named person / client figure.
3. CEA review + standard disclaimer line present.
4. Any structure/tax claim: legal review done; ABSD re-verified; not in cold ads.
5. Platform specs verified or flagged.
6. Forbidden-language and bloat checks passed (`BRAND_VOICE.md` §7–8).
