## 1. The governing voice rule (judge every asset against this first)
> "Marketing should not make Edmund and Cindior look like property salespeople. Marketing should make them look like strategic property advisors who help existing private-property families make better next-move decisions."
> — `06_COMMUNICATION_STYLE.md` §1

Advisor, not salesperson. Every draft passes this sentence before the forbidden-language and bloat checks.

## 2. Numbers-as-proof discipline (hard rule)
From `06_COMMUNICATION_STYLE.md` §3:
- "Real details. Real names, real numbers, real specifics. '$45,000 in two calls' beats 'great results' every time."
- **"Never make numbers up."** Use a `[FILL IN]` placeholder if a real figure is not on hand.
- State exact entry / exit / dollar gain — never a vague claim.
- "Don't teach the chart. State what the chart means." Always translate data into its implication.
- Caveat: past figures are proof; **future/projected figures are illustrative — conservative/base/upside, caveated** (per Advisory Philosophy §7). Founder figures remain `[VERIFY]` (C10).

## 3. The method is the hero, not the founder
- "The method is the hero, not you. Even your wins should prove the system works."
- Choose the **most unlikely** proof person — the more unlikely, the more credit goes to the system, not the person.

## 4. Two registers — keep both, never mix in one asset
| Register | Where it lives | Character |
|---|---|---|
| **Polished MAS ad/script copy** | All written ads, scripts, posts, decks | Advisory, clean, boardroom-adjacent, "trusted advisor" |
| **Colloquial Singlish consult register** | Live/in-person consults, trigger questions | Warm, local — e.g. "Eh bro, have you ever thought ah…" |

The Singlish register is for live one-to-one only; it does **not** belong in polished ad copy, and vice-versa. (`06_COMMUNICATION_STYLE.md` §5)

## 5. Brand signature lines (keep intact)
- "We reject 9 out of 10 properties."
- "90% of new launches should be rejected."
- "Most buyers ask 'Do I like it?' We ask 'Who buys it from me?'"
- "It's a trap with nice flooring." (`§6`)

## 6. Approved language (use freely)
strategic property advisory · property decision clarity · safer next move · exit-safe strategy · family wealth protection · risk-managed property move · structured property decision · property wealth strategy · long-term net worth growth · property as an asset engine · avoid costly property mistakes. (`§7`, from `02_PROPERTY_POSITIONING_RULES.md`)

## 7. Forbidden language (never use — advisory/CEA risk)
guaranteed profit · sure make money · best buy · cheapest unit · confirm good investment · once-in-a-lifetime opportunity · everyone is buying · last chance · must enter now · no-brainer · fastest way to get rich · property guru · top agent · number one agent · trusted advisor without proof · buy because market is hot. (`§8`)

## 8. Banned bloat phrases (Tight Ship)
"In today's fast-paced world…" · "It depends…" (without a pick) · "There are many factors…" · "Let's dive in…" · "Here's a comprehensive breakdown…" · "Absolutely, you're right…" · "Great question…". (`§9`)

## 9. Objection handling — empathy first
Objections are protective. Agree/empathise first, then reframe — never argue. Signature: Control objection → "You don't lose control. You gain clarity." (`§10`)

## 10. Tone words
- **Yes:** clear · calm · strategic · advisory · protective · practical · educational · direct · emotionally aware · "hard truth with soft heart."
- **No:** pushy · desperate · arrogant · overly technical · academic · generic · fear-driven · hype-driven. (`§11`)

## 11. Script output rules (Doc 19, verbatim source)
Word-for-word (not outline); natural spoken English, short paragraphs; use "you" / "your family"; avoid corporate phrasing and dense jargon; pauses / line breaks / emphasis cues. "Every script must sound like a trusted advisor, not a salesperson." (`§2`)

## How to use this file
- Load before writing any client-facing copy, script, ad, post, or consult line.
- Run every draft against: the governing rule (§1) → forbidden list (§7) → bloat list (§8). Fail any = rewrite.
- Real exact numbers or `[FILL IN]` only; never invent figures; translate data into implication.
- Match register to context; keep the method as the hero; reframe objections empathy-first.
- **Engine-2 (agent) voice** is not covered by this source — do not assume it; build/point to the agent voice separately and never blend registers across engines.
