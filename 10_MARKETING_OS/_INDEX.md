## Purpose

Marketing OS governs acquisition strategy, audience application, channel playbooks, experiments, performance decisions and cross-functional campaign handoffs.

It does not own live campaign production, permanent content doctrine, positioning/IP masters, proof/media files, client PII or Sales follow-up.
