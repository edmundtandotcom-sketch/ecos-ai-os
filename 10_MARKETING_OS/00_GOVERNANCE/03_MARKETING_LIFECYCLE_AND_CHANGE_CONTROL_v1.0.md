# Marketing Lifecycle and Change Control
Version: v1.0
Status: APPROVED
Date: 2026-07-20

Every material marketing change must identify:
- governing objective and target audience;
- approved positioning/offer input;
- channel and campaign home;
- hypothesis and success/failure rule;
- evidence source and freshness;
- Sales and Operations handoffs;
- assets required and their permanent homes;
- privacy risk;
- reviewer and decision date.

Archive replaced governance before structural change. Live campaign edits belong in the active campaign workspace. Platform-dependent guidance must be verified before use. A campaign result becomes reusable doctrine only after explicit review; one outcome is evidence, not universal truth.
