## PART A — THE CORE ARCHITECTURE (final state reached in the thread)

### The four-level journey
```
PRODUCER  →  ADVISOR  →  BUSINESS OWNER  →  MULTIPLIER
"I generate    "Clients seek   "My business has    "My systems, media, AI,
transactions"  my judgment"    systems, numbers     techniques and people
                               and predictability"  produce beyond my
                                                     personal capacity"
```

### The spine
**ATTRACT × CONVERT × DELIVER, powered by COMMAND.** (Note: a later refinement round in the same thread shifted the public-facing spine to **CREATE → CONVERT → CONTROL** for the VSL/Intensive — see Part D. The thread does not fully reconcile these two spines; both appear in the source material.)

COMMAND is explicitly *not* a fourth curriculum pillar — it's the operating layer underneath (CRM, GHL, AI, automations, data, dashboards, SOPs) that every multiplier eventually connects into.

### The Nine Agent Multipliers™ (diagnostic universe, not curriculum — see Part C)

| ATTRACT | CONVERT | DELIVER |
|---|---|---|
| 1. Best Client | 4. Pre-Sell | 7. Advisor Model |
| 2. Advisor Authority | 5. Diagnose | 8. Client Experience |
| 3. Lead Machine | 6. Follow Through | 9. Client For Life |

### Entry point / audience
Established Singapore property agents producing **~S$250K+/yr** (later tightened in the VSL work to **S$100K+ GCI**, so the thread itself has drifted on this number). The threshold is proof they can already sell — Agent Edition does not teach salesmanship, it teaches business architecture underneath production.

### Core belief
Income doesn't multiply because you generate more activity. It multiplies when more of the opportunities already entering the business turn into productive outcomes.

## PART B — THE NINE MULTIPLIERS, DETAILED

**ATTRACT**
1. **Best Client** — mine the last ~20 deals, score on Economic Value / Outcome / Fit / Expertise / Repeatability / Future Value; pattern replaces invented ICP. Newer agents without history use a Market Fit Matrix instead (score potential markets on Experience/Access/Interest/Proof/Demand; pick a 90-day lane, not a lifelong identity).
2. **Advisor Authority** — the "Advisor Authority Stack™": Position, Point of View, Proof, Method, one Authority Asset, one Pre-Sell Asset. Buildable in 30 days — not "start a YouTube channel and wait 3 years."
3. **Lead Machine** — three prebuilt machines, pick ONE: (A) Database Revival — GHL reactivation of existing contacts; (B) Property-to-Client — one property becomes multiple advisory conversations, not one unit enquiry; (C) Advisor Acquisition — ads → diagnostic → authority video → consult, for agents ready to attract on a problem rather than a property.

**CONVERT**
4. **Pre-Sell** — everything that happens between booking and the appointment: confirmation → "what happens next" video → case study 24–48h before → prep checklist → reminder → frame-setting. Mostly automatable in GHL; a fast, high-leverage Boardroom build.
5. **Diagnose** — "diagnose before you prescribe." A universal consultation architecture (FRAME → WHY NOW → CURRENT POSITION → DESTINATION → GAP → CONSEQUENCE → OPTIONS → RECOMMENDATION → COMMITMENT), explicitly **not** the full REI Method — that stays a proprietary ascension advantage (see the 3-layer IP structure below).
6. **Follow Through** — prospect → decision tracks: no-booking, no-show, post-consult undecided, not-ready nurture, dormant revival, event-triggered reactivation. A relay-race metaphor: don't drop the baton.

**DELIVER**
7. **Advisor Model** — a 4-box canvas (WHO → WHAT → HOW → EXPERIENCE), explicitly framed as "we're not asking you to invent a business, we're documenting what you already do" — not a proprietary-methodology exercise.
8. **Client Experience** — map Before Engagement / Engagement / Transaction / Completion once, so agents stop reinventing the process per client.
9. **Client For Life** — the loop (Client → Transaction → Review → Next Move → Introduction → Client) vs. the standard agent model (Lead → Deal → Goodbye). Distinct from Follow Through: Follow Through is prospect→decision, Client For Life is client→next opportunity.

### The three-layer IP structure (protects the REI Method)
- **Layer 1 — Agent Edition Advisory Blueprint:** the universal 9-box consultation architecture, available to everyone.
- **Layer 2 — Build Your Method:** Year 2, members build their own frameworks from their own experience.
- **Layer 3 — REI Practitioner:** potentially reserved for agents who join the PropNex practice — makes the REI Method an ascension advantage rather than something given away Day One.

## PART C — THE MAJOR COURSE CORRECTION (critical — read this section)

The thread **self-corrected mid-development**: the first full draft turned the 3×3 into nine subjects to teach and nine systems to build across a 3-day Boardroom, and Edmund flagged it as overboard. The revised design:

> **The 3×3 is NOT the curriculum. It is the diagnostic map.**
> Participant sees all nine. Leaves working on only: **1 GOAL, 2 MULTIPLIERS, 30 DAYS.**

This became **"THE 3–2–1 BOARDROOM":**
1. **3** — diagnose the three business areas (Attract/Convert/Deliver)
2. **2** — choose the two highest-impact Multipliers (red/amber/green self-score, circle two)
3. **1** — get one of them live before leaving

Worked examples in the source material: "Jason" (S$320K, 70% referral, no automation) prioritizes Lead Machine + Pre-Sell; "Sarah" (S$450K, strong lead flow, terrible follow-up) prioritizes Follow Through + Client Experience. Same programme, personalised by what's actually broken — not everyone builds all nine.

**The staircase visual carries this:** Producer → Advisor → Business Owner → Multiplier, with the caption *"You only need the next step."* Explicitly designed to counter the audience's likely reaction ("I don't have any of this — CRM, AI, positioning, content, funnels...").

## PART D — THE VSL / INTENSIVE LAYER (separate offer, separate spine language)

A parallel refinement thread built the **S$197 "Agent Income Multiplier Live Intensive"** (2.5hr, live online, no replay) as the front-door product feeding the Boardroom. This layer uses **CREATE → CONVERT → CONTROL** as its public spine (not Attract/Convert/Deliver — the thread never reconciled the two).

**Core reframe:** more leads will not fix a broken lead-to-commission process. The complete journey: Contact → Engaged Lead → Appointment-Ready → Booked Appointment → Appointment Completed → Qualified Opportunity → Client Strategy → Transaction → Commission.

**CREATE** — better-qualified enquiries. Tease: *One Property, Three Opportunities™* / *Property Decision Offer™*.
**CONVERT** — appointments that show up prepared. Tease: *Lead-to-Appointment System™*. Three lead routes: book now / prepare & nurture / deprioritise.
**CONTROL** — visibility + follow-up + source-to-commission tracking, AI-supported. Explicitly not sales training — if appointments exist but transactions are weak, that's a different constraint (sales capability), not a CONTROL problem.

**The VSL rule (important, reusable beyond this offer):**
> Painful current situation → desirable outcome → why it matters → name the system → proof/visual → open the loop.
> **Not:** teach the framework → explain every component → show how to build it → give them enough to recreate it.
> *Sell the destination, not the map.* The Intensive lets them understand and map the vehicle. The Boardroom builds it.

**Tangible Intensive asset:** *The Agent Income Multiplier 30-Day Workbook™* — guided self-diagnosis (numbers, CREATE/CONVERT/CONTROL assessment, Priority Multiplier, first 30-day focus), not a custom plan Edmund prepares personally. Justifies the S$197 even for someone who buys nothing further.

**Economic mechanism (the "why it's a multiplier" explanation):**
```
Leads Generated × Meaningful Engagement Rate × Appointment Booking Rate
× Appointment Show-Up Rate × Transaction Rate × Average Commission
= Commission Capacity
```
Improve more than one stage and the gains compound — hence "multiplier," not "more leads."

**Ascension logic:** VSL (see it) → Intensive S$197 (diagnose/understand/map) → Boardroom (personalise/design/build) → 30-Day Activation (launch/measure/correct).

**Guardrails already locked in this thread (worth preserving regardless of reconciliation):** no guaranteed income claims, no fake urgency/scarcity, AI never replaces professional judgement or makes client recommendations, S$ not $ for SGD, an explicit "who this is NOT for" list, an outcome-based (not income-based) refund guarantee tied to leaving with the workbook + Priority Multiplier + 30-day focus.

## PART E — DELIVERY MODEL: PROGRESSIVE INSTALLATION™ (the strongest single idea in this thread)

Edmund's own correction to a Learn-Day1/Build-Day2/Install-Day3 structure: **don't make people wait until Day 3 to start building.** Every major section instead runs the same five-step rhythm, repeated throughout all days:

1. **SEE IT** — understand the problem and framework
2. **SCORE IT** — diagnose it in their own business
3. **DECIDE IT** — make the business decision
4. **BUILD IT** — create the first usable version (not the full technical build)
5. **QUEUE IT** — flag what needs specialist installation afterward

Three levels of implementation, deliberately separated:
- **Level 1 — Business Design** (Edmund teaches: *what* should happen)
- **Level 2 — Asset Creation** (they draft it live: the actual video hook, the actual message, the actual questions)
- **Level 3 — Technical Installation** (specialists build it afterward, from an **Installation Brief** the Level-2 work generates automatically)

This solves the real operating constraint named directly in the thread: **Edmund is one person teaching the whole event** and cannot also be GHL support, a Claude engineer and a Meta Ads specialist on stage. His role is **architect**; specialists are **installers**. Weekly continuity calls split into distinct lab types (Business Lab / Command Lab / Authority Lab / Conversion Lab) so members attend only what's relevant to their current Priority Multiplier.

**Target ratio inside any live block: 40% teach/context, 40% application (score/decide/draft), 20% review + installation-brief handoff.** Almost none of the room time is "click here" technical setup.

**One live result per person is the non-negotiable bar** — even if nothing else, one thing must actually go live (a message sent, a video recorded and used, a follow-up tree mapped) before the room ends. Activation beats perfection.
