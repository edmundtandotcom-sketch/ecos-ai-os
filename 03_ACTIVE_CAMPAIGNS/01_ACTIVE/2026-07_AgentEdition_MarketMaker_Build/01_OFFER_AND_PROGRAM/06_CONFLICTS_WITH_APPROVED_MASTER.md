## The specific collision that now compounds

`REF_TakiMoore_*` flagged that **`The Boardroom`** is Taki Moore's mastermind trade name, and that it was already live in the approved public spine (`The Market Makers' Circle → The Boardroom`). The new Design Bible material independently — and apparently without knowledge of that flag — adopted **"Boardroom" as the name of its own 2–3 day flagship live event**, with real pricing already built around it (S$4.8–6.8K standalone, credit-into-Foundation mechanics). So there are now **three different things all called "Boardroom"**: Taki's mastermind (do-not-copy), our approved continuity tier, and the new material's flagship event. This needs to be resolved before any of it goes into copy.

## What is NOT in conflict — safe to treat as additive regardless of which naming wins

- The **3-2-1 Boardroom mechanism** (diagnose 3 → pick 2 → install 1) — a delivery-design improvement, orthogonal to naming.
- **Progressive Installation™** (See it → Score it → Decide it → Build it → Queue it, across three implementation levels) — solves a real single-teacher operating constraint regardless of brand.
- The **illustration system** (Part F of the Design Bible) — visual metaphors are brand-agnostic.
- The **VSL rule** (sell the destination, not the map; painful-situation → outcome → why → name → proof → open-loop) — a reusable copywriting discipline.
- The **guardrails already locked** in the VSL work (no income guarantees, no fake urgency, AI-never-replaces-judgement, outcome-based refund) — these strengthen, not conflict with, the existing proof guardrails in the Big Idea doc.
