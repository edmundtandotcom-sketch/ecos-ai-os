## 0. THE BIG IDEA (the campaign hangs on this)
> **You don't have an income problem. You have an *agent* problem.**
> Your income swings because you're built as an **agent** — a person who hunts deals — not a **business** that owns a market. Agents eat what they hunt: feast when they catch, famine when they don't. That's not an effort flaw. It's the design. Change the design and the swings stop.

**The new cause (what makes this un-echoable):** inconsistent income is not a leads problem, a market problem, or a discipline problem. It's that you're structured as an **agent, not a business.** Agents' income swings *by design*; businesses' income compounds *by design*. No competitor is saying this.

## 1. THE MECHANISM (Edmund's method, named — obey exactly)
The 3 moves that turn an agent into a business (= consistent, growing income):
1. **Own Your Market** — stop chasing the market 37,989 agents chase. Pick one, dominate it, and it feeds you. *(Make the market — Market Maker.)*
2. **Own the Conversation** — the advisory conversation system: lead as a trusted advisor so you're **chosen, not compared** on price.
3. **Business in a Box** — the operating system (pipeline, follow-up, referrals, positioning) that runs so income **stops depending on this month's hustle.**

**Identity payoff:** stop being an agent. Become the business the market comes to — a **Market Maker.**
**Promise:** income the business *produces*, not income you *chase*.
**Proof spine:** Edmund's own before → after (founder proof). *⏳ NEED: real before/after GCI numbers to finalize proof creatives. "$400K from closing training" confirmed usable.*
