R5 · NO APPLE TO APPLE  (39.6s)
================================================================
HEADLINE
There's no apple-to-apple comparison. Here's how to adjust for it.

DESCRIPTION
Comparison mistake #2: expecting a project with every same attribute. It
doesn't exist. So you find the closest comparison — and you ADJUST.

Thomson Reserve starts with a fresh 99-year lease. The neighbours haven't.

So take the lease years the older project has used up and work out what it
costs to top it back to 99.

JadeScape, lease from 2018 — 8 years gone:
$2,620 ÷ 91 years × 99 years = $2,850 psf

That's the like-for-like number. Not $2,620.
