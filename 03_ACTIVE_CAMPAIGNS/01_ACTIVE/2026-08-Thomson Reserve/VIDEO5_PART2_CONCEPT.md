## Part 1 was a COMPARISON. Part 2 is a CLIMB.

Part 1 put each Thomson Reserve layout beside its neighbour. Part 2 follows one buyer climbing from the
1,152 (where Part 1 ended) up to the 1,808 — and asks, at every rung, what the extra square feet and the
extra cheque actually buy. Four rungs, four answers:

| Rung | From → to | What the step buys | The cheque (est. $2,800–$3,000 psf) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 1,152 → **1,238** | +86 sqft = the 4th room (study becomes a bedroom). Still Classic, still floors 03–21. | $3.47m – $3.71m |
| 2 | 1,238 → **1,367** | +129 sqft = the private lift, the lobby, the sliding-glass kitchen, floors up to **30**. | $3.83m – $4.10m |
| 3 | 1,367 → **1,485** | +118 sqft = the LANDSCAPE layout: 5.25 m frontage, kitchen in the room, the protected view. | $4.16m – $4.46m |
| 4 | 1,485 → **1,808** | +323 sqft = the 5th room, a 6 m frontage, the dumbbell. | $5.06m – $5.42m |

## Six presentation devices that Part 1 did not use

1. **Dark field.** Navy theme (the deck's Luxury tier), gold rules, cream plan cards. Part 1 was bone/light.
   Instantly reads as "the other video".
2. **THE LADDER — a persistent tracker.** A slim vertical ladder on the right edge of every display scene:
   five rungs (1,152 · 1,238 · 1,367 · 1,485 · 1,808), the rung he is on lit gold, the step size and
   "what it buys" written beside it. It climbs as the video climbs. Replaces Part 1's punchline-only header.
3. **THE 30 FLOORS — height instead of compass.** For each stack, a block elevation strip: floors 03–21
   (Classic) against 03–30 (Luxury), the stack's range lit, with the developer's own level-5/10/15/21 view
   photos beside it. Facing text comes from the developer's view pages verbatim (South / South-West /
   MacRitchie / Windsor / GCB). Part 1 used a compass panel and a site crop; Part 2 uses height and the view.
4. **THE FRONTAGE RULER.** An animated width ruler drawn on the living room of each plan: 3.0 m → 3.4 m →
   5.25 m → 6 m, the number he says on camera at each rung. One motif, four sizes.
5. **WHERE DID THE SPACE GO — red/green overlay.** Comparisons are not side-by-side "VS" cards. The TR plan
   wipes across to the competitor on the same card, then the competitor's balcony / PES / lobby / aircon
   ledge fill red and TR's shelter / yard / junior suite fill green, with the sqft delta in the corner
   (+21, +280, +291). Same-scale, one card, one question.
6. **OPEN ↔ CLOSED and the three moves.** The developer's sliding-glass kitchen photos (DP1 and E1) as a
   flick driven by his words "closed" / "open". The 1,485 study reconfiguration as a three-move animation
   on the plan (seal the door → open the wall → shift the master door), one move per sentence.

Plus: unit mix as a ring (202 of 1,268), the two-card A/B hook ("limited supply or more transactions?"),
the price as a single horizontal cheque bar from $3.4m to $5.4m with TR's estimates and the real
JadeScape sales pinned on it (replaces Part 1's grid), PiP as a rounded square on the LEFT with content
to its right (Part 1: circle on the right).

scheduled subscribe pop-ins · estimate disclaimer on every price · gap-free cut · check board before render.
