# THOMSON RESERVE — THE LAYOUT COMPARISON (next video)

Brief written 23 Sep 2026 from the four official PDFs Edmund supplied, while
THE LAYOUTS v6 was rendering. Nothing here has been cut or rendered yet.

## Objective (Edmund's words, condensed)

Compare **Thomson Reserve's layouts against nearby comparable projects** on
**efficiency**, and say plainly what the pros and cons of each layout and each
facing are — so a buyer does not "blindly buy any kind of unit and any kind of
layout". Cover **2, 3 and 4 bedroom only** (no 5-bedroom). Carry indicative
**price points against the other projects**, so the viewer can see why they
would choose Thomson Reserve, or which Thomson Reserve layouts to be careful
about.

Axes to compare: **layout / efficiency / facing / price.**

## Efficiency — the argument that carries the video

The deck already frames it correctly and we should extend it rather than
invent a new method. Two usable methods:

1. **Room-by-room area, summed against strata area.** Pages 29-32 of the
   Tri-Force deck label every room in sqm on the plan. For BPS1 (68 sqm):
   living 23.5, master 10.9, bedroom 2 8.9, study 5.2, master bath 5.1,
   kitchen 4.9, balcony 4.8, bath 2 3.9, store 0.4 — summing to ~67.6 of 68.
   Almost nothing is unaccounted for.
2. **Liveable versus non-liveable share.** Pages 34-37 shade the comparison
   unit's planter, open terrace and balcony in red against Thomson Reserve's
   in green. Sky Habitat's 2-bedroom is 1,195 sqft but a large share is open
   terrace and planter; Thomson Reserve's 732 sqft is almost all enclosed.
   **This is the single most persuasive frame in the whole deck** and it is
   exactly what Edmund means by "efficiency level".

Compute a **liveable-area percentage per layout** and use it as the spine of
the video. Do NOT state a percentage that has not been measured off the plan.

## Facing

Every plan lists its blocks and stack numbers (e.g. BPS1 is BLK 1 #03-04 to
#21-04, BLK 3 #03-11 mirrored, BLK 11 #03-54). The site plan is in the unit
mix PDF. Facing claims must come off the site plan, not be inferred from the
floor plan alone. The 4-bed Premium + Study and 5-bed Suite slides claim
unblocked views of the GCB cluster, Windsor Nature Park and MacRitchie.

## Constraints carried over from the video work

These are Edmund's standing rules; they are not negotiable and they have all
been learned the hard way. The full list is in `EDITING_PLAYBOOK.md`
CH.14-16, and the working conventions are in the project memory files
`thomson-layouts-spinoff` and `thomson-reserve-part2-pipeline`.

- Ten out of ten, or it does not ship. Audit every instruction given.
- Never cut a sentence off mid-statement.
- Distinct version names on every render so he never reviews a stale file.
- Same video source and audio throughout; do not silently swap footage.
- The brand lockup and caption style are fixed (Part 2's `Ident`, 46px Barlow
  Condensed with gold hot words). "New creative" means the presentation layer.
- Size everything for a phone.
- A developer's numbered callouts and their numbers belong to the drawing.
  Reuse them verbatim; only the reveal timing is ours to choose.
- Every figure on screen must be traceable to one of these PDFs or to a
  transaction record. No invented numbers, and the estimate disclaimer travels
  with any price table.
- Named-entity constraints from `PART2_cross_check_resources.md` still apply:
  the ballot client's project is never named on camera.
