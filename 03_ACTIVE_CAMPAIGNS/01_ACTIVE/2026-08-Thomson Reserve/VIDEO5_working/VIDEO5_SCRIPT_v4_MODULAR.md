**Everything together is 36 minutes delivered — measured from 6,160 spoken words at 150 wpm and 1.15×, not estimated.** This is a series, not a video. Assembly options are at the end.

**Every segment in Module A follows the same six beats**, so the audience learns
the rhythm by the third one and stops needing to be told what's coming:

> **① THE PLAN** — what's drawn, where the door opens, where the furniture sits
> **② THE STEP UP** — what this type gains and loses against the one below it
> **③ HOW YOU'LL LIVE IN IT** — dining, kitchen, laundry, where guests go
> **④ AGAINST THE NEIGHBOURS** — the matched AMO / JadeScape unit, layout first, price second
> **⑤ FOR / AGAINST** — plainly, both ways
> **⑥ WHO IT'S FOR** — one sentence

Read the ▶ blocks aloud. `SLIDE:` names what's on screen. `SRC:` is where every
figure comes from. Two figures still carry `[SIGN-OFF]` from the audit.
