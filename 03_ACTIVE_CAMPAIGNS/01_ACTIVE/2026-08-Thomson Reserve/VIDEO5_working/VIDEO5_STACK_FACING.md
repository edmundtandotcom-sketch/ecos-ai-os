### **The 10 m is a boundary setback, not a block-to-block gap**

It runs east from **BLK 11 stack 50** to the site edge by the Bright Hill Drive
driveway. **The script v1 line — "ten metres, that's you and your neighbour's
living room" — is wrong and must not be recorded.** There is no block ten
metres from another block anywhere on this site.

Also: **stack 50 is not one of the comparison types.** BLK 11 carries BP3 at
#47 and BPS1 at #54. Nothing we name in this video sits on the 10 m setback.

**The tightest genuine block-to-block distance on the whole site is 26 m,
BLK 9 #41 to BLK 3 #15/16.** Neither of those is a named comparison type
either — Blk 9's comparison stacks are #38, #39 and #44; Blk 3's are #10, #11,
#12, #17 and #18. Say "26 metres between Block 9 and Block 3" and it is exactly
true. Say "26 metres if you buy the 775" and it is not.

## 3. WHAT THIS ACTUALLY SAYS ABOUT THE 2- AND 3-BEDDERS

This is the "don't blindly buy any layout" finding, and it is defensible.

**The long open spans belong to the private-lift blocks.** 240 m, 190 m and
182 m all start at **BLK 7**; 171 m starts at **BLK 5**. Blocks 5 and 7 are the
western cluster and they hold **DP1 (L), DPS1 (L) and E1 (L)** — the 4- and
5-bedroom private-lift stock. They also run to 30 storeys against 21.

**Every 2- and 3-bedroom type sits in BLK 1, 3, 9 or 11 — the eastern cluster**,
where the internal spans run 26 m to 95 m.

So the honest headline: **you are not choosing between layouts at the same
outlook. The 84% of the project that is 2- and 3-bedroom is concentrated in the
denser end of the site, and the long water frontages are attached to the units
that cost two to three times as much.** That is not a criticism of the layouts —
the layouts test extremely well. It is a reason to care which stack you are put
in on selection day.

### Block by block, for the 2- and 3-bedders

**BLK 1 — the one to want.** Stacks 01–09, an isolated point block at the
Bright Hill Drive end. It carries **one of every comparison type**. It has
**no block-to-block arrow to any other block on the map** — its three arrows
are all to boundaries: 24 m east, 36 m south-east, 81 m south-east. Nothing
in this development faces it. The trade is that it is the furthest block from
the main pool and clubhouse.

**BLK 3 — the one to check hardest.** Stacks 10–18, in the middle of the
eastern cluster. It carries CPS1 (#10), BPS1 (#11), BP3 (#12), B1 (#17) and
CP1 (#18). It is on the receiving end of **more measured distances than any
other block**: 240 m from Blk 7, 171 m from Blk 5, 80 m / 77 m / 63 m from
Blk 11, 26 m from Blk 9, and 24 m to the south boundary. Within one block the
outlook ranges from a 240 m water span to a 26 m gap. **This is the single
strongest argument in the video for asking which stack before you commit.**

- **#18, CP1 3-Bed Premium 1,055** is the comparison type on the tightest
  measured block-to-block span, **63 m to Blk 11**.
- **#15/16** face Blk 9 at **26 m** — these are not comparison types, but they
  are the same block and the same price list.

**BLK 11 — north edge.** Stacks 46–55. Carries BP3 (#47) and BPS1 (#54).
The north face (46–50) looks out over the landscaped north-east corner; the
south face (51–55) looks across to Blk 3 at 63–80 m. Stack 50 is the 10 m
boundary setback — not a comparison type.

**BLK 9 — west of the eastern cluster.** Stacks 37–45. Carries BP1 (#38),
BP3 (#39) and BPS4 (#44). 190 m and 182 m across to Blk 7, 95 m to Blk 11,
28 m to the north boundary — but **#41 is 26 m from Blk 3**. Wide spans on the
west face, tight on the east.

## 4. WHAT TO SAY ON CAMERA, AND WHAT NOT TO

**Safe, sourced, and strong:**
- "Every 2- and 3-bedroom type in this project has a stack in Block 1, and
  Block 1 has no other block facing it."
- "Block 3 has a 240-metre span on one side and a 26-metre gap on the other.
  Same block, same price list, same layout."
- "The 190 and 182-metre water frontages start at Block 7 — that's the 4-bedroom
  private-lift block. Not the 2-bedders."
- "The 2- and 3-bed stacks run to the 21st storey. The private-lift stacks run
  to the 30th."
- "Ask for the block-to-block distance for the exact stack, and ask whether the
  number you're quoted is to another block or to the boundary — the developer's
  own map has both on it and doesn't label which."

**Do not say:**
- "Ten metres, that's your neighbour's living room." The 10 m is a boundary
  setback at Blk 11 #50.
- Any compass facing for any stack. Not released.
- That a named comparison type sits on the 10 m or the 26 m. None of them do.
- That the block/stack table is the complete project. It is one plan per family.
