## 3. TWO THINGS THAT ARE NOT ERRORS BUT NEED A DECISION

### The two decks harmonise by different amounts

- **Your deck:** +5% harmonisation, plus 2% per year since lease start.
- **PropNex Tri-Force deck (`TF p07`):** +7% harmonisation, applied to a psf
  that has first been pro-rated to a 98-year lease.

Both are defensible. They are not the same calculation and they give different
answers. Slides 7, 10 and 11 of your deck are pasted PropNex slides, so **the
video currently contains both methods without saying so.**

Pick one to narrate as "the test", show the other as corroboration, and say out
loud that they are two different adjustments landing in the same band. That is
stronger than either alone, and it costs one sentence.

### You are quoting the top of each range, not the average

Slide 4: the six AMO 743 sqft rows run $2,383 to $2,565 psf. You quote $2,565.
That matches PropNex's own stated approach — "pro-rated **highest** PSF for
level of acceptance" — so it is consistent, not cherry-picked. But say it on
camera: "I'm using the strongest price each of these projects has achieved,
because that is what a new launch has to beat."
