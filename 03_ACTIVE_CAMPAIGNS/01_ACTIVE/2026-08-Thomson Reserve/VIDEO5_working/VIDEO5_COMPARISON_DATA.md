### What the red/green shading on these slides actually is

**It is not a liveable-versus-non-liveable area shading.** Read across all three
slides, green (Thomson Reserve) and red (the comparison unit) mark the **same
rooms on both plans** so the viewer compares like against like:

- `p34` TR green = balcony, living, dining, study, master, master bath, kitchen.
  Sky Habitat red = open terrace, 2 planters, AC ledge, open kitchen, balcony.
- `p35` TR green = master bedroom, HS, dining, store, yard, kitchen.
  Jadescape red = balcony, master bedroom, dining, kitchen, AC ledge, store/laundry.
- `p36` TR green = balcony, junior master, junior master bath, walk-in wardrobe.
  Jadescape red = balcony, bedroom 2, bedroom 4, AC ledge, powder room.

On `p34` the red happens to land on Sky Habitat's genuinely non-liveable areas,
which is why it reads as an efficiency shading. On `p35` and `p36` it does not —
red covers bedrooms and master suites. **There is no legend anywhere in the
deck** (`p33` is a title card). So: the shading can be shown and talked through
qualitatively, but **no liveable-area percentage can be derived from it**, and
we must not present it as a measurement.

## 6. DERIVED COMPARISONS — clearly marked as ours, not the deck's

Arithmetic only, from figures above. Each needs the estimate disclaimer.

**Same-money, 3-bedroom** — TR CP1 1,055 at $2,900 = **$3,059,500**; Jadescape
3 BR 1,152, 6 Jul 2026 = **$3,055,000**. Difference **$4,500**. A new 99-year
lease and a harmonised floor plate against a 2023 TOP, for effectively the same
cheque — but **97 sqft smaller**.

**Same-size, 3-bedroom (the comparison the deck skipped)** — TR 3 BR Premium +
Study **1,152 sqft** at $2,900 = **$3,340,800** against Jadescape's 1,152 sqft at
**$3,055,000**. On identical square footage Thomson Reserve is **~$286,000
more**, about **9.4%**. This is the honest counterweight and it is the strongest
"be careful" material in the video. It does not contradict the same-money frame —
it prices it.

**4-bedroom, cheque-for-cheque** — TR DP1 (L) 1,367 at $2,900 = **$3,964,300**
against Jadescape 4 BR 1,647 at **$4,100,000** (25 May 2026). TR is **$135,700
cheaper** — but only at **$2,900 psf or below**. At $3,000 psf TR is $4,101,000,
which is $1,000 *above* the Jadescape transaction. **State the psf the claim
depends on, every time.**

**Caution:** the Jadescape 4 BR plan on `TF p36` shows **PRIVATE LIFT** and
**PRIVATE LIFT LOBBY**. Private lift is **not** a Thomson Reserve differentiator
in this pairing. Do not say "and it has a private lift" as though the comparison
unit does not.

**Liquidity vs scarcity** — 716 two-bedders are your in-project competition on
exit; 174 four-bedders, 84 of them private-lift, are not. Counts from §1.
