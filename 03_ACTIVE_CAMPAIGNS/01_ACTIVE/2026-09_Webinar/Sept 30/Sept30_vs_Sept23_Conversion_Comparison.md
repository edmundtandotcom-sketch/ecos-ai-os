Read it this way:

1. **The webinar itself converted 6x worse.** 1 live application from 50 people vs 2 from 16 the week before. The $509 CPA is carried by the 5 pre-webinar applications — by the ad/WhatsApp sequence, not by the session.
2. **The room changed.** CPL fell 28% in one week and 14 people came from non-ad sources. Cheaper, broader traffic + organic = a "property learners" room, not a "Thomson buyers" room. The chat shows it: "TR price will start from $5k psf", "squeeze them dry", "It's for the fur baby", a "Why So Serious" handle asking at [045:02] *"Share but please get to the actual analysis of Thomson Reserve after."* Engagement was actually higher (19 unique typers of 50 = 38%, vs 5 of 16 = 31% on the 23rd) — people were entertained, not moved.
3. **The high-intent segment was harvested before the session.** The pre-webinar "apply now" flow produced 5 applications. Those are the people who on the 16th/23rd applied live. So the live session now has to convert the mid-intent majority — and the mid-intent majority needs the demonstration (price matrix, the $3M verdict) BEFORE the offer. On the 30th it got it AFTER.

Open question: what changed in the ads between 24 and 30 Sept (audience broadened? new creative? the Unit Mix video running as an ad?). CPL -28% plus a different chat personality says the targeting moved, and that is half the story.

## 3. What regressed — the six causes of 1 application

### 3.1 The price matrix was held hostage behind the pitch and the break
[105:32] *"later on I will also be sharing with you all the price matrix, so stay all the way to the end"* → [129:55] *"stay on, because in the Q&A I will be going through the price matrix"* → [134:27] "I'll be back in about 5 minutes" → 6-minute testimonial video → [141:21] price matrix finally begins: 35 minutes after the pitch started, 105 minutes after the hook.

Why this kills applications: the matrix is the reason the Thomson buyer came. Held behind the offer, it turns the pitch into a toll booth — people sit through it waiting, not deciding. When the payoff finally arrives the sales energy is gone, the QR has been on screen for 40 minutes, and the people who left at the break never saw it. The Sept 23 diagnosis (3-S) put this block at minute 40–46, BEFORE the transition. On the 30th it moved the other way.

Also missing from the matrix: the verdict. You gave ranges by unit type but never closed the title question — *"With >$3M, would I buy?"* The 3-bed compact at $2.55M+ and the 1,055 sqft at ~$2.9M were both on screen; "so with $3M I'd ballot for X, not Y" was never said. That one sentence is the demonstration the session sells.

### 3.2 First Thomson number at minute 54 — and the pre-content doubled
[054:33] "the land price was bought at 1178" is the first Thomson-specific number. On the 23rd it was ~67 of the meeting; 54 is better on paper, but the audience felt it worse because the self-focused content is now duplicated:

- [012:19]–[018:03] a 6-minute pre-recorded bio video (bankruptcy, dad, "my name is Edmund Tan and welcome to…")
- [044:47]–[048:48] the SAME backstory told live again
- [040:33]–[043:30] the consumer-vs-investor mindset block the diagnosis said to cut
- [049:16]–[050:42] the property portfolio walk (Tre Ver, Parc Clematis, United Square, the 4-bed)
- [036:42] "in the next 9 minutes I'm going to build Thomson Reserve's fair price live" — it started 18 minutes later

A chat message at [045:02] asking you to get to Thomson Reserve is the room saying the same thing.

### 3.3 The QR was shown four times before the pitch, with three different asks
[033:37] "scan the QR code for a private showflat visit… 17th October" · [072:53] "scan the QR code first so we can give you priority to book" · [095:46] "you don't need to wait until the end — scan, we'll put you as priority… limited time, limited stock" · [109:22] "scan the QR code first… you will get priority".

By the real close at [127:42] the QR is 95 minutes old and has meant a showflat tour, a priority booking and a one-on-one consult. One ask, made once with full weight, converts; the same ask made four times with three meanings reads as "he keeps trying". Keep the 30-second pre-frame at minute 4 (no QR), one soft mention in the ballot-process block, then the close.

### 3.4 Thirteen minutes of floor plans = teaching the HOW, mid-session
[082:53]–[095:28]: 2B2B 678, 2B1B 592, 2+S 732 (three layouts), 2+S 775, 3-bed compact 947 (no WC / yard / store), 3-bed 1,055 (seven layouts, 18 stacks, no dumbbell), 3+S 1,152, 4-bed 1,238 (toilets, no private lift), balconies.

Three problems. (a) It is execution detail — exactly the work the session is for; giving it all away removes a reason to apply (your own rule: teach WHAT, sell HOW). (b) It sits at minutes 82–95, the attention trough; the chat goes silent from [087] to [097]. (c) It spends the "stay to the end" bonus in the middle, and the promised *"how you can claim this full layout analysis"* [035:45] is never explained. Two layouts live (one to fight for, one to avoid — the 947 with no yard is the perfect "avoid"), the rest as the emailed pack, is the version that keeps the promise and preserves the reason to apply.

### 3.5 Three lines that repel the room
- [113:24]–[113:56] *"if you take whatever I teach you today and do it yourself, chances are you're going to make a mistake and you're going to lose money… a million-dollar mistake"* — a threat to the DIY-proud majority; it triggers pushback, not urgency. Replace: "You can run this yourself. What you can't do alone is be told 'not this one' by someone with no stake in you buying."
- [155:34] *"ideally you want to take action within the next one to three months"* in answer to "I'm not buying for 1–2 years — is this useful?" This contradicts slide 109 ("not yet is a valid outcome") and the FAQ slide, and it dismisses the Accumulate majority who typed B at [039]. The deck's own answer (prepare ahead, don't decide live) is the right one.
- [069:54] and [156:28] *"you can also ask Bangla to help you buy the property"* — twice, on a recording that goes to replay. Part of the room hears it as a slur, and it is the kind of line that gets a clip shared for the wrong reason. Cut it permanently.

### 3.6 Application friction at the moment of decision
[158:03] *"some of you have some problems entering the link… the system kind of jammed."* And [020:25] "I will address every single question" vs [128:15] "we won't answer every single one of them." A form that fails for even a few people at minute 158 of 162 is a direct leak — test the link on a phone 10 minutes before every session and put the WhatsApp fallback on the CTA slide, not just in speech.
