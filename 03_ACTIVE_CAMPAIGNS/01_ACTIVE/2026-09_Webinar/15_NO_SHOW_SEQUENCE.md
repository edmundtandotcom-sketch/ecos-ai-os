## NOTES

- **The "irony" line in Email 3 is used exactly once, on purpose** — it's
  a sharp callback to the webinar's own core belief (missing what you
  didn't know to check, until it's too late), but using it more than
  once would tip from "sharp observation" into "gimmick."
- **All three CTAs (replay / direct message / next session) appear in
  every email**, just reordered by priority as the replay window
  shrinks — by Email 4, replay is gone entirely and only the other two
  remain.
- **No manufactured urgency beyond the real 48-hour replay window** —
  consistent with the no-fake-urgency standard used everywhere else in
  this campaign.
