# Thomson Reserve Webinar — Meta Ad Copy (v4)
One universal Primary Text, run under every image and video creative.
Headline + Description still vary per angle so each still matches what's
on screen.

Status: v4 — rewritten for stronger copy, not just added length. v2 was
punchy but thin (no credibility). v3 fixed the credibility gap but the
extra paragraph flattened the momentum — it read like an ad, not like
something worth stopping for. v4 rebuilds around agitation and curiosity
instead of stacking facts. Ready to load into Ads Manager once the

## THE UNIVERSAL PRIMARY TEXT

Structure: **pattern interrupt → agitate the real problem → compress
credibility into one line → curiosity-loaded bullets → urgency → CTA.**
Every line is doing one job; nothing is there just to be informative.

> 🚨 Thomson Reserve is about to launch. I'm not sure I'm buying it — and
> that should worry you more than if I was.
>
> Everyone's rushing to ballot. Almost nobody's asking the one question
> that actually decides whether you make money or lose it: is it worth
> YOUR money, at THIS price, right now?
>
> I'm Coach Edmund Tan — I've balloted new launches with my own capital
> for years, and walked 450+ families through the same decision, building
> a combined $328M+ portfolio doing it.
>
> Before I put my own money in, I'm running my full process live and free
> — and letting you watch every part of it:
>
> 💰 The exact price where this stops being worth it
> 🚩 The 3 things about this launch nobody's telling you
> 📊 Whether it can actually beat just leaving your money in the S&P 500
> 🔑 What I'd buy instead if the numbers don't work
>
> No hype. No sales pitch. Just the real numbers, live — while there's
> still time to do something with them.
>
> 🎟️ Claim your free seat (limited): [LINK]

**What changed, and why it's stronger:**
- **The hook is a threat, not a mood.** "I'm not sure I'm buying it — and
  that should worry you more than if I was" reframes Edmund's uncertainty
  as a warning aimed at the reader, not just a personal admission. That's
  a sharper hook than "deciding in public" — it creates a reason to keep
  reading in the second half of the same sentence.
- **There's a real agitation beat now.** "Everyone's rushing to ballot.
  Almost nobody's asking the one question that actually decides whether
  you make money or lose it" names the reader's actual risk before asking
  for anything — this is the beat both v2 and v3 skipped straight past.
- **Credibility is one sentence, not a paragraph.** Same 450+/$328M+ stat,
  but folded into a single line that keeps moving instead of stopping the
  ad to introduce itself.
- **The bullets are curiosity gaps, not topic labels.** "The 3 things
  nobody's telling you" and "the exact price where this stops being worth
  it" create an open loop each; the old bullets ("what makes it worth the
  money") just named a category.
- **The close adds real urgency** ("while there's still time to do
  something with them") and a scarcity cue on the CTA ("limited") instead
  of ending on the flat "no sales pitch" line, which is a cliché this
  category over-uses.
- **Still fully universal** — no angle-specific number (no $3.5M, no psf
  figure, no "daughter") — the S&P and "what nobody's telling you" bullets
  tease content the webinar actually covers for everyone, they don't
  commit the whole ad to one angle.
