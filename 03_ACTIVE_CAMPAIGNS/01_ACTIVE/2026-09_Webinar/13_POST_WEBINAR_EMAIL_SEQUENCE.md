# Thomson Reserve Webinar — Post-Webinar Email Sequence (v1)
4 emails, sent to attendees after the live session, driving toward the
Next Move Strategy Session / Thomson Reserve Balloting Support booking.

Structural pattern borrowed from the reference sequence (soft invite →
objection+story+reason to act → FAQ+proof+urgency → final identity
choice) — content is entirely original to this campaign, using only
what's already established and real: your own deals, the actual client
case study from the deck, the real objections from the webinar script,
and honest urgency (preview timing + genuine one-on-one capacity, no
manufactured countdown).

Registration/booking link: `https://legacylaunch.com.sg/webinar`
(swap for the actual consult-booking link if different)
