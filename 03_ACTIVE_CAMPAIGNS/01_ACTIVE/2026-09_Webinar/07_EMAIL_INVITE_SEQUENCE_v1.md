## HOW MANY EMAILS, AND WHY

**7 emails** for the main sequence, one per day from tomorrow (Fri 11
Sep) through the day of (Wed 16 Sep gets two — a morning reminder and a
final call a few hours before 8PM). Six days is a short runway, and live
webinar attendance is typically only 20–35% of registrants even with a
good list — the extra touches on the last day aren't excessive, they're
what actually moves attendance, since most no-shows aren't hostile,
they're just forgetful.

**If daily feels like too much for your list**, the two safe cuts are
Email 2 (Saturday) and Email 4 (Monday) — drop to 5 emails. Don't cut
either Wednesday email; day-of reminders carry the most weight of the
whole sequence.

**3 emails** for the no-show follow-up, spaced across roughly a week
after the missed session, ending with a hard deadline for the next one.
