# Thomson Reserve Webinar — Structure Breakdown (v3)
**Content brief for the deck — not a script.** Rebuilt around one master
filter: **teach WHAT to check, never HOW to check it.** That's the fix for
the over-teaching pattern — not fewer topics, less depth per topic.

Runtime: **60 minutes flat**, then open Q&A (unbounded).
Status: v3 — supersedes v2. Structure locked pending Edmund's review of
placeholders; deck to be built from this separately.

---

## THE ONE RULE THIS WHOLE DOCUMENT ENFORCES

> **WHAT to check builds authority and is free. HOW to check it — the
> actual math, the worked comparables, the verdict on YOUR unit and YOUR
> numbers — is the paid service.**

Every module below was audited against this. If a module was teaching
someone to *do the analysis themselves*, it got cut back to *naming what
the analysis covers.* This is the actual answer to "am I teaching too
much" — it was never a question of topic count, it's a question of
whether you hand over the category or the execution.

**Quick self-check for any future edit:** if an attendee could screenshot
the slide and go run the exact same analysis on their own unit with zero
follow-up, it's a HOW and it's cut too deep. If they'd screenshot it and
still need you to actually run it on their numbers, it's a WHAT and it's
fine.

---

## LIVE Q&A HANDLING RULE (standing, unchanged from v2)

Any live "should I buy X" question: **reflect it through the filter, teach
the thinking, never the verdict**, then bridge to a call. This is the same
WHAT/HOW discipline applied conversationally.

1. "Good question — what does that look like against [filter point]?"
2. Give them the *category of thing to check*, not the answer.
3. "That's exactly what we'd map properly on a call."

## MODULE 1 — OPEN (0:00–3:00)

**Two hook options** (pick per audience / A-B across runs):
- Process hook: *"Should You Buy Thomson Reserve? The Process I Run
  Before Any Client Ballots — Live."*
- Personal-stakes hook: *"I Have a $3M Budget. Am I Actually Going to Buy
  Thomson Reserve?"*

**Content:** NOT a Thomson Reserve pitch. IS a live demonstration of the
process. Thomson Reserve is today's live example, not the subject.

**Frame lines (use language like this, don't state conclusions
directly):**
- *"I'm not going to tell you what to buy tonight. I'm going to show you
  what to check — and let you notice what you've never been shown
  before."*
- *"By the end of the hour you'll know exactly what separates a good
  decision from a lucky one. What you do with that is up to you."*
- *"If Thomson Reserve isn't even on your list — stay anyway. What you're
  about to see works on whatever IS."*

## MODULE 2 — AUTHORITY: DID I REALLY MAKE MONEY? (3:00–7:00)

**Content, self-interrogating sequence:** who I am (fast) → NAV

**Frame lines:**
- *"Anyone can say property went up. The only number that actually means
  anything is what it did next to the alternative — which for most of
  you is just leaving the money where it is."*
- *"I'm showing you this not to impress you. I'm showing you so that when
  I ask 'will Thomson Reserve beat this' in a few minutes, you know
  exactly what bar I'm holding it to."*
- *"This is the same bar every client's numbers get held to. Not 'did it
  go up.' 'Did it beat doing nothing.'"*

## MODULE 5 — THE 5-POINT FILTER, WOVEN LIVE WITH THOMSON RESERVE (13:00–28:00)

**This is the core module. Structure: name a filter point (10–15 sec) →
give Thomson Reserve's one-line read on that point only (10–15 sec) →
next point.** Never separate "here's the theory" from "here's the
application" — they happen in the same breath, every time. Two points get
real weight; three get a single line. That variable depth is deliberate —
it mirrors what actually matters, and it keeps the module moving.

**The 5-Point Filter** *(consider naming it — "THE REI 5-Point Filter" or
similar — a named framework reads as proprietary IP, a bare list reads as
generic advice):*

1. **Land Cost & Timing** *(fast — one line)* — what the land cost today
   vs. what tomorrow's land will likely cost. → *TR read: land was bought
   below what's currently trading nearby — one line, move on.*
2. **Entry Price** *(real weight — the honest gap)* — is the launch price
   fair against cost, margin, and what's proven nearby. → *TR read: "this
   is the one I genuinely cannot answer for you tonight — there's no
   official price list yet. That's not a dodge. Nobody honestly knows
   this yet, including the agents telling you it's a steal."*
3. **Exit Strategy** *(fast — one line)* — do you know how you get out
   before you get in. → *TR read: one line, flag it depends on unit
   type/objective, don't resolve it.*
4. **Who You Sell To** *(real weight)* — who is the realistic future
   buyer for this exact unit, and does that pool actually exist yet. →
   *TR read: the project hasn't launched, so there's no resale track
   record for it specifically — genuinely unproven, said plainly.*
5. **Rentability** *(fast — one line, investment objective only)* — what's
   coming to compete with your unit for tenants. → *TR read: one line,
   large single-release caveat, move on.*

**Close the open loop from Module 3 here, explicitly:** *"So — will it
beat S&P? Land cost says there's room. Entry price, I can't tell you yet.
That's the honest answer — and it's exactly why 'I'm waiting for the
price list' is a real strategy, not an excuse."*

**Frame lines:**
- *"Five things. Ninety seconds each, at most. Notice I'm not showing you
  the math behind any of them — that's not because it's complicated, it's
  because it's specific to your numbers, not mine."*
- *"Most people find out about #2 and #4 the hard way — years after they
  bought."*
- *"I can tell you what to check. I can't tell you what it means for your
  $2M vs your $4M budget in the same sentence — those are different
  conversations."*

## MODULE 6 — ALTERNATIVES, NAMED, ONE SHARP REASON EACH (28:00–34:00)

**Content:** name 1–2 real, currently-live launches — not a category-level
gesture, actual projects — each with exactly ONE sharp, specific reason
it could beat Thomson Reserve for a particular objective (e.g. proven
resale liquidity for someone planning a 5–7 year exit; stronger
rentability read for the pure-investment objective). **Do not explain how
you arrived at that shortlist — that's the demonstration of process this
module deliberately withholds.** State the conclusion, own it, move on.

**Frame lines:**
- *"Thomson Reserve is not the only launch on the table right now — and
  if tonight ends with you sure it's the answer, I've done this badly."*
- *"I'm not going to walk you through how we found these — that's the
  part that takes a proper conversation. But I'll tell you plainly: for
  [objective], there's currently something that beats it on the one thing
  that matters most to that buyer."*
- *"This is the part most webinars skip, because it's inconvenient — it
  means the project you came here for might not be the answer. I'd
  rather you know that now than after you've balloted."*

## MODULE 7 — BUYER PROFILE + THE "DIDN'T KNOW THAT" BEAT (34:00–39:00)

**7a. Buyer-profile poll (2 min):** "Are you someone who takes a chance,
or someone who wants facts and data before you commit?" Both answers pull
toward booking — chance-takers hear "even more reason to have a backup
system," data-driven attendees hear "then you're exactly who this is
built for."

**7b. The genuinely non-obvious fact (3 min):** pick ONE — top candidate
is **harmonisation** (newer launches measure floor area differently than
older resale stock, so a naive psf comparison between an old project and
a new launch isn't apples-to-apples). State it as a fact, not a
methodology — don't teach the adjustment math, just the existence of the
trap. This is the single moment in the whole webinar built to produce an
audible "huh, I didn't know that."

**Frame lines:**
- *"I'm not asking to find out which type you are for my sake. I'm asking
  because it changes what kind of help actually serves you — one of you
  needs a plan, the other needs a plan you can point to when the room
  gets loud."*
- *"Here's something almost nobody watching this has been told straight
  before..."* [→ harmonisation fact]
- *"If that one fact changed how you'd read a price list, imagine what
  else you haven't been told."*

## MODULE 8 — THE REFRAME: GAMBLE VS. PREDICTABLE (39:00–42:00)

**Content:** the explicit choice, stated directly, not hedged — this is
the belief-shift the pitch is built on:

*"Here's the actual decision in front of everyone watching. Thomson
Reserve previews in October. You can wait for the ballot and hope your
number and the price both land in your favour — that's a gamble on two
things you don't control. Or you can walk in with a plan that's already
priced out, already knows your walk-away, and already knows what beats
this project if it doesn't work out — that's not a gamble, that's just
prepared."*

**Frame lines:**
- *"Notice I didn't say 'don't buy Thomson Reserve.' I said don't gamble
  on it. Those are different things."*
- *"Every client who works with us gets their alternatives checked — even
  the ones who end up balloting for Thomson Reserve anyway. That's not
  about talking anyone out of anything. It's about not finding out later
  there was a better answer sitting next to it."*
- *"The people who lose money in this market aren't the ones who picked
  Thomson Reserve. They're the ones who never checked what else was on
  the table before they picked anything."*

## MODULE 9 — THE BRIDGE (42:00–43:00)

*"Five-point filter. Applied live, honestly, including the one thing I
genuinely couldn't answer. At least one alternative on the table. And a
choice between gambling on the ballot or walking in prepared. Everything
tonight was general — mine, or an average scenario. Yours is different.
Here's what happens next."*

## MODULE 10 — THE PITCH (43:00–58:00)

**10a. The 3-path fork (43:00–46:00).** Explicit, visible, three real
options — this is what makes "work with us" read as the smart pick, not
the only pick:
1. **Do nothing** — go back to deciding on hype and queue numbers.
2. **DIY with tonight's filter** — genuinely usable, said with respect;
   the real gap is no read on YOUR numbers, no access to what's off the
   public record, and no one to tell you "wait" when that's the honest
   call.
3. **Work with us** — the filter run properly, on your numbers, alongside
   the alternatives, supported through to ballot day if it's Thomson
   Reserve you land on.

**10b. Offer stack, personalised by objective (46:00–51:00).** Callback to
Module 4's poll: *"If your objective was stay-for-free, here's what I
want to do for you... if it was investment, here's what changes..."*
Two tracks, same booking action:
- **Track 1 — Next Move Strategy Session:** position mapping, your
  personal version of the 5-Point Filter run on YOUR numbers, Thomson
  Reserve and the live alternatives checked against it, an honest
  verdict — including "wait" as a real outcome.
- **Track 2 — Thomson Reserve balloting support:** Track 1, plus stack
  strategy once the site plan drops, walk-away set in writing weeks
  ahead, live support on ballot day itself.

**10c. Rapid proof stack (51:00–53:00).** 2–3 fast result snippets across
different objectives. **[PLACEHOLDER — real, consented material from
Edmund; do not fabricate.]**

**10d. Real urgency (53:00–55:00).** Preview timing + genuine one-on-one
capacity only. No countdown, no manufactured scarcity.

**10e. Objection handling (55:00–57:00).** Same three reversals: "not
ready yet" / "what if TR isn't right for me" / "is this secretly a TR
pitch" — each answered by pointing back at proof already shown (Module
5's honest gap, Module 6's alternatives), never asserted fresh.

**10f. CTA (57:00–58:00).** Two-track booking link. *"Don't walk into a
showflat carrying someone else's excitement instead of your own numbers."*
