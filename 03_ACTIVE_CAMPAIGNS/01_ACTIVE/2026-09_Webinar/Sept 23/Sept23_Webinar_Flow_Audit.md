## 4. The pitch (01:28–01:58, 30 minutes)

What it contains, in order: invitation → Review/Evaluate/Identify → six levels → "bring your spouse and kids" → Paul testimonial → onboarding two audits → clarity plan / vision blueprint → 5-step financial framework → property insider navigator → "DIY and you will lose money" → Rachel testimonial → "not a one-time meeting" → if-all-this-did (×3) → Arasi testimonial → $2,000 anchor → 2019 $1.3M → +$300k example → backend team (bankers, lawyers, ID) → review every 4–5 years → eight questions → post-purchase (loan, construction videos, defect check, key collection) → Adlers testimonial → complimentary → guarantee (inverted) → two paths → QR → application walk-through → first 7 → first 10 → FAQ.

Problems:
1. The offer is explained four times (Review/Evaluate/Identify at 01:29, again at 01:37, again at 01:50, again at 01:54).
2. Five testimonials in the pitch (Paul, Rachel, Arasi, Adlers + Eugene/JY earlier). One well-told beats five read out.
3. Post-purchase agent services (defect checks, construction photos, ID/handyman, key collection) sit inside a "strategy session" pitch. They dilute what the free session IS and answer "are you an agent?" before anyone asked it.
4. Scarcity mismatch: "looking at today's room, that's a lot of you — opening from 7 to 10" with 16 participants visible in a Zoom meeting. 2 applied live.
5. 30 minutes with no Thomson Reserve content in it. The TR-motivated buyer's reason to apply NOW (preview 17 Oct, showflat registration) was mentioned at minute 9 and 01:43, never used as the close.

## 6. Fixes ranked by expected effect on applications

1. **Answer the title before the pitch.** New block at ~minute 45 of a 90-min show: "My number is $2,8xx. At that psf, $3M buys ~1,050 sqft = a 3-bedroom, not a 4. Will we ballot? Yes — for a 3-bed under $X; no — for a 2-bed unless Y." Deck slides 172–175 already exist for this. This is the climax; everything else in the content block feeds it.
2. **First Thomson number by minute 10.** Cut 00:18–00:23 (mindset lecture) entirely; backstory to 3 min; property journey and all testimonials out of the content block. Target: welcome 3 → hook 3 → stage poll 2 → who I am 3 → fast facts + "developer only released the mix this week" red flag 3 → 3 forces/4 factors from minute 14.
3. **Deliver the layout bonus live** — 3 minutes, two layouts (one to buy, one to avoid), the rest emailed to everyone who stayed, within 12 hours. Promise it twice, not six times.
4. **Pitch 30 → 12 minutes.** Transition (3 paths) → what the session is, once → the $1.9M "we said no" as the proof → ONE testimonial → $1,997 → complimentary → who it's for / not for → guarantee said correctly → QR + how to apply → 3 FAQs → Q&A. Cut six levels, family learning, navigator, backend team, defect checks, construction updates, 4–5-year review.
5. **Close on the Thomson deadline.** "Apply this week and we register you for the preview weekend from 17 Oct and walk the showflat with you" — concrete, time-bound, TR-specific. It is the strongest reason to apply tonight and it was used as a throwaway at minute 9.
6. **Scarcity that matches the room.** 16 attendees → "4 slots before the preview weekend" or a hard date ("applications close Friday 9pm"). Never "that's a lot of you" when everyone can see the count.
7. **Fix the guarantee.** Slide 129 and slide 146 contradict each other; slide 140's note already flags it. Say one line: "A good session ends yes, no, or not yet — and the decision is yours."
8. **Q&A before attrition.** Skip the 6-min testimonial video; 3-min form break with QR + countdown on screen, then Q&A with QR still visible. Q&A is where the last applications come from.
9. **Engagement: 8 prompts, each with a payoff.** Zoom polls for stage and objective (visible results, no typing). Read names back every time. Drop "does it make sense".
10. **No-show funnel (off-recording, biggest CPA lever).** 13/27 never attended. Replay link within 12 h with chapter timestamps to the $1,178 build and the 712/350 reveal; callback all 13 + the 14 attendees who didn't apply. If 1 more application comes from that, CPA drops from $515 to $387.
