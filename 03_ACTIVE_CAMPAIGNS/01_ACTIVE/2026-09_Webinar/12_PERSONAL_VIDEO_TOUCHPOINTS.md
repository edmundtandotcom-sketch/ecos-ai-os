# Thomson Reserve Webinar — Personal Video Touchpoint Scripts (v1)
Short, personal-camera video scripts for WhatsApp/email, across the full
journey: registration → webinar → consult application → booked call →
post-call, plus re-engagement and social. Each one is written to be
recorded in one take, 15–40 seconds, natural spoken delivery — not
read-aloud copy.

**Legend:** 🟢 = your own idea, tightened for recording · 🆕 = gap I think
is missing from the journey, added here.

---

## PART 1 — EVERGREEN / REUSABLE

### 🆕 V0 — Personal Intro (usable anywhere: registration page, first
WhatsApp touch, embedded in ads)
> "Hey, I'm Coach Edmund Tan, Singapore Real Estate Insider.
>
> I built my own property portfolio from under $600K to sitting around
> $6.6 to $9 million today — and every launch I look at, mine or a
> client's, goes through the exact same process. No exceptions.
>
> This webinar is that process, live, on Thomson Reserve, with real
> numbers — including the parts that don't make it look perfect.
>
> See you there."

**Use case:** this is your one reusable "who am I" asset — drop it on the
registration page itself, or as the very first video touch the moment
someone registers, before the more time-specific messages below.

---

## PART 2 — PRE-WEBINAR (Registration → Live Session)

### 🟢 V1 — Immediate Post-Registration (urgent-help offer)
> "Hey, I saw you just registered for the Thomson Reserve webinar —
> thanks for that.
>
> There's still a few days before we go live, so if something urgent
> about your own property plans needs sorting now, you don't have to
> wait for the session.
>
> Click the link below and let's have a direct conversation before then."

**Setup note:** WhatsApp deep-link with a pre-filled message to your
number, exactly as you described — matches the pattern of the invite
link already built for this campaign.

### 🟢 V2 — 3 Days Out
> "Hey [Name], quick one — we're 3 days out from the Thomson Reserve
> session.
>
> If you've already got specific questions about your own situation,
> start jotting them down now, so you're not trying to remember them on
> the night.
>
> And if it's urgent, you don't have to wait 3 days — click below and
> let's get you on a call with me directly before then.
>
> See you [date]."

### 🆕 V3 — Day Of (separate from V2, sent morning/afternoon of the session)
> "Hey [Name] — tonight's the night, 8pm.
>
> Come ready. I'm giving you my honest read on Thomson Reserve, live —
> including the parts that genuinely give me pause, not just the parts
> that sell it.
>
> Bring your questions, I'm answering everything at the end.
>
> See you at 8."

**Why this is separate from V2:** a 3-days-out video and a day-of video
do different jobs — one gives them time to prepare questions, the other
is pure excitement/urgency right before showtime. Sending only one loses
one of the two effects.

## PART 3 — POST-WEBINAR: APPLIED FOR CONSULT

### 🟢 V4 — Congrats / Confirmation After Application
> "Hey [Name], Coach Edmund here — your application just came through,
> thank you for that.
>
> I read what you shared, and I'm genuinely looking forward to speaking
> with you properly.
>
> My team will be in touch shortly to lock in a time. Talk soon."

### 🟢 V5 — Early Slot Opened Up
> "Hey [Name], Property Coach Edmund here.
>
> Saw you applied for a 1-on-1 session — a slot just opened up earlier
> than planned, in the next hour.
>
> If you're able to jump on now, click below and let's talk today
> instead of waiting."

### 🟢 V6 — "We're About To Get On" (minutes before the call)
> "Hey, we're about to jump on the call — genuinely looking forward to
> going through your property plans with you.
>
> Quick reminder: if your spouse or partner's part of this decision,
> grab them now so everyone's aligned from the start.
>
> And if you can, get to a laptop or a bigger screen instead of your
> phone — I'll be sharing some research and numbers that'll be too small
> to see otherwise.
>
> See you in a few minutes."

### 🆕 V7 — Pre-Call Reminder (the night before or morning of — earlier
than V6, which is minutes-before)
> "Hey [Name], quick reminder — we're speaking tomorrow at [time].
>
> Two things to sort before then: if your spouse or partner's involved
> in this decision, loop them into the call too.
>
> And find a proper screen — not just your phone — I'll be walking
> through real numbers and research that need a bit more room to see
> properly.
>
> Looking forward to it."

**Why both V6 and V7:** V7 gives them time to actually *act* on the
"bring your partner, find a screen" instructions — sent minutes before
(V6 alone), there's no time left to arrange either.

### 🆕 V8 — Post-Call Follow-Up (after the consult happens, any outcome)
> "Hey [Name], thanks for jumping on the call earlier — really enjoyed
> going through your situation with you.
>
> Wanted to send a quick personal note, not just an email — [reference
> something specific you discussed, e.g. 'especially on the timeline for
> your current place'].
>
> If anything comes up as you think it over, you know where to find me."

**Why this matters:** a personal video after the call — regardless of
whether they book on the spot — reinforces the relationship and keeps
the door open without being a hard follow-up pitch. Most funnels stop at
"we spoke," which wastes the goodwill a good call just built.

## PART 4 — RE-ENGAGEMENT

### 🟢 V9 — Attended Webinar, Never Applied
> "Hey [Name], Coach Edmund here — saw you on the session, thanks for
> staying to the end.
>
> Just a heads up, 2 more consult slots opened up this week if you want
> to go deeper into your own numbers.
>
> No pressure — didn't want you to miss it if the timing's better now.
> Link's below."

### 🟢 V10 — Registered, Never Showed Up
> "Hey [Name], missed you at the session — no worries, life happens.
>
> I'm running it again, link's below if you want in on the next one.
>
> And if you'd rather just talk directly instead of waiting for another
> session, hit reply or click below for that too."

### 🆕 V11 — Booked a 1-on-1 Call, Then No-Showed
> "Hey [Name], we had you down for a call earlier and I didn't see you
> come on — hope everything's alright.
>
> No worries at all, these things happen. Click below and grab another
> time that works better for you."

**Why this is different from V10:** a webinar no-show and a booked-
call no-show are different levels of investment — someone who booked a
1-on-1 and vanished needs a warmer, more personal recovery than a
generic "missed the webinar" message, since more trust was already
extended.

## PART 5 — SOCIAL / SHORT-FORM

### 🟢 V12 — Teaser Reel Script (for reels/social sharing, invite-a-friend)
> "Thomson Reserve is everywhere right now. Everyone's asking one
> question: is it good?
>
> I'm asking two, live — is it worth it, and can you actually get it.
>
> I'll show you my honest price read, not a guess — actually built out.
> And the mistakes I've watched other buyers make chasing hyped launches
> like this one.
>
> If someone you know is looking at this too — send them this. Link's
> below."

**Variants to cut down for different angles**, per your own list:
- *"Is this project going to help you"* → open on that exact question,
  cold, before introducing yourself — strong pattern-interrupt for a
  reel's first 2 seconds.
- *"The things we help others avoid"* → lead with a single specific
  mistake (e.g. "watched someone almost buy a unit type with terrible
  resale odds — here's why") rather than a generic claim.
- *"Honest price matrix, not a guess"* → this pairs directly with your
  deck's price-matrix reveal; consider recording this one *after* you've
  finalized your own Thomson Reserve estimate, so the reel can show the
  actual number on screen.

## RECORDING NOTES

- Keep each one to one take, no re-shoots for small stumbles — the
  slight imperfection is what makes these read as personal rather than
  produced, which is the entire point of this format over a templated
  text message.
- V0, V9, V10, V12 can be recorded once and reused indefinitely.
  V1–V8, V11 ideally get a `[Name]` recorded verbally if your tools
  support quick personalization, or stay generic enough to work without
  it.
- Film V6 and V7 back to back in one sitting since they're similar in
  content but different in timing — easy to confuse which is which
  later if recorded separately, days apart.
