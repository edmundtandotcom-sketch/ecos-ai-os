# The Second Property Ladder™ Canonical Angle Control Register

**Version:** 1.1 — Locked 40-Angle Master  
**Date:** 18 July 2026  
**Purpose:** Establish one permanent angle architecture for all Door A and Door B marketing assets.

## Governing rule

An angle is not a headline and not a script format.

Each angle must own:

1. one primary audience moment;
2. one hidden cause;
3. one belief to overturn;
4. one distinct reframe;
5. one appropriate bridge into The Second Property Ladder™.

Straight For Kill, 2 Types, Illustrative Scenario, Case Story and Don’t Do That are **formats beneath the angle**. They must never change the canonical angle ID or quietly introduce another angle.

## Door-routing rule

### Door A

Use when the dominant decision is about a second-property route, joint ownership, decoupling, ownership-name deployment, restructuring or adding another property.

### Door B

Use when the dominant decision is about what the current HDB or EC should do next: keep, sell, upgrade, rebuild, release equity or prepare for a later move.

### EC routing

- EC owner focused on decoupling, joint names or adding another property → Door A.
- EC owner focused on keep/sell/upgrade or deploying current EC equity → Door B.

## Locked overlap rulings

### Ruling 1: A02 absorbs the former A06 decoupling concept

**Final ruling:** Do not retain two separate decoupling-without-purpose angles.

- A02 owns both “Should we decouple?” and “We freed the name—now what?” as different executions of the same strategic angle.
- These can become A02 variations without consuming two canonical angle positions.

A06 is permanently reassigned to **The DINK Planning Window**.

### Ruling 2: B11 and B17 remain separate with different mechanisms

**Final ruling:** Keep both and enforce the following boundary.

- B11: replacement deadline, temporary-housing anxiety, emotional urgency and forced compromise after sale.
- B17: structurally triggering a sale before the complete sequence is mapped.

Any B11 script that teaches only “do not sell first” must be rejected or rewritten because it has drifted into B17.

### Ruling 3: B02, B06, B13 and B14 remain separate

**Final ruling:** Keep all four and enforce four different measurements.

- B02: total post-upgrade position.
- B06: monthly lifestyle pressure.
- B13: trade-off between visible lifestyle gain and financial control.
- B14: accessible liquidity after completion.

### Ruling 4: A15 and A16 remain separate

**Final ruling:** Keep both.

- A15: external conflict—different agents, different recommendations.
- A16: internal overload—more research without a filter.

## Definition of a genuine variation

A variation must change at least two of the following while preserving the same angle:

- hook family;
- awareness stage;
- emotional driver;
- example or situation;
- proof type;
- objection addressed;
- mechanism depth;
- creative device;
- CTA promise.

Synonym changes, reordered sentences and different section headings do not qualify.
