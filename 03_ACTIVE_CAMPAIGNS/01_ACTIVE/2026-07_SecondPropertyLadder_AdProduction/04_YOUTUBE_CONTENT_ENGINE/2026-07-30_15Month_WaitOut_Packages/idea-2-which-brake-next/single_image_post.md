## POST CAPTION (paste under the image)
One brake came off Singapore's property market on 28 Jul 2026.

Private owners — and former owners, any age — can now buy a non-subsidised HDB resale flat with no wait-out period, any size, if they take no HDB loan. [Source: Article, HDB (official), https://www.hdb.gov.sg/hdb-pulse/news/2026/removal-of-the-15-month-wait-out-period-for-private-residential-property-owners, accessed 2026-07-30]

But read the fine print before you move:
· Take an HDB loan → 15-month wait still applies.
· Subsidised flat / BTO → 30-month wait still applies.
· You still must sell the private within 6 months.
· ABSD and loan limits? Unchanged. [Source: Article, EdgeProp Singapore, https://www.edgeprop.sg/property-news/government-removes-15-month-wait-out-period-private-property-owners-buy-hdb-resale-flats, accessed 2026-07-30]

This is the first *easing* of the 2022 cooling suite — a signal about direction, not a starting gun. The forecasters are split: analysts cheered it, while the resale index has just printed two negative quarters. [Source: Article, The Edge Singapore, https://www.theedgesingapore.com/cityandcountry/residential/analysts-cheer-removal-15-month-wait-out-period-private-home-owners, accessed 2026-07-30]

So don't pick a forecast. Build a next move that wins whether the brakes stay, ease, or tighten.
