## POST COPY (verbatim)

**The rule that boxed in Singapore owners for almost 4 years vanished on 28 July. And most people are about to misread it.**

Here's the honest version.

Yes — private property owners can now buy a non-subsidised HDB resale flat with no 15-month wait-out, if they don't take an HDB housing loan. `[Source: Article, HDB (official), https://www.hdb.gov.sg/hdb-pulse/news/2026/removal-of-the-15-month-wait-out-period-for-private-residential-property-owners, accessed 2026-07-30]`

But the number that decides whether this move works for you isn't a price. It's a date: you must dispose of your private property within **6 months** of completing the resale purchase. `[Source: Article, EdgeProp Singapore, https://www.edgeprop.sg/property-news/government-removes-15-month-wait-out-period-private-property-owners-buy-hdb-resale-flats, accessed 2026-07-30]`

That single clock changes everything. This isn't "keep your condo and add a flat." It's *replace one with the other, on a timer* — in a resale market that has cooled, not heated. `[Source: Article, The Edge Singapore, https://www.theedgesingapore.com/cityandcountry/residential/analysts-cheer-removal-15-month-wait-out-period-private-home-owners, accessed 2026-07-30]`

The lesson was never HDB. It's that the rulebook you're planning your life around can expire without telling you. If a barrier that solid can vanish overnight, the "we can't" you shelved a few years ago deserves a fresh look — on today's rules, not the ones in your head.

That's the whole point of a next move: it's a decision, not a reaction.
