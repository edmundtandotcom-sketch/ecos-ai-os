## Audience-fit note (flagged plainly, per doctrine)
Strong fit on the audience *profile*: financially-ready existing condo/EC/high-value-HDB owners, DINK priority, ~30-55, ~$300k+ cash/CPF, 6-24 month window — the worked example is built on exactly this avatar.

**Weak-fit point, stated openly (and handled inside the content):** the specific policy change primarily serves owners moving *from* private *into* HDB, who must *dispose* of their private property within 6 months — i.e. a rightsizing/swap-down move, NOT adding-and-holding a second property. So this news does not *directly* enable the campaign's core "own one more property" action. The package handles this honestly rather than overstating fit: the news is used strictly as TRIGGER/PROOF of the market-prediction reflex, and the Two-Clock Rule module explicitly says that if your move is to *add* a second property rather than swap into a flat, this specific rule may not be your move — while the Personal Runway Method still applies identically. This keeps the piece doctrine-compliant (trigger, not subject) and avoids fearmongering or false relevance.

**No price predictions anywhere.** Every asset states plainly that no one — including Edmund — can promise where prices go; analysts are cited as split. Depth Ceiling respected: the method is taught in full, but the viewer's personal runway/rate/structure numbers are deliberately never completed — that gap is the Strategy Session.
