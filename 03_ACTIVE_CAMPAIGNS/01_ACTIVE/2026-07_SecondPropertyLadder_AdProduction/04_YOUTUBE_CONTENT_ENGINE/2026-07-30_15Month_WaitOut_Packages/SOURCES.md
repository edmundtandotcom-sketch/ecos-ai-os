## Verified facts (safe to state)
- Effective **28 Jul 2026** (immediate): private residential property owners AND former owners, **all ages**, buying a **non-subsidised HDB resale flat WITHOUT an HDB housing loan** → **no wait-out period, any flat size**.
- If they **take an HDB housing loan**, the **15-month wait-out still applies**.
- The **30-month wait-out still applies** for **subsidised** flats (including new BTO).
- Buyers must still **dispose of their private property** (in Singapore or overseas) **within 6 months** of completing the resale-flat purchase.
- Introduced **30 Sep 2022** as a cooling measure to prioritise first-time buyers.
- Announced by National Development Minister **Chee Hong Tat**; government said the measure had "served its purpose."
- Market context: HDB resale price growth **10.4% (2022) → 2.9% (2025)**; Resale Price Index **−0.1% q-o-q in 1Q2026** and **−0.3% q-o-q in 2Q2026**; slower/no growth 4Q2024–4Q2025.
- Do NOT predict specific future prices. Analysts are split on whether the removal re-accelerates prices.
