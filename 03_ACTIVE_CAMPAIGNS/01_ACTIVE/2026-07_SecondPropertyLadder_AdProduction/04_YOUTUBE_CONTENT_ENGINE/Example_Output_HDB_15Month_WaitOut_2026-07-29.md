## Key talking points extracted

**Verified facts (CNA / Minister's speech):**
- Removed with immediate effect, announced 2026-07-28 at the Singapore Economic Review Conference
- Introduced September 2022; carve-out existed for seniors selling private property to buy 4-room-or-smaller flat for retirement (now moot — whole rule gone)
- Government's stated reason: HDB resale price growth moderated 10.4% (2022) → 2.9% (2025) → −0.1% (Q1 2026) → −0.3% (Q2 2026)
- Same speech: ABSD remission timeline extended for en-bloc developers (700+ units: 6mo→1yr extension, total 6yrs; 1,400+ unit mega sites: 2yr extension, total 7yrs)
- MND "looking at" lowering singles HDB-purchase eligibility age below 35 — explicitly under review, not decided

**From Yvette Huang (data/analysis, verified where checkable):**
- HDB loan-to-value dropped 90%→85%→80%→75% between 2021–2024
- Concrete example: on an $800,000 flat, downpayment jumped from $80,000 to $200,000 — $120,000 difference
- Critical distinction most coverage blurs: only the 15-month wait-out (private→resale HDB) is gone. The separate 30-month wait-out (BTO/HDB loan/CPF grants) is untouched
- MOP supply pipeline: low point 2025, rising 2026–2028

**From Joy Shi (data/analysis, independently cross-verifies CNA's price figures):**
- Same price figures as CNA (0% Q4 2025, −0.1% Q1 2026, −0.3% Q2 2026) — independent corroboration
- Unit-type segmentation: 3-room/4-room barely move (steady BTO supply pipeline). 5-room/EA/multi-gen down ~1,000 units/year in demand since Sept 2022 — where the real effect concentrates
- Demand within that segment further concentrates in near-MRT/mature-estate/high-floor units specifically, not spread evenly

**From Eric Chiew (structure/angle, not copied verbatim):**
- Clean 3-part promise-and-deliver teaching structure
- Winner segmentation: retirees, right-sizing empty-nesters, estate planners
- FOMO objection-handling beat (don't rush a decision off a headline)

**From Aaron Lin (opinion, explicitly labeled as such in his own video):**
- Contrarian read: could be bad for the *private* resale market — HDB resale demand at a multi-year low, oversupply from second-timer flat owners on a forced 6-month sell clock, this move may pull more private sellers in without matching demand
