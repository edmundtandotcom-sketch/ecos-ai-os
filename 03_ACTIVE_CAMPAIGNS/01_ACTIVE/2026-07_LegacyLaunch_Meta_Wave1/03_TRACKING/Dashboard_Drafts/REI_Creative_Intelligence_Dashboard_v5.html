📊 Google Sheet Sync

Sheet is always kept as backup. Even when Meta + Google Ads APIs are connected via Apps Script, all data is written to the Sheet first. This dashboard reads from the Sheet. Your data is always in Drive.
To enable Sheet sync: In your Google Sheet, go to Extensions → Apps Script → Deploy → Web App. Copy the URL here. Then click ↻ Sync Sheet in the header.

📋 Kill / Scale Rules

These rules fire automatically on every creative in the Creatives tab. Rules are based on your confirmed thresholds.

  • ⛔ KILL — CPL >2× average CPL after SGD 80 spend
  • ⛔ KILL — Zero leads after SGD 150 spend
