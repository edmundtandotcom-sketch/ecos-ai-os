## SCRIPT 01 — "Went Up, Then What"

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Script ID | LL-CB001-LLS01-VID-PRIVATETHOUGHT-60-V01 |
| Concept Batch | CB001 — Appreciation Is Not Progression |
| Script Pattern | Private Thought |
| Hook Type | Private Thought Hook |
| Target Viewer | Owner of one appreciated condo, no next plan (LL-S01) |
| Private Thought | "My property has gone up, but I don't know what it should unlock next." |
| Core Problem | Appreciation is being treated as the finish line, not a starting point. |
| Hard Truth | A gain that isn't connected to a plan is just a number on paper. |
| Reframe | Appreciation is not progression unless it's connected to your next 10–15 years. |
| Soft CTA | Save this if your property went up but your plan didn't. |
| Estimated Duration | ~60s (Structure A) |

**HOOK** *[0–3s]*
Your property went up. But did your options go up with it?

**MIRROR** *[3–15s]*
You bought it years ago. On paper, it's done well. Friends say you're sitting on a good asset. And quietly, you agree — but you also can't quite say what happens next.

**HIDDEN PROBLEM** *[15–30s]*
Because "it went up" is not the same as "it's going somewhere." Most owners stop thinking the moment the value rises. The gain becomes a nice fact — not a next move.

**HARD TRUTH** *[30–45s]*
Appreciation is not progression. A property can keep climbing on paper while your actual options quietly stay exactly where they were ten years ago.

**REFRAME** *[45–60s]*
So the question isn't "how much did it go up." It's "what is that gain supposed to unlock for your next ten to fifteen years?" That's the difference between owning something that grew — and owning something that moves you forward.

**SOFT CTA** *[60–72s]*
Save this if your property went up but your plan didn't. Follow for more ways to think before your next property move.

## SCRIPT 06 — "Three Questions Before You Assume You're Fine"

| Field | Value |
|---|---|

| Concept Batch | CB003 — Stable Is Not Optimised |

| Private Thought | "I think I'm okay, but I'm not sure I'm making the most of this." |
| Core Problem | No lens to tell stable apart from optimised (BB04 / BB01). |
| Hard Truth | If you can't answer three simple questions, "okay" is a guess, not a plan. |

| Soft CTA | Sit with the one you couldn't answer. Follow for the full lens. |
| Estimated Duration | ~105s (Structure B) |

**HOOK** *[0–3s]*
Before you assume your property is fine, three quiet questions.

**MIRROR** *[3–20s]*
You're comfortable. The loan is manageable or gone. Nothing is going wrong. So you've never really stopped to check — you just carry a general feeling that it's all okay, and you leave it there.

**WRONG BELIEF** *[20–35s]*
The belief underneath is: "nothing's wrong, so nothing needs looking at." But a property that isn't causing problems today can still be quietly wrong for where you're heading.

**HARD TRUTH** *[35–50s]*
Stable is not optimised. And the fastest way to tell which one you're in isn't a feeling — it's whether you can answer three questions without guessing.

**REFRAME** *[50–80s]*
One: if you held this property for the next ten to fifteen years, what does it actually do for you — beyond just existing? Two: if life changed — retirement, family, health — could this position flex, or would it trap you? Three: if you had to explain why keeping it as-is is the best move, could you? Or is "it's fine" the only answer you've got?

**THINKING LENS** *[80–100s]*
If any of those made you pause, that pause is the real signal. It doesn't mean sell. It doesn't mean move. It means your property is currently running on assumption instead of a plan — and that's the one gap worth closing.

**SOFT CTA** *[100–108s]*
Sit with the question you couldn't answer cleanly. Follow for the full way to tell stable apart from optimised.

## SCRIPT 09 — "Hold, Sell, or Buy Again — Start Here"

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Script ID | LL-CB009-LLS07-VID-3QUESTION-110-V01 |
| Concept Batch | CB009 — Hold / Sell / Buy Again Confusion |
| Script Pattern | 3-Question Diagnostic |
| Hook Type | Wrong Question Hook |
| Target Viewer | Owner stuck between hold / sell / buy again, every option feels risky (LL-S07) |
| Private Thought | "I don't know whether to hold, sell, or buy again — every option feels risky." |
| Core Problem | Trying to pick an option before knowing the fit (BB11 / BB02). |
| Hard Truth | You can't choose the right move by staring harder at the options. |
| Reframe | The first job isn't to choose — it's to map which move fits your life, money, exit, and next 10–15 years. |
| Soft CTA | Before choosing hold, sell, or buy — answer these first. |
| Estimated Duration | ~110s (Structure B) |

**HOOK** *[0–3s]*
Hold, sell, or buy again? You're asking the wrong question first.

**MIRROR** *[3–20s]*
You go in circles. Sell now and you might not buy back in. Hold and you might be wasting the window. Buy again and it might be too aggressive. Every option has a reason to say no — so you stay stuck.

**WRONG BELIEF** *[20–35s]*
The belief keeping you stuck is that if you just think about the three options hard enough, the right one will reveal itself. It won't. You're comparing answers before you've defined the question.

**HARD TRUTH** *[35–50s]*
Here's the truth: hold, sell, and buy again aren't the decision. They're just three doors. The real decision is which door fits your life, your money, your exit, and your next ten to fifteen years.

**REFRAME** *[50–85s]*
So before you pick, answer three things. One: what does this move need to protect — income, family, flexibility, retirement? Two: what does it need to open — options later, a second asset, an exit? Three: which choice keeps your downside safe while still leaving the right doors open? Once those are clear, one option usually stops feeling risky — because it finally has a reason.

**THINKING LENS** *[85–102s]*
Notice what changed. You stopped asking "which option is best" and started asking "which option fits me." That's the whole shift. The confusion wasn't a lack of options. It was a lack of a filter.

**SOFT CTA** *[102–112s]*
Before you choose hold, sell, or buy again — sit with those three questions. Follow for the full decision lens.

## SCRIPT 10 — "The Two of You Aren't Wrong"

**MIRROR** *[3–18s]*

**HIDDEN PROBLEM** *[18–33s]*
But you're not actually arguing about the property. You're arguing from two different maps in your heads — one built on opportunity, one built on safety — and neither of you has put the maps side by side.
