# Legacy Launch — Copy Deck (Two-Step GHL Funnel, v2)

## Creative rationale

This deck is written to read like a private-bank memo, not a property advert. The premise: sophisticated Singapore buyers with $1M+ to move are not short of hype — they are short of a disciplined process that tells them which new launch to *avoid*. So every line leads with a number, treats disqualification as a status marker ("we turn away 90% of projects"), and pre-empts the reader's skepticism before it forms. The controlling fear is the "dead-end property" — a unit you can afford to buy but cannot afford to exit — and the promise is a mapped sequence of 2–3 moves, decided exit-first, before a single cheque is written. Voice throughout: calm, exact, unhurried. No exclamation marks, no timers, no adjectives doing a number's job. The reader should finish feeling evaluated, not sold to.

## 3. Problem / agitation — the dead-end property

**Section eyebrow:**
The mistake that costs $200K and five years

**Headline:**
The Dead-End Property: The Unit You Can Buy, But Can't Sell Without Losing

**Body:**
There is a specific kind of loss that doesn't announce itself on completion day. You buy the showflat you fell for. The renovation is beautiful. And then — three, five, seven years on — you try to move, and the numbers refuse to cooperate.

The unit hasn't kept pace. The layout the market wanted was next door. The stack you chose faces the wrong way for resale. You are not broke; you are *stuck* — capital locked in a home that became a forever home by accident, while the moves you meant to make quietly closed.

Run the arithmetic and it is rarely small:

- **Stuck 5–10 years** in an asset that won't appreciate or won't sell, watching better positioned owners move twice while you can't move once.
- **$200,000+** in unrealised gains and misplaced entry — the gap between the unit you bought on emotion and the one the data would have chosen.
- **One compounding move lost** — because the next purchase you were counting on to build the portfolio can't happen until the first one lets you out.

A showflat is engineered to make you feel. An exit is decided by numbers. The buyers who lose confuse the two. The cost isn't the mistake itself — it's the decade you spend paying for it.

## 4. Unique mechanism — why the usual way fails vs the New Launch Ladder™

**Section eyebrow:**
Why the usual way fails

**Headline:**
Agents Sell What They Have. We Decide What You Should Never Touch.

**The usual way (why it fails):**
The conventional path is built to close a transaction, not to build your position. An agent representing a project is paid to move *that* project — so every launch becomes the right launch, every stack a "good buy", every showflat visit an exercise in managed emotion. You are shown the upside and steered past the exit. Decisions get made on hype, floor-plan aesthetics, and the pressure of a launch weekend — the three worst inputs a serious buyer can use. It is not dishonest. It is simply not on your side of the table.

**The New Launch Ladder™ (why it works):**
We start from the opposite end. Before we look at a single unit, we look at how you get *out* of it — and what it lets you buy next. That is the Exit-First method: every entry is judged by its exit, and every purchase is one rung on a ladder of 2–3 planned moves, not a standalone bet.

- **We disqualify 90% of projects** on data — pricing, location, unit mix, exit liquidity — before any of them reaches your shortlist. Most of the work is subtraction.
- **We map 2–3 moves before you buy the first** — so today's purchase is chosen for where it takes you in five and ten years, not for how the showflat feels on Saturday.
- **We decide with the numbers on the table** — entry PSF, holding costs, ABSD exposure, projected exit window — the way a fund evaluates a position, not the way a buyer falls for a home.

**The three reasons buyers work with us:**

**1. Exit-first, always.**
We won't recommend an entry we can't show you the exit for. If we can't map how and when you get out at a gain, you don't buy — full stop. It's the discipline agents can't offer because their incentive ends at your signature.

**2. We turn away most of the market.**
Ninety percent of projects fail our filter and never reach you. What survives is a short, defensible list — chosen for liquidity and upside, not commission or convenience. Disqualification is the product.

**3. We invest the way we advise.**
Our own portfolio is built on this exact method — the same ladder, the same exit-first rule, the same numbers. We eat our own cooking. You're not buying a theory; you're buying the process we use with our own capital.

## 9. FAQ

**Is this a sales pitch in disguise?**
No. It's a working strategy session. We map your position, tell you which launches to avoid, and outline one or two entries that fit — and you leave with that whether or not you ever engage us. If a project genuinely suits you, we'll say so; if none does, we'll say that too. The disqualification is real.

**What does the call cost?**
Nothing. The hour is free. It's limited to buyers moving in the next 3–6 months because that's who we can actually help — which is also why we ask a few qualifying questions before booking.

**How are you different from a property agent?**
An agent representing a project is paid to move that project. We start from your exit and work backwards — disqualifying 90% of launches before any reach you, and mapping 2–3 moves ahead rather than closing one. Our incentive is your position over the next decade, not your signature this weekend.

**What if I haven't shortlisted anything yet?**
That's often the better starting point. It means we build the filter with you from the ground up — instead of talking you out of choices you've already grown attached to. Bring what you have, or bring nothing but your numbers.

**Do you earn commissions?**
Where we transact, we're compensated like any professional in this market — and we're transparent about it on the call. What's different is the order of operations: nothing is recommended unless it survives the exit-first filter first. The process disqualifies before it ever earns.

**Is my information safe?**
Yes. Your details are used only to prepare for and arrange your strategy call, held in line with Singapore's PDPA (2012), and never sold or passed to third parties. You can ask us to remove your data at any time.
