# Legacy Launch V2 — GHL "Build with AI" Prompt Kit

Use this with GHL's **Generate with AI / Build** panel in the page builder.
Workflow: fill the structured fields → paste the MASTER PROMPT into the Build/Generate
box → Generate → then run the REFINEMENT PROMPTS one at a time → finish with the
CLEANUP checklist. Do this on the **Strategy Call Opt-in** step (page 1) and the short
one on the **Book Your Strategy Call** step (page 2).

## 2) MASTER PROMPT  (paste into the Build / "Plan, build, modify anything" box)

Build a long-form, high-converting landing page for a Singapore new-launch property
ADVISORY (not a normal property-agent page). It must feel like a private-bank / family-office
memo: calm, precise, numbers-first, editorial. Premium and understated — NOT salesy.

DESIGN SYSTEM (apply exactly):
- Colours: deep navy #0A1C30 for dark sections, ivory #F7F4EE for light sections, white #FFFFFF
  for cards, champagne gold #C2A15C for accents (eyebrows, thin rules, numbers, buttons), ink
  #16202C for text on light and #EDE8DD for text on dark. ABSOLUTELY NO RED anywhere.
- Fonts: "Playfair Display" (serif) for ALL headlines; "Inter" (sans-serif) for all body and buttons.
- Style: generous whitespace, thin 1px gold horizontal rules as dividers, small UPPERCASE
  letter-spaced gold "eyebrow" labels above each headline, large serif numbers for stats.
- Buttons: solid champagne-gold background with navy #0A1C30 text, ~8px corners, tall (min 52px),
  full-width on mobile. Every primary button label = "Check If You Qualify".
- Alternate section backgrounds for rhythm: navy hero, then ivory, white, navy, ivory, white,
  ivory, white, navy, dark footer.
- Mobile-first, fully responsive. No stock smiling-agent photos; keep it clean and typographic.

PAGE STRUCTURE & COPY (use this copy VERBATIM, in this order):

1. THIN TOP BAR (navy, gold text, small): "For Singapore buyers moving in the next 3–6 months — with $1M+ to place and no appetite for a mistake."

2. HERO (navy):
   - Eyebrow: "The New Launch Ladder™ — Exit-First Property Advisory"
   - Headline (H1): "We Disqualify 90% of New Launches — So You Buy the One You Can Actually Exit"
   - Subhead: "Most buyers pick a unit they can afford to enter. We map the unit you can afford to leave — with your next 2–3 moves planned before you commit a single dollar. This is advisory, not agency."
   - Button: "Check If You Qualify"
   - Under button: "Free 1-hour strategy call. By application only."
   - Trust row (4 gold stat blocks): "$160,014,029 invested in exit-ready launch units" · "100+ buyers guided" · "90% of projects disqualified" · "600+ videos, 2,000+ subscribers"

3. PROBLEM (ivory):
   - Eyebrow: "The mistake that costs $200K and five years"
   - Headline: "The Dead-End Property: The Unit You Can Buy, But Can't Sell Without Losing"
   - Body: explain that buyers fall for the showflat, then 3–7 years later can't exit because the
     unit didn't keep pace, the layout/stack was wrong for resale — capital locked in a "forever
     home by accident." Then 3 bullet points: "Stuck 5–10 years in an asset that won't appreciate
     or sell" · "$200,000+ in unrealised gains and misplaced entry" · "One compounding move lost".
   - Close: "A showflat is engineered to make you feel. An exit is decided by numbers."

4. MECHANISM (white):
   - Eyebrow: "Why the usual way fails"
   - Headline: "Agents Sell What They Have. We Decide What You Should Never Touch."
   - Two short columns: (a) "The usual way" — agents are paid to move their project, so every
     launch becomes 'the right launch'; decisions get made on hype, floor-plan looks and launch-
     weekend pressure. (b) "The New Launch Ladder™" — we start from the exit: every entry judged
     by its exit, every purchase one rung on a 2–3 move ladder. We disqualify 90% of projects on
     data before any reach your shortlist.
   - Then 3 pillars (gold numbers 1–3): "Exit-first, always." / "We turn away most of the market."
     / "We invest the way we advise." (short paragraph each; the third says the coaches use this
     exact method on their own portfolio — "we eat our own cooking").

5. CASE STUDIES (navy, gold numbers):

     • MoK & Jine: 15 yrs in a ~$600K HDB → split into a ~$1.2M home + ~$1M rental unit; rent offsets

6. TESTIMONIALS (ivory):
   - Eyebrow: "In their words"

     "I came in guessing. I left with a framework I could actually follow." /

10. OPT-IN FORM SECTION (navy):
    - Eyebrow: "By application"; Headline: "Check If You Qualify for a Strategy Call"

11. FINAL CTA (navy): Eyebrow "The next move"; Headline "Map the Exit Before You Commit the Capital";
    short recap line; button "Check If You Qualify"; trust line "$160M+ placed · 100+ buyers guided ·

## 3) REFINEMENT PROMPTS (run one at a time after the first generation)

Use these in the Build box to fix what the AI gets wrong (it usually does on the first pass):

  #F7F4EE, accents champagne gold #C2A15C, no red anywhere. Make all buttons gold with navy text."
- "Change all headline fonts to Playfair Display and all body/button fonts to Inter."
- "Add more whitespace: increase top and bottom padding on every section, and add a thin 1px gold
  divider line between sections."
- "Make each section background alternate: navy, ivory, white, navy, ivory, white, ivory, white,
  navy, then a dark footer — in that order."
- "Add a small uppercase gold eyebrow label above every section headline."
- "Make it fully mobile responsive: full-width buttons, larger tap targets, one idea per screen."
- "Rewrite any headline that lost a number so it keeps the exact figures from my copy (90%,
  $160,014,029, $200K, $1M, 2–3 moves)."

## 4) POST-GENERATION CLEANUP CHECKLIST

The AI scaffolds fast but rarely finishes clean. After generating, check:

- [ ] Colours are navy/ivory/gold only — no leftover blue/red template colours.
- [ ] Headlines are Playfair Display; body is Inter.
- [ ] All the exact numbers survived ($160,014,029, 90%, $200K, $5M+, $3.5M, $235K, $1.8M).
- [ ] The two dropdowns exist on the form with the exact options, plus the PDPA consent checkbox.
- [ ] Form submit → set "On submit" to redirect to the Book Your Strategy Call step URL.
- [ ] Every button says "Check If You Qualify" (form button "Request My Strategy Call") and scrolls to the form.
- [ ] No exclamation marks, no countdown timers, nothing reading as a hard-sell.
- [ ] Mobile view looks clean (toggle the phone icon in the builder).

> Reality check: GHL's AI will get you ~70% there natively (and editable). To get the polished
> private-bank finish, you can also paste our hand-built `page1-custom-css.css` into Page Settings →
> Custom CSS as a top-up, but note it only styles our custom-code blocks (class `llv2`), not AI-
> generated elements — so treat the AI route and the custom-code route as two separate options, not
> mixed. The AI route = native + editable + ~70% look. The custom-code route (in ghl-build-guide.md)
> = pixel-perfect but pasted as HTML blocks.
