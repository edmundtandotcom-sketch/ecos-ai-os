# CRO Research Synthesis — High-Ticket Call Funnels (for Legacy Launch v2)

## Winning section order (gold standard)
1. Thin trust strip (hard numbers) / eyebrow audience callout
2. Hero: eyebrow → headline (outcome + timeframe + number) → subhead (kills #1 objection) → CTA → micro-trust line
3. Problem/agitation: name the pain in reader's language, quantify cost of inaction
4. Unique Mechanism: (a) why what they've tried fails, (b) why this method works — the logical bridge most pages skip
5. Proof stack: named case studies with number deltas + timeframe (Before/After), max 2–3 proof types, re-seeded every 2–3 scroll lengths
6. Offer stack: what they GET from the call (value-stack even for a free call)
7. "What happens on the call": format, length, who, what to prepare, no-hard-sell promise (reduces no-shows)
8. Qualification/disqualification block: for-you/not-for-you two-column (checks vs X's) RIGHT BEFORE final CTA
9. FAQ: objection handling (how is this different, is this a sales pitch, cost)
10. Final CTA: restated promise + trust element

## Copy formulas that win
- Headlines: number + outcome + timeframe; self-diagnostic questions; loss-aversion framing; process-not-luck ("predictable", "system", "down to the dollar")
- Put a hard number in every headline (Stacked Homes convention: "$1.5M", "$2,350 psf") — adjectives are discounted by sophisticated audiences
- Subhead exists to pre-empt the #1 objection
- CTA buttons: outcome verbs, never "Submit" — "Book Your Strategy Call", "Check If You Qualify", "Get My Exit Plan"
- Disqualify at the door (banner before content) AND before final CTA
- Qualification reframes seller as evaluator, not supplicant — essential for premium positioning
- BANT-lite questions on the form itself (3–5 fields max on mobile): timeline, budget/current property, decision authority

## Design register: "advisory" vs "salesy"
- Serif display headlines (Playfair/Canela/Instrument Serif) + clean sans body = editorial authority. Bold-sans-everywhere = internet marketer.
- Navy/deep charcoal + white + ONE restrained accent (gold/deep green, never neon). No red urgency bars.
- Generous whitespace lifts perceived value (research: up to +300% perceived value). Dense = hard-sell.
- Monochrome line icons, never colorful illustration sets.
- Numbers presented plainly like a financial publication, not cartoon counters.
- Photography: restrained/architectural, not smiling-agent stock.
- Structure = funnel-world; skin = advisory-world.

## SG property specifics
- PropertyLimBrothers: dual-headline hero (promise + positioning), institutional-scale stats, testimonials at TWO scroll depths, CEA license number as regulatory trust signal, outcome-verb CTAs.
- Stacked Homes: data-forward numeric headlines, verifiable readership stats ("52,400+ readers") more credible than client claims, editorial black/white restraint.
- WHITE SPACE OPPORTUNITY: neither runs a hard qualification block — a disqualification-led advisory page is differentiated in SG market.

## Mobile
- Sticky slim bottom CTA bar (+15–25% on long pages), dismissible, body gets bottom padding
- Full-width 48px-height buttons, repeat CTA after every major section
- One idea per screen-height; total length can be long, density is the enemy
- Embedded scheduler inline > external redirect; form 3–5 fields max
