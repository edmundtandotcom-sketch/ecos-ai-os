## 1. Purpose of This Engine

The Legacy Launch Video Script Engine v1.1 converts the Legacy Launch campaign strategy into high-quality video scripts across the full video ecosystem.

It does not replace the Concept Batch Map. It converts the Concept Batch Map into video stories, hooks, bodies, CTA tags, filming briefs, editing notes, and trackable modular assets.

This engine must:
- prevent random video scripts
- prevent every reel and VSL from sounding the same
- preserve advisory trust
- make scripts teleprompter-ready

- separate hook testing from body testing
- separate CTA testing from story testing
- keep every asset traceable to Concept Batch Map and performance data

## 3. The Core Conversion Spine

Every conversion video uses the same persuasion spine, adapted by length:

HOOK -> STORY -> OFFER

The Story layer is built from:
Mirror -> Lie -> Hard Truth -> Reframe -> Method -> Proof

Mandatory blocks:
- Hook
- Mirror
- Hard Truth
- Reframe
- Method or Mechanism
- CTA / Offer

Conditional blocks:
- Lie: optional in short reels, stronger in VSLs and retargeting when it lands cleanly.
- Proof: mandatory for VSLs, YouTube, retargeting, and warm/hot audiences. For cold reels, use light proof, pattern proof, or diagnostic proof if hard proof is unavailable.
- Offer: must promise the opposite of the hook pain, but for property advisory it should not use hard-sales price/guarantee language.

Property advisory adaptation:
The goal is not to sell a project. The goal is to make the viewer want a Property Position Review, diagnostic, WhatsApp conversation, or Legacy Launch Clarity Consult.

## 4. Modular Video Assembly System

This is the most important operational upgrade.

Instead of treating each video as one fixed file, we treat video as three separate modules:

1. Hook Bank
The first 3-8 seconds. Tests scroll-stop and self-identification.

2. Body Bank
The main story/script. Tests message, angle, retention, belief shift, and trust.

3. CTA Bank
The final action tag. Tests whether the audience is ready for comment, checklist, WhatsApp, review, or booking.

Assembly formula:
HOOK CLIP + BODY SCRIPT + CTA TAG = COMPLETE VIDEO ASSET

Example:
H2 Hard Truth Hook + Body 2 Direct Diagnosis + CTA Soft Review = Final Ad V01

Why this works:
- If hook hold is weak, improve hooks without rewriting the body.
- If 50% watch rate is weak, improve body story, pacing, or reframe.
- If CTR/WhatsApp response is weak but retention is strong, improve CTA.
- If lead quality is poor, tighten audience, hook specificity, or CTA qualification.

Important:
Hooks and CTAs should be filmed as separate clips from the body. Bodies should start cleanly after the hook and end cleanly before CTA so the editor can swap modules without awkward transitions.

## 5. Multiple Body Scripts - Allowed, But Controlled

Yes, we can have multiple body scripts. But we should not create unlimited bodies at the start.

Correct rule:
One Concept Batch can have multiple body scripts if each body tests a different strategic variable.

A body script should differ by one of these:
- script pattern
- awareness stage
- angle
- founder delivery mode
- proof mechanism
- funnel role

A body script should not differ just because we want “more content.”

Recommended body limits:
- New concept batch: start with 1 primary body.
- Strong concept batch: create 2 bodies.
- Proven high-intent concept batch: create 3 bodies.
- Mature concept with proven lead quality: create body bank of 5-7, like the G1 Idle Million model.

Do not start every concept batch with 7 bodies. That creates tracking confusion and production waste.

Recommended initial body bank per concept:
Body A - Problem Aware Core Body
Best for cold traffic. Usually Private Thought, Direct Diagnosis, Contrarian Reframe, or 3-Question Diagnostic.

Body B - Solution Aware Authority Body
Best for warm audience. Usually Founder POV, Consult Pattern, Case Review, Before / After Thinking, or Tool Walkthrough.

Body C - Product Aware / Retargeting Body
Only after data. Usually Objection Handling, Proof + Objection Stack, Warning Story, Composite Case, or WhatsApp Follow-Up.

Body compatibility rule:
Not every hook should go with every body. Each hook must match the body’s promise, audience stage, and emotional temperature.

## 7. Hook Library v2.0

The engine now uses a broader hook library. The goal is not to use all hook types in every batch. The goal is to choose the right hook role for the audience temperature and format.

Core Conversion Hooks:
1. Call-Out Hook
Names exactly who the video is for.
Example: If you own a condo but have no clear next move, this is for you.

2. Hard Truth Hook
Makes a blunt statement they cannot easily ignore.
Example: A million-dollar condo can still leave you stuck.

3. Question / Curiosity Gap Hook
Asks a question with an uncomfortable gap.
Example: Your condo went up. But did your options go up too?

4. Contrarian Hook
Says the opposite of what the market expects.
Example: Upgrading is not always the right move.

5. Private Thought Hook
Names the internal thought they have not said out loud.
Example: My property is worth over a million. Shouldn’t something be happening with that money?

Expansion Hooks:
6. Open Loop / Story Hook
Starts a situation that must be resolved.
Example: A couple came in thinking their property was safe. The numbers told a different story.

7. Number / Stat Hook
Uses a real number, approved range, or pattern.
Example: Some owners have six figures of usable equity but no idea what it should unlock next.

8. Warning / Cost of Delay Hook
Names the cost of not reviewing.
Example: The danger is not that your property did not grow. The danger is waiting until options narrow.

9. Pattern Interrupt Hook
Says something unexpected.
Example: Sell your condo. I know - hear me out first.

10. Authority Hook
Uses experience, consult pattern, or founder POV.
Example: After reviewing hundreds of property positions, this is the gap I see most often.

Warm / Retargeting Hooks:
11. Objection Hook
Starts with the hesitation.
Example: If you are afraid of making the wrong move, that is not a bad sign.

12. Proof Hook
Starts with a proof pattern or case.
Example: Three owners. Three different situations. Same problem - no clear next move.

13. Scenario Hook
Starts with a very specific situation.
Example: You are in your 50s, your condo is paid down, and everyone tells you you are safe.

YouTube / Authority Hooks:
14. List / Checklist Hook
Example: Before you hold, sell, or buy again, check these five areas.

15. Problem-Solving Hook
Example: Here is how to tell whether your property is still working for your next 10 years.

16. Inspiration / Future Self Hook
Use sparingly. Best for YouTube, warm audience, legacy/family, or long-form.
Example: Your property should not only protect your present. It should give your future more options.

Hook selection rule:
For first production tests, use mostly Hard Truth, Question, Contrarian, Call-Out, and Private Thought. Add Open Loop, Warning, Number, Pattern Interrupt, and Authority after early data shows which hook family wins.

## 10. Approved 120s Reel Structure

Primary use: paid reel ad and high-conversion social content.

0-3s: Hook
One sentence. No greeting, no logo, no intro. First cut by 1.2s.

3-20s: Mirror
Describe their real situation. Use 2-3 parallel lines. Real scenes, not vague emotions.

20-35s: Lie / Hidden Assumption
Optional, but powerful if it lands. Say the story they tell themselves in their own words.

35-50s: Hard Truth
One blunt sentence. No softening.

50-80s: Reframe
Show the wrong way first. Then flip to the better way. The gap is the big idea.

80-105s: Method + Proof
Name the method or mechanism. Use one proof type only. For cold traffic, use pattern proof or diagnostic proof if full case proof is not available.

105-120s: CTA
One action. CTA must mirror the pain in the hook.

120s rule:
One idea only. If the viewer cannot explain the video in one sentence, the script is too complex.

## 11. Approved 6-Min VSL Structure

Primary use: warm or retargeting audience. Landing page, pre-consult, YouTube pre-roll, or embedded VSL.

0-15s: Hook / Diagnosis
Name the exact person and current situation. No warmup.

15-60s: Deep Mirror
4-5 parallel lines. Build the week they are living. Bury the Lie if possible.

60-90s: The Lie
In a VSL, the Lie can become its own block. Say it clearly, then show empathy.

90-120s: Hard Truth
One blunt statement. Prime them to accept the reframe.

120-180s: Reframe
State the wrong model. Flip it. Name the gap. This is the emotional centre of the VSL.

180-270s: Method
Name the REI / property review method. Break into 3 clear steps.

270-330s: Proof
Use one proof type. Real, approved, anonymised where needed.

330-360s: CTA
Invite one next step. No over-explaining. No multiple offers.

## 13. Proof Rules for Singapore Property Advisory

The uploaded video foundation uses proof as a key trust block. We keep that principle, but adapt it for compliance and privacy.

Allowed proof types:
1. Track Record
Use approved personal or business track record only.

2. Anonymised Client Pattern
Use real patterns without exposing private client identity.

3. Composite Case
Allowed only when clearly framed as a composite / common pattern and not presented as a literal single case.

4. Own Story
Use Edmund/Cindior’s own property journey or lessons when relevant.

5. Warning Story
Use carefully. Show cost of not reviewing, not scare tactics.

6. Diagnostic Proof
Show how a tool, checklist, scorecard, or framework reveals a gap.

7. Pattern Proof
Use “we often see...” or “many owners...” only when grounded in real consult patterns.

Proof restrictions:
- Do not invent names, numbers, or results.
- Do not imply guaranteed returns.
- Do not use client details that breach privacy.
- Do not make the proof the hero. The method is the hero.
- Do not over-focus on profit. Focus on clarity, safety, sequence, options, and position review.

## 16. Performance Diagnosis Rules

Use performance data to diagnose the weak module.

Hook Hold Rate:
Target: >40% watch past 3 seconds.
If weak: rewrite hooks, first frame, on-screen text, or delivery.

Body 50% Watch Rate:
Target: >20% reach 50% of body.
If weak: body has pacing, mirror, reframe, or complexity issue.

CTA Click / Reply Rate:
Target: >2% click or meaningful response for paid reel tests.
If weak while retention is strong: CTA or offer mismatch.

Lead Quality:
If engagement is high but leads are poor, tighten call-out, MAS-fit, concept specificity, and CTA qualification.

Consult Rate:
If leads do not book, add warm retargeting, proof, objection handling, or WhatsApp follow-up video.

Do not rewrite everything at once. Change one module at a time:
- Hook problem = change hook only.
- Body problem = rewrite body.
- CTA problem = test CTA tag.
- Lead quality problem = tighten audience/angle/CTA.

## 17. Script Scoring Rubric Before Filming

Every script should be scored before filming.

Hook Power /20:
Does it stop the right person in the first 3 seconds?

Self-Recognition /20:
Does the viewer feel “this is me” from real scenes, not generic feelings?

Retention Flow /15:
Does each block earn the next 5-10 seconds?

Belief Shift /20:
Does it clearly replace the wrong model with a better model?

Trust / Authority /10:
Does the method feel concrete? Is proof specific and compliant?

Action Pull /10:
Does CTA mirror the hook pain and give one clear step?

Simplicity /5:
Can the viewer explain the idea in one sentence?

Total: 100.
Below 70 = rewrite. Do not film. Do not deploy.
70-79 = usable but improve before paid spend.
80-89 = production-ready.
90+ = priority filming candidate.
