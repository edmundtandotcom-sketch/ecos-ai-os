\#\# 1\. Master Message

Your property has grown. Has your strategy grown with it?

\#\# 2\. Core Belief Shift

Old belief:

“My property has appreciated, so I am fine.”

New belief:

“Appreciation is only useful when I know what it unlocks next.”

\#\# 3\. First Audience Lane

G1 — Idle Million

Audience:

Mature private property owner with one valuable property and no clear plan.

Private thought:

“My property is worth over a million. Shouldn’t something be happening with that money?”

\#\# 4\. Active Angle Bank

\#\#\# ANG-001 — Idle Million

Core idea:

A million-dollar property with no plan may be sitting idle.

Best format:

\- image ad;  
\- private thought reel;  
\- notes-style post.

Hook examples:

\- “Your property is worth over a million dollars. Is it actually working for you?”  
\- “Your property has gone up. That is not a plan. That is just time passing.”

\#\#\# ANG-002 — Appreciation Is Not Progression

Core idea:

Property value can rise while the owner’s life-money-exit strategy stays stuck.

Best format:

\- strategic gap chart;  
\- report-style image;  
\- short explainer reel.

Hook examples:

\- “Your property grew. But did your options grow?”  
\- “A gain is not a plan until you know what it unlocks.”

\#\#\# ANG-003 — Stable Is Not Optimised

Core idea:

Comfortable does not always mean future-ready.

Best format:

\- direct diagnosis reel;  
\- advisory image ad;  
\- owner review post.

Hook examples:

\- “Paid off does not mean optimised.”  
\- “Stable does not always mean safe for the next stage.”

\#\#\# ANG-004 — Owner vs Strategist

Core idea:

Owning property is not the same as having a property strategy.

Best format:

\- comparison image;  
\- founder POV reel;  
\- carousel.

Hook examples:

\- “There is a difference between owning property and having a property strategy.”  
\- “The biggest mistake may be owning the right property and doing nothing with it.”

\#\#\# ANG-005 — Review Before Options Narrow

Core idea:

Age, loan tenure, family needs, and market movement can quietly narrow options.

Best format:

\- timeline visual;  
\- warm retargeting reel;  
\- consult CTA post.

Hook examples:

\- “The safest time to review your property is before you are forced to.”  
\- “Most owners wait until their options have already narrowed.”
