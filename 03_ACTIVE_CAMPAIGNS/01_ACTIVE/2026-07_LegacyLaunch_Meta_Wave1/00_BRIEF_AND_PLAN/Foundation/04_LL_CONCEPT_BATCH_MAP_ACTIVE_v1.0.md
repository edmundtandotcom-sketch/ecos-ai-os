# 4\. Frontend Production Questions

For every future output, answer these 7 questions before writing:

2\. What situation are they in?  
3\. What private thought are we naming?  
4\. What wrong belief are we breaking?  
5\. What better question are we giving them?  
6\. What format are we using?  
7\. What action do we want them to take?

# 12\. Performance Learning Rules

After each test, do not rewrite the whole system.

Only update the weak layer:  
\- weak audience response → review Core Buyer Profile or Production Lane;  
\- weak lead quality → review MAS-Fit Filter and CTA;  
\- weak click-through → review Creative Execution or Copy Framework;  
\- weak comments / saves → review Private Thought and Belief Block;  
\- weak consult bookings → review CTA Path and WhatsApp follow-up;  
\- weak trust → review Mechanism / Proof / Insight;  
\- high engagement but poor quality → tighten MAS-Fit and direct the CTA.

Decision types:  
Kill — concept, angle, or format has no usable signal.  
Keep — signal exists but needs refinement.  
Scale — concept and lead quality are strong.  
Rewrite — idea is good but copy or execution is weak.  
Reframe — belief block or better question is wrong.
