\#\# 1\. Campaign Job

Legacy Launch helps Singapore property owners review whether their current property position is still working for their next life stage.

This is not a project-selling campaign.

This is a property-position review campaign.

\#\# 2\. Shared Intelligence Rule

This campaign does not own all client intelligence.

It only extracts the intelligence needed for Legacy Launch production.

Shared intelligence remains in:

03\_CONTENT\_MARKETING\_ENGINE / 01\_INPUT\_LIBRARY

\#\# 3\. Primary Audience

Singapore private property owners who have built value in one property but have not mapped what that value should unlock next.

Likely situation:

\- owns one private property;  
\- property has appreciated;  
\- feels comfortable but uncertain;  
\- not sure whether to hold, sell, restructure, decouple, buy again, or prepare for retirement;  
\- does not want to be sold a project.

\#\# 4\. First Production Lane

Start with G1 only.

G1 — Idle Million

Audience:

Mature private property owner with a valuable property but no clear next-move plan.

Private thought:

“My property is worth over a million. Shouldn’t something be happening with that money?”

Core pain:

Appreciation is not progression.

Belief to break:

“If my property has gone up, I am fine.”

Replacement belief:

“A property gain only becomes useful when it is connected to a next-stage strategy.”

\#\# 5\. Campaign Promise

Legacy Launch does not promise profit.

It promises clarity:

\- review your property position;  
\- understand what your current property can or cannot unlock;  
\- compare safer options before acting;  
\- map your next 2–3 property decisions.

\#\# 6\. Offer

Primary offer:

1-Hour Property Position Review / Legacy Launch Clarity Consult.

Soft CTA option:

Comment or WhatsApp REVIEW to start with a Property Position Checklist.

\#\# 7\. Message Boundaries

Use:

\- strategy before project;  
\- property position review;  
\- appreciation is not progression;  
\- stable is not optimised;  
\- map your next 2–3 moves;  
\- review before options narrow.

Avoid:

\- guaranteed profit;  
\- sure-win returns;  
\- buy now urgency;  
\- fear countdown;  
\- all owners should sell;  
\- all owners should decouple;  
\- project hype.
