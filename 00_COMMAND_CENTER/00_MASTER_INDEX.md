## 1. What the system is

The system is separated into three daily surfaces:

| Surface | Role |
|---|---|
| `00_AI_OPERATING_SYSTEM` | How the business thinks, decides and operates |

1. AI OS `CLAUDE.md`

## 6. Asset Library lifecycle

`01_ASSET_LIBRARY` follows this lifecycle:

1. Upload Inbox
2. Approved Library
3. Source Archive
4. Creative Intelligence
5. Expired and Retired
6. Migration Archive

Do not store new bulk media inside the AI OS. AI OS files may link to assets where strategic retrieval requires it.

## 7. Active-campaign rule

Keep each active campaign together in `03_ACTIVE_CAMPAIGNS` while it is being built or run.

After completion:
- reusable approved assets → Approved Library
- raw/editable sources → Source Archive
- distribution-ready finals → Campaign Finals
- tests and performance learning → Creative Intelligence

## 8. Controlled evolution

Decision 095 removed the formal 90-day freeze.
