# Outcome and Handoff Model
Version: v1.0
Status: APPROVED
Date: 2026-07-20

## Canonical outcomes

- `Proceed` — advance to the approved next commercial/advisory step.
- `Prepare` — prerequisites must be completed before proceeding.
- `Nurture` — timing or readiness is not current; follow-up is scheduled with a reason.
- `No Fit` — do not proceed; capture reason and close respectfully.

An outcome is not a diagnosis. Advisory conclusions must come from Client Advisory.

## Required handoff record

- person/opportunity identity in the private/live system;
- current stage and outcome;
- owner and next action;
- due date;
- relevant consent/channel;
- reason code;
- advisory or operational dependency;
- evidence link inside the authorized private system.

General AI OS documents hold rules only, never the person-level handoff.
