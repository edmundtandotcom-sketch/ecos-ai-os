# Objection Routing
Version: v1.0
Status: APPROVED
Date: 2026-07-20

Classify before responding:
- information gap;
- trust/proof concern;
- timing/readiness;
- cost/value;
- authority/decision process;
- fit or risk;
- advisory question.

Respond by clarifying, answering with approved evidence, or routing to the correct owner. Advisory questions go to Client Advisory. Proof uses approved records/assets only. Do not overcome a genuine no-fit decision; record `No Fit` and close respectfully.

Store person-level notes only in the authorized live/private system.
