## Why this file exists

`01_OBJECTION_ROUTING_v1.0.md` defines **how to classify** an objection. It does not say **which objections actually occur, how often, or what has been proven to work on them.** That evidence exists — mined verbatim from the real consult corpus — but lived entirely outside Sales OS until now.

This file is the bridge. It carries no objection content of its own: it maps the field evidence to the routing taxonomy and points at the source. **Cite it, do not duplicate it.**

## Frequency reality — what to pre-empt first

From 376 logged objection rows across the corpus. Counts approximate (many rows carry two type tags) — use for prioritisation, not precision.

- **Trust concern** ~91 rows
- **Market timing** ~82 rows
- **Price** ~71 rows

**Trust + Timing + Price ≈ two-thirds of all objections.** Pre-empt these three before the client voices them. Spouse/alignment (~4%) is under-surfaced relative to its deal-killing power — it is the single biggest late-stage stall risk.

**The core insight:** behind most objections sits a **confidence gap, not an information gap.** Numbers and worked proof beat reframes alone. A client who already has all the knowledge and still cannot act needs external conviction and a forcing event, not more research.

## Category map — 16 field categories → 7 routing buckets

The bank organises objections **topically** (what the client said). `01_OBJECTION_ROUTING_v1.0.md` classifies them **functionally** (how to respond). This is the crosswalk.

| Bank category | Routes to |
|---|---|
| 1 — Price | cost/value |
| 2 — Market timing | timing/readiness |
| 3 — Monthly commitment | cost/value |
| 4 — Sell-high-buy-high | information gap · fit or risk |
| 5 — Spouse / alignment | authority/decision process |
| 6 — Emotional attachment | fit or risk |
| 7 — Renting concern | information gap |
| 8 — Trust concern | trust/proof concern |
| 9 — Need to think | timing/readiness |
| 10 — Need to compare | information gap · timing/readiness |
| 11 — Loan concern | cost/value · advisory question |
| 12 — CPF concern | advisory question |
| 13 — Exit risk | fit or risk |
| 14 — Opportunity cost / competing asset class | cost/value · advisory question |
| 15 — Other (recurring sub-types) | classify case-by-case |
| 16 — Pre-empted (advisor raised first) | not an objection — see below |

**Category 16 is a behaviour, not an objection.** It logs the advisor proactively raising and defusing the likely top objection *before* the client voices it — the single largest structural change in bank v1.1 (~65 primary instances). Treat it as a target behaviour to train toward, not an objection to route.

Categories routing to **advisory question** (11, 12, 14) hand off to Client Advisory per `01_OBJECTION_ROUTING_v1.0.md`. They are not closed in the sales motion.
