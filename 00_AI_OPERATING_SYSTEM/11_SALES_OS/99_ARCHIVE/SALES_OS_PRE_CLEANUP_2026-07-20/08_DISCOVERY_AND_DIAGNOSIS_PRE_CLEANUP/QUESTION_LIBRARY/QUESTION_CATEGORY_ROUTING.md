## Purpose
Maps the **8 mandate question categories** onto the existing (approved) question banks. This is the routing spine of the question library: for each category, where the questions already live. No question text is duplicated. `[T5]`

## The 8 categories → existing banks
| # | Category | Diagnostic job | Primary source (point) | Secondary |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Situation** | Current property/position facts | Trigger Q Bank §4 (Property Position Context); Diagnosis Call §3 Phase 3 | — |
| 2 | **Motivation** | Trigger + desired future ("why now", "what unlocks") | Trigger Q Bank §2 (Core Opening) + §3 (Ascent Stage); Diagnosis Call Phases 2, 4 | Belief-Shift §3 (Property Went Up) |
| 3 | **Problem** | The real decision / hidden gap | Belief-Shift §2 (Universal); Trigger Q Bank §5 (Belief Block) | Strategy Session §4 Step 5 |
| 4 | **Consequence** | Cost of inaction / delayed constraint | Belief-Shift §4–§5 (Comfort / Waiting); Trigger Q Bank §5 (Timing/Comfort rows) | Decision Psych RB-01, RB-03 evidence `[T3]` |
| 5 | **Financial** | Capacity, numbers, loan/CPF/cash | Diagnosis Call §3 Phase 3 + §7 (required info); Risk Assurance §5 | RAM v1.0 (deployment) |
| 6 | **Decision** | Who decides; alignment; decision style | Diagnosis Call §5 (Decision-Maker rule) + §3 Phase 6; Belief-Shift §13 (Spouse) | Trigger Q Bank §6 |
| 7 | **Suitability** | Advisory fit / Tier / archetype match | Diagnosis Call §6 (Tiering); Trigger Q Bank §6 (Readiness/Tier); Buyer Segment Library (branch 08 archetypes) | Readiness Stage Model v2.0 |
| 8 | **Commitment** | Next safe action + dated step | Trigger Q Bank §7 (Next-Action); Belief-Shift §15 (Closing the Loop); Risk Assurance §6–§7 | Decision Psych §3 step 9 `[T3]` |

## Notes
- Categories 3 and 4 are where objections originate — they hand off to branch 11 (RB-01..RB-18) when resistance surfaces.
- Category 5 (Financial) is the hard P2 gate: no numbers → no strategy.
- Category 7 (Suitability) is the tiering decision; a weak answer routes to nurture (branch 13), not a Strategy Session.

## Coverage gap `[T7]`
The banks are strong on situation/motivation/problem/financial/decision. **Consequence** questions are thinner as standalone items (mostly embedded in belief-shift sequences) and **suitability** relies on advisor judgement rather than an explicit scored checklist — candidate for a future scored suitability filter (propose to Edmund; not built here).
