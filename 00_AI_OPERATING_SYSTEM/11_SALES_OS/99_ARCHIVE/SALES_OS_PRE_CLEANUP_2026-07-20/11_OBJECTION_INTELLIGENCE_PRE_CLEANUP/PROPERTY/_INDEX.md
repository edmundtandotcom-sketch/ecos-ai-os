## Category
Product- and asset-level objections: new-launch-vs-resale, emotional attachment, renting/transition gap, exit risk / being stuck. `[T3]`

| Sub-type | RB block |

## Field rule
- New launch/resale: predict-then-reveal PSF comparison (client picks first, data corrects); reframe quantum as value not price; never dismiss an off-list project qualitatively only — build the same side-by-side table. `[T3]`
- Exit risk: exit-first framing with 3/5/8-year checkpoints; honest hedged en-bloc math; buyer-pool sizing. `[T3]`
- Attachment: separate "where you'll be happy living" (client's call) from "where your money grows" (numbers' call); respect legitimate emotional boundaries. `[T3]`

## When to stop pushing
Attachment to a first *home* is a legitimate emotional boundary — do not pitch rent-and-invest at a couple who explicitly want a home. `[T3]`
