## Category
"Too complicated / decoupling sounds risky / I don't understand" + "need more research / still studying". The danger is *misread understanding*, not real risk. `[T3]`

## Routes to
- Belief blocks: **RB-08** (Complexity Means Danger — Level 1 info gap), **RB-02** (Need More Certainty — Level 1.5).
- Sequences: Belief-Shift v2.0 §9 (Decoupling), §2 (Universal).

## Field rule
Patient re-explanation using principal-vs-interest / step-by-step roadmap; pause for an explicit "does this make sense now?" and send a written recap. A step-by-step roadmap converts perceived danger into sequence. For RB-02, add a forcing event + dated checkpoint (confidence gap, not information gap). `[T3]`

## When to stop pushing
Never move on before comprehension is confirmed — a numerically confused client will quietly disengage from a correct plan. `[T3]`
