# SPOUSE_AND_FAMILY — objection routing
Version: v0.1 · Status: DRAFT · Date: 2026-07-11 · Supersedes: none
Sources: Decision Psychology v2.1 §5 RB-07, RB-09; E: Master Objection Bank Cat 5 (Spouse/Alignment, ~13 rows)

## Category
"Spouse won't agree / need to discuss / parent-child not aligned / family not ready." Low frequency (~4%) but **the single biggest late-stage stall risk** — deals die at contract stage. `[T3]`

## Routes to
- Belief blocks: **RB-07** (Spouse/Family Not Aligned), **RB-09** (Child Should Decide Alone — theory-strong/field-light).
- Sequences: Belief-Shift v2.0 §13 (Spouse Not Aligned), §12 ("Family Legacy" — a family-situation conversation sequence used inside standard A1/A2 engagements; the Family Legacy door, A3/A4 levels, and SI-06 code are retired per Decisions 058/063/067).

## Field rule
Invite the **quieter/absent partner to frame the disagreement first, in their own words** (offer a joint Zoom/coffee brief). For deadlock, a values-level forced choice ("family staying together, or the money?"). **Never resolve disagreement through a proxy** — hear the absent partner directly. `[T3]`

## When to stop pushing
When the real blocker is an absent decision-maker: stop re-convincing the present partner; the next action is a joint session, not more argument (Diagnosis Call §5 decision-maker rule). `[T5]`
