## Gap definition
The blocker is downside fear — being wrong, being stuck, exit/liquidity, lifestyle threat (RB-01 Level 4, RB-15). Respect the fear; show survivability. `[T3]`

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Objective | Make the downside visible, bounded, and survivable — then let the client decide |
| Assets | RAM Safety-Net worked layers; exit-first framing (3/5/8-yr checkpoints); buyer-pool sizing; case study (Exit Before Entry) |
| Channel | Email (downside structure) → WhatsApp; call if fear is acute/trauma-linked |
| Timing | Within 72h; single considered touch, then space — do not pile on |
| Owner | Consult advisor |
| Exit condition | Client sees downside is survivable → re-book / NURTURE; or genuine no-fit → NO FIT |
