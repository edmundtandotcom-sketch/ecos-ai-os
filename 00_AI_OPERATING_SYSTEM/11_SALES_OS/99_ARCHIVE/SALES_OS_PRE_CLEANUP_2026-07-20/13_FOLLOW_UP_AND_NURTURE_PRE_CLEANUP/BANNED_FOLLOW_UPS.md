# BANNED_FOLLOW_UPS
Version: v0.1 · Status: DRAFT · Date: 2026-07-11 · Supersedes: none
Sources: Risk Assurance v2.0 §10 ("follow-up is not chasing"); Decision Psychology v2.1 (no-pressure doctrine); CRM SOP 03 §6

## The Rule
A follow-up must add **value or a decision**. If a message contains neither — it does not go out. Follow-up is staying relevant to the client's next trigger, never chasing. `[T5]`

## Banned openers / patterns (do not use)
- "Just checking in"
- "Following up on my last message"
- "Any update on this?"
- "Did you get a chance to think about it?"
- "Just wanted to touch base"
- "Circling back"
- "Bumping this to the top of your inbox"
- "Are you still interested?"
- Any artificial urgency / scarcity: "units are moving", "price going up soon", "last chance", countdowns
- Any takeaway-close pressure ("I'll have to give your slot away")
- Guilt or pressure framing ("I spent time preparing this…")

## The replacement
Every touch instead does ONE of:
1. Sends a **value asset** tied to the named gap (recap, framework, case, worked number).
2. Reframes the blocker with **the one number or case** that moves it.
3. Offers a **clean decision** ("review now, or park to [trigger date]?").
4. **Gracefully closes** with the door open and a named future trigger.

See `NO_RESPONSE\_INDEX.md` for the ladder that operationalises this.
