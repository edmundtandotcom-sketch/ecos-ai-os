# NO_RESPONSE — recovery ladder
Version: v0.1 · Status: DRAFT · Date: 2026-07-11 · Supersedes: none
Sources: CRM SOP 03 §6 (follow-up accountability); CRM SOP 04 (nurture/reactivation); BANNED_FOLLOW_UPS.md

## Purpose
A bounded recovery ladder for a follow-up that gets no response — so a lead is neither abandoned nor pestered. Each rung adds *value or a decision*, never a nag. `[T5]`

## The ladder (each rung dated; stop when it resolves)
| Rung | Timing | Move | What it must contain |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | +3–4 days | Value touch | A useful asset tied to their gap (not "checking in") |
| 2 | +7–10 days | Reframe touch | The one number or case that reframes their blocker |
| 3 | +2–3 weeks | Decision touch | A clean either/or: "shall we review, or park this to [trigger date]?" |
| 4 | +4–6 weeks | Graceful close | "I'll stop nudging — reach out when [trigger] happens; the door's open." → branch 14 REVIVAL |

## Rules
- Never send the banned openers (see BANNED_FOLLOW_UPS.md). `[T5]`
- Max ~4 touches before graceful close; then move to branch 14 (revival) with a next Ascent trigger, not repeated chasing. `[T5]`
- Every rung is logged (owner, date, outcome) — no lead sits in a vague state. `[T5, CRM SOP 03 §6]`

READS FROM: all gap folders. FEEDS INTO: branch 14 (REVIVAL / LOST).
