## Gap definition
Client left the consult understanding the direction but unclear on the decision or the mechanics (RB-02 need-more-certainty, RB-08 complexity). `[T3]`

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Objective | Convert confusion into a clear decision filter / written roadmap |
| Assets | Written recap (branch 13 CONSULTATION_RECAPS), decision framework, step-by-step roadmap; case study matched to their Step |
| Channel | Email (recap) → WhatsApp nudge; call if numeracy confusion |
| Timing | Recap within 24h; framework +3 days; review checkpoint +7–10 days (dated) |
| Owner | Consult advisor |
| Exit condition | Client confirms the decision filter is clear → re-book, OR converts to another gap |
