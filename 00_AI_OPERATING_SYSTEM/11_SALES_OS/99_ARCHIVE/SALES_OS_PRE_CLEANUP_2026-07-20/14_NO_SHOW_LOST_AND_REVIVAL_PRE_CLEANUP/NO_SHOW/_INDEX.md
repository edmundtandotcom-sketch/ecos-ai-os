Sources: CRM SOP 01 §6 (booking status: no-show / reschedule); CRM SOP 03 §6 (follow-up accountability); branch 13 NO_RESPONSE ladder

## Purpose
Handles booked consults the client did not attend. A no-show is a scheduling failure, not a lost lead — recover before writing off. `[T5]`

## Routing / play
| Step | Move |
|---|---|
| Same day | Warm reschedule touch (assume good faith): "we missed you — shall we find a better time?" |
| +2–3 days | Second reschedule offer with 2 concrete slots |
| No response | Enter branch 13 NO_RESPONSE recovery ladder |
| Ladder exhausted | Log booking status `no-show`; route to REVIVAL with next Ascent trigger |

## Rules
Zero guilt/pressure language (BANNED_FOLLOW_UPS.md). Distinguish a no-show (attend-able) from `went-cold-no-response` (LOST). `[T5]`
