## Purpose

Holds the sales-function's view of business strategy: how the Second Property Ladder™ positioning translates into sales targets, capacity planning, and advisor headcount/quota logic. Not a positioning master — a usage layer on top of one.

## When To Use

When a sales-side decision needs grounding in the governing positioning (e.g. "does this campaign's promise match what advisors can actually deliver in a consult") or when sales capacity/targets need to trace back to the business's 10-15 year direction.
