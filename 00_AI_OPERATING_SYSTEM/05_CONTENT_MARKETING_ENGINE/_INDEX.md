## Purpose

This branch is the reusable production system for turning approved positioning, client intelligence, research and proof into content.

It governs:
- reusable video, reel and VSL production rules;
- reusable trigger and hook logic;
- format-level quality standards;
- production registries that have not yet been converted into Markdown masters.

It does not store:
- active campaign scripts, ads, landing pages or creative production;
- finished media;
- campaign-specific strategy;
- buyer/advisory authority;
- performance dashboards.
