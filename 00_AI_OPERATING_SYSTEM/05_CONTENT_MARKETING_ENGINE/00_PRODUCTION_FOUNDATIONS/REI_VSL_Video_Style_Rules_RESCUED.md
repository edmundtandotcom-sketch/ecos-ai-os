Permanent video style rules from Edmund's SI-05 VSL v5 review (12 Jun 2026):

1. **Colour grade:** clean + bright — natural skin tones, lifted exposure, commercial polish. No heavy stylised LUTs.
2. **Punchline/overlay text:** NEVER cover Edmund's face. Position at bottom of frame, above the caption zone. Must look designed (backing shape, brand-blue #005CE6 + yellow accent, animation), not plain drawtext.
3. **Lower thirds & graphics:** require creative treatment — slide-in animation, layered reveal, branded panels. Flat boxes are not acceptable.
4. **B-roll:** mix of stock video + pipeline-rendered motion graphics (≈50/50 of B-roll time). Pure generic stock reads cheap.
5. **Transitions:** white flash + zoom punch mix — zoom punch on emphasis/punchline cuts.
6. **3-second rotation rule:** visual layer must change every ~3s in the cycle face-cam → motion graphic → B-roll, for the full video. Audio always continuous face-cam. Boundaries snap to word gaps. (Supersedes the earlier 21-cut / ~31% B-roll structure.)
7. **Approved and locked:** caption system (yellow karaoke, Impact 88px, ALL CAPS), music at 4%.

**Why:** v5 scored 5/10 on these exact points; Edmund expects each round of feedback to persist so rebuilds never regress.
**How to apply:** Treat as defaults for every REI video build (VSL, reels, all campaigns), not just v6. See [[rei-vsl-pipeline-recurring-bugs]].
