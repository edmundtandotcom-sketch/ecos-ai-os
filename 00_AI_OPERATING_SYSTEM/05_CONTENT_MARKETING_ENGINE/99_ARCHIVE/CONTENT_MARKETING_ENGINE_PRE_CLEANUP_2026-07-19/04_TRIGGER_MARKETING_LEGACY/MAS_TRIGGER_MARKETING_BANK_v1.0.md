## PURPOSE

This bank stores all approved trigger marketing messages — the content that causes the right prospect to feel seen, lean forward, and take action. Every message in this bank must:

1. **Speak to a belief block or life trigger** — not a product feature
2. **Open a campaign door** — Legacy Launch or Family Legacy
3. **Invite, not pressure** — MAS does not use fear tactics or false urgency
4. **Lead to a conversation** — the goal of every trigger message is one thing: get the right person to raise their hand

---

## TRIGGER MARKETING PRINCIPLES

### The MAS Content Hierarchy
```
Level 1 — Awareness: "That's about me"
Level 2 — Interest: "I hadn't thought of it that way"
Level 3 — Desire: "I want that life"
Level 4 — Trigger: "I should talk to someone about this"
```

Every piece of trigger content should move the prospect from Level 1 to Level 4.

### The BNTTS Framing Rule
Trigger marketing must plant the BNTTS seed without using the term "Buy Not To Stay." The prospect must begin to see property as a vehicle — not a home — through story and illustration, not instruction.

### Campaign Door Alignment
All trigger content is tagged to a campaign door:
- **[LL]** = Legacy Launch (SI-05 audience — condo owners, late 30s–50s, first or second property)
- **[FL]** = Family Legacy (SI-06 audience — parents with working-age children, idle capital holders)
- **[M]** = Mash-up (targets both simultaneously)
- **[U]** = Universal (can be used across both campaigns)
