## IDEAL PROSPECT PROFILE

### Primary Prospect — The Parent

**Demographics:**
- Age: 48–65
- Relationship status: Married with working-age children (child aged 23–32)
- Citizenship: Singapore Citizens (primary); PRs eligible
- Income: Combined $10,000–$20,000/month (may be lower if semi-retired)
- Retirement horizon: 5–15 years

**Financial Position:**
- Owns 1 property (own stay) — may be fully paid or low outstanding loan
- Idle capital: $150,000–$500,000+ in FD, savings, or low-yield instruments
- CPF OA: may have significant balance not allocated (especially if property is fully paid)
- Concern: Capital is not working hard enough; FD rates will drop; retirement income insufficient

**Mindset Triggers:**
- "We want to help our child but don't know how"
- "Our FD is about to mature — what do we do with it?"
- "We have one property and it's our only asset"
- "We don't want to leave our child with nothing when we're gone"
- "Our savings feel safe but they're not really growing"
- "Is it too late for us to build a portfolio?"

### Secondary Prospect — The Child (triggered separately or referred by parent)

**Demographics:**
- Age: 23–32
- Status: Working for 1–5 years; income $3,500–$7,000/month
- Property status: None (or HDB — looking to upgrade/invest)
- Thinking: "I want to build wealth but I don't know where to start"

## BELIEF BLOCKS (to be resolved)

**Parent:**
- "ABSD will make it too expensive" — Resolution: Child is likely a first-time buyer (0% ABSD if SC)
- "Our child isn't ready / stable enough" — Resolution: Co-borrowing structure supports their income; rental covers instalment
- "We might need the money" — Resolution: Property is more liquid than FD over 5-year horizon; clear exit plan
- "Property is too risky" — Resolution: RAM module applied; 5 Keys framework; historical data

**Child:**
- "I can't afford a condo on my income" — Resolution: Parent co-borrows; downpayment handled; instalment manageable
- "I should buy HDB first" — Resolution: BNTTS reframe; condo as investment vehicle first
- "What if I want to move?" — Resolution: Investment property does not need to be lived in; tenant pays loan

## CAMPAIGN OBJECTIVE

**Primary Goal:** Generate qualified appointments — parent-child pairs or parents with idle capital who are open to a Family Legacy planning session.

**Not the goal:** Immediate property commitment. Family Legacy prospects often need more conviction-building than Legacy Launch prospects — the advisory cycle is longer but the commitment is deeper.

**Success Metric per prospect:**
- Parent + Child both present in Conversation 1 (if co-purchase)
- Or Parent alone with idle capital mandate confirmed
- Discovery completed
- REI Method applied to parent AND child positions
- ProDeck delivered showing combined family Ascent trajectory

## CONTENT STRATEGY (30-DAY CYCLE)

### Week 1 — Awareness / Recognition
- Content Type: "Is your idle capital working?" hooks; parent-child legacy stories
- Goal: Make parent feel seen — they have something valuable and aren't using it fully
- Key Message: "Your savings are safe. But safe isn't the same as working."
- Target: Parent audience (Facebook primary)

### Week 2 — Education / Possibility
- Content Type: Explainer content on co-purchase mechanics, first-time buyer ABSD advantage
- Goal: Show that the structure is real and viable
- Key Message: "Here's how a parent and child can buy a property together — and why it works"
- Target: Parent + young adult audience (dual content track)

### Week 3 — Story / Hope
- Content Type: Anonymised family case studies; "what this family's portfolio looks like now"
- Goal: Make the outcome tangible and aspirational
- Key Message: "Their child is 28. Their portfolio is already at Level 3. Here's how."
- Target: Parent audience (Facebook) + child audience (Instagram)

### Week 4 — CTA / Action
- Content Type: Direct offer for Family Legacy Planning Session
- Goal: Convert warm audience into booked family advisory sessions
- Key Message: "Book a complimentary Family Legacy review — bring your child"
- Target: Both audiences; encourage parent to bring child to session

## QUALIFYING QUESTIONS (for DM / first contact)

When a prospect responds to Family Legacy content, qualify with:

**For parents:**
1. "Do you currently own a property — paid off or with an outstanding loan?"
2. "Do you have savings or CPF OA that isn't being actively invested?"
3. "Do you have a child who is currently working?"
4. "Have you ever discussed buying a property with or for your child?"

If YES to Q1 + Q2 or Q3 → book Family Legacy Planning Session.

**For children (young adult responding independently):**
1. "Are you currently renting or living with parents?"
2. "Have you looked at buying your own property?"
3. "Do your parents own a property?"
4. "Would they be open to having a conversation about helping you buy?"

If YES to Q1/Q2 + Q3 → invite child AND parent to Discovery session.

## CAMPAIGN RULES

1. **Both generations must eventually be present** — a parent-only session without child data cannot produce a complete Family Legacy strategy
2. **Lead with the parent's aspiration, not the child's need** — the trigger is the parent's desire to leave something, not the child's difficulty affording property
3. **Never pitch a specific property in marketing** — the campaign is about strategy and structure, not product
4. **Idle capital framing: compare to FD, not stocks** — the target audience is risk-aware, not risk-tolerant; anchor to safety narrative with upside
5. **BNTTS framing for the child must be introduced early** — the child needs to see the investment property as a vehicle, not their future home, from the very first conversation
6. **Do not rush to co-purchase recommendation** — some parents are better served by idle capital deployment alone; allow Discovery to determine the right structure
