## CAMPAIGN IDENTITY

**Campaign Name:** Mash-up
**Campaign Door:** Entry point for prospects who qualify for BOTH SI-05 and SI-06 — typically couples with a working-age child who can decouple AND co-purchase simultaneously
**Strategic Intents Served:** SI-05 (Legacy Launch) + SI-06 (Family Legacy) in sequence or parallel
**Ascent Model Target:** Household jump from Level 2 → Level 4 (Legacy) in one coordinated campaign cycle

---

## WHAT MAKES A MASH-UP CANDIDATE

A prospect enters the Mash-up campaign when ALL of the following are true:

1. **They are an SI-05 candidate** — own a condo jointly with spouse, equity exists, decouple is viable
2. **They are also an SI-06 candidate** — have a working-age child (23–32) who earns an income and has clean ABSD status
3. **The decouple releases capacity** — one spouse's cleared ABSD slate can be used for a parent-child co-purchase in the same cycle
4. **Child's ABSD position is first-property clean** — most effective when child is a Singapore Citizen buying for the first time (0% ABSD)
5. **Timeline can be coordinated** — the decouple and co-purchase can be executed within 6–12 months

**If only SI-05 or only SI-06 applies, route to the appropriate single-campaign strategy instead.**

## IDEAL PROSPECT PROFILE

### The Mash-up Family Unit
- **Age (parents):** 45–58
- **Relationship:** Married couple, joint condo ownership, working-age child
- **Income:** Combined household $15,000–$28,000/month (parents + child combined)
- **Property Position:** 1 joint condo (parents), no property (child)
- **Equity:** Significant — typically $300,000–$600,000+ unrealised
- **Child's ABSD status:** First-time SC buyer (0% ABSD) — this is the strategic advantage

### Why This Family is the Most Powerful MAS Client
The Mash-up family can achieve what no single-SI campaign can: **move from 1 property to 3 properties** — one own stay + two investment condos — in a single coordinated cycle, with one of those using the child's first-time buyer ABSD advantage.

This is the highest-leverage Advisory outcome in the MAS Client Edition.

## QUALIFYING THE MASH-UP DURING DISCOVERY

When Conversation 1 reveals a potential Mash-up candidate, the advisor checks:

**Mash-up Qualification Checklist:**
- [ ] Couple owns condo jointly — decouple is legally viable
- [ ] Child is working — income confirmed (minimum $3,000–$3,500/month)
- [ ] Child is SC and first-time buyer — 0% ABSD confirmed
- [ ] Decouple proceeds + available capital can cover two downpayments
- [ ] TDSR for child's loan is serviceable (with or without co-borrower)
- [ ] Legal/financial timing can accommodate both transactions within 6–12 months

If all boxes checked → upgrade to Mash-up. Build dual scenario. Extend Conversation 2 to cover both SIs.

If one box fails → remain on SI-05 or SI-06 as appropriate. Do not force Mash-up.

---

## ADVISORY DELIVERY RULES FOR MASH-UP

1. **Build both scenarios before Conversation 2** — presenting two coordinated transactions requires more preparation than a single SI session; do not improvise
2. **Present the combined Ascent trajectory** — show the family what Level 4 looks like for the household, not just for each individual
3. **Address child's BNTTS separately** — child may have different mindset barriers than parents; each generation needs their own belief alignment
4. **Sequence clarity is critical** — confusion about what happens first kills the Mash-up; the advisor must present a clear timeline with milestones
5. **Combined Reward Projection required** — show the household net portfolio value at Year 5 and Year 10; compare to Do Nothing at both the parent level and child level
6. **Flag the ABSD arithmetic** — explicitly show how the child's 0% ABSD eliminates what would have been a significant cost barrier
