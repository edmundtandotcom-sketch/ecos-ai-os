## PART 1 — HOW TO USE THIS RULEBOOK

### Two-Document System
This Rulebook and the G1 Production Brief serve different purposes:

| Document | Purpose | Who Uses It |
|----------|---------|-------------|
| **This Rulebook** | Strategic reference — all ingredients, rules, combinations, tracking | Campaign manager, strategist |
| **G1 Production Brief** | Filming guide — hooks + bodies + CTAs in order, teleprompter-ready | Edmund, videographer, editor |

**For filming:** Open the G1 Production Brief. Do not use this document on set.
**For campaign planning, tracking, or building new variations:** Use this Rulebook.

### The Four Dimensions
Every reel variation is defined by exactly 4 dimensions:
1. **G-CODE** — Who you are speaking to (behavioural profile)
2. **AWARENESS STAGE** — Where they are mentally (PA / SA / PA2)
3. **SCRIPT PATTERN** — How you structure the message
4. **ANGLE** — Which pain or belief you attack

### Hook + Body Independence Rule
- Hooks (0:00–0:05) and Bodies (0:05–2:00) are filmed and assembled **separately**.
- 1 body × 8 production hooks = 8 unique ad variations from one filming session.
- Never refilm a body to change a hook. Swap the hook clip at assembly stage.

### Asset Code Format
`LL_[STAGE]_[GCODE]_[CONCEPT]_[ANGLE]_[PATTERN]_[DURATION]_V[#]`

Example: `LL_PA_G1_IDLEMILLION_ANG001_PRIVTHOUGHT_120_V01`
With hook: `LL_PA_G1_IDLEMILLION_ANG001_PRIVTHOUGHT_120_V01_H3`

### Generation Steps
1. Select G-code (G1 / G2 / G3 / G4 / G5 / G8)
2. Select Awareness Stage (PA / SA / PA2)
3. Select Angle from Angle Library (Part 2D)
4. Select Script Pattern (Part 2C)
5. Confirm combination exists in Generation Matrix (Part 3)
6. Use body script or structure brief from Part 4
7. Assign asset code and log in tracking sheet
8. Film body → swap hooks from Hook Swappable Reel System v1.1

### Pack Lock Rule
G1 must achieve **20 qualified leads + 5 consultations + 1 creative winner** before G2 bodies are filmed.

### 2B — Awareness Stages

**PA — Problem Aware (Cold Traffic)**
Where they are: Know something is off. Haven't calculated opportunity cost.
Ad goal: Name the silent problem. Create recognition, not education.
Tone: Observational. Mirror internal thought. Do not sell.
CTA type: Soft — checklist, quiz, free guide. Do NOT push consultation.

**SA — Solution Aware (Warm Traffic)**
Where they are: Know property can be optimised. Thought about it. Hasn't committed to an approach.
Ad goal: Show REI Method as the right approach. Differentiate from "just sell and upgrade."
Tone: Logical + slightly urgent.
CTA type: Medium — free session, mini-consult, webinar.

**PA2 — Product Aware (Hot Traffic)**
Where they are: Know Coach Edmund / Singapore Real Estate Insider. Need a final push.
Ad goal: Social proof, authority, specificity. Remove last objection.
Tone: Confident. Direct. Results-first.
CTA type: Strong — book strategy session, limited slots, direct link.

### 2C — Script Patterns (Pack 1 uses 8 of 12)

| Code | Pattern | Best For | Stage |
|------|---------|---------|-------|
| PRIVTHOUGHT | PRIVATE_THOUGHT | G1, G3, G4, G8 | PA |
| DIRECTDIAG | DIRECT_DIAGNOSIS | G1, G2, G5 | PA |
| CONTRARIAN | CONTRARIAN_REFRAME | G1, G2, G3 | PA |
| CONSULT | CONSULT_PATTERN | G1, G4, G5, G8 | SA |
| BEFOREAFTER | BEFORE_AFTER_THINKING | G1, G2, G5 | SA |
| COMPOSITECASE | COMPOSITE_CASE | G1, G3, G4, G8 | PA2 |
| WARNING | WARNING_STORY | G3, G4, G5 | PA/PA2 |
| SPOUSECONV | SPOUSE_CONVERSATION | G8 | All |
| PERSONALPOV | PERSONAL_POV | G1, G2 | PA2 |

**PRIVATE_THOUGHT:** Open with internal monologue viewer recognises but has never said out loud. Mirror → Escalate → Reframe → Method → CTA. Direct to camera, slow pace, no B-roll in first 10s.

**DIRECT_DIAGNOSIS:** State problem as a diagnosis. No build-up. Hard truth in first sentence. Evidence → Why → Method → CTA. Strong eye contact, authoritative posture.

**CONTRARIAN_REFRAME:** "Most people think X. The truth is Y." Expose gap → Show real picture → Method → CTA. Can use whiteboard or graphic.

**CONSULT_PATTERN:** Open with real consultation scenario (anonymised). Discover → Plan → Change → Open loop for CTA. Storytelling format.

**BEFORE_AFTER_THINKING:** Describe thinking BEFORE vs AFTER a consult. Not before/after transaction — before/after clarity. "Most clients come in thinking… They leave knowing…"

**COMPOSITE_CASE:** Blend 2–3 anonymised real cases into one narrative. Range of situations, one common outcome. Name the composite characters.

**WARNING_STORY:** "This is what happened to a client who waited." Stakes → Lost → What to do instead → Method → CTA. Serious tone, no humour. On-screen text for numbers.

**SPOUSE_CONVERSATION:** Recreate real-sounding couple conversation. One reluctant, one ready. REI Method as neutral third party. One-person narration or two-person dialogue.

**PERSONAL_POV:** First-person advisor perspective. "Here's what I see every week in consults…" Pattern → Insight → Change → CTA. Casual, authentic.
