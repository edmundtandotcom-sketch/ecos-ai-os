### 1C — G-Code System Clarification 🟩 *(merged from rival v1.2 §1C — CRITICAL)*

**There are two separate G-code systems. Do not mix them.**

| System | Scope | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| MAS Client Intelligence Engine v3.1 | ALL MAS campaigns | Universal buyer profiles across the Ascent Model |
| Layer 3A Rulebook G-codes (this document) | Legacy Launch ONLY | Campaign-specific creative production profiles |

The G-codes below are built on the 6 LL Master Sub-Types (FCC, DINK, FHA, DFP, RM, HECO) — not imported from the MAS CI Engine. G4 and G5 in the MAS CI Engine mean different things than G4 and G5 in this Rulebook.

### The Four Dimensions
1. **G-CODE** — Who you are speaking to (behavioural profile)
2. **AWARENESS STAGE** — Where they are mentally (PA / SA / PA2)
3. **SCRIPT PATTERN** — How you structure the message
4. **ANGLE** — Which pain or belief you attack

### Hook + Body Independence Rule
Hooks (0:00–0:05) and Bodies (0:05–2:00) are filmed and assembled separately. 1 body × 8 production hooks = 8 unique ad variations from one filming session. Never refilm a body to change a hook.

### Asset Code Format
`LL_[STAGE]_[GCODE]_[CONCEPT]_[ANGLE]_[PATTERN]_[DURATION]_V[#]`
Example: `LL_PA_G1_IDLEMILLION_ANG001_PRIVTHOUGHT_120_V01` · With hook: `..._V01_H3`

### Generation Steps
1. Select G-code · 2. Select Awareness Stage · 3. Select Angle · 4. Select Script Pattern · 5. Confirm combination exists in Generation Matrix (Part 3) · 6. Use body script or structure brief (Part 4) · 7. Assign asset code, log in tracking sheet · 8. Film body → swap hooks from Hook Swappable Reel System v1.1.

### Pack Lock Rule
G1 must achieve **20 qualified leads + 5 consultations + 1 creative winner** before G2 (and any other pack) bodies are filmed.

### Scoring Signals
| Signal | Target |
|---|---|
| Hook Hold Rate (past 3s) | >40% |
| Body 50% Watch Rate | >20% |
| CTA Click Rate | >2% |
| Lead Quality (FCC-qualified) | Own private property, family, age 45–65 |
| Consultation Rate (of leads) | >25% |

# PART 7 — MARKETING LANGUAGE RULES 🟩 *(new — merged in full from rival v1.2 Part 7, LOCKED)*

These rules are drawn from the LL Client Intelligence Master v1.1. They apply to **ALL** Legacy Launch content without exception — no conflict with anything in the v1.1 base, adopted as-is.

### NEVER USE:
- "belief" / "belief block"
- "mindset"
- "false belief"
- "limiting belief"
- Any language implying the client is psychologically defective

### ALWAYS USE INSTEAD:
- "outdated strategy"
- "old calculation"
- "position not reviewed"
- "silent cost"
- "what worked before may not apply now"
- "the numbers have changed"

### Reason:
Legacy Launch clients are successful, high-income professionals. Belief-block language positions the advisor as a therapist and the client as broken. Strategy/calculation language positions the advisor as a professional peer reviewing a position — not fixing a problem.

G1's SCRIPT READY scripts do not use banned terms as written, so no rewrite is triggered — but any new copy written from this point forward must comply, and G2–G10 structure briefs should be checked against this list before full scripts are written.
