## Purpose

Market intelligence inside MAS is not property news.

It is not project launches, showflat visit reports, or agent gossip.

Market intelligence inside MAS answers one question: **Does the market currently reward the client's Strategic Intent?**

If yes — what is the evidence, and how does the advisor frame it?
If no — what does the advisor do with that conflict?

---

## Core Principle: Intelligence Serves the Advisory

Most property agents serve the market. They report what is happening and let the client interpret.

MAS advisors do the opposite. They interpret the market through the lens of the client's Strategic Intent and Ascent Level — then deliver a positioned intelligence brief.

The question is never "What is the market doing?"

The question is always: **"What is the market doing for THIS client, at THIS stage, pursuing THIS intent?"**

## Intelligence Categories

### Category 1: New Launch Pipeline Intelligence

**What it covers:** Upcoming new launches (6–18 months). Current releases. Sell-through rates.

**Key data points:**
- Project name, developer, district, tenure (freehold vs 99-yr leasehold)
- Price per square foot (PSF) quantum and unit mix
- Estimated TOP date
- Launch sell-through rate (% sold at launch weekend)
- Payment scheme: NPS (Normal Progressive) vs DPS (Deferred Payment)
- Comparison: PSF vs comparable launch in same district 3 years ago

**Source hierarchy:**
1. URA REALIS — official transaction data; most reliable
2. Developer price lists and e-saleboards
3. CEA-licensed data services
4. REI direct market access (Edmund's live pipeline knowledge)

**Advisory use for SI-05:**
- Compare new launch PSF to client's existing condo PSF
- Calculate whether decoupling now and acquiring new launch creates stronger 7-year portfolio vs. holding one condo
- Identify "buy the next rung" projects — launches in districts positioned for the next 5–7 year run

**Advisory use for SI-06:**
- Identify launches where adult child buys as first-timer at competitive PSF
- Find projects with DPS or low initial cash outlay to reduce strain on parent capital
- Prioritise projects near MRT or with strong rental yield to service child's loan

---

### Category 2: Resale Market Intelligence

**What it covers:** Secondary market transactions, price trends, district performance.

**Key data points:**
- URA non-landed private residential price index (quarterly release)
- District-level resale price trends (12-month vs 36-month)
- Median PSF for comparable resale units in client's submarket
- Days on market for comparable properties (demand signal)
- Transaction volume (market depth signal)

**Advisory use:**
- Benchmark client's existing property: Is it in a high-demand resale submarket?
- Determine exit viability: Can client sell at plan and redeploy capital?
- Identify if client's property has plateaued (case for exit now) vs. still running (case for hold)

---

### Category 3: Rental Market Intelligence

**What it covers:** Rental yield benchmarks, vacancy rates, demand-supply dynamics.

**Key data points:**
- Median monthly rental for comparable unit size in district
- Gross rental yield % (annual rent / purchase price)
- Vacancy rate in sub-market
- Expat demand trends (especially for Districts 9, 10, 11 and CCR)
- Supply pipeline: new units entering rental market in next 2 years

**Advisory use for BNTTS frame:**
- Show client rental income a new launch generates from TOP
- Calculate net cash flow: rent minus mortgage and maintenance
- Build the BNTTS case: "You are not living there — someone else is servicing your mortgage."

---

### Category 4: Macro Market Intelligence

**What it covers:** Interest rate environment, government policy, cooling measures, GDP signals.

**Key data points:**
- MAS monetary policy stance
- SORA rate and trend (Singapore Overnight Rate Average)
- Outstanding cooling measures: ABSD rates, TDSR cap (55%), LTV limits
- Government Land Sales (GLS) pipeline — supply signal
- Population growth and household formation (demand signal)
- URA Master Plan strategic areas: Jurong Lake District, Paya Lebar, Woodlands, North-South Corridor

**Advisory use:**
- Rate environment: higher SORA = higher holding cost; factor into Reward Calculator monthly commitments
- Policy: any ABSD changes directly affect SI-06 structuring math — verify structure is still valid at time of advising
- GLS signal: large awards in a submarket = more supply in 3–5 years; moderate projected price growth accordingly
- Master Plan alignment: clients targeting Master Plan growth areas often outperform over 7–10 year hold

---

### Category 5: Competitive Project Intelligence

**What it covers:** How target new launch projects compare against each other and resale alternatives.

**Key data points:**
- 3–5 comparable new launches in same or adjacent district
- Side-by-side: PSF, quantum, location score, developer track record
- Historical performance of same developer's past projects (% appreciation from launch to resale)
- Buyer composition for each project (upgrader, investor, foreign buyer mix)

**Advisory use:**
- Build the project selection argument: why THIS project is the right vehicle for THIS client's intent
- Pre-empt "show me other options" — have the comparison ready before the client asks
- Identify differentiated advantages: MRT proximity, top school catchment, leasehold vs freehold at same PSF

## Intelligence Delivery Format

Market intelligence inside MAS is always delivered in **advisory framing**, not raw data framing.

### Weak delivery (raw data — do not use):
"The URA price index for non-landed private residential increased 4.2% in Q1 2026."

### Strong delivery (advisory framing — use this):
"In the district where this project sits, comparable non-landed private units have increased 4.2% over the past 12 months. At your target quantum of $1.5M, that represents $63,000 in appreciation that already accrued — on a property you haven't bought yet. The entry window at current pricing is now."

The advisor's role is to translate data into implication — specifically the implication for the client's next move.

## Intelligence Conflict Resolution

If market intelligence conflicts with the client's intended Strategic Intent, do not suppress the data.

Present the conflict directly:

*"The data suggests [market condition]. For your intended pathway of [SI / pathway], this creates [implication]. Here is how MAS frames this and what the advisory recommendation is given this market context."*

MAS is an advisory model. The advisor's job is to help the client make the right move — not to close a transaction regardless of the market signal. If the market does not currently reward the client's intent, the advisor must say so.
