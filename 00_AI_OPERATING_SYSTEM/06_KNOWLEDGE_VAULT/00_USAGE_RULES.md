## Definition

Knowledge Vault preserves reusable intelligence and reference material.

**It improves. It does not govern.**

## Correct uses

- market context and historical comparisons;
- buyer language and psychology;
- de-identified transcript patterns;
- reusable research methods;
- external marketing/sales technique references;
- evidence pointers;
- dated weekly and topic libraries.

## Incorrect uses

Do not store active campaign production, final media, PII, named transcript extracts, CRM data, software runtime, obsolete frameworks or guru methods as authority.

## Promotion test

A Vault insight may be promoted only when it is repeatedly useful, broadly applicable, sourced, non-conflicting, de-identified and adopted by the correct owner. Promote the principle—not the whole source file by default.

## Freshness levels

- **Current-input candidate:** recent enough to inspect, still requires verification.
- **Historical reference:** useful for patterns, not current truth.
- **Superseded:** a newer reference exists; archive it.
- **Unverified:** source/date/claim quality is insufficient.

## Mandatory verification

Verify policy, ABSD/SSD/TDSR/LTV/CPF, interest rates, launch facts, prices, statistics, platform specifications, quotes, testimonials and outcome claims before external or client use.

## Privacy rule

Named transcript extracts, inventories, contacts, household financials and case records belong in restricted systems. The general Vault may retain de-identified patterns, approved language banks and source registries.

## Final rule

Reference material can sharpen a decision. It cannot silently become the decision.
