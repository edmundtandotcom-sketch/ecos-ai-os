## 1. THE CONSULT OPERATING SYSTEM (stage map)

Every arc runs the identical machine. Cadence compresses as conviction builds: weekly → daily → next-morning.

**STAGE 0 — TRIAGE CALL (15–20 min, pre-consult)**
Purpose: qualify the WHY, not the what. Filter within 10 minutes ("80% are info-seekers, 20% genuine — qualify to disqualify").
Master opener: *"Before I share with you [pricing], maybe you can share your current property situation and what plans do you have in mind that you need my help?"* — then pause.
Mechanics: never call leads back instantly (offer your slots); inception technique (what properties have you seen / which stands out / why never buy → extracts budget + urgency indirectly); Google form with BOTH parties' full financials; book the findings appointment 2–3 days out BEFORE the form is filled; group chat with both spouses. Hard rules: no numbers = no advice ("you don't expect the doctor to diagnose before the scan and MRI"); all owners present or reschedule.
**Gate:** form completed + both decision-makers booked.

**STAGE 1 — REVIEW (1st consult, 90 min)**
Purpose: convert vague comfort-seeking into a quantified money objective + establish advisor authority.
Sequence rule (taught explicitly): **PLAN first → PROPERTY second → MONEY last.**
Core ritual: retirement quantification (retire at what age / need how much per month / × years = gap — e.g., $5k×2×30yrs = $3.6M) — replaces "make as much as possible" with a gap only property leverage closes. Finance X-ray: max purchase reverse-engineered from funds, monthly-payment comfort anchor ("if you pay $4,160/month — difficult, or quite relaxing?"). Find the emotional open loop (Mindy: "I cannot sleep tonight. That is my money question.").
Frame discipline: "Let us study this together" (never "let me tell you"); "my role is not to tell you what to do"; hold your bullets (no decoupling mechanics / stretch-tenure / undervalued list revealed early); generalize property data — "I can't do detail without your full information" is the engineered hook to session 2. Pre-extract objections: *"What will be the number one thing you are concerned about, even though you want to do it?"*
Homework close: 3 questions (can you stretch? mentally ready to buy in 1–2 weeks? is the first ~10% liquid?) + specific next slot proposed.
**Gate:** money objective owned by client + budget band + monthly comfort accepted + homework assigned.

**STAGE 2 — EVALUATE (up to 3 hrs, often 2 parts)**
Purpose: install the market lens so the client can self-select the route. "Now that you are ready, let's get educated, so that the fear and concern will be addressed during this time."
Content: transformation doctrine, cooling-measure history ("Government Game"), supply-demand depletion math, cost-of-funding (resale vs new: $101k vs $20k interest), 4% stress test, A/B exercises where the client chooses ("90% choose wrong; we're the 10% selling to the 90%"). The Tuesday group webinar is a designed module slotted into the 1:1 journey — it handles rates/recession/inflation one-to-many.
Timing move: Part 2 deliberately lands on a money event (funds unlocked, FD matured, settlement).
**Gate:** 3 route decisions (monthly comfort $, new vs resale, one property or two) + explicit process consent — "Do you have any objections or do you want to follow this?" + (where used) exclusivity signed, funds liquid, WhatsApp standby group.

**STAGE 3 — IDENTIFY (proposal session, often night-before)**
Purpose: present 2–3 pre-filtered candidates, each vetted against the 4 filters: (1) transformation story, (2) new-vs-old price gap, (3) entry price never the area's most expensive, (4) exit to Singaporeans/PRs. Each option carries comps + tenant story + exit story. Client self-ranks the winner on paper. Options presented most-resistance-first → least-resistance-last so the client self-selects ("self-realization is more powerful than telling them").
Decision variable reduced to emotion at the end: *"Which one excites you? The first thought — that is the one."*
**Gate:** front-runner picked + all objections surfaced and numbered + budget ceiling set + showflat booked <24h ("Do you have any space tomorrow? Things are fresh").

**STAGE 4 — VIEW + CLOSE (showflat day = execution, not decision)**
The winner was decided the night before. Showflat: stack/floor micro-selection, payment table, Edmund as counter-salesman to the developer's agent. Scarcity is real and attributed to the market, never to him (unit lost Monday costing $30k more; developer repriced +2% overnight; parallel client seeing the same units tomorrow). Wobble → takeaway close: *"If after so many rounds you cannot understand your investment journey with us… I don't want to waste time"* — client re-sells themselves. Risk reversal at peak fear: *"Even you don't do it, I'll pull you out"* / *"If you cannot sell, I wouldn't sell it to you."*
**Gate:** cheque.

**STAGE 5 — LIFETIME LOOP**
*"Every 3–5 years we review. Even if you don't come to us, I will go to you — we are monitoring your property… exit, then we enter again."* Feeds the 4R engine (Reactivate, Reconnect, Retention, Referral) and the sell-side entry (several arcs BEGIN with an exclusive listing, then flow into purchase advisory).

**Velocity pattern (Mindy benchmark: 5 sessions/13 days):** every session ends with the next touchpoint booked <24–96h; research framed as perishable ("if I can spot this unit, others can spot it") so the CLIENT requests acceleration; speed pre-framed early ("Don't be shocked if it could be as quick as next week you buy property"; "we're not rushing the purchase, we're speeding up the decision-making process"). Taught cap: quickest close 2 weeks, hard cap ~1 month, minimum 3 rounds before selection.

## 2. MASTER QUESTION BANK (by stage, verbatim)

**Triage:** "Share your current property situation and what plans you have in mind that you need my help?" · "Who's helping you currently? Why call me if you have an agent?" · "What properties have you seen? Which stands out? Why did you never buy?"

**Review openers (every session):** "Any major changes we should be aware of first?" · "What have you discussed since the last session?" · "Share with me your thoughts, so I can understand more."

**Goal quantification:** "Why do you want to make money from the property? What do you want the money for?" · "Which age do you want to retire — totally no income? …a taboo topic — when do you think we will pass on? How much per month per person?" · "What's the end game — your ideal last-property lifestyle?"

**Financial boundary:** "What's the most comfortable amount you're willing to put in? This wipes out your cash and CPF — are you okay first?" · "If every month you pay $X — difficult, or quite relaxing?" · "If the interest rate goes up to 5%, can you still pay?" · "Is there stretch beyond $1.3M — or will it burst your buffer?"

**Belief-shift (pre-sold):** "Is the market up or down? If it's up, will you dare to buy? When COVID happened, would you dare?" · "Who are your second buyers? Who will buy from you?" · "Five years later, how much do you intend to sell for?" · "Buying lower price safer or higher price safer?" · "Why is there only one transaction a year in this development — what do you think is happening?" · "Five years later, if this property doesn't make money, are you okay with it?"

**Readiness / commitment:** "What is stopping you from moving forward, when you roughly knew you could?" · "Mentally, are you ready — within the next one to two weeks you buy?" · "If I tell you NO on a property you like, can you accept that?" (advisor-authority contract) · "Do you have any objections or do you want to follow this?"

**Trade-off forcing:** "What can you not accept — price, size, or location?" · "Which is more important, the size or the value? If forced to choose one?" · "What if the property with potential is OUT of your preferred area — will you consider?"

**Selection close:** "Which one is more exciting to you? That's likely the one we should go for." · "What is the earliest you can do?"

## 3. BELIEF-SHIFT MOVE LIBRARY (named, reusable)

1. **Retirement Gap Ritual** — quantify the gap ($720k–$3.6M) that only property leverage closes.
2. **Exit Ladder / First-Owner Pricing** — roleplay with clients' own names: first-hand buyer owns the price floor and has three exit points; resale buyer at $1,250psf "confirm stuck." "Everybody dies, I want to die last."
3. **Government Game** — cooling measures = engineered gradual growth; "you and me are commodities Singapore uses to make money — if they make money, we make money; just follow." Master Plan as "magic crystal ball."
4. **Transformation Doctrine** — "Growth is always equal to transformation" (Jurong vs Bishan; Sky Habitat counter-case).
5. **Supply-Demand Depletion** — 13–14k inventory vs 10–11k annual take-up; universal answer to rates/recession/waiting.
6. **Cost of Funding** — resale bias killed with interest math ($101k vs $20k over 3 yrs); rate fear applies to a $300 progressive trench, not $4,600.
7. **55% Loan Magic Number** — tenure 17→27 yrs, installment halved, tenant-pays-positive-cashflow; pledging "magic" conjuring $160–250k capacity.
8. **Land-Price Floor** — land cost → breakeven → future launch floor: "the next plot cannot sell below $3,000psf; you're the cheapest in the zone."
9. **Entry-Price Safety Net (Kiasu-Kiasi)** — "Can we project the profit? We cannot. Whoever tells you is bluffing… we reverse it: am I buying $100–200k cheaper than my competition?" Protection, not prediction.
10. **2018 Time Machine** — "your concerns today versus last time is the same"; they bought then, +$520k.
11. **Age Countdown** — "after 40 the bank sees you as a liability; loan drops $100k in 5 years."
12. **3 Property Cycles / Expiry** — "you cannot save to buy in cycle 2; you use property to accumulate"; "every property has an expiry date."
13. **Rent-Where-You-Live / Buy-Where-It-Grows** — "Buying is to make money, renting is to stay — split this thought process."
14. **Bankruptcy Origin Story** — dad lost everything 2009, family rented HDB 10 years: risk-guardian trust frame (also the webinar spine).

## 4. CLIENT PSYCHOLOGY MAP (their words)

**Fears:** "It's the biggest investment of our life. It's serious shit… this one is our life savings." · "If I enter now and the market goes down… I should have waited and paid 10, 20, 50 thousand less." · "My student's family losing one, two million is probably losing one, two dollars to them. But this is my hard-earned money."
**Doubts:** "Condos doesn't make sense, so let's move to resale, then resale doesn't make sense again… it makes the decision making harder and harder." (analysis paralysis) · "We can't have it all — something got to trade off." (settling instinct)
**Desires:** stability for family ("she feels she doesn't have her own room… that's why I'm in the hurry"); retirement security; not being left behind ("if we have kids… we're all literally booked within minutes").
**Greed hooks:** "I'm pumping 200K cash… if I can get 30% return using the 200K elsewhere." (opportunity-cost objection)
**Aha markers (testimonial-grade):** "Those are very sobering questions… it expands us." · "You have just given me the answer I wanted to hear." · "You can truly tell that I'm not investor — I'm so much emotions in it."

## 5. OBJECTION → HANDLING LIBRARY

1. **Fees/commission** → "My consultation is complementary — my business is repeat business over a journey. Today we don't even know if you're selling; why talk about commission?" (say "marketing fees," never "commission").
2. **"Just tell me if this project is good"** → "Unfair for me to share — I don't know your finances or plans. What if I recommend wrongly?" Insist → cut loose.
3. **"I've done my IPA, I know my numbers"** → "True — but you don't expect the doctor to diagnose before the scan and MRI" → back to the form.
4. **Size/location rigidity** → "You've seen everything and all cannot — if really cannot find, then how? Which is more important — size or value?"
5. **"We'll just hold / wait"** → empathize + matched case study: "a client with exactly your profile one month ago had the same concern; after my assessment they felt confident."
6. **Rate/recession fear** → supply-depletion math + 4% stress test + pessimistic underwriting (low-balled rent, preserved buffers) — safety closes, not upside.
7. **"Think about it" at the close** → takeaway ("you're still not ready… I shouldn't even run this session") + risk reversal ("even you don't do it, I'll pull you out").
8. **Opportunity cost ("30% elsewhere")** → leverage math: returns on total asset vs cash outlay; worst-case exit priced in dollars then insured ($430/mo top-up yr 1 → +$565 positive yr 2).

## 6. CLOSE TRIGGER STACK (what makes clients move)

1. **A personal number replaces fear** — the installment/buffer math lands ("40-month reserve — even on sabbatical you last 3.3 years").
2. **Undervaluation proof** — client feels they FOUND an unfair deal, not got sold one ("new integrated at resale price," "CCR at RCR price").
3. **Worst-case safety story** — exit-safety logic, never upside projection.
4. **Self-authorship** — boundary built from the client's own numbers; recommendation mirrors their own shortlist ("stars align"); "purely on numbers, which one?" → client argues Edmund's case.
5. **Micro-commitment stacking** — "does it make sense?" loops; homework; process consent; calendar.
6. **Scarcity with a mechanism** — always attributed to market/other buyers, never pressure from him; anchored to the client's own history (their 2018 hesitation, their missed queue).
7. **Takeaway at wobble** — withdrawal triggers pursuit.
8. **Risk reversal at peak fear** — personal guarantee framing.

Taught meta-principle: *"Closing a sale does not happen at the end. It happens from the start — the moment you shake this client's hand, your closing starts."* / *"80% of the battle is done before the appointment… the closing is automatic."*
