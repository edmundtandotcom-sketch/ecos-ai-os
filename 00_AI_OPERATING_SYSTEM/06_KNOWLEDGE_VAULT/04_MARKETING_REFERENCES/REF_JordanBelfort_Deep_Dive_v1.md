## 1. THE BUSINESS IN ONE PARAGRAPH
Jordan Belfort sells the **Straight Line Persuasion System** — a repeatable, structured sales methodology that compresses a sale onto a "straight line" from open to close, managing the prospect's certainty on three axes (the product, you, your company) through **tonality, rapport, and looping objection-handling**. Distribution is courses (Straight Line Persuasion / Fast Track), live trainings, and keynote/brand leverage from his infamous story. The transferable gold for MAS: **tonality** (he claims most of persuasion is *how* you say it) and the **certainty model** — directly usable in advisory conversations.

## 3. KEY MECHANICS
1. **The Straight Line** — keep every sale moving open→close; control the deviation.
2. **Three Tens (certainty model)** — raise the prospect's certainty on product, salesperson, company.
3. **Tonality** — the *how* carries the persuasion; a small set of influential tones, drilled.
4. **Looping** — structured objection-handling that raises certainty rather than arguing.
5. **Rapport + state management** — control your state and the prospect's.

## 5. REPOSITION INTO THE 3-DAY WORKSHOP
- Fold the **certainty model + tonality + looping** into the sales-skill track (with Lok + Hopkins) — but explicitly **re-framed as ethical advisory persuasion**, not pressure selling.
- Use the **straight-line** as the structure for the in-room advisory roleplays.
- Caveat in materials: methods adopted, the "Wolf" ethos and branding deliberately rejected.
