## 1. THE BUSINESS IN ONE PARAGRAPH
Tom Ferry is the **#1 real-estate coach** and the most relevant benchmark: he monetises a **recurring coaching membership** (not one-off courses) wrapped around an annual flagship event (Success Summit) that functions as the emotional + recruitment engine. Agents pay monthly for accountability + systems; the Summit is the ascension ritual and the place coaching is sold. The model is **routine over inspiration** — weekly coaching calls build the habit that retains the subscription. This is the model MAS is structurally most similar to and must out-position locally.

## 3. KEY MECHANICS
1. **Recurring coaching, not courses** — predictable revenue; the business is retention.
2. **Tiered access (Core → Elite → Team)** — clear status ladder; Summit ticket bundled at Elite to lock loyalty.
3. **Annual flagship event as the engine** — Summit creates belonging, testimonials, and the upsell room.
4. **Routine/accountability is the product** — weekly calls; agents pay for the discipline, not just info.
5. **8 Levels of Performance** — a named progression model so members always see the next rung.
