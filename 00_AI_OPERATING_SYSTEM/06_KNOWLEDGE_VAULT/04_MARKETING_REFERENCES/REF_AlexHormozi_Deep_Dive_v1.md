## 1. THE BUSINESS IN ONE PARAGRAPH
Alex Hormozi **gives away the entire curriculum for free** and monetises on the back end through equity (Acquisition.com takes stakes in businesses) and platform ownership (Skool). The free content ($100M Offers, $100M Leads, daily YouTube) is the top of funnel; the business is *not* courses — it's deal flow and ownership. The lesson for MAS: radical generosity in free content builds such trust and authority that the paid ascension becomes inevitable, and the real money can sit in a layer most coaches never build (equity / platform / recurring infrastructure).

## 3. KEY MECHANICS
1. **Give everything away** — the free content is better than most paid courses; trust compounds.
2. **Monetise the back end (equity/platform)** — not the education. Sells "from a buck to $1M+."
3. **Value-first offer design** ($100M Offers) — make an offer so good people feel stupid saying no.
4. **Volume of free content** — relentless output keeps top-of-funnel enormous.
5. **Own the infrastructure (Skool)** — collect rent on the category, not just teach it.
