## 1. THE CENTRAL FINDING

**We are already half-Taki — at the wrong layer.**

The Agent Edition has borrowed his *naming style* and, in one case, his *actual trade name* (`The Boardroom`), while missing the thing that makes his business work: **a single three-word spine that operates as curriculum, diagnostic, content taxonomy and sales logic simultaneously.**

Our `Authority · Advisory · Amplify` is currently a *pillar list* — three words that appear in positioning documents. His `Attract · Convert · Deliver` is a *spine* — the same three words are his blog categories, his 9-part curriculum, his self-diagnosis question, his workshop agenda, and the structure of every sales conversation. Same asset; ten times the work extracted from it.

**The rebuild is therefore not a rename. It is putting weight on the spine we already have, and removing the borrowed names we should never have taken.**

## 3. THE DELIVER-FIRST REORDER

His 9 Accelerators start with **Deliver**, not Attract. He fixes the business model before turning on traffic, because scaling a broken model breaks it faster.

Our current flagship builds: *positioning → conversation → machine*. The economics fix (Millionaire Math) sits inside Day 1 as a block.

**Proposed:** the Millionaire Math becomes the **entry gate** to the whole system — the first thing the Scorecard measures, the first thing the workshop fixes, the precondition for the three pillars. An agent who has not confronted their own model math cannot usefully be taught positioning.

This preserves the existing Dated Million → Re-Date bookends and strengthens them: the Dated Million *is* the gate.

## 5. THE REBUILD SEQUENCE

Ordered by leverage-per-effort. Each phase completes before the next opens (per CLAUDE.md §3.2).

**Phase 0 — Stop the bleeding (immediate, no decision needed)**
- Rename `The Boardroom` out of the locked public spine. It is his trade name and it is already published-facing.

**Phase 1 — Put weight on the spine (highest leverage)**
- Adopt the 3×3 from §2. Update `02_PROGRAM_GOVERNANCE_AND_OFFER_LADDER` and the Big Idea to express the ten assets as canvas + gate + 3×3 rather than a flat list.
- Make `Authority · Advisory · Amplify` load-bearing: three Scorecard scores, three workshop days, three content categories, one diagnostic question.

**Phase 2 — Complete the missing pieces**
- Build **The Gap Call** (9 steps, our language, our market) — fills the empty Advisory slot and replaces any generic discovery call.
- Build **The Four Pressures** page and wire `03_DEEP_BUYER_PROFILE` → hooks → ad copy through it.

**Phase 3 — Rebuild the event as four campaigns**
- Restructure the S$287 Preview into Sign Up / Show Up / Pay Up / Follow Up.
- The four-segment follow-up (buyers · no-shows · early bailouts · watched-didn't-buy) is currently absent and is the largest identified revenue leak.

**Phase 4 — Reorder the flagship**
- Millionaire Math to entry-gate position; one day per pillar; every block ships a template; add the post-event implementation call as part of the product.

**Phase 5 — Rebuild the content engine**
- Install The Weekly Seven as the sourcing method.
- Apply the Dangerous + Prolific selection rule.
- Resolve the founder-voice/AI tension per Decision D4 below.

**Phase 6 — Continuity**
- Apply The Three Windows to the renamed continuity offer, within existing proof guardrails.
