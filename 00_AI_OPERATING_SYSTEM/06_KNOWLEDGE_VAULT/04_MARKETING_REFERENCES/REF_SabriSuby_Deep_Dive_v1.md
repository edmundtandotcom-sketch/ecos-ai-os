## 1. THE BUSINESS IN ONE PARAGRAPH
Sabri Suby built **King Kong** (a performance agency) and uses a **free book — *Sell Like Crazy* — as the master lead magnet** that feeds both done-for-you agency services and a done-with-you training (Quantum Growth). His core doctrine: a **value-first "Halo Strategy"** (give a high-value free asset / advance before asking for the sale) and the conviction that "your offer is weak" — fix the offer first. The model: free book → application/webinar → high-ticket agency or 6-week program. Education and free value are the front; the agency margin and program are the monetisation.

## 3. KEY MECHANICS
1. **Free book as lead magnet** — *Sell Like Crazy* does the selling and pre-frames the methodology.
2. **Halo Strategy** — lead with a genuinely valuable advance (gift/insight) before any ask; reciprocity.
3. **"Your offer is weak"** — diagnoses the offer as the bottleneck; sells the fix.
4. **8-phase selling system** — a named, sequential framework (familiar, repeatable).
5. **Agency + education hybrid** — DFY and DWY share the same front-end funnel.
