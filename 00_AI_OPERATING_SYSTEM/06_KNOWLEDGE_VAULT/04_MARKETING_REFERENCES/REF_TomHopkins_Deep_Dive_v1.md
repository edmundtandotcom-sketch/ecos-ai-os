## 1. THE BUSINESS IN ONE PARAGRAPH
Tom Hopkins is a **sales-skills master** who came up in real estate and built a training empire on the **repeatable craft of selling** — scripts, closes, and word-for-word technique. The model is **seminars + an annual flagship boot camp (Boot Camp Sales Mastery, Scottsdale)** plus an enormous catalogue of books/materials (*How to Master the Art of Selling*, 1.7M+ copies). The lesson for MAS: sales is a **learnable, drillable skill** taught through memorised language and repetition — the opposite of "be authentic and wing it." Most RE agents have never been trained on actual closing language.

## 3. KEY MECHANICS
1. **Scripts + memorised language** — selling taught as exact words, drilled to mastery (not vague principles).
2. **Closing technique as craft** — named closes, objection responses, tonality.
3. **Annual immersive boot camp** — scarce (once a year, his town) = pilgrimage value.
4. **Roleplay + repetition** — skill installed through practice, not lecture.
5. **Real-estate origin** — every technique already proven in property sales.
