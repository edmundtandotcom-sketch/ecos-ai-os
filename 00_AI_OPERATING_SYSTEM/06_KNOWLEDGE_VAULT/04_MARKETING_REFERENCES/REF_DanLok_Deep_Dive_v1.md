## 1. THE BUSINESS IN ONE PARAGRAPH
Dan Lok built his brand on one core thesis: **high-ticket sales is a skill, and that skill is learnable**. His flagship High Ticket Closer (HTC) certification taught salespeople to close $3,000–$100,000+ offers using a question-based conversation methodology — not pitching, not pressure, but creating conditions where the prospect sells themselves. His "Wealth Triangle" (High Income Skill → Scalable Business → High Return Investments) is the framework he teaches as the path to financial independence. The model: content + books (FU Money) → HTC certification (~$2,495) → Dragon 100 mastermind (high-ticket apex). The transferable gold for MAS: his **premium positioning philosophy** (price signals expertise), his **question-based closing methodology** (advisory consultations, not presentations), and the **client-selection discipline** (not everyone deserves your time — filter hard, serve well).

## 3. KEY MECHANICS

1. **High-ticket positioning** — the price IS the message. A $50 coaching session signals $50 thinking. A $3,000 advisory fee signals expertise. Price signals the calibre of the advisor, not just the cost of the service.
2. **Closing with questions** — "Lok's method" is to ask questions that surface the prospect's own pain and desired future state, then be silent. The prospect closes themselves. The advisor's job is to listen, not sell.
3. **The Wealth Triangle** — a simple, memorable three-part progression that explains where financial freedom comes from (skill → business → investment), not a single tactic.
4. **Client selection / repel to attract** — actively disqualify unsuitable clients. The harder you make it to work with you, the more desirable you become to ideal clients. Scarcity creates gravity.
5. **The skill-income link** — one rare, well-compensated skill unlocks income. For agents: the advisory conversation IS the skill. Everything else is leverage.
6. **FU Money philosophy** — financial independence as the real goal, not just commission. Reframes why agents should care about building a business, not just closing deals.
