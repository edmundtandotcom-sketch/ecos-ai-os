## Purpose

The standing reference model of **Taki Moore's business** — how he structures, teaches, sells, delivers and communicates.

Held for two uses:
1. **Agent Edition** — raw material for what we teach agents, to be built onto existing Agent Edition work (Edmund furnishing updated material).
2. **Delivery craft** — his teaching and explaining method applied to client consults, videos and demonstrations, to make complex things easy for anyone to understand.
