## 2. THE HIGHEST-LEVERAGE FIVE

If only a handful ever get used:

1. **`Why · What · How · Now`** — costs nothing, applies to every consult segment, video, demo, slide and objection answer we will ever produce.
2. **The Signature System (Problem + Promise + Process)** — turns any expertise into something sellable and teachable. Works for the Agent Edition curriculum *and* for explaining our own advisory method to a client.
3. **Modular months** — removes the enrolment constraint from any program we run.
4. **The Four Campaigns** — our events almost certainly leak at Show Up and Follow Up, not at the presentation.
5. **Draw it, count it, one pill at a time** — the three teaching moves that make a consult land.

## 3. WHAT WE DO NOT TAKE

- **His trade names.** Full list in `01_THE_BUSINESS.md` §10. Structure travels; names don't.
  ⚠️ **`The Boardroom` is currently live in the Agent Edition locked public spine.** That is his mastermind's name.
- **His visual identity.** Navy/white, plain, headshot-led. It's the part of his brand doing the least work — his brand is carried by the spine, the framework library and the voice, not the palette. Copying the skin gets the risk without the benefit.
- **His numbers as benchmarks.** Every figure is self-reported marketing. Ours must be independently real.
- **His warm-audience conversion assumptions.** Brandwidth and sell-by-chat presume a decade of accumulated content. Cold traffic is a different problem.
- **His lifestyle framing, unmodified.** "Money · meaning · freedom" and "fun tickets" convert coaches. On Singapore property agents the same language reads as get-rich-quick and collides with our proof guardrails. The transferable version is **ownership and control**.

## 4. OPEN QUESTION WORTH RESOLVING DELIBERATELY

**Founder voice vs. AI-assisted content.** His most emphatic warning — outsourcing voice produces disengagement then a 30–60% ad-spend increase with no revenue lift — sits directly against a heavily automated content engine.

His position is not "don't use tools". It's that **opinion, story and point of view are load-bearing**. A workable split: AI does research, structure, editing and repurposing; Edmund's opinion, story and point of view are never generated.
