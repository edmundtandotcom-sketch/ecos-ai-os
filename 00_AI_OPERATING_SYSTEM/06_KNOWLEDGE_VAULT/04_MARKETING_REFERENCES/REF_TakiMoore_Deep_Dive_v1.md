## 1. THE BUSINESS IN ONE PARAGRAPH

Taki Moore sells **the business model a coach should be running**, not coaching skills. His market is coaches who are already earning — stuck at six figures, trading time for money, delivering 1:1 and bespoke. He calls the fix **Coaching Business 2.0**: a business built on *systems and IP* rather than *time and effort*, producing **money, meaning and freedom**. The entire company is organised around one three-word spine — **Attract · Convert · Deliver** — which is simultaneously his curriculum, his blog taxonomy, his diagnostic, and his sales logic. Revenue comes from a recurring group program (`Black Belt`, six-to-seven figures) and a higher mastermind (`Boardroom`, seven-to-eight figures), fed by a monthly workshop that functions as both a profit centre and the enrolment room. He is, structurally, a *framework factory*: his durable moat is a library of named, drawable, one-page models that make him the person who gave you the language you now think in.

**Self-reported scale** (his marketing claims — treat as directional, not verified): 600+ coaches trained past seven figures; $50M+ combined client coaching revenue; ~12 hours/month of founder content producing ~$1.2M/yr of attributable revenue; a recent workshop cycle at ~$80K ticket sales plus ~$112K/month recurring added; ad spend cut from ~$60K/month to ~$3–4K/month when founder voice replaced produced content.

## 3. KEY MECHANICS

1. **One three-word spine, used everywhere.** `Attract · Convert · Deliver`. It is the curriculum, the blog categories, the diagnostic ("which of the three is broken?"), and the sales logic. Because a prospect can hold three words, they can self-diagnose — and self-diagnosis is the sale.

2. **The 9 Accelerators** — three per pillar, so the spine has depth without losing memorability:
   - **DELIVER** — *Decide Your Model* (escape 1:1) · *Unpack Your IP* (lead groups proactively from your own content) · *Harness Talent* (virtual team)
   - **ATTRACT** — *Fill Your Funnel* (webinar/case-study/appointment) · *Build Authority* ("be a big deal in your space") · *Install Automation*
   - **CONVERT** — *Audition Clients* (assess fit, don't impress) · *Sell with Webinars* (one-to-many) · *Rock Events* (live, 30–40% conversion target)
   > Note the ordering: **Deliver is first.** He fixes the model before turning on traffic — deliberately, because scaling a broken 1:1 model just breaks it faster. This is the single most transferable sequencing decision in his whole business.

3. **Three plateaus as the diagnostic ladder:** *Survival → Stability → Success*. Names the prospect's current rung so the gap is felt before the offer appears.

4. **Done-With-You is the delivery doctrine.** Done-for-you burns you out; DIY produces poor results and no proof. Done-with-you = *teach the model + hand over the template*. "It's not 'I do it' or 'you do it', it's let's do it together." This is what makes a group program deliver 1:1-grade outcomes.

5. **Client selection as a growth lever, not a nicety.** Only accept perfect-fit clients; respectfully turn the rest away. Misfit clients consume the founder capacity that growth requires. Price is the "moat" — high price funds ads, team and margin, which is what actually protects the business.

6. **The `Triage Call` — qualification inverted into positioning.** A 9-step, ~10-minute call whose explicit purpose is *deciding fit*, not selling. The power dynamic is engineered: "you're in the audition seat." Steps: (1) take control of the first minute (2) why now / why me (3) 60-second business scan (4) **widen the gap** — quantify current vs desired in dollars (5) what's missing or broken (6) what do you need from me (7) when — later thing or sooner thing (8) problem check-in — reflect back 2–3 problems until they say "yes, that's me" (9) show them the door — either a genuine no-fit referral, or book the 1-hour strategy call. Selling a *next conversation*, never the program.

7. **The Four Forces — the message engine.** A page split into quadrants on two axes (Away↔Toward × Immediate↔Future): **Frustrations** (away/now) · **Fears** (away/future) · **Wants** (toward/now) · **Aspirations** (toward/future). Every hook, ad, email and webinar title is pulled from one quadrant. This is his research-to-copy pipeline in one drawable square.

8. **The 3 Timeframes — the commitment de-risker.** *Year* = the commitment (12-month goals imply a 12-month engagement). *Quarter* = the ROI (first 90 days repay the year). *Month* = the risk reversal (30-day walk-away). Large commitment made safe by shrinking the window the buyer is actually risking.

9. **Webinar as a four-campaign system, not an event:** **Sign Up** (Four Forces targeting; 4 emails — Problem, Promise, Proof, Reminder) → **Show Up** (countdown, curiosity handout with open loops, teaser video, SMS) → **Pay Up** (content that *teaches and sells simultaneously*; belief-shifting about you / the product / themselves) → **Follow Up** (four segments: buyers, no-shows, early bailouts, watched-didn't-buy, each with its own sequence).

10. **The framework factory.** Everything is named, trademarked, and drawable in under 60 seconds — *The 6 Magic Pills™*, *The Gameplan™*, *The 3 Timeframes™*. Naming converts advice into an asset: it becomes ownable, teachable by others, referenceable by clients, and impossible to un-attribute.

11. **Founder voice is non-delegable.** He explicitly warns: outsourcing voice to a team or AI produces temporary relief → audience disengagement → sales decline → 30–60% ad-spend increase with no revenue lift. His counter-formula is **"Dangerous + Prolific"** — strong opinions on topics that give the founder energy, published consistently. Sourced by a weekly **7-category content mine**: calendar · clients · DMs · community · creations · consumption · circumstances.

12. **Lifestyle as the product's proof.** *Money · meaning · freedom.* "As your business gets bigger, your life should get better, not worse." Money reframed as **"fun tickets"** redeemable for experiences. The **swimming pool campaign** — design a specific offer to fund a specific personal goal (he ran 12-person $6–8K workshops at his home to buy a Land Rover). Calendar design: book the adventure and the revenue events *first* ("big rocks"), fit operations around them; target four days a week, four hours a day. He is his own case study, which is why the promise is believed.

## 5. THE WORKSHOP ARCHITECTURE (what `Clients 3.0` teaches us)

Three days, one per pillar, with a strict rhythm: **short teach → immediate build → next teach**. Attendees leave with assets, not notes. There is a post-event implementation call to convert momentum into completion.

| Day | Focus | Deliverable |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Offer design | A designed, priced, high-impact offer |
| 2 | Lead generation | Content system that produces leads from zero audience |
| 3 | Conversion | Call frameworks, scripts, pricing conversation, closing without pressure |

**The transferable design rules:**
- One day per pillar — the spine *is* the agenda, so the structure teaches itself.
- Teaching block length is capped by the build that follows it.
- Every block ends with a completed artefact, not an insight.
- A follow-up implementation call is part of the product, not aftercare.
- The event is priced to profit *and* to enrol — it does not have to choose.

**Where our flagship already beats his:** the Millionaire Map (a single physical artefact carrying all 3 days), the Builder's Scoreboard game layer, the Dated Million → Re-Date bookends. These are genuinely stronger than a standard teach-implement workshop. Keep them.

## 6. HOW HE TEACHES (the method under the method)

1. **Draw, don't slide.** Hand-drawn frameworks, live, "scribbling as we speak." The drawing *is* the teaching — watching a model get built is retained; a finished slide is not.
2. **Framework-first, story-second.** Model goes up, then a story hangs on it. Never a story hunting for a point.
3. **Number the parts.** "Nine steps." "Four forces." "Three timeframes." A counted framework promises completion and tells the listener when they can stop worrying they've missed something.
4. **Role-play the script live.** He performs both sides of the call rather than describing it. Language patterns are demonstrated, never summarised.
5. **Teach and sell in the same breath.** Every teaching element must simultaneously advance the sale, deliver value, hold engagement, and prove capability. No "now let me tell you about my program" pivot.
6. **Belief architecture before offer.** Three belief targets — about *you*, about *the product*, about *themselves* — plus pre-handled objections and pre-killed alternatives.
7. **Give the actual tool.** The free lead magnet is the real script he uses, not a teaser of it. Reciprocity plus proof of depth.

## 7. HOW HE COMMUNICATES (voice specification)

**Sentence rhythm:** short declaratives dominate. Fragments allowed. A long explanatory sentence is followed by a three-word one. *"It's super easy."*

**Transitions:** `So…` opens sections. `Here's the thing.` `Notice how…` `The reality is…`

**Structure:** numbered lists for steps · bold for key phrases and script lines · italics for dialogue speakers · heavy white space around role-play blocks · sub-heads with em-dashes.

**Rhetoric:** self-reflective questions ("Who's the one with the power?") · metaphor-forward ("you're the one with the ticket to Hollywood") · repetition for reinforcement ("Don't beg, don't plead") · direct commands ("Lead with 'no' before 'yes'").

**Tone:** authoritative without arrogance · tactical over theoretical · warm open, warm sign-off · transparent about his own motives, which reads as honesty and disarms scepticism.

**Openings:** context (where he is, who he's with) → what this tool does for you → build the framework.
**Closings:** social proof by named client → 2 conversion paths → personal sign-off.

## 8. HIS CONTENT & VIDEO FORMAT

- **Cadence:** ~5 emails/week, ~30 min each — roughly 12 founder-hours/month total.
- **Sourcing:** the 7-category weekly mine (calendar, clients, DMs, community, creations, consumption, circumstances) → pick the 5 with real energy.
- **Selection rule:** "Dangerous + Prolific" — a strong opinion he *wants* to argue, published consistently. Energy is the filter, not keyword volume.
- **Format:** one framework per piece. Name it, draw it, walk the parts, role-play the application, hand over the tool, point to the next step.
- **The two forces to balance:** the **Artist** (expression, originality, authenticity — irreplaceable, drives connection) and the **Business** (consistency, volume, schedule — drives reach). Neither alone works.
- **The stated failure mode:** outsource voice → relief → disengagement → sales drop → ad spend up 30–60% with no lift.

## 10. THE HONEST TENSIONS (where copying him would hurt us)

1. **Founder voice vs. the AI content engine.** His most emphatic warning is precisely what our content engine automates. Unresolved, this is a real strategic collision — not a detail. Recommendation: AI does research, structure, editing and repurposing; Edmund's opinion, story and point of view are never generated. Flagged for ruling.

2. **Coaches vs. property agents.** Coaches buy freedom and identity. SG agents are licensed, competitive, income-anxious and compliance-bound. Freedom-and-lifestyle framing that converts coaches reads as get-rich-quick to agents — and collides with our proof guardrails. Translate to **ownership and control**.

3. **His model assumes a warm audience.** Book, podcast, years of email. We are running cold-traffic acquisition. His conversion numbers are not transferable to cold; our existing Cold Market Conversion Model still governs.

4. **His proof is self-reported.** Every number in §1 is a marketing claim from his own funnel. We hold ourselves to a stricter evidence bar (Edmund's before/after GCI proof is *still pending*). Do not import his numbers as benchmarks, and do not let his claim-style seed ours.

5. **We are already borrowing at the name level.** `The Boardroom` in our locked spine is his mastermind's name. Several Decision 046 asset names track his curriculum's shape. Borrowing the *name* while missing the *system* is the worst of both — legal exposure with no operating benefit. Fix the direction: take the system, drop the names.
