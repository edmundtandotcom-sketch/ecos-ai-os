## Rule

The villain is the model, dependency or false solution—not the agent, agency, vendor or technology.

Every villain must land on the Market Maker mechanism:

**Authority · Advisory · Amplify.**

## Approved villain families

### 1. Market Taker dependency

Waiting for the agency, market, portal, project or referral to provide demand.

### 2. Transaction restart

Every month and every deal begins from zero because no relationship or pipeline compounds.

### 3. Rented demand

Followers, portal leads and paid reach are treated as owned business assets.

### 4. WhatsApp/memory operating system

Communication tools carry pipeline, follow-up and business-visibility jobs they were not designed to perform.

### 5. Prompt-collector trap

AI creates faster activity without improving positioning, decisions or revenue architecture.

### 6. Motivation-without-installation loop

Training produces energy and notes but leaves no system running.

### 7. Generic-agent invisibility

Experience exists, but the market cannot explain why this agent should be chosen.

### 8. Marketing-on-weak-positioning

More spend amplifies an unclear offer, audience and market position.

### 9. Referral illusion

A good reputation produces referrals, but the agent has no deliberate relationship and referral system.

### 10. Copy-the-output trap

The agent copies visible posts, scripts and tactics without installing the architecture that produced them.

## Copy method

1. Name the recognisable symptom.
2. Remove shame.
3. Reveal the structural cause.
4. Show the cost.
5. Introduce the Market Maker mechanism.
6. Offer the Scorecard or relevant next step.

## Safety

- Do not invent first-person Edmund stories.
- Do not invent lost-lead counts, income gains or agent outcomes.
- Do not attack PropNex, another agency, vendor or platform.
- Do not use fake urgency.
- Do not imply AI or systems guarantee income.
- Verify every market statistic before publication.

## Final rule

The villain creates recognition. The method creates agency and a credible next step.
