## Purpose

The reusable narrative spine for Market Maker ads, pages, videos, previews, sales assets and nurture. Platform-specific execution belongs in the active campaign.

## The seven-part spine

### 1. Identity mirror

Name the agent’s real operating state without shaming them:

- hardworking but income remains volatile;
- experienced but plateaued;
- busy, reactive and dependent on memory;
- using more tools without building a business.

### 2. Problem ownership

The problem is not laziness or lack of talent. The effort sits on weak architecture.

Core reframe:

**You upgraded the agent. You did not build the business underneath the agent.**

### 3. Enemy

Attack the model, not the person:

- transaction-by-transaction hustle;
- rented demand and platform dependency;
- random referrals mistaken for a system;
- WhatsApp/memory as operating infrastructure;
- prompt collecting without business design;
- training that inspires but does not install.

### 4. Promise

A Market Maker owns:

- a recognised position;
- a repeatable advisory conversation;
- an attract-and-capture system;
- visible pipeline and follow-up;
- a 90-day operating rhythm.

Do not attach unverified income promises to this transformation.

### 5. Mechanism

**Authority · Advisory · Amplify**

Authority makes the right market notice.  
Advisory makes the right clients trust.  
Amplify makes the business repeat and compound.

AI accelerates each layer after the agent understands the underlying decision.

### 6. Proof

Use only:

- verified Edmund/Cindior operating evidence;
- real funnel/process evidence with context;
- approved participant or client results with consent;
- live demonstrations of systems currently running.

Use `[PROOF NEEDED]` when evidence is missing. Never create a testimonial pattern and present it as a real case.

### 7. Offer

Move through one coherent ladder:

Market Maker Scorecard → Market Maker Live — Preview → Market Maker Live → Circle/Boardroom.

The deeper Agent Business & Growth Profiler supports diagnosis after opt-in or inside preview preparation. It does not create a second competing funnel identity.

## Audience application

### Grinding/volatile agent

Lead with business-system instability, invisible leakage and month-to-month restarting.

### Plateaued/experienced agent

Lead with ceiling, positioning weakness, non-compounding referrals and the cost of being experienced but indistinguishable.

## Voice

- direct;
- practitioner-led;
- specific;
- non-hype;
- hopeful;
- Singapore-property specific;
- hard truth without humiliation.

## Final rule

Agreement precedes action. Make the agent feel accurately understood before presenting the Market Maker Method.
