## THE POSITION IN ONE PARAGRAPH

Most Singapore property agents already know how to sell — that's not the gap. The gap is a business underneath the licence: systems, numbers, predictability. **Agent Edition** exists for established agents (S$100K+ personal GCI) who are proven **Producers** and want to become **Advisors**, then **Business Owners**, then **Multipliers** — where their systems, media, AI and people produce beyond their own personal capacity. We do not teach salesmanship. We install the business architecture behind production that's already working.

**Core belief:** Income doesn't multiply because you generate more activity. It multiplies when more of what's already entering the business turns into productive outcomes.

## WHY "DIAGNOSE" IS NOT A CORE FOUNDATION MULTIPLIER (2026-08-13 refinement)

An earlier pass of this reconciliation used "Diagnose" (the consultation architecture, "Diagnose Before You Prescribe") as CONVERT Multiplier #5. Edmund's follow-up refinement corrected this: **teaching the consultation/presentation methodology itself belongs to sales training, not business architecture** — it risks becoming a rabbit hole (questions, framing, objections, full REI methodology), is slow to show installed results, and duplicates what agents already have their own working approach for. Agent Edition's value is explicitly **before the meeting and after the meeting, and the infrastructure connecting them** — not replacing whatever consultation method already works for the agent.

**Multiplier #5 is now Lead-to-Appointment™** (see naming table above) — a repeatable system that moves an enquiry into a booked, qualified appointment. Highly tangible, fast to install, heavy GHL/AI leverage.

**"Diagnose Before You Prescribe" is not deleted — it's relocated.** It becomes an optional Year 2 Acceleration offering (working name: **Advisory Conversation Lab**), alongside "Build Your Method" and REI Practitioner, per the IP protection structure below.

## THE IP PROTECTION STRUCTURE

Three layers, so the real REI Method is never given away on Day One:

- **Layer 1 — Agent Edition Advisory Blueprint:** whatever consultation method the agent already has stays theirs; Foundation does not touch it. Available to everyone, Year 1.
- **Layer 2 — Build Your Method / Advisory Conversation Lab (Year 2):** members develop their own frameworks from their own experience; "Diagnose Before You Prescribe" lives here as an optional deeper offering, not Core Foundation.
- **Layer 3 — REI Practitioner:** reserved for agents who join the PropNex practice — makes the deeper REI Method an ascension advantage, not a Day-One giveaway.
