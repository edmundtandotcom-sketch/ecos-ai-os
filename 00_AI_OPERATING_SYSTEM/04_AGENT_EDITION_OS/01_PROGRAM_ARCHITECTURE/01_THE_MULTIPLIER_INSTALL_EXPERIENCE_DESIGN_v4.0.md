## PART 1 — WHAT THIS EVENT IS AND ISN'T

**The Multiplier Install is a 3-day live event where established agents diagnose their business against the Nine Agent Multipliers, choose two to work on, and leave with one already live.** It is not a 3-day seminar, not a technical GHL-building workshop, and not an attempt to install all nine multipliers on everyone.

**The governing mechanism: 3-2-1.**
1. **3** — diagnose the three pillars (Attract / Convert / Deliver)
2. **2** — choose the two highest-impact multipliers, self-scored red/amber/green
3. **1** — get one of them live before leaving

Two attendees in the same room can legitimately leave working on entirely different multipliers. The programme personalises by what's actually broken, not by forcing everyone through nine modules.

## PART 2 — WHY THIS REPLACES THE OLD MILLIONAIRE MAP ARCHITECTURE

The prior v3.0 design (Millionaire Map, four-stage spine, Booking Hour, Dated Million/Re-Date, Builder's Scoreboard) is retired along with the Market Maker brand it was built for. Its individual mechanics — a single physical canvas that fills across three days, a scoreboard, a before/after recalculation moment — were genuinely strong and are **not disparaged here**, but they are not part of the source material this reconciliation is built from. ## PART 3 — THE DELIVERY DISCIPLINE

Two nested rhythms govern the room. The programme-wide rhythm (Progressive Installation™) still governs every section; the room-specific sequence (PLAN → EXPERIENCE → PERSONALISE → QUEUE) is how that rhythm is actually run live, refined 2026-08-13 to pull technical installation even further out of the room.

### 3.1 Progressive Installation™ (programme-wide)

1. **SEE IT** — understand the problem and the framework
2. **SCORE IT** — diagnose it in their own business (red/amber/green)
3. **DECIDE IT** — make the actual business decision
4. **BUILD IT** — create the first usable version (the asset, not the technical build)
5. **QUEUE IT** — flag what needs specialist installation afterward

### 3.2 PLAN → EXPERIENCE → PERSONALISE → QUEUE (the room, block by block)

This is how "Build It" actually runs inside The Multiplier Install, for every system-building block (Lead Machine, Lead-to-Appointment, Pre-Sell, Follow Through):

1. **PLAN** — the member manually designs the process first, with zero automation. *"If you had to make every prospect arrive better prepared yourself, what would you send, when, and why?"* They make the decisions. This is what makes the automation legible later — they understand what it's actually doing because they designed it first.
2. **EXPERIENCE** — walk the painful manual version at volume. *New appointment → copy WhatsApp message → find the video link → send the case study → set a reminder → remember tomorrow → follow up again. "Now imagine doing this 25 times a month."* They feel the gap before anything is offered to close it.
3. **PERSONALISE** — reveal the prebuilt Snapshot (80% done): trigger → messages → delays → conditions → reminders → pipeline movement. **Don't build it — reveal it.** The member customises only what's theirs (their message, their video, their case study, their timing, their CTA), assisted by a custom GPT that produces a first draft from client type, positioning, case study, tone and objective, which they then edit.
4. **QUEUE** — "ready for installation," explicitly not "installed." The specialist takes the member's decisions, assets, personalised copy and the prebuilt Snapshot, and installs/configures it afterward.

**Never reveal the prebuilt system before the member has felt the manual pain.** The reveal is the payoff of Experience, not the opening move — revealing it first (*"we've got a GHL snapshot for you"*) means they never value it.

### 3.3 The four-layer implementation hierarchy

| Layer | Job |
|---|---|
| **The Multiplier Install** | Business decisions + personalisation |
| **Snapshot / GPT** | ~80% prebuilt |
| **Specialist Call** | Technical configuration |
| **Agent** | Activate / use |

Edmund is the **architect**. Specialists are the **installers**. The room never becomes a technical-support session — this is the actual answer to a single teacher running a multi-day live event without becoming GHL/Meta/Claude support on stage.

### 3.4 The explicit scope boundary (taught early, on its own slide)

**WE WILL:** diagnose · make decisions · map systems · customise assets · test examples · start Priority #1.
**WE WILL NOT:** configure every GHL workflow · connect every account · build every automation · troubleshoot software · finish all nine multipliers.
**WHAT HAPPENS AFTER:** Specialist Installation Labs.

Stated on Day 1, not discovered on Day 3 — prevents *"why isn't my whole CRM done"* frustration.

### 3.5 Recognition, not competency

All Nine Multipliers are taught to **recognition** (enough to self-score "is this working or broken in my business") — not to **competency** (enough to go home and master it). ~30 minutes per pillar, ~8–10 minutes per multiplier, covers the full nine in roughly 90 minutes. Only the two chosen Priority Multipliers get the full PLAN→EXPERIENCE→PERSONALISE→QUEUE build; the other seven stay at recognition level, available as optional worksheets and weekly-Lab depth, not skipped or lost.

**The non-negotiable bar regardless: one live result per person before the room closes.**

## PART 4 — THE THREE-DAY SHAPE (revised 2026-08-13 — lighter Day 2, DELIVER stays light in the room)

| Day | Theme | Weight |
|---|---|---|
| Day 1 | Diagnose + Decide — the business map, taught to recognition, Top 2 chosen | Light build, heavy clarity |
| Day 2 | Build the Front End — Attract + Convert, the most commercially exciting day | Heaviest build day, but only 2 major systems, not 4 |
| Day 3 | Build the Back End + Gameplan — Deliver stays light, COMMAND reveal, roadmap | Strategic, not technical |

**Day 1 — Diagnose + Decide**
- The 4-level journey (Producer → Advisor → Business Owner → Multiplier), illustrated as a staircase: *"you only need the next step"*
- The Nine Multipliers taught to recognition (~90 min, ~30 min/pillar) → self-score red/amber/green → circle Top 2
- Best Client Mining (or the Market Fit Matrix for agents without enough transaction history)
- Advisor Model Canvas (WHO → WHAT → HOW → EXPERIENCE) — light touch, not a full build
- 12-month destination + 30-day outcome direction

**Day 2 — Build the Front End (only two major builds, not four)**

*AM — Build Your First Opportunity Engine (Lead Machine, ~2–2.5 hrs incl. examples/hot seats):* teach the three Lead Machines → decide ONE → design (audience → hook/reason → response → next step) → **PLAN/EXPERIENCE the manual version** → **PERSONALISE via Snapshot reveal** → queue for installation.

*PM — Build Your Appointment Engine (Lead-to-Appointment + Pre-Sell & Prepare, combined as ONE system, not two lessons):*
```
ENQUIRY → RESPONSE → CONVERSATION → BOOK → PRE-SELL → PREPARE → APPOINTMENT
```
Members design the response path, booking CTA, confirmation, one authority asset, and reminders — then the Snapshot reveal, personalise, queue.

**Authority is not a standalone Day-2 workshop.** It's taught inside the Day 1 recognition pass, then resurfaced here: *"Remember Authority? This is one place we use it."* Members pick ONE usable authority asset from three options — (A) an existing video, (B) an existing case study, (C) a simple new 2–3 min "Before We Meet" video recorded later — not a full content strategy. The content engine is a Year 2 conversation.

*End of Day 2 — the system-connection reveal:* zoom out and show the whole journey (lead source → GHL contact → response → conversation → appointment → pre-sell → reminders → appointment → follow through) first as it runs manually today, then the same journey marked **AUTO / AI+HUMAN / HUMAN**. This is the single strongest "AI isn't replacing me, it's removing repetitive movement around me" moment in the event — stronger than teaching any tool technically.

**Day 3 — Build the Back End + Gameplan**

*AM:* Follow Through — map the single biggest current leak (no-booking / no-show / undecided / dormant) and its routing logic, PLAN/EXPERIENCE/PERSONALISE/QUEUE. Then Client Experience and Client For Life **at recognition + light-touch level only** — not full builds. Members choose ONE client-continuity improvement (e.g. an annual review), not eight nurture campaigns. Whichever two multipliers weren't chosen as Top 2 stay light here regardless of pillar — **the 3×3 diagnoses the whole business; it does not dictate the full Boardroom syllabus.**

*PM:* COMMAND reveal (shown last, as the connective layer underneath everything just built — never taught as a separate module) → Business-in-a-Box → installation roadmap → specialist structure explained → Priority #1 activated live → 30-day sprint (NOW/NEXT/LATER, Top 2 mapped to NOW/NEXT) → 90-day roadmap.

## PART 5 — THE ILLUSTRATION SYSTEM

Full metaphor set (13 concepts): `03_ACTIVE_CAMPAIGNS/.../05_AGENT_EDITION_DESIGN_BIBLE_v1.0_DRAFT_UNRECONCILED.md` Part F. Governing rule: **if an agent can't redraw it from memory on a napkin after seeing it once, it's too complicated.**

Key ones for the room: Attract = a leaking pipe; Best Client = 100→20→5 circled; Advisor Authority = a lighthouse, not a boat chasing ships; Lead Machine = three taps, pick one; Convert = a bridge with three planks; Lead-to-Appointment = enquiry→response→conversation→book, one continuous path; Pre-Sell = "before the room"; Follow Through = a relay baton; Advisor Model = a blueprint vs. improvised wiring; Client Experience = a railway with stations; Client For Life = a circle vs. a dead-end arrow; the 4-level journey = a staircase, "you only need the next step."

**The whole-event metaphor (added 2026-08-13): a hospital assessment + first treatment.** Not "we repair every issue in three days." Instead: **Diagnose** what's actually wrong → **Prioritise** what matters most → **Start Treatment** (get the first intervention moving) → **Care Plan** (specialist Labs continue it afterward). Useful for framing the whole Multiplier Install to participants on Day 1, especially alongside the explicit scope-boundary slide in Part 3.4.
