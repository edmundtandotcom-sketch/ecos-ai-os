This is the operating system for the Agent Edition engine (recruitment/training — other Singapore property agents). It holds the locked positioning, the 3-day workshop design lineage, and the marketing assets built to sell the workshop under the Market Maker brand. It does not hold buyer psychology deep-dives, guru research, CRM/GHL config, or bulk creative files — those live in `06_KNOWLEDGE_VAULT`, `08_OPERATIONS`, and `07_BRAND_AND_PROOF_BANK`.

Everything here obeys the AI OS root `CLAUDE.md` §2 Business North Star: **Agent Edition = The Market Maker Method** — "Stop chasing the market. Start making it." Never blend with Client Advisory / Second Property Ladder™ assets in one output.
