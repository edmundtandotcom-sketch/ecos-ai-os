## PART 2 — COHORT SIZING & ECONOMICS (how many to make it worth it)

**The 3-day comfortable size: 20–30 agents.** Why: intimate enough for roleplay, hot seats, and advisory positioning (the premium signal); large enough for team-game energy (6 tables) and event economics. Above ~35 it stops feeling exclusive and the Inner Circle funnel weakens. Below ~15 the room feels flat and the math is thin.

**Event economics (illustrative, 30 seats @ $2,997):**
- 3-day gross ≈ **$90K** + downstream: continuity tier (say 50% take ~$197/mo = ~$3K/mo recurring) ⚠️ pre-Market-Maker pricing — current continuity is S$30,000/yr (Decision 083); confirm with Edmund if a monthly tier is still offered — + Inner Circle (2–4 invites × $35K = $70–140K). The workshop fee is the *smallest* line — the back end is the business (Hormozi/Kennedy lesson).

**Funnel math to FILL 30 seats** (tune with real numbers):
- Preview → 3-day conversion: assume **15–25%**. To fill 30, run **~150–200 preview attendees** per cohort cycle (across multiple previews).
- Ad/registration → preview show-up: assume ~40–60% of registrants attend. So **~300–500 preview registrations**.
- Cost per preview registration (cold, SG): test — budget a **cost-per-qualified-lead target** in the tracker (reuse the dashboard). The $97 preview price partially self-liquidates ad spend (Peng Joon's "front end filters + funds").

**Validation-first sequence (sell before you over-build):**
1. Run ads to a **simple preview registration page** + micro-book. Measure cost per registration and willingness to pay the $97.
2. Run **one preview**, pitch the 3-day, measure preview→3-day conversion and price resistance at $2,997.
3. Only after the first paid cohort commits do you fully build the Snapshot + all assets. This de-risks effort against real demand. (You can pre-sell the 3-day at a founding-cohort price to validate hard.)

## PART 3 — THE 1-DAY PREVIEW EVENT (run-of-show, live + online)

**Job of the preview:** teach enough to prove the method works, install belief + desire, and sell the 3-day. It is the 3-day's funnel — every minute sequenced toward enrollment (Peng Joon: "the event IS the funnel"). It is the agent-side equivalent of the property "checklist/session" offer.

**Reverse-engineered from Version B's peak moments** (Anatomy lite + Millionaire Math lite + 5-question demo is the skeleton):

| Time | Block | Job |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Open + the promise + who this is/ isn't for (identity filter — Lok) | Frame + qualify |
| 0:15–0:55 | **The Million-Dollar Anatomy (lite)** — dissect a real $1M advisory practice | The reveal; the destination (pull) |
| 0:55–1:25 | **The Millionaire Math (lite)** — attendees compute their own number + gap live | Make it personal; the gap creates desire |
| 1:25–1:35 | Break | |
| 1:35–2:15 | **The Method demo** — the 5-question advisory conversation, live | Prove the craft works |
| 2:15–2:40 | **Proof** — short success panel / named results (Cindior or a graduate) | Evidence at peak desire (Peng Joon) |
| 2:40–3:00 | **The Flywheel** drawn by hand + "here's the whole system" | One image they own |
| 3:00–3:30 | **The 3-Day offer** — the stack (10 assets, 3 signatures), founding-cohort price + fast-action bonus, enroll in room | The close (restrained, advisory register) |

**Live vs online:**
- **Online (Zoom/webinar):** cheaper, scalable, wider reach, lower show-up + lower close. Best for *volume* and early validation. Use Henry's webinar mechanics.
- **Live (in-person):** higher cost, higher commitment, much higher close + better Inner Circle seeding. Best once demand is proven.
- **Recommendation:** start **online** to validate cheaply and cost-effectively fill the pipeline; move the preview **live** once conversion is proven and you want premium positioning. Run both — online for reach, periodic live for high-intent.
