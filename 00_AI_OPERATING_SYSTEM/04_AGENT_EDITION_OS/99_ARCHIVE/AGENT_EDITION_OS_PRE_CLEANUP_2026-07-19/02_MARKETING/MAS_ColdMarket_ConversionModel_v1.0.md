# THE COLD-MARKET CONVERSION MODEL — "ATTRACT, DON'T CHASE" v1.0
**Date:** 2026-07-06 · For: MAS Agent Edition · Answers: *how do we convert cold agents to do business with us without chasing them?*

## THE STRATEGIC HINGE (why this model, not warm outreach)
The agent's disease is **warm-market / referral dependency** — they only know how to chase people they already know. The cure we sell is a **cold-market attraction system**. So our own marketing must *be* the cure:
> **We never chase an agent. We attract, we let them self-select, and they raise their hand. By the time they buy, they've already experienced the exact system we're going to install in their business.**
The medium is the message. Our funnel is a live demo of the product. That is our unfair proof.

## THE PAIN WE CONVERT (Edmund's words, locked)
Trained to **sell** — never trained to **build a business or a sales process**. → Firefighting every month. → Inconsistent income and results. → Worrying when the next paycheque comes. → Dependent on referrals and warm market. **No cold-market engine of their own.**

## WHY IT CONVERTS COLD AGENTS (the psychology)
- **Category of one:** they've seen 100 "how to sell / close more" ads. "Build the business, not just the sale" is new → pattern-interrupt.
- **Solution-aware & burned:** they've tried portals, vendors, AI, courses (see Failed-Fixes Ladder). We name the failures, so we earn the diagnosis they've never received.
- **Self-selection over persuasion:** the Scorecard makes *them* find the gap. People believe their own conclusions, not our claims.
- **Proof by demonstration:** the funnel *is* the product. The strongest possible risk-reversal — they felt it work before paying.

## THE ONE-LINE FUNNEL
Cold ad (pain reframe) → free Scorecard (self-diagnose the gap) → value+proof nurture → apply for the 3-Day → *"we never chased you — now let's build that for you."*
