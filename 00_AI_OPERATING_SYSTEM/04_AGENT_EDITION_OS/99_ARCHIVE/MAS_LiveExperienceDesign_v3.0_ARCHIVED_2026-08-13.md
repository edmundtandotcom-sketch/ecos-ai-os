# PART 1 — THE HONEST DIAGNOSIS (v2 base, unchanged)

v1 was operationally complete and emotionally flat. Five specific gaps:

1. **No millionaire through-line.** v1 confronted them with their broken business ($82/hour) but never showed them THEIR mathematical route to $1M.
2. **Day 3 was a tech class.** Building pipelines from blank screens is slow and emotionally dead.
3. **Deliverables had no names and no dollar values.** Unnamed deliverables can't be desired in advance or stacked in an offer.
4. **No score, no stages, no game.** The participant never knew where they stood.
5. **The event never paid for itself in the room.** No engineered moment produced booked revenue conversations before Day 3 ended.

v2 (this document's base) fixed all five with the Millionaire Map / four-stage spine. Everything from v1 that worked — facilitation protocols, room/staffing design, energy rules, qualification grid, proof capture — carries over unchanged.

> 🟩 **RIVAL'S PARALLEL DIAGNOSIS (Part 2 below expands this):** the rival draft names the same 5/10 gap independently but decomposes it into seven missing elements, with two the base v2 diagnosis doesn't name explicitly: **(6) no status/graduation architecture** and **(7) no show production.** Both are folded into this merge as Parts 5 and 8.

# PART 2 — THE SPINE: THE MILLIONAIRE MAP (v2 base, unchanged)

One physical artifact runs the entire 3 days: **The Millionaire Map** — an A2 canvas at every seat, mostly empty Day 1 morning, completely filled by Day 3 evening.

| Stage | Zone on the Map | The Question It Answers | When Built |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Stage 1 — SEE THE MILLION** | The Math | "What does MY million actually require — in deals, conversations, and leads, by what date?" | Day 1 morning |
| **Stage 2 — POSITION FOR THE MILLION** | The Who + The One-Liner | "Who pays it, and why me?" | Day 1 afternoon |
| **Stage 3 — THE MILLION-DOLLAR CONVERSATION** | The Method | "What do I say that a $200K agent can't?" | Day 2 |
| **Stage 4 — THE MACHINE** | The System | "What runs without my memory, my mood, or my hustle?" | Day 3 |

**The opening move (Day 1, 9:20) — The Dated Million.** Every participant computes the calendar year their cumulative GCI crosses $1M at current numbers. They write it, large, at the top of their Map.

**The Day 3 closing move — The Re-Date.** Same calculation, new inputs. The million moves from "2037" to "2029." Conservative assumptions only. The wall photo of 30 re-dated Maps is the best marketing asset the programme will ever produce.

# PART 4 — THE GAME LAYER: THE BUILDER'S SCOREBOARD (v2 base, unchanged)

A scoreboard wall tracks every participant — one point per completed build, posted at each break. Completions only, no quality scores.

- 10 scored builds across 3 days.
- **Full Build status** (10/10) announced by name at Day 3 close — this cohort's Implementors.
- Gives the producer a real-time read on who's stuck and gives Edmund IC shortlist data for free.

# PART 6 — THE CINDIOR APPEARANCES (RESOLVED 2026-07-11 — Edmund ruling: BOTH, different roles)

Cindior appears **twice**, not once. These were never true alternatives — they do different jobs in the room and both stay in the run sheet.

**Appearance 1 — Day 2, 14:45, 25 min — "The Success Panel." Role: social proof.**
Five fixed questions, interviewed by Edmund, data-anchored: What did your week look like before the rebuild? Which system changed your numbers first? What broke during the transition? What do your client conversations sound like now? Closer (the explicit IC-anchor beat): *"The honest answer to 'how do you stay ahead' is — I never stopped working with him. That's the room he's opening tomorrow."* Placed at peak desire, the day before ascension, per Peng Joon's proof-timing rule.

**Appearance 2 — Day 3, 9:30, 45–60 min — "A Day Inside the System." Role: operations credibility.**
Operational walkthrough of a real working day: yesterday's 8am pipeline view → what ran overnight → 3 manual touches → one live client thread end-to-end → "what I stopped doing." Closes with: *"I run the system. He builds and updates it."* Placed the morning of install so the Attract Engine install (was: the Snapshot — Decision 046) converts from "tech setup" to "installing what I just saw."

**Ruling logic:** Day 2's Panel sells the *outcome* (proof it works, at peak desire, before ascension). Day 3's walkthrough sells the *mechanism* (proof the system is real and running, right before install). Different audiences of one, different jobs — running both is stronger than picking one. Both confirmed in Part 5's run sheet.
