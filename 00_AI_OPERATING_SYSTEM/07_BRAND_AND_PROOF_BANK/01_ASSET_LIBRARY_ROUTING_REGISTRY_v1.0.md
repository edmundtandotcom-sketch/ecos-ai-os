## Promotion rule

An asset enters Approved Library only when its purpose, audience, brand status and proof/consent status are known; it is reusable; its filename is understandable; and an authoritative duplicate does not already exist.

## Working-file rule

Active drafts stay in `03_ACTIVE_CAMPAIGNS`. Promote the approved final—not the whole working folder.
