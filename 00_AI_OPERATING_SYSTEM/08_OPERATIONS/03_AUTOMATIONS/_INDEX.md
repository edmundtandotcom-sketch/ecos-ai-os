This branch contains governed operational automation assets, prompt templates and skills retained from the pre-cleanup structure.

## Rules

- Confirm the business owner before implementing automation.
- Sales supplies pipeline and follow-up semantics; Marketing supplies campaign requirements; Operations implements.
- Never place named contact data or client transcript evidence in prompt/template files.
- Record inputs, outputs, failure states, human approval gates and rollback.
- Verify current external platform behavior before release.
- Retain rescued prompts as reference unless separately approved for live use.
