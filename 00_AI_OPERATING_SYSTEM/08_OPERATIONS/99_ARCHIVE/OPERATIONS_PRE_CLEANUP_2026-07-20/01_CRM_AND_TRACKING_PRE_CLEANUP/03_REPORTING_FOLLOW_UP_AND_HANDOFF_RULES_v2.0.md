## 1. Reporting Rhythm

Recommended cadence:

| Rhythm | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Daily quick check | Spot broken campaigns, bad spend, tracking issues, urgent lead follow-up |
| Weekly review | Decide kill / scale / iterate, review lead quality and bookings |
| Monthly review | Promote learnings, clean dashboards, update campaign priorities |

## 2. Daily Quick Check

Daily check should answer:

- Did new leads come in?
- Were they contacted?
- Any high-quality lead not followed up?
- Any campaign spending with poor lead quality?
- Any tracking or automation failure?

## 3. Weekly Review

Weekly review should produce:

- campaigns to scale;
- campaigns to pause;
- creatives to duplicate or iterate;
- lead quality notes;
- follow-up gaps;
- next actions for Edmund / Cindior / team.

## 4. Handoff to Content Marketing

Send learning back to Content Marketing when:

- a hook works;
- a buyer segment responds;
- an objection appears repeatedly;
- a campaign attracts wrong-fit leads;
- a campaign needs a new angle or landing page change.

Do not store campaign drafts in Operations. Store the learning here, then rebuild the campaign pack inside Content Marketing.

## 5. Handoff to Knowledge Vault

Promote reusable learning to Knowledge Vault when it is not campaign-specific, such as:

- recurring buyer language;
- repeated objections;
- strong consult proof;
- market sentiment;
- reusable case insight.

## 6. Follow-Up Accountability

Every serious lead must have:

- owner;
- last touch;
- next touch;
- next action;
- lead quality grade;
- follow-up reason.

No lead should sit in a vague state like "maybe later" without a next trigger.

## 7. Operational Rule

Operations exists to make decisions clear.

If a tracker or report does not help decide what to do next, simplify it, archive it, or stop using it.
