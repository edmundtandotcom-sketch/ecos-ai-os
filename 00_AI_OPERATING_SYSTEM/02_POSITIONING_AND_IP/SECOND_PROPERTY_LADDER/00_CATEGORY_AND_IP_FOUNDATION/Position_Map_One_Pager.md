**THE POSITION MAP**

**The One-Pager — Deliverable of the Next Move Strategy Session™**

*The single page a Property Climber leaves the 45-minute session holding: their numbers, their constraints, their direction, their Move, and their one next move.*

REI Advisory · Coach Edmund Tan & Cindior Ho · Singapore Real Estate Insider

Version 1.0 · 9 July 2026 · Built from Mechanism v1.3 (Rank 2\) · Fulfils the diagnostic’s promise: “45 minutes, and you leave with your numbers”

**WHAT THIS IS.**  The Position Map is the product of the session every diagnostic result sells. One printed page, completed live with the client. It covers Move 1 (Map the Position) in full and Move 2 (Set the Direction) in writing — and it ends by marking the client’s Move and their one next move. The client keeps the page. The page keeps the relationship.

**THE RULE.**  The Position Map never names a property to buy, a project, or a launch. It maps the client’s position — nothing else. The sequence, exit, and cadence editions of the session do their own jobs later. One page, one job.

**1\. Where the One-Pager Sits**

| Stage | Asset | What the client holds |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Front of funnel** | Which Move Are You On? diagnostic | A Move number and a booked session |
| **The session (45 min)** | **THE POSITION MAP — this one-pager** | Their numbers on one page: defendable value, CPF refund, walk-away cash, constraints, Direction Statement, their Move, one next move |
| **After the session** | Sequence / exit / cadence editions | Booked as the “one next move” where the Map shows the client is ready |

Every diagnostic result page promises the same thing: **“45 minutes, and you leave with your numbers.”** This page IS the numbers. If a client walks out without this page completed, the session failed its promise — whatever else was discussed.

**3\. The 45-Minute Run Sheet (Advisor Side)**

The session is the one-pager, completed live, in order, on paper or tablet with the client watching every number land. Never pre-fill Sections C or D — the client’s own words go in those boxes, or the commitment is yours instead of theirs.

| Minutes | Block | What happens |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **0–5** | **Frame \+ their diagnostic** | Open with their quiz result: their Move, and which answers scored 0\. “You told us three things in the diagnostic — let’s prove them right or wrong with real numbers.” The internal booking notification carries their 10 raw checkpoint answers — walk in knowing the zeros. |
| **5–20** | **Section A — The Position** | Pull 3 comparable transactions live (URA/HDB data on screen — let them watch you pull it). Land the defendable value. Then the walk-away calculation line by line: loan statement, CPF property statement (principal AND accrued interest — this is the number that shocks), selling costs. Write WALK-AWAY CASH large. Pause on it. This is usually the first time they have ever seen it. |
| **20–28** | **Section B — The Constraints** | MOP/SSD clear date to the day. Names on title and income shape. Tenure runway. ABSD: tick the box, do NOT compute it here — “that exact figure is a session of its own, and it’s worth six figures to get right.” The unanswered ABSD box is the pull to the sequence session. |
| **28–40** | **Section C — The Direction** | The alignment conversation, live, both partners. Three destinations on the table — they choose one out loud. Then the Direction Statement in THEIR words, with a number and a year. Both sign the “Agreed by” line. A signed sentence beats an hour of talk. |
| **40–45** | **Section D — Move \+ Next Move** | Mark their Move — now proven by the page, not the quiz. Write ONE next move with a date. If their Move is 3+, the next move is the sequence session — book it before they leave. Set the next review date regardless. Hand them the page. They keep the original; you keep the copy. |

**THE CLOSE IS THE PAGE.**  There is no pitch at minute 40\. The page closes: an unanswered ABSD box, a signed Direction Statement, and one next move with a date. If the page is completed honestly, the next session books itself.

**Pre-Session Checklist**

* Client brings: latest mortgage statement · CPF property statement (my cpf online → Property) · both partners present.

* Advisor prepares: their 10 raw diagnostic answers · 3 comparable transactions pre-pulled (re-pull live in session for effect) · blank one-pager printed, two copies.

* If the client arrives without CPF figures: complete the page with estimates in pencil, mark “EST”, and set a 48-hour follow-up to ink the real numbers. Never leave the page abandoned.

**4\. Rules & QC**

* **One page, one job. The Position Map maps the position and sets the direction. It never names a property, computes ABSD, or profiles an exit — those are the sequence and exit editions.**
