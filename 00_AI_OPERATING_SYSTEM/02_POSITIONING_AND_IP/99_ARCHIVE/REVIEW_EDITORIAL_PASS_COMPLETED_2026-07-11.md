The delivery assets actually produce a **Direction Statement** and **Selection Research**. Two of five named deliverables don't exist as named in the room. A client is promised one thing and handed another.
- **The Mechanism (Rank 2) already claims naming authority** and explicitly rules "Step" public, "Move" CTA-only. The live revenue assets (Diagnostic, Position Map one-pager) are already built on "Step." So Camp B is the de-facto standard — but the *highest* doc, the Constitution, and the positioning summary both say "5 Moves." **This is an authority clash the source-of-truth hierarchy cannot resolve on its own** (Constitution outranks Mechanism, but Mechanism owns naming). Edmund must pick the word.
- **My recommendation:** ratify **Camp B ("Step" public, "Move" as the CTA verb only)** — it is already built into live assets, is internally the more rigorous rule, and "What's your next Move?" survives intact as the CTA. Then fix CategoryKing, `01_BUSINESS_POSITIONING.md`, and the Constitution North Star to match. Cheaper than relabelling built assets.
