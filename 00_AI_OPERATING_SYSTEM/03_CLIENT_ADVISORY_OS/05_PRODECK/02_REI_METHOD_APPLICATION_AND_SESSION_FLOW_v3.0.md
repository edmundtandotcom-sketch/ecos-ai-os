## REI spine

### Review Real Position
Inputs: property, ownership, loan, capital, household, timeline, constraints, decision-makers.  
Primary output: **Position Map**.

### Evaluate Market Rewards
Test relevant routes, market/exit conditions, affordability, holding strength and opportunity cost.  
Primary outputs: **Direction Statement** and route comparison.

### Identify Next 2–3 Moves
Sequence dependencies, safeguards, verification and review triggers.  
Primary output: **Move Sequence**.

## Relationship to The 5 Moves

| The 5 Moves | REI contribution |
|---|---|
| Map the Position | Review |
| Set the Direction | Review + Evaluate |
| Sequence the Moves | Identify |
| Research the Exit First | Evaluate; detailed execution after engagement |
| Execute & Repeat | Identify review triggers and handoff |

## Session flow

1. Confirm the diagnosis.
2. Present Position Map.
3. Agree the Direction Statement.
4. Compare three routes.
5. Present Move Sequence.
6. Clarify exit/survivability.
7. State advisory outcome.
8. Assign next commitment.

## Stop conditions

Stop or downgrade the output when numbers, decision-makers, professional verification or route evidence are missing.

## Final rule

REI is the diagnosis order. The 5 Moves is the product order. ProDeck makes both understandable.
