## Purpose

This branch is the reusable **property diagnosis, advisory-conversation and consult-delivery system** for Client Advisory.

It tells an advisor how to understand the household, diagnose the position, clarify the route, explain risk, structure the Next Move Strategy Session™ and produce the advisory outputs that later feed selection and implementation.

It does **not** own:
- lead qualification, appointment conversion, closing, follow-up, revival or referrals — `11_SALES_OS`;
- GHL implementation, dashboards or workflow automation — `08_OPERATIONS`;
- campaign production — `03_ACTIVE_CAMPAIGNS`;
- approved reusable media/assets — `01_ASSET_LIBRARY`;
- identifiable client records — GHL or restricted `00_E.C.O.S_CLIENTS_PRIVATE`.

## Advisory-to-Sales boundary

Client Advisory determines:
- what the household position means;
- what information is missing;
- which route is feasible;
- what risk must be understood;
- which advisory outcome applies;
- what next commitment is appropriate.

Sales OS determines:
- lead stage and qualification workflow;
- booking/show-up;
- closing and objection process;
- follow-up/nurture/revival;
- pipeline movement and referral process.
