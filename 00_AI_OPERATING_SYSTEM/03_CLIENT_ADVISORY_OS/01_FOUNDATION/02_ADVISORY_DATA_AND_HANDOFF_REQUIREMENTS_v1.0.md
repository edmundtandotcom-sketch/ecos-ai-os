## Purpose

Define the minimum information Client Advisory needs and the clean handoff it produces. This is not a CRM build specification.

## Required advisory information

- household and decision-makers;
- current property/ownership position;
- estimated value, loan and relevant capital information;
- timeline and policy clocks;
- desired future position;
- route being considered;
- key constraints;
- strategic intent;
- belief/risk gaps;
- information still missing;
- professional verification required.

## Advisory outputs to hand off

- concise position summary;
- route hypothesis or approved route;
- main risks and accepted trade-offs;
- decision-makers and alignment status;
- advisory outcome: Proceed / Prepare / Nurture / No Fit;
- next commitment required;
- documents/data required;
- review trigger and due date;
- promises made to the client.

## Ownership

| Item | Owner |
|---|---|
| Advisory meaning and route | Client Advisory |
| Lead stage, qualification and follow-up | Sales OS |
| GHL fields/workflows and dashboards | Operations |
| Client record and attachments | GHL / restricted private drive |
| Campaign attribution | Marketing + Operations |

## Privacy

No PII, financial statements, contact records or case notes are stored in the general AI OS.

## Final rule

Client Advisory defines **what the handoff must mean**. Sales and Operations define **how it is processed and implemented**.
