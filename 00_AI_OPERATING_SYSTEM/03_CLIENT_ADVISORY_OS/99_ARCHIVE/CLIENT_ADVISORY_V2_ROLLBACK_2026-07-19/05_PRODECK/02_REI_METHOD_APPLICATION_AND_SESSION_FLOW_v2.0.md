# 02_REI_METHOD_APPLICATION_AND_SESSION_FLOW_v2.0
Status: ACTIVE SOURCE OF TRUTH

Purpose: Define how the REI Method runs inside a live ProDeck session.

## 1. REI Method Spine
Every ProDeck consult uses the same spine:

Review Real Position -> Evaluate Market Rewards -> Identify Next 2-3 Moves

This is not a slogan. It is the delivery order.

## 2. Step 1: Review Real Position
Purpose: make the client see their true starting point before any recommendation.

Capture:
- current property type, value, tenure, lease age, ownership, loan, CPF usage;
- cash and CPF OA available;
- household income and loanability;
- decision-makers and spouse / family alignment;
- timeline triggers such as MOP, lease decay, school, retirement, job change, new child, aging parents;
- current belief block or private hesitation.

Output:
Real Position Snapshot.

Plain-English line:
"Right now, your property has done [X], but your structure is still doing [Y]. The main decision is not which project to buy yet. It is whether your current structure still supports the next stage of your life."

## 3. Step 2: Evaluate Market Rewards
Purpose: show what the market is rewarding and what the client may miss by staying passive.

Use 2-3 signals only:
- current property vs target property performance;
- new launch vs resale quantum gap;
- exit liquidity and buyer pool;
- rental / holding strength;
- policy or financing constraint;
- lease decay or ageing-asset risk;
- opportunity cost of not unlocking equity.

Output:
Market Reward Lens.

Plain-English line:
"The question is not whether the market is good or bad in general. The question is what the market is rewarding for someone in your exact position."

## 4. Step 3: Identify Next 2-3 Moves
Purpose: convert diagnosis into a clear roadmap.

Move structure:
- Move 1: immediate decision or preparation step;
- Move 2: next structural move after Move 1 succeeds;
- Move 3: optional future move or contingency.

Each move must include:
- what action is needed;
- why it exists;
- what it unlocks;
- what must be checked before execution;
- what could make it unsuitable.

Output:
Next Move Roadmap.

Plain-English line:
"Based on your numbers and timeline, these are the next two or three decisions that matter. The property choice comes after this sequence is clear."

## 5. Ascent Stage Application
Use Ascent Stage as the live stage anchor.

| Stage | What the client is trying to solve | ProDeck emphasis |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A1 First Climb | First serious property wealth move or current property went up, now what? | Position clarity, upgrade window, not getting stuck |
| A2 Compound | Turning one asset into a stronger next structure | Reward framing, risk assurance, sequencing |

> **RETIRED (Decision 063, 2026-07-11):** the former A3/A4 ProDeck rows are removed — the Ascent Model is A0–A2. Family, retirement, child-entry and complex multi-asset situations are delivered inside standard A1/A2 ProDeck engagements (structure, safety and family-alignment emphasis where the case calls for it).

Do not create a second active level system. Property Position is only context.

## 6. Segment-Specific ProDeck Notes
### DINK / Golden Window
Focus on flexibility, time, dual income, and the risk of wasting the most flexible years.

### Decoupling Planner
Frame clearly: decoupling is not the strategy. It only opens the next decision.

### Qualified HDB / EC Upgrade Window
Do not assume every HDB / EC owner should upgrade. Check MOP, equity, household income, cash / CPF, ownership names, risk capacity, and realistic timing first.

### Existing Condo Owner
Focus on whether the gain has created real options or only paper wealth.

### Family Asset Planner
Focus on child entry, parent capital risk, ownership clarity, and future exit path.

### New Launch vs Resale Decision
Do not reduce this to product preference. Compare timing, exit liquidity, holding power, cash flow, opportunity cost, and life fit.

## 7. Stop Rules
Stop and do not proceed to full recommendation when:
- both decision-makers are not aligned;
- numbers are missing;
- the move depends on legal / tax assumptions not yet verified;
- affordability is unclear;
- the client is emotionally project-first and cannot evaluate strategy yet;
- the analysis shows the move is unsafe.

Stopping is not failure. It protects trust.

## 8. ProDeck Close Questions
Use one close question based on the actual gap:

| Gap | Close question |
| --- | --- |
| Missing numbers | "What number do we need to confirm before this becomes a real decision?" |
| Spouse alignment | "Which part of this would your spouse question first?" |
| Risk fear | "Which risk still feels uncomfortable even after the safety frame?" |
| Timing uncertainty | "What happens if you wait 12 more months and nothing changes?" |
| Project-first behaviour | "Before choosing a project, what job must this property perform for your next stage?" |
| Ready to move | "What is the next action that turns this from discussion into execution?" |

## 9. CRM / Follow-Up Handoff
After the session, log:
- Ascent Stage;
- property position context;
- buyer segment;
- readiness stage;
- belief block;
- main risk concern;
- recommended review type;
- next action;
- follow-up date;
- what was promised.

This belongs operationally in CRM / Operations, but ProDeck must produce the handoff cleanly.
