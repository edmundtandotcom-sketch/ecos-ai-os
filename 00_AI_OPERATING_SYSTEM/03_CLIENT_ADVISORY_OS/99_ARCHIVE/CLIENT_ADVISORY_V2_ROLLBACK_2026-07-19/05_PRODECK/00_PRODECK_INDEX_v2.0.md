## 3. Folder Boundary
Keep inside ProDeck:
- live consult delivery structure;
- REI Method session flow;
- risk and reward presentation rules;
- client-facing roadmap output templates;
- session handoff formats;
- advisory deck logic.

Do not keep inside ProDeck:
- raw campaign packs, hooks, ads, scripts, landing pages, or Meta creative systems;
- CRM dashboards, ad performance, lead quality trackers, or reporting sheets;
- long research archives or old PWS reference files;
- untested transcript extracts unless they are promoted into a clean consult rule.

## 6. ProDeck Operating Principle
The advisor must not start with the property.

The order is:
1. Position.
2. Market rewards.
3. Risk assurance.
4. Next 2-3 moves.
5. Decision confidence and next action.

This protects the advisory standard:
Diagnosis before prescription.
