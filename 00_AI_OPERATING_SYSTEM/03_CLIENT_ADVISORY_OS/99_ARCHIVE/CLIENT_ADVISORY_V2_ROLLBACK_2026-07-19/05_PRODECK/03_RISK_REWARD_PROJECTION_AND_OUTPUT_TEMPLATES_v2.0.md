# 03_RISK_REWARD_PROJECTION_AND_OUTPUT_TEMPLATES_v2.0
Status: ACTIVE SOURCE OF TRUTH
Folder: 02_CLIENT_OS / 04_PRODECK
Purpose: Define how risk assurance, reward framing, projection templates, and client handoff outputs are used inside ProDeck.

## 1. Core Principle
Numbers create clarity only when they are honest.

ProDeck must never use projections to impress the client. Use projections to test whether the strategy is sensible.

If the numbers are weak, say so.

## 2. Risk and Reward Delivery Order
Use this order:

1. Evaluate the market reward conceptually.
2. Check the risk frame.
3. Show projection ranges only after the client understands the risk controls.
4. Connect the reward to the next Ascent move.
5. State what must be verified before action.

This resolves the old conflict where some files showed reward before RAM and others showed full projections only after RAM.

## 3. RAM Inside ProDeck
RAM is used as a risk-assurance lens, not a separate sales module.

Use the relevant RAM lens only:
- cash-flow stress;
- safety net;
- CPF and cash preservation;
- tenure / monthly instalment management;
- downside exit options;
- ownership and stamp duty exposure;
- family or spouse risk;
- lease decay or exit liquidity.

Do not run every RAM concept. Select based on the client's actual risk block.

## 4. Projection Safety Rules
All projections must follow these rules:

1. Use conservative, base, and upside cases.
2. Do not present only the best case.
3. Round numbers to avoid false precision.
4. Separate cash proceeds from CPF refund.
5. Show holding cost, exit cost, and capital required.
6. Compare against "do nothing" only when the assumptions are fair.
7. Never state or imply guaranteed profit.
8. Verify current rules, rates, and financing assumptions before client use.
9. Recommend legal, tax, and financing verification where relevant.

## 5. Policy and Rate Verification Rule
Do not hardcode live stamp duty, ABSD, LTV, TDSR, SSD, CPF, or tax rules into ProDeck as permanent truth.

Use this wording:
"Rates and eligibility must be verified against the latest official sources and professional advice before execution."

For internal calculation templates, include a verification checkpoint rather than a stale static table.

After each session, capture:
- recommended review type;
- main gap detected;
- missing data;
- next action owner;
- due date;
- documents to prepare;
- risk still unresolved;
- client readiness signal.

## 8. What To Bring Into The Next Consult
Use when the client must come back with numbers:

- latest CPF OA estimate;
- outstanding loan amount and tenure;
- current property purchase price and year bought;
- estimated current value;
- cash / CPF available for next move;
- income documents for loan check;
- spouse / co-owner availability;
- renovation / family / school / retirement constraints;
- any existing banker or legal advice.

## 9. Compliance Language
Use simple language:
"This is not a guaranteed-return projection. It is a decision model. The purpose is to see whether the move is safe, logical, and worth exploring before you commit."
