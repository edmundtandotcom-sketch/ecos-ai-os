## 5. Current Ascent Stages

| Stage | Simple Meaning | Typical Client Thought |
|---|---|---|
| A1 First Climb | One main property position is ready to become a strategic next move | “My property went up. Now what should it unlock?” |
| A2 Compound | First strategic move has been made; now timing, exit, redeployment and optimisation matter | “When and how should we compound this properly?” |

> **RETIRED (Decision 063, 2026-07-11):** the former A3 (Legacy/family) and A4 (Bespoke Portfolio / Mash-Up) stages are removed from the Ascent Model — it is now A0 · Activation → A1 · First Climb → A2 · Compound. Multi-name/family situations are handled inside standard A1/A2 engagements without a dedicated level.

A0 / Activation is not a main active stage. It is an awareness moment inside A1 First Climb.

## 6. Legacy Launch Anchor

Legacy Launch mainly speaks to A1 First Climb.

A1 includes both:

- Recognition moment: “My property went up. Now what?”
- Movement moment: “Should I hold, sell, decouple, upgrade, or buy again?”

## 7. HDB / EC Guardrail

HDB/EC upgrade segment does not mean anyone who owns HDB/EC.

It means a qualified next-move household with relevant timing, financial capacity, ownership/name structure, upgrade intent, and MAS-fit advisory behaviour.

Do not create broad HDB/EC mass-market positioning unless a specific campaign requires it.
