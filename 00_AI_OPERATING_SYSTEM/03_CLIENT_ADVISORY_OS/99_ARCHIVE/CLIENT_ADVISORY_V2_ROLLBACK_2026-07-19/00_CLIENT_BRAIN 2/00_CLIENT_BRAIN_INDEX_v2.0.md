## 1. What Client Brain Is

Client Brain is the compact AI behaviour layer for Client Edition.

It tells AI:

- how to think;
- what principles to protect;
- which decisions are locked;
- how to handle migration and folder cleanup;
- what to avoid when producing Client Edition work.

Client Brain is not the place for campaign files, ProDeck files, creative prompts, raw ads, landing pages, client case documents, or performance dashboards.

## 2. What Changed From v1

The old Client Brain package tried to cover too many things: advisory, content, ads, leads, nurture, proof, operations, and experiments. That made the folder feel like a second operating system instead of a clear AI behaviour layer.

The v2 structure fixes this by separating the system:

| Layer | Correct Home |
|---|---|
| AI behaviour and principles | 01_CLIENT_BRAIN |
| Advisory intelligence and consult system | 02_CLIENT_OS |
| Campaign packs and creative production | 03_CONTENT_MARKETING_ENGINE |
| Actual client case work | 04_CLIENTS |
| Reusable research and swipe intelligence | 05_KNOWLEDGE_VAULT |
| CRM, dashboards, SOPs, ad results, lead quality | 06_OPERATIONS_AND_TRACKING |
| Superseded versions and old logs | 99_ARCHIVE |

## 5. Client Brain Operating Boundary

Use Client Brain when the question is:

- How should AI think?
- What should AI avoid?
- What principles are protected?
- Which decisions are locked?
- How should old docs be migrated?

Do not use Client Brain as the storage place for:

- campaign packs;
- campaign angles;
- creative concept libraries;
- ProDeck content;
- trigger question banks;
- client assessment files;
- ad outputs;
- platform-specific scripts;
- dashboards or performance results.

## 6. HDB/EC Segment Guardrail

HDB/EC upgrade segment does not mean any HDB or EC owner.

For Client Edition, HDB/EC matters only when the household is connected to a MAS-fit upgrade or next-move pathway.

Qualified HDB/EC upgrade-window signals include:

- nearing or past MOP/TOP;
- meaningful equity, CPF, cash, income, or family capital;
- realistic holding power;
- two-name structure relevance;
- ability to consider private property safely;
- a serious next-move decision, not casual browsing;
- willingness to diagnose numbers, risk, and structure;
- fit with the advisory model, not discount/project-tip behaviour.

Do not let broad HDB/EC content become the core of Client Edition.
