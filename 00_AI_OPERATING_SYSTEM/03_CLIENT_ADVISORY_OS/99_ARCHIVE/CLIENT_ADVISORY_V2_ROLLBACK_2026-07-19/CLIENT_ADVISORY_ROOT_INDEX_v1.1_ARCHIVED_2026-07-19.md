## Purpose

This is the advisory and diagnosis layer for Client Advisory (property buyers). It holds MAS Client OS v2.0: how the AI behaves for Client Edition, how we diagnose and advise, and the consult delivery system (ProDeck). It does not hold campaign packs, creative outputs, client case files, or CRM data — those live in `05_CONTENT_MARKETING_ENGINE`, `06_KNOWLEDGE_VAULT`, and `08_OPERATIONS`.

Everything here traces to the Second Property Ladder™ positioning (`02_POSITIONING_AND_IP`) and the MAS 7-layer stack defined in the AI OS root `CLAUDE.md` §10: MAS → Ascent Model → REI Method → ProDeck → Trigger Questions → campaign entry doors → BNTTS (mindset filter only).
