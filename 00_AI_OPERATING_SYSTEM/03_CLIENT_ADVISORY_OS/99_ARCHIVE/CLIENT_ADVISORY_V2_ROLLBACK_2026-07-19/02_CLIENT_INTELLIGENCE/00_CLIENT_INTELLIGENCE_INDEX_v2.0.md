Purpose: This file defines what belongs inside Client Intelligence and what must move to Content Marketing, Operations, Knowledge Vault, or Archive.

## 1. Simple Rule

Client Intelligence is the reusable client psychology and diagnosis layer.

It answers:

- Who is this client type?
- What are they quietly thinking?
- What decision are they avoiding?
- What trap are they stuck in?
- What better question should we help them ask?
- What readiness stage are they in?

It does not answer:

- Which ad image should we generate?
- Which campaign pack should we launch?
- Which reel script should go live?
- What was the ad result?

Those belong in Content Marketing Engine and Operations & Tracking.

## 7. Ascent Stage Rule

Use one active level system only: Ascent Stage.

Do not create a separate level ladder for property count.

Property position is context, not a second ladder.

For Legacy Launch and most Client Edition acquisition work, the primary stage is:

A1 — First Climb

A1 First Climb includes the early recognition thought:

“Your property went up. Now what?”

This is not a separate Activation level. It is the early awareness moment inside First Climb.

## 8. HDB / EC Guardrail

HDB/EC upgrade segment does not mean anyone who owns HDB or EC.

It means a qualified upgrade-window household with a real decision to make.

Valid signals may include:

- approaching or past a meaningful sale / upgrade window
- meaningful equity or capital that can support a safer move
- household income and loan capacity that make private-property planning realistic
- ownership-name structure that affects the next move
- willingness to plan beyond “just upgrade bigger”
- MAS-fit advisory behaviour: serious, planning-oriented, not bargain-hunting only

Public copy should not blindly say “HDB/EC owners” unless the campaign is specifically for that segment and the compliance context is checked.

## 9. Public Language Rule

Use public language that is simple and safe:

- your property
- your current home
- your next move
- your position
- your options
- before you sign
- before you free a name
- before you sell

Avoid overusing technical internal labels in public ads.

## 10. Working Rule

If a file is mainly about the client’s psychology, it belongs here.

If a file is mainly about how to market, produce, launch, or track, it belongs outside this folder.

When unsure, ask:

“What job is this file doing now?”
