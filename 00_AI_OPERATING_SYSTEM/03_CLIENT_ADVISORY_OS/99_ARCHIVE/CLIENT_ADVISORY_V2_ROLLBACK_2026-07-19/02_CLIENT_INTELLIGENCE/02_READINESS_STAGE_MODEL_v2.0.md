Purpose: Define readiness stages across Client Edition so campaigns, consult prep, and follow-up match the client’s actual decision state.

## 1. Readiness Rule

Readiness stage is not the same as buyer segment.

A DINK, decoupling planner, HDB/EC upgrade-window household, or ready mover can each appear at different readiness stages.

Segment = who they are.  
Readiness = how close they are to action.

## 2. The 4 Readiness Stages

| Stage | Name | Buyer State | Main Job |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Comfortably Stuck | Stable, not actively searching, but unreviewed | Activate hidden dissatisfaction |
| R2 | Actively Confused | Aware something needs review, but unclear how to decide | Give a decision filter |
| R3 | Project-Pressured | Close to commitment or being pushed by a project/mechanism | Interrupt bad momentum |
| R4 | Framework-Ready | Accepts that strategy is needed and is open to advisor help | Convert into review / consult |

## 3. R1 — Comfortably Stuck

### Buyer State
They are stable. Life is fine. The property may have appreciated. Nothing feels urgent enough to force action.

### Private Thought
“Our property went up, but I do not really know what to do with the gain.”

### What They Say
- “No rush.”
- “Maybe we just hold.”
- “We are comfortable.”
- “The property is doing okay.”

### Main Trap
Holding without review.

### Marketing Job
Make the silent gap visible without creating panic.

### Best Message Types
- “Your property went up, but your strategy did not.”
- “A higher valuation is not the same as a plan.”
- “Comfort can quietly become delay.”

### Best CTA Style
Soft review / map the next move.

### What Not To Do
Do not push aggressive booking language too early. Do not make them feel wrong for being comfortable.

## 4. R2 — Actively Confused

### Buyer State
They know something needs to be reviewed. They may be comparing hold, sell, buy again, decouple, redeploy, upgrade, or wait.

### Private Thought
“I know we need to review this, but I do not know what decision filter to use.”

### What They Say
- “Should we hold or sell?”
- “Should we decouple?”
- “Should we buy resale or new launch?”
- “We spoke to a few agents and everyone says something different.”

### Main Trap
Too many options, no decision framework.

### Marketing Job
Reduce noise. Name the real decision filter.

### Best Message Types
- “The question is not which option looks best. The question is which option improves your next position.”
- “More opinions do not create clarity. A decision filter does.”
- “Before you compare projects, compare pathways.”

### Best CTA Style
Review options / decision filter / map the move.

### What Not To Do
Do not add another list of generic options. They already have too many.

## 5. R3 — Project-Pressured

### Buyer State
They are close to a decision. They may be near OTP, showflat booking, new launch selection, decoupling execution, or purchase commitment.

### Private Thought
“I like this option, but I am afraid I am missing something before I commit.”

### What They Say
- “The unit looks good.”
- “We need to decide soon.”
- “The pricing seems okay.”
- “The agent says the good units are moving.”

### Main Trap
Project-first momentum.

### Marketing Job
Interrupt the decision before commitment locks them into the wrong position.

### Best Message Types
- “Before you sign, know who exits from you later.”
- “A good project can still be the wrong move.”
- “Do not let the showflat answer a strategy question.”

### Best CTA Style
Pause before commit / review before signing / send shortlist.

### What Not To Do
Do not sound like another project agent fighting for the same transaction.

## 6. R4 — Framework-Ready

### Buyer State
They accept that they need strategy and are ready to speak to an advisor. They may still have concerns, but they now value clarity over more browsing.

### Private Thought
“I want someone to help us structure this properly.”

### What They Say
- “Can you help us review?”
- “What should we bring?”
- “Can you compare our options?”
- “We need to see the numbers properly.”

### Main Trap
Delaying the consult even after recognising the need.

### Marketing Job
Convert into a useful review with the right expectation.

### Best Message Types
- “Bring your current property, numbers, and shortlist. We will map what is actually sensible.”
- “The goal is not to buy more. The goal is to make the right next move.”
- “We disqualify before we recommend.”

### Best CTA Style
Book review / strategy session / bring spouse / bring shortlist.

### What Not To Do
Do not over-teach. They are ready for action.

## 7. Stage Movement Triggers

| Movement | Trigger |
|---|---|
| R1 → R2 | They realise appreciation has not become a plan |
| R2 → R3 | They start shortlisting or are exposed to urgency/project pressure |
| R3 → R4 | They realise a wrong decision can lock them in for years |
| R4 → Consult | They trust the method and see the review as useful, not salesy |

## 8. CTA Pressure Rule

| Stage | CTA Pressure |
|---|---|
| R1 | Soft: “Map what this could unlock.” |
| R2 | Moderate: “Review your options before deciding.” |
| R3 | High but calm: “Review before signing / committing.” |
| R4 | Direct: “Book the review.” |

## 9. Creative Direction Rule

Readiness stage should guide the message intensity.
