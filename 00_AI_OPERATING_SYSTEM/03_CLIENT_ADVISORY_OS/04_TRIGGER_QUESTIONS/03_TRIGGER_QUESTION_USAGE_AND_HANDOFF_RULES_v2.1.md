## Selection rule

Before choosing a question, identify:
- current position;
- strategic intent;
- dominant segment;
- readiness;
- belief/risk gap;
- decision-maker;
- advisory purpose.

Use the smallest set that creates new information or self-realisation.

## Question sequence

1. Situation
2. Meaning
3. Consequence
4. Trade-off
5. Better decision standard
6. Next commitment

Do not stack questions when the answer is already clear.

## Evidence capture

Record:
- the question;
- the client’s answer in concise form;
- what changed in the diagnosis;
- the follow-up evidence needed;
- whether the wording is candidate reusable insight.

Do not copy raw PII or private quotes into the general AI OS.

## Handoffs

- Advisory meaning → Advisory Conversation/ProDeck
- Lead or conversion implication → Sales OS
- CRM implementation → Operations
- Reusable anonymised evidence → Knowledge Vault after validation
- Marketing insight → Content/Marketing, never raw private quote by default

## Final rule

A trigger question earns its place only when the answer changes the advice, risk read or next commitment.
