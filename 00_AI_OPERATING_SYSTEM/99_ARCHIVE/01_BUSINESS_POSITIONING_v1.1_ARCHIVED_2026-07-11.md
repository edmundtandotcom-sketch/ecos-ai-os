## 2. ENGINE 1 — CLIENT ADVISORY · The Second Property Ladder™

### The category (LOCKED)
**The Second Property Ladder™** — the step-by-step order Singaporeans use to climb toward two properties, or a higher-value private one, safely, at the right time, without the traps that wipe people out.

**Critical reframe: the ladder is net asset value, not door-count.** Two-name buyers climb by holding two; single-name buyers climb by trading up one at a time (often faster, no 2nd-property ABSD drag). Same ladder, same destination (higher NAV, more options), different vehicle by name-count. **Singles are included.**

**Why "ladder" not "tactic":** 38,000 agents all claim to "guide." What no competitor owns is *the order* — which move first, how to structure it, how to plan the exit before the entry. We own the sequence, not a noun.

### The 5 Steps (mechanism)
1. **Map the Position** → *Position Map* (equity, name-count, structure, loan headroom, policy clocks in numbers).
2. **Set the Direction** → *Direction Statement* (option-value / NAV target, horizon, hold-two vs trade-up vehicle).
3. **Sequence the Moves** → *Move Sequence* — **the order is the product.** Options create paralysis; order creates action.
4. **Research the Exit First** → *Selection Research* (select each asset on exit-strength — transformation, buyer pool, rentability — before entry). *Note: cash-buffer / downside-survivability is not a Step — ruled 2026-07-11; it may inform risk content (see Mechanism v1.4 Appendix A).*
5. **Execute & Repeat** → *Climb Cadence* (review triggers, re-sequence on life/rate/policy change).

### The primary buyer (LOCKED)
Singaporeans who already own (or are about to own) their first private-capable property (high-value HDB, EC, or private) and want a numbers-backed plan to climb — captured at whichever rung they're on. Sub-types run one funnel, differing only by starting rung and structural vehicle: joint-name HDB/EC/private, single-name climbers, first-private buyers, progressing families, DINK couples. Beachhead: joint-name EC/private owners, high-value HDB/EC upgraders, DINK couples.

### Marketing hook vs sales USP (keep separate — this was the #1 past confusion)
- **Hook (gets the click):** the climb — the next move, higher net worth, the missed window. Safety is invisible (nobody clicks "safe").
- **USP (wins trust in the room):** truth-telling, diagnosis, the safe order. *"Sometimes the answer is two; sometimes one stronger asset."*
- **The rule:** market the upside, deliver the safety. Never lead a cold ad with "safe" or "truth."

## 4. Why Two Engines, One House

Both engines share the same physics: a **Ladder** the client climbs, an **advisor** who has already climbed it, and a **sequence** that beats guesswork.

- **Client Advisory:** the client climbs the *property* ladder (their next 3 moves).
- **Agent Edition:** the agent climbs the *income* ladder ($100K → $1M GCI) by installing the same advisory business.

The Second Property Ladder™ is the customer-facing category. The Market Maker Method teaches agents to run a Ladder-style advisory business of their own. **Never blend them in one asset** — different audience, different funnel, different copy.
