## Permanent versus active

Marketing OS stores reusable governance and learnings. `03_ACTIVE_CAMPAIGNS` stores current briefs, working copy, creative, links, launch checklists and campaign-specific results.

Current active production must be discovered in:
`03_ACTIVE_CAMPAIGNS/01_ACTIVE/`

Do not recreate campaign folders or campaign records inside Marketing OS. Prior Marketing OS campaign records are archived history, not current status.

## Lifecycle

1. Marketing OS defines strategy and experiment logic.
2. Active Campaigns executes and holds working files.
3. Approved reusable media is promoted to Asset Library.
4. Reusable production doctrine is promoted to Content Marketing Engine.
5. De-identified reusable research may be promoted to Knowledge Vault.
6. Marketing OS records the cross-campaign learning and decision.

Use links/pointers at boundaries; do not copy files.
