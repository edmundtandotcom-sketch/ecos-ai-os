# Marketing Authority and Boundaries
Version: v1.0
Status: APPROVED
Date: 2026-07-20

Marketing owns:
- funnel and campaign strategy;
- acquisition audience application;
- channel selection and sequencing;
- test hypotheses, offers-in-market and performance decisions;
- Marketing-to-Sales handoff requirements.

Marketing reads, but does not duplicate:
- category/positioning/IP from `02_POSITIONING_AND_IP`;
- production doctrine from `05_CONTENT_MARKETING_ENGINE`;
- research from `06_KNOWLEDGE_VAULT`;
- approved assets from `01_ASSET_LIBRARY`;
- proof registries from `07_BRAND_AND_PROOF_BANK`.

Marketing does not own:
- active campaign working files;
- sales qualification, booking, closing or follow-up;
- GHL implementation;
- client-identifiable evidence.

When authority conflicts, stop and route to Command Center/Decision Memory.
