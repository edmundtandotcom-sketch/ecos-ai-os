The retained templates are planning aids, not independent authorities.

Before using a template:
- replace stale path, platform and campaign references;
- read current Positioning/IP, Active Campaign and relevant channel authority;
- keep working outputs in the active campaign folder;
- keep actual approved media in Asset Library;
- route sales and operational sections to their owning systems;
- remove named client/contact data.

A completed template has the same permanent-home rules as any other file; do not store a second copy here.
