# Positioning, Offer and Audience Usage
Version: v1.0
Status: APPROVED
Date: 2026-07-20

Marketing must apply the current approved positioning and offer architecture; it must not rewrite their masters.

For each campaign, record:
- source authority and version/date;
- audience and situation;
- problem/desired outcome;
- approved mechanism and claim boundaries;
- offer and next step;
- channel adaptation;
- excluded or unverified claims.

If the market suggests a positioning change, log evidence and route a proposal to `02_POSITIONING_AND_IP`. Do not silently change category language inside a campaign.
