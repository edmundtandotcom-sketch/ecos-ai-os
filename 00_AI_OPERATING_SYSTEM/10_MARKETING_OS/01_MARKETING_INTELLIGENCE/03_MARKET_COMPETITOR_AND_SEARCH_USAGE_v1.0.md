# Market, Competitor and Search Usage
Version: v1.0
Status: APPROVED
Date: 2026-07-20

Research authority lives in `06_KNOWLEDGE_VAULT`. Marketing OS stores its application to acquisition decisions.

Every time-sensitive input must include:
- source and retrieval date;
- market/geography;
- freshness requirement;
- whether it is verified fact, interpretation or hypothesis;
- decision affected.

Competitor material is evidence, not copy. Search and platform data may guide channel choices but cannot override approved positioning or privacy boundaries. Move reusable validated research back to Knowledge Vault; keep campaign-specific evidence with the active campaign.
