# Experiment and Learning Register
Version: v1.0
Status: APPROVED TEMPLATE
Date: 2026-07-20

For each experiment record:
- ID, owner and active-campaign link;
- decision to improve;
- audience, channel and offer;
- single primary hypothesis;
- variant/control and protected constants;
- primary metric, guardrails and minimum evidence;
- start/stop dates;
- result and data-quality note;
- decision: adopt, iterate, reject or inconclusive;
- reusable learning and destination.

Do not store working creative or raw person-level data here. A learning must state its limits; inconclusive tests are not failures and must not be promoted as doctrine.
