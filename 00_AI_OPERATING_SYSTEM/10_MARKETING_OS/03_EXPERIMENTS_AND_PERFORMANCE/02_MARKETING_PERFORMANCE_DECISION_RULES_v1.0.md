# Marketing Performance Decision Rules
Version: v1.0
Status: APPROVED GOVERNANCE
Date: 2026-07-20

Before launch, define the decision a metric will trigger. Separate:
- delivery metrics;
- attention/engagement metrics;
- lead/conversion metrics;
- sales progression outcomes;
- revenue/economic outcomes.

Rules:
- name denominator, window and source;
- compare like with like;
- record attribution limits and data latency;
- do not optimize an upstream metric while a downstream guardrail fails;
- do not declare a winner without the pre-agreed evidence threshold;
- route tracking defects to Operations and sales-progression questions to Sales;
- keep named records private.

Marketing decides acquisition action; Operations assures measurement; Sales validates downstream quality.
