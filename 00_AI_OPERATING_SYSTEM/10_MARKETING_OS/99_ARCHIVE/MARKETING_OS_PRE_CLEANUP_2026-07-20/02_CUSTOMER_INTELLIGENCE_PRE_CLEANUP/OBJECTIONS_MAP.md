## What Wins / What Loses (cross-cutting, Appendix A of source)
Wins: numbers-based answers to numbers-based objections · predict-then-reveal comparisons · non-self-serving/cautionary case studies · proactive pre-emption of top 2–3 objections · skin-in-the-game proof · lock a dated next step every session.
Loses: defensiveness when trust is challenged · verbal-only reframes with no worked number · deferring the live concern to "next session" · leaving a contradiction unnamed · resolving spousal disagreement through a proxy instead of hearing them directly.
