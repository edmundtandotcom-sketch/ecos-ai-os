### FR-06 — 5-Stage Cold-Market Attract Model
**Purpose:** Convert cold Agent Edition traffic without chasing — the funnel itself demonstrates the Market Maker product live. **Best use:** the planned Market Maker validation campaign's top-of-funnel (cold Meta/Instagram traffic). **Not for:** Client Advisory (Agent Edition only). **Inputs:** cold ad angle naming the pain (trained to sell, never to build), a free self-diagnostic (Market Maker Scorecard), automated value/proof nurture, an application-gated invite to the 3-Day. **Structure outline:** Stage 0 Position (own the category) → 1 Interrupt (cold ad, self-filtering) → 2 Self-Diagnose (free Scorecard, low friction) → 3 Nurture (automated value/proof) → 4 Invite (application-based 3-Day). The close names the mechanic explicitly: "we never chased you." **Failure mode:** manufacturing scarcity in Stage 4 instead of using a real cohort cap — breaks the model's own proof-by-demonstration logic. **Evidence:** Tier 5, zero live data. **Status:** DRAFT. **Source:** `04_AGENT_EDITION_OS\02_MARKETING\MAS_ColdMarket_ConversionModel_v1.0.md`.

### FR-08 — MAS 16-Section Landing Page Formula
**Purpose:** Fixed, non-negotiable section order for every Agent Edition landing page, from urgency bar to sticky CTA. **Best use:** any Agent Edition landing page build — Market Maker Scorecard opt-in, 3-Day application page, Circle/Boardroom page. **Not for:** Client Advisory landing pages (Agent Edition only, and STALE — see below) without a full re-verification pass. **Inputs:** transformation-promise headline, mechanism subheadline, 3–5 real proof stats, named/numbered case studies, offer stack, price + anchor (revealed last), guarantee, top-5-objection FAQ. **Structure outline:** 1 Urgency Bar → 2 Nav → 3 Hero → 4 Proof Strip → 5 Problem (40% of page) → 6 Agitation → 7 Mechanism → 8 Proof → 9 Who It's For/Not For → 10 Offer Stack → 11 Price+Anchor → 12 Guarantee → 13 Income Gap Visual → 14 FAQ → 15 Final CTA → 16 Sticky Bar. **Failure mode:** revealing price before section 11 — the doc is explicit that value must be established first. **Evidence:** Tier 5. **Status: STALE (C05)** — built on retired 3C/1-Day-Bootcamp branding; use the section skeleton only, re-verify every claim and all copy against the locked Market Maker Method before deploying. **Source:** `04_AGENT_EDITION_OS\02_MARKETING\MAS_MARKETING_INTELLIGENCE_OS_v1.md` §6.
