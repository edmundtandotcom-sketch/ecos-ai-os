### FR-09 — 7-Rung Yes-Ladder Agreement Stack
**Purpose:** Micro-agreement-stacking copy structure that must run through every MAS funnel touchpoint before the reader is asked for a decision. **Best use:** ad copy, landing-page problem/agitation sections, VSL, email sequences — anywhere an offer needs to be earned across several beats rather than pitched cold. **Not for:** short-form Reel hooks (7 rungs don't fit 120 seconds — use FR-01's compressed spine instead and treat the Reel as Rungs 1–3 only). **Inputs:** Identity Mirror line, Problem Ownership reframe (market/agency blame → structure), negative future pace (specific cost of staying), positive future pace (new identity, not just a tactic), specific social proof (never "many agents" — always a named, numbered result), Offer framed as the obvious conclusion, real (not manufactured) urgency detail. **Structure outline:** Rung 1 Identity Mirror → 2 Problem Ownership → 3 Future Pace Negative → 4 Future Pace Positive → 5 Social Proof (specific) → 6 Offer as Obvious Conclusion → 7 Urgency as Truth. **Failure mode:** jumping to Rung 6 (the offer) before Rungs 1–5 are earned — the whole point of the stack is sequence. **Evidence:** Tier 5. **Status: STALE (C05)** — usable skeleton only, re-verify against locked Market Maker Method (retire any 3C/Bootcamp-era proof points before reuse). **Source:** `04_AGENT_EDITION_OS\02_MARKETING\MAS_MARKETING_INTELLIGENCE_OS_v1.md` §2.

### FR-19 — MAS Offer Ladder Structure (Client + Agent)
**Purpose:** The two locked/candidate offer ladders governing every campaign entry point, one per engine. **Best use:** deciding what a new campaign, funnel, or CTA should actually be asking for at each stage. **Not for:** blending the two ladders in one asset — this is a hard rule (brief §2 rule 11), not a style preference.
- **Client Advisory ladder (naming locked by Decision 055):** free content → diagnostic quiz → **Next Move Strategy Session™** *(locked by Decision 055)* → Second Property Ladder Blueprint™ → monitoring. **Two-Property Club™ is PARKED — do not build.**
- **Agent Edition ladder (LOCKED 2026-07-06):** free Market Maker Scorecard → S$297 Preview → S$7,000 Market Maker Live (3-Day) → Circle/Boardroom.
**Failure mode:** using the retired "Position Map session" or "Step" names in a public asset (Decision 055 locked Move / Next Move Strategy Session™), or advancing a lead past the Preview without the 3-Day application gate on the Agent side. **Evidence:** N/A — this is the locked/candidate positioning spine, not a performance claim. **Status:** LOCKED (Agent) / LOCKED naming, CANDIDATE content (Client — Decision 055). **Source:** `00_COMMAND_CENTER\01_BUSINESS_POSITIONING.md`.
