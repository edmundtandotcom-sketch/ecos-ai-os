### FR-07 — 1CC Hook-Story-Offer Ad Scripting Handbook
**Purpose:** Third-party technique handbook teaching Hook → Story → Offer ad scripting through 4 worked examples from Caleb Sim's own ads. **Best use:** structural reference when writing any short-or-medium ad copy — copy the structure, never the voice, stories, or numbers. **Not for:** presenting as REI performance evidence — it is explicitly third-party training material. **Inputs:** hook type (Call-Out or Open Loop), Mirror lines, optional Lie line, Hard Truth, Reframe, named Method, ONE proof type, Offer that mirrors the hook's pain in reverse. **Structure outline:** identical spine to FR-01 (Video), applied here to static/short ad copy rather than filmed video. **Failure mode:** copying his stories/numbers instead of the structure — the source doc explicitly warns against this ("Don't copy his voice. Don't copy his stories. Copy the structure."). **Evidence:** Tier 4, third-party. **Status:** CANDIDATE (converted from .docx, fidelity review pending). **Source:** `06_KNOWLEDGE_VAULT\02_BUYER_PSYCHOLOGY\1CC_TOP_PERFORMING_ADS_BREAKDOWN_v1.0_CANDIDATE.md`.

### FR-18 — MAS Design Intelligence & Visual Hierarchy Rules
**Purpose:** Visual hierarchy, typography, colour system, and mobile-first rules for Agent Edition production assets. **Best use:** landing pages, static ad creative, presentation decks for Agent Edition. **Not for:** Client Advisory creative without a full re-brand pass — the colour system (dark theme, gold/#C9952A gradient) is Agent-Edition-specific production styling, not confirmed Client Advisory brand. **Inputs:** colour tokens, typography scale, mobile-first checklist. **Failure mode:** applying the Agent-Edition dark/gold system to a Client Advisory asset — brand-mixing across engines. **Evidence:** Tier 5. **Status: STALE (C05)** — usable skeleton only. **Source:** `04_AGENT_EDITION_OS\02_MARKETING\MAS_MARKETING_INTELLIGENCE_OS_v1.md` §9.
