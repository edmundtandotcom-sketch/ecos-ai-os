## VSL — Covered
### FR-01 (VSL application) — Hook-Story-Offer, 4–6 Minute Format
**Purpose:** Deeper belief work than a Reel, for warm or retargeting audiences, before a booking ask. **Best use:** Facebook/Instagram feed, YouTube pre-roll, or landing-page embed for a warm/retargeted viewer who has already seen a Reel or shorter asset. **Not for:** cold traffic — 4–6 minutes is too long a commitment before trust is built (use the 120–180s Reel format first). **Inputs:** same spine as the Reel format, stretched: 0–15s Hook/Diagnosis, 15–60s Mirror (45s), 60–90s The Lie (its own block), 90–120s Hard Truth, 120–180s Reframe (most craft goes here), 180–270s Method, 270–330s Proof, 330–360s Offer/CTA. **Structure outline:** same 7-dimension 100-point scoring rubric as the Reel format (Hook Power, Self-Recognition, Retention Flow, Belief Shift, Trust/Authority, Action Pull, Simplicity) — score below 70 = rewrite, not film. **Failure mode:** rushing the Reframe block — the source doc flags this as "most craft goes here," i.e. the highest-leverage 60 seconds of the format. **Evidence:** Tier 5. **Status:** APPROVED MASTER. **Source:** `05_CONTENT_MARKETING_ENGINE\00_FOUNDATIONS\REI_Video_Production_Foundation_v1.1.md` Format 2.
