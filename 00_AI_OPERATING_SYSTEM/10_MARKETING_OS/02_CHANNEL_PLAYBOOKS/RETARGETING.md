## Strategic role

Agent Edition: the STALE MAS skeleton (C05) recommends Angle 2 (PLATEAU) and Angle 4 (IDENTITY/CATEGORY) as "best for retargeting," with scorecard completers as the named custom-audience source — re-verify before reuse.

## Best use cases
Video-view custom audiences (YouTube/Meta reel viewers) re-served a belief-shift or proof asset; scorecard/diagnostic completers who didn't convert; warm landing-page visitors who didn't book.

## Required inputs
Pixel/CRM tracking in place (sits with the Operations Desk, out of this desk's scope per the Marketing & Ads Desk SOP); a named source audience (video-view %, page visit, form-start-no-submit); a retargeting-specific creative distinct from the cold-traffic ad.

## Process
Define audience window (e.g. 25/50/75% video viewers, 30/60/90-day site visitors) → select or produce a proof-forward retargeting creative, not a repeat of the cold-traffic hook → verify current custom-audience platform specs → launch → track distinctly from cold-traffic KPIs (retargeting should show lower CPL, higher CTR than cold).

## Outputs
Audience definitions, retargeting-specific ad copy/creative — saved alongside cold-traffic assets in the campaign folder, tagged distinctly.

## KPIs
Retargeting CPL vs cold CPL (should be lower), CTR, frequency (capped to avoid fatigue), view-to-lead rate. Baseline: the one named historical retargeting campaign sits inside the blended Tier-2 $153.62 CPL aggregate (brief §5) with **no retargeting-only breakout** — cite as directional evidence only, not a retargeting-specific baseline.

## Common failure modes
Serving the identical cold-traffic ad to a warm audience (no incremental reason to convert now); no frequency cap (ad fatigue); an audience window too narrow to sustain spend or too wide to still be "warm."
