## 3. Sky Vue (Bishan) 3-bedder — investment read

General market comparison from public listing and transaction data. **Not personalised
financial advice**, and it was framed that way at the time: any decision needs checking
against financing, holding period and ABSD or loan exposure.

**Sky Vue snapshot** — 99-year leasehold from 2013, TOP 2016/17, 694 units in two
37-storey towers, 3 minutes' walk to Bishan MRT (NS/CC interchange).

| Project | Lease from | 3-bed PSF range | Avg PSF |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sky Vue | 2013 | ~$2,070–2,279 | ~$2,190 |
| Sky Habitat (across the road) | same era | ~$1,856–2,368 | ~$2,095 |
| The Gardens at Bishan | 1997 | ~$1,257–1,890 | ~$1,744 |

- Sky Vue rental yield ~3.0–3.4%, with 97 rental transactions in six months — brisk,
  so vacancy risk between tenants is low.
- **Sky Habitat is the real head-to-head comp**: same developer, same launch era, same
  MRT-adjacent location. Moshe Safdie architecture gives it a more distinctive resale
  story but a wider price scatter. Sant Ritz and Skyline Residences were dropped from
  the comparison on checking — they are in Toa Payoh and Telok Blangah, not Bishan.
- **Gardens at Bishan is ~$450 psf cheaper** but its lease starts 16 years earlier,
  which matters for financing and CPF usage as lease decay sets in.

**Layout faults found in the actual developer floor plans** (types Cb2, 1,141 sqft,
about 20 of the 24 three-bed units; and Ca1(P), 1,259 sqft premium):

1. BR2 and BR3 share **one balcony** while the master gets its own — the two common
   bedrooms don't function independently.
2. The mandatory household shelter sits at the kitchen entrance beside the yard,
   congesting that corner and making the kitchen zone tighter than the floor area suggests.
3. The utility room is **windowless** — no natural ventilation, a real minus if a
   helper actually lives there.
4. Common Bath 2 is also internal, on mechanical exhaust only.
5. The Cb2 kitchen is small and fully enclosed; one small yard opening ventilates the
   entire back cluster of four rooms.
6. Ca1(P) circulation routes past the common bath to reach the kitchen cluster.

**What is *not* a fault, and is worth saying on camera:** Sky Vue launched in 2013,
after URA's October 2012 rule counted balconies and bay windows into GFA. Its
living and dining areas are regular and fully usable — no paid-for bay windows or
planter boxes, unlike 1990s stock such as Gardens at Bishan.

**Reusable method here:** pull the developer floor plan, not the marketing copy, and
check the ventilation and circulation of the back-of-house cluster. That is where
layout waste hides, and it is invisible in a PSF comparison.

## 4. Realsee 360 tour → MP4 moving tour

**The problem:** turning a manual 360 walkthrough into an auto-running video tour.

**What was established:**

- The `my.realsee.ai/capture-3d/...` link is the private creator/management URL. It
  redirects to a login page and needs Realsee account credentials. Signing in on
  Edmund's behalf was declined, so steps were researched for him to do himself.
- **Realsee's own video export is a paid add-on**, documented under
  *Download Space Video from Add-ons* in their help centre, alongside add-on downloads
  for panorama, floor plan, point cloud, model, raw file and CAD.

**What was actually used:** a screen recording of the tour
(`Recording 2026-10-05 110949.mp4`), delivered at **16:9**.

**Takeaway for next time:** if a 360 tour needs to become a video and the add-on isn't
being bought, screen-record the walkthrough. Decide the aspect ratio up front — 16:9
for long-form, 9:16 if it is going to reels, per the standing social video format rule.
