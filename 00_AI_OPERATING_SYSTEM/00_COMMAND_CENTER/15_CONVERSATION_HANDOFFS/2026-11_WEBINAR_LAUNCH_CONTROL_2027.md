## 2. The launch mechanics everything is built on

Edmund supplied this as the fixed shape of **any** Singapore new launch. All
scheduling works backwards from it:

| Day | What happens |
|---|---|
| Launch window | Two full weekends (e.g. 15–26 Jan) |
| **Tuesday** | Cheque submission cut-off |
| Wednesday | Tabulation of cheques collected |
| Thursday | Balloting — clients learn their queue position |
| Friday | VIP, developer clients, bulk purchases |
| Saturday | Public |

**The hard rule:** anything that is meant to feed a launch must land **before the
Tuesday cut-off**. Friday is too late. Pre-warm video can still go out after the
cut-off, but no webinar is run once cheques have closed.
