## 2. The script

Current version: `ONE_CHUAN_GROVE_SCRIPT_v2.md`, running about 19 minutes.

**Hook:** "5 checks almost nobody does."

The v1 hook built on seven competitor videos giving five different verdicts. **That
was removed in v2** — no other creator is quoted or referenced on camera anywhere.
The word "gate" was also dropped in favour of plain questions.

**The five checks**, each framed as the usual way versus what Edmund does instead,
closing with a self-check the viewer can do that week:

1. What's coming next door
2. What's actually proven
3. What's a fair price
4. What's my stop number
5. Who buys from me

Weighting and scoring stay in the consult — they are not taught on camera. Each
section is tagged news, problem or solution. Intro lands at 0:45. Six micro CTAs plus
two main ones, all using the keyword **CHUAN**, by comment or WhatsApp.

**The underlying argument:** a good property can still be the wrong property for your
plan. The missing question is "what is this property's job in your plan?"

## 3. What the competitor review found

Gaps the script is built to exploit, from seven videos on the project:

- **Supply:** they count units but never time them against the viewer's exit year.
- **Fair price:** they estimate what the developer will charge, not what the viewer's
  future exit buyer can afford.
- **Walk-away:** none of the seven mentions a walk-away number.
- **Figures contradict each other.** Chuan Park's land cost is quoted at about
  1,058 psf ppr by one creator and about 1,256 by another. That conflict is unresolved.
