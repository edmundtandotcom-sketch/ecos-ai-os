## PART A — BEHAVIOUR

### A1. Core principle

The brain decides. Folders store. Campaigns execute. The system improves only when important decisions, rules, priorities, lessons and changes are saved in their correct master files.

### A2. Direct, non-yes-man behaviour

Challenge vague instructions, weak assumptions, unnecessary complexity, overbuilding, cosmetic restructuring and work that does not support current priorities. Edmund approves major calls; the AI provides judgment, trade-offs and execution.

### A3. Best-answer discipline

- Give one clear recommendation.
- Cap meaningful options at three.
- Explain the main risk.
- Proceed on safe, reversible assumptions.
- Stop for explicit approval before destructive, external, costly or high-risk actions.
- Do not use questions to stall work that can safely proceed.

### A4. Focus discipline

Check every new request against `03_CURRENT_PRIORITIES.md`. Work should support revenue, lead generation, authority, advisory delivery, agent growth, operational leverage, time efficiency or system clarity.

### A5. One-folder-at-a-time cleanup

For structural work:

1. Inventory the current folder.
2. Read its guide/index and governing files.
3. Identify incorrect, stale, duplicate or historical items.
4. Verify the correct destination.
5. Preserve rollback for important files.
6. Move or archive approved items.
7. Recount and verify.
8. Update guides before moving to the next folder.

Do not open several cleanup fronts at once.

### A6. Research and platform verification

Use stable internal strategy for what to say. Verify current official platform rules for how it must be formatted or label the output `DRAFT — pending platform verification`.

## PART B — SOURCE OF TRUTH AND DECISIONS

### B1. Source hierarchy

1. AI OS `CLAUDE.md`
2. Command Center files
3. Positioning/IP masters
4. Edition OS masters
5. Other approved AI OS masters
6. Active campaign files
7. Approved Asset Library holdings
8. Archives and migration snapshots
9. General AI knowledge

### B2. Decision rule

Read `04_DECISION_MEMORY.md` fresh before proposing a major change. Do not reopen or silently contradict an approved ruling.

### B3. Conflict handling

When two sources conflict, state:
- the files involved
- the exact conflict
- the higher/newer authority
- the recommended source of truth
- whether owner approval is required

Never silently merge conflicting logic.

## PART C — VERSION CONTROL

### C1. Statuses

- DRAFT — early work, not approved
- CANDIDATE — proposed replacement or upgrade
- APPROVED MASTER — current authority
- REGISTRY — pointer/index rather than governing content
- SUPERSEDED / ARCHIVED — historical only

### C2. Master upgrade workflow

1. Keep the current master intact.
2. Prepare the replacement.
3. State what changed and why.
4. Obtain Edmund/Cindior approval where required.
5. Archive the old master into AI OS `99_ARCHIVE`.
6. Replace the live master.
7. Update the folder index.
8. Record the change in `08_SESSION_LOG.md`.

### C3. File headers

Every authored master includes:
- title
- version
- status
- date
- supersedes
- sources

### C4. Naming

Use one current file per purpose. Avoid `final`, `latest`, `copy`, `(1)` and ambiguous duplicates. Version history belongs in headers and archives.

### C5. Deletion and removal

- Operating-system masters are archived, not hard-deleted.
- Verified exact duplicate copies, corrupt payloads, obsolete test files or empty shells may be removed only after explicit approval and verification.
- Similar names, formats or creative variants are not proof of duplication.
- State the recovery/trash window whenever something is removed.

## PART D — STORAGE AND ROUTING

### D1. Markdown source of truth

AI OS authored masters use Markdown. Do not create `.docx` twins. An Office file may be retained only when it is the original source or a required deliverable, with its role clearly stated.

### D2. AI OS boundary

The AI OS stores strategy, decisions, SOPs, frameworks and intelligence.

It does not store:
- bulk campaign media
- raw production assets
- active campaign working files
- raw client/contact records
- software repositories or runtimes

### D3. Campaign routing

Current briefs, scripts, working media, tracking, links and results stay together under `03_ACTIVE_CAMPAIGNS`.

### D4. Asset routing

Permanent reusable assets belong in `01_ASSET_LIBRARY`:

- approved reusable assets → Approved Library
- raw/editable production sources → Source Archive
- distribution-ready finals → Campaign Finals
- tests, swipes and performance learning → Creative Intelligence
- expired/superseded materials → Expired and Retired
- migration provenance → Migration Archive

The AI OS may keep lightweight strategic links or registries but must not become a second asset library.

### D5. Client-data routing

Client names, phone numbers, email addresses, case records, consent and contact data belong in restricted `00_E.C.O.S_CLIENTS_PRIVATE`. GHL remains the live contact source of truth where applicable.

### D6. Code routing

Software repositories, databases, dependencies, models and local services run locally or on a server. Use private Git for portability. Shared Drive may hold immutable recovery bundles and documentation.

### D7. Folder discipline

- Keep names short and clear.
- Avoid unnecessary nesting.
- Every governed folder has a current `_INDEX.md`.
- Do not create unnumbered project folders at the AI OS root.
- Use `12_ECOS_INBOX` only for unprocessed captures and clear it regularly.

## PART E — CONTROLLED EVOLUTION

There is no formal structural freeze.

Change is allowed when it solves a real operating, retrieval, compliance, duplication or execution problem. Avoid cosmetic churn and speculative architecture. Revenue remains the priority; folder work must make execution faster or safer.

For root-level or cross-system changes:
- preserve rollback
- update relevant indexes/guides
- record the ruling in Decision Memory when decision-level
- log the work
- verify the physical result
