This is the root map for the AI Operating System. Read `CLAUDE.md` first every substantial session. For detailed retrieval and current priorities, continue to `00_COMMAND_CENTER/00_MASTER_INDEX.md`, `03_CURRENT_PRIORITIES.md`, and `04_DECISION_MEMORY.md`.

## Routing Boundaries

- Active campaign briefs, scripts, working media, links and results → `03_ACTIVE_CAMPAIGNS`
- Approved reusable assets and raw/editable production sources → `01_ASSET_LIBRARY`
- Client/contact PII → `00_E.C.O.S_CLIENTS_PRIVATE`
- Software runtime and repositories → local runtime/private Git; recovery packages may live in `_BACKUPS`
- Uncertain or deletion-candidate items → relevant review or quarantine folder

Do not create unnumbered project folders at the AI OS root.
