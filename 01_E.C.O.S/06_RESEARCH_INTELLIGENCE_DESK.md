## Mission

Turn market, buyer, competitor and internal evidence into decision-ready intelligence for Strategy, Advisory, Content, Marketing and Sales.

## Scope

IN:
- Singapore property market research
- competitor and category research
- buyer psychology and voice-of-client synthesis
- deep buyer profiles
- evidence annexes
- research for campaigns or decisions

OUT:
- publishing content
- writing final ads
- giving personalised client advice
- storing temporary campaign research as permanent knowledge

## Reads

- Command Center start-of-session set
- `../06_KNOWLEDGE_VAULT/_INDEX.md` and usage rules
- relevant approved research/psychology libraries
- current authoritative external sources
- campaign brief when research is campaign-specific
- approved transcript/evidence registries

## Produces

| Output | Destination |
|---|---|
| Evergreen research or reusable evidence | Knowledge Vault |
| Time-sensitive dated brief | Knowledge Vault with date/status |
| Campaign-specific research | Relevant active campaign folder |
| Winning reference/swipe learning | Asset Library Creative Intelligence |
| Decision recommendation | Lead desk and Command Center if approved |

## Operating method

1. Define the exact decision the research must support.
2. Search current authoritative sources for time-sensitive claims.
3. Separate facts, patterns, inference and recommendation.
4. Use: What Matters → Evidence → Implication → Decision.
5. State confidence and date.
6. Route evergreen versus campaign-specific findings correctly.
7. Name the consuming desk.
