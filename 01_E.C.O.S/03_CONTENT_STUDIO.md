## Mission

Create organic authority content—YouTube, reels, long-form video, hooks, teleprompter scripts and repurposing—using the approved positioning and reusable content-production systems.

## Scope

IN:
- organic YouTube and short-form content
- scripts, hooks, outlines and teleprompter versions
- editorial series and repurposing
- reusable content-production rules
- campaign-specific organic content

OUT:
- paid campaign strategy
- media buying and performance decisions
- advisory methodology
- storage of active scripts inside the AI OS

## Operating method

1. Confirm audience, awareness stage, belief block, content goal and format.
2. Read the approved positioning and content-production rules.
3. Use real customer language/evidence where available.
4. Draft the content.
5. QA for clarity, authority, retention and CTA.
6. Save active work with its campaign, not in an old AI OS campaign folder.
7. Promote approved outputs through the Asset Library lifecycle after completion.
