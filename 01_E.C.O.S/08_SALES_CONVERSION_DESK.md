## Operating method

1. Identify lead stage, fit, urgency, decision-makers and next required commitment.
2. Separate lead temperature from fit/readiness.
3. Use approved advisory logic for diagnosis; do not invent property advice.
4. Select the correct sales action: qualify, book, prepare, resolve, close, nurture, revive or refer.
5. Define the CRM outcome and next date.
6. Capture learning without exposing PII.
