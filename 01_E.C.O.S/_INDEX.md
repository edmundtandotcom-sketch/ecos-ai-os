## Purpose

`01_E.C.O.S` is the specialist routing layer for the business.

A desk is a functional responsibility, not a separate storage system and not necessarily a separate chat. The router selects the best desk, loads only the relevant sources, delegates execution to the suitable builder, and returns one integrated answer.

## Orchestration rule

Required working header:

> you are the orchestrator, delegated the building to sonnet 5, opus 4.8, and never spawn another fable 5 agent. you plan, you delegate to cheaper model and you do the QA at the end. /compact

Fable 5 coordinates. Sonnet 5 is the default builder. Opus 4.8 is reserved for high-stakes or unusually complex work. Fable 5 never spawns another Fable 5 agent and does not do the heavy build itself.

## Routing boundaries

- Strategy and governance → Strategy Office
- Property diagnosis and advisory method → Client Advisory
- Organic content → Content Studio
- Paid acquisition and funnel strategy → Marketing & Ads
- Agent Edition → Agent Edition Desk
- Research and evidence → Research & Intelligence
- CRM, automation and technical operations → Operations
- Lead handling, sales conversion and follow-up → Sales & Conversion

## Storage boundaries

- Reusable strategy, SOPs and frameworks → relevant AI OS branch
- Active campaign production → `H:\Shared drives\00_E.C.O.S\03_ACTIVE_CAMPAIGNS`
- Approved assets and source files → `H:\Shared drives\00_E.C.O.S\01_ASSET_LIBRARY`
- Client/contact PII → restricted `00_E.C.O.S_CLIENTS_PRIVATE` or GHL
- Software runtime/repositories → local runtime or private Git

No desk may create a second storage home for the same fact or asset.

## Handoffs

Desks hand off through a clear output and destination, not by duplicating entire files.

- Research feeds Strategy, Advisory, Content, Marketing and Sales.
- Marketing hands qualified demand and campaign context to Sales.
- Advisory supplies the diagnostic/advisory method used inside consultations.
- Sales runs the lead-to-client process and hands operational requirements to Operations.
- Operations implements and maintains the plumbing.
- Approved campaign outputs are promoted to the Asset Library after completion.
