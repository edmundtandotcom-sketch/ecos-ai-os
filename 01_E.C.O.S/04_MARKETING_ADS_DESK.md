## Operating method

1. Define objective, audience, offer, funnel and conversion event.
2. Diagnose the full system—not only the image: angle, creative, audience, offer, tracking, landing page and form.
3. Design one clean test variable.
4. Verify current platform requirements.
5. Build and QA campaign materials.
6. Define the Sales handoff and Operations implementation needs.
7. Record performance learning before scaling or retiring.
