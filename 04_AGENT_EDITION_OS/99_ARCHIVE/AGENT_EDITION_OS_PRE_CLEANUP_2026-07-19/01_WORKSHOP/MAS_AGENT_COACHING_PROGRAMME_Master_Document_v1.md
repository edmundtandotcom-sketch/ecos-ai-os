### Three Core Pillars (Original Version — now updated in Part B)

**Pillar 1: IDENTITY**
*(Note: This was the original framing. As confirmed in Session 2, this has been renamed BUSINESS ARCHITECTURE and the approach is activity-based, not mindset lecture. The original framing below is kept for reference only.)*

The fundamental shift from agent thinking to business owner thinking. Most agents have never audited their business, defined their client, or thought about their income as a system. This pillar creates that shift through structured exercises and a new frame for how they see their work.

Original concept: "You are not a salesperson who works in property. You are an advisory business. You have clients, not customers. You deliver outcomes, not transactions."

**Pillar 2: ADVISORY METHOD**
The advisory conversation system. How to move from showing properties to diagnosing client situations and prescribing multi-move strategies. The REI Method (R→E→I) is the core teachable framework:
- R — Review Real Position: current holdings, equity, liabilities, financial standing
- E — Evaluate Market Rewards: timing, asset class opportunity, capital growth potential
- I — Identify Next 2–3 Moves: recommended sequence of property moves

Applied in the two-conversation advisory structure:
- Conversation 1: Discovery (diagnostic questions, 12-Level Intelligence Capture)
- Conversation 2: Strategy Session (recommendation presentation, not a pitch)

**Pillar 3: SCALE ENGINE**
The systems layer. CRM (GHL), automated follow-up sequences, content strategy, referral loops, AI tools for efficiency. The difference between an agent who earns $200K and one who earns $600K is not work ethic — it is the presence or absence of a system that generates and converts leads without requiring constant manual input.

### The Live vs Webinar Decision

**Recommendation confirmed:** Run the first event as a paid live online masterclass. Not a webinar, not a physical event for the first run.

Why:
- Webinar = commodity framing in Singapore. No premium signal.
- Physical event = expensive to test. No iteration room.
- Paid live online = commitment filter ($97) + no venue cost + full iteration capability

**Do not call it a webinar.** Call it "MAS Live: 1-Day Online Implementation Session" or "MAS Live: The Architecture Revealed." Cap at 200 seats. Run on Zoom.

Once the programme is proven (after 2 runs), move the preview to a physical venue. The live event format closes more enrollment and commands higher prices — but build the content and pitch in the lower-risk format first.

### Pillar 1 Redesign — Business Architecture

Remove: all mindset and identity language from the programme.

The identity shift happens through three activities — not through being told:

**Activity 1 — The Business Audit**
Pre-work submitted before Day 1. Agents track: transactions in last 12 months, average commission per deal, total hours worked per deal from first contact to close, where each deal came from. Most agents who think they earned $200K discover their real hourly rate is $30–$80 once time is factored. Edmund does not say they are thinking like agents. The number says it.

**Activity 2 — The Client Sort**
Agents categorize every client in the last 12 months: high-value vs time-wasting, referral vs cold, easy vs difficult. The pattern reveals 20% of clients produced 80% of income and required 20% of the time. They see for themselves that they have been running a reactive service, not a structured advisory business.

**Activity 3 — The Business Owner's Revenue Map**
They calculate what $500K actually requires: average deal size, number of transactions needed, number of leads each transaction requires, length of pipeline. Most have never done this calculation. When they see the math, the shift from "I need to work harder" to "I need a system" happens without Edmund saying a word.

This is how Pillar 1 works. Activity → realisation. Never lecture → conclusion.

### The "Teach Everything" Decision — Resolved

Edmund's fear: teaching the REI Method fully turns agents into competitors for his clients.

The resolution:

Edmund's clients are condo-owning families building multi-generational wealth through portfolio sequencing at the highest advisory level. Agents who go through the MAS programme serve a different market — new launches, resale buyers, upgraders, first-time HDB buyers at different income levels and decision stages. Even if they learn the same framework, they are not calling on the same clients.

The framework is learnable. The judgment cannot be copied. Edmund can teach every question in the discovery process. No one who attends will execute it as well as Edmund does because doing it well requires 17 years of calibration. Attending a 3-day workshop accelerates their competence — it does not replicate Edmund.

**The practical boundary:**

| Framework and process | Current market intelligence |
| How to run the Advisory Method | Edmund's live read on which plays to make right now |
| Templates and scripts | Edmund working through the member's specific business |
| System structure | Done-with-you execution support |

Teach the framework completely. The Inner Circle is where Edmund stays in their business. That distinction is the moat.

### The Teaching vs Selling Formula

Edmund's pattern: falls into teaching mode, gives too much value, forgets to sell.

The fix is structural. Every teaching block ends with a visible gap. The gap is what the 3-day closes.

**The mechanism (used after every teaching block):**
> "Here is the framework. [TEACH IT FULLY]. Now — the challenge is that knowing this and having it running in your business are two different things. The knowing happens today. The running happens in 3 days."

Edmund never has to hold anything back. He just needs to show the distance between "knowing this" and "having this working in your business."

**The pre-frame technique (use at the start of every block):**
> "I am going to give you this completely. Use it this week if you want to. The reason you will come back for more is not that I held something back — it is because applying this without support is harder than it looks. I will show you why by the end of this section."

**Seeding language to use throughout Day 1:**
- "This is the structure. In 3 days, you build your version of it."
- "Today you see the framework. In the implementation workshop, you install it."
- "What we are doing right now is giving you the map. The workshop is driving the route."
- "I'm NOT going to cover [X] today — that is a 4-hour session in Day 2. But I will show you what the outcome looks like when it is running."

### Marketing and Ads in the Programme

Marketing and ads are NOT a pillar. They are a consequence of positioning.

An agent who runs ads without a clear advisory positioning amplifies a broken signal. Edmund does not teach ads in the open programme because:
1. It changes the audience (attracts agents who want a marketing course, not a business transformation)
2. Ads change constantly — teaching tactics makes the programme time-dated
3. Positioning must come first. Ads are a Pillar 3 amplification tool, not a standalone skill

Where ads appear in the programme:
- Day 3 Content Brief Builder addresses what to say, to whom, and why — this is marketing through the lens of positioning, not media buying
- The Referral Loop covers organic prospecting systematically

Deeper ads work — video ads, image ads, AI-powered ad creation — is Inner Circle content only.

To agents who ask about ads: *"Your ads will tell you when your positioning is wrong because they will produce bad leads. Fix the positioning first. Once you are clear on who you serve and what you promise, the ads become the amplifier — not the solution."*
