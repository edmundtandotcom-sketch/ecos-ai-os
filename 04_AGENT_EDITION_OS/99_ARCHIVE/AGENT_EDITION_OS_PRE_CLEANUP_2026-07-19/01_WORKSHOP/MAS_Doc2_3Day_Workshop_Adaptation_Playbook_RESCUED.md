### 9:00am — Registration & Room Warm-Up

**Borrow from: Tom Ferry + Russell Brunson**

**ADOPT — The Physical Welcome Kit (Brunson/Ferry)**
Every participant receives a physical kit when they sit down:

*Why it works: Brunson ships physical kits for his OFA Challenge specifically because physical objects create psychological investment and reduce no-shows. Ferry's Summit Workbook is famous precisely because agents use it for months post-event. Your kit signals "this is premium."*

**ADAPT — Room Energy Setup (Tony Robbins/Tom Ferry)**
- Play curated music during registration (not generic background music — build to it)
- Have 1 facilitator per table of 8 (not just stage presence) to start conversations
- Table name cards that show their current GCI tier (pre-filled from application form) — this creates immediate peer context
- Opening question on screen: "What year do you think you'll cross $1M GCI?" — prime the question before Edmund answers it

*Reject the Robbins fire-walking and jumping — too theatrical for Singapore professional audience.*

### 9:20am — The Dated Million (Edmund's Signature Opening)

**Borrow from: Alex Hormozi + Dan Kennedy**

**ADOPT — Financial Transparency (Hormozi)**
The Dated Million works precisely because it uses real math, not inspiration. Hormozi's key principle: "Show the numbers. Don't ask them to believe, show them how to calculate."

Upgrade: build a live calculator on screen (simple Google Sheet or app) where Edmund inputs assumptions live:
- Current GCI: _____
- Current # of transactions: _____
- Average commission per transaction: _____
- "At this rate, you cross $1M GCI in the year: **[CALCULATED LIVE]**"

Then show the 3 levers that move the date:
1. More deals (volume)
2. Higher-value deals (positioning)
3. Better conversion (conversation)

"We're going to move your date. By end of Day 3, you'll recalculate. Most agents move it 5–8 years closer in 3 days."

**ADOPT — Specificity Creates Credibility (Kennedy)**
Kennedy's rule: "The more specifically you describe the problem, the more they trust you know the solution."

During the opening, Edmund should name 5 specific agent profiles:
- "The transactional agent making $60K, stuck because every deal starts from zero"
- "The relationship agent at $150K, too busy to systemise"
- "The $300K agent who can't break to $500K because they haven't positioned"

Let agents self-identify. This is the emotional hook that proves Edmund understands their exact situation.

**ADAPT — The Identity Frame (Dan Lok)**
Dan Lok doesn't sell courses — he sells identity: "You are a High Ticket Closer."

MAS parallel: "By end of Day 3, you are not a property agent. You are a Property Wealth Architect."

The language shift matters. It changes how they introduce themselves at parties. It changes who they think they can approach. It changes their pricing.

*Singapore caveat: don't overdo the motivational identity talk. Singapore professionals respond to proof and logic more than emotional activation. The identity shift must be grounded in the math (the Dated Million) not in chanting.*

### 10:30am — Agent Story + Social Proof Block

**Borrow from: Tom Ferry + Jordan Belfort**

**ADOPT — Agent Case Study on Stage (Ferry)**
Tom Ferry's summit always features 2–3 real agents telling their before/after story. This is NOT a testimonial video. It is a live interview, on stage, with Edmund asking specific questions:

Suggested format (12 minutes per agent story):
1. "What was your GCI when you started MAS?" (2 min)
2. "What specifically did you change?" (4 min)
3. "What is your GCI now, and what's your Dated Million?" (3 min)
4. "What would you tell the person in this room who's where you were?" (3 min)

Run 2 of these before lunch. This does more selling than any pitch.

**ADAPT — The 3 Tens Framework (Belfort → Singapore Property)**
Belfort's insight: buyers must reach 10/10 certainty on Product, Company, and Salesperson before buying.

Teach agents this framework for THEIR clients:
- 10/10 on the Property: "Does this unit genuinely fit what they need?"
- 10/10 on the Market: "Do they trust that now is the right time?"
- 10/10 on the Agent: "Do they believe this agent has their interests at heart?"

Workshop this before lunch: "Which of the 3 Tens do you lose clients on most often?" (show of hands, then table discussion)

This creates both self-awareness AND desire for the solution you teach in the afternoon.

## Afternoon Block: "Position" (2:00pm–6:00pm)

### 2:00pm — The Client X-Ray Workshop

**Borrow from: Dan Kennedy + Alex Hormozi**

**ADOPT — The Ideal Client Avatar (Kennedy)**
Kennedy's most powerful teaching: if you can describe your ideal client's problem more specifically than they can, they believe you're the only one who can solve it.

Client X-Ray Workshop (45 minutes):
Agents answer 12 questions about their best-ever client:
1. Age range
2. Life stage (first-timer, upgrader, investor, downgrader)
3. Core fear (losing money, missing the window, making the wrong choice)
4. Core dream (financial freedom, kids' education, retirement security)
5. What they searched online before calling you
6. What objections they raised
7. What finally made them commit
8. Where they came from (referral, portal, social media)
9. How long the sales cycle was
10. What they said when they referred you to someone else
11. What makes them different from your worst clients
12. What would make 10 more of them find you?

Agents share answers at tables. Tables identify patterns. Patterns become their positioning statement.

**ADOPT — The Value Equation as Positioning Tool (Hormozi)**
After Client X-Ray, introduce the Value Equation:

"Value = (Dream Outcome × Likelihood of Success) ÷ (Time Delay + Effort)"

Each agent fills in the equation FROM THEIR BEST CLIENT'S PERSPECTIVE:
- What was their Dream Outcome? (Specific: "sell in 30 days at $X price")
- What made them believe you'd deliver? (Specific: "past sales in same block")
- How long did it actually take? (Specific data point)
- How much effort did they need to put in? (Easy = better)

"Your marketing message should answer all 4. If it doesn't, leads won't convert."

### 3:30pm — Positioning Statement Workshop

**Borrow from: Dan Kennedy + Dan Lok**

**ADAPT — Magnetic Marketing = Specialist Positioning (Kennedy)**
Kennedy's core: stop being a generalist. The generalist competes on price. The specialist commands premium.

MAS Positioning Workshop (60 minutes):
Each agent drafts a positioning statement using the MAS Formula:

**"I help [SPECIFIC CLIENT] achieve [SPECIFIC OUTCOME] in [SPECIFIC TIMEFRAME] without [SPECIFIC FEAR]"**

Examples:
- "I help HDB upgraders move to their first private property in 90 days without overpaying or making a decision they'll regret."
- "I help Singapore PR couples navigate their first resale HDB purchase without missing the 5-year wait timeline."
- "I help property investors add a second income-generating unit to their portfolio without tying up too much cash."

Then: every agent stands up and says their positioning statement to the room. Group feedback.

**ADOPT — Identity-Based Framing (Dan Lok)**
After the positioning statement, Edmund reframes:
"You're not a property agent who specialises in X. You are THE [niche] specialist in Singapore."

- "THE HDB Upgrader Specialist"
- "THE Lentor Corridor Expert"
- "THE PR First-Home Guide"

This language shift from "specialises in" to "the specialist" is subtle but powerful.

### 9:30am — Script Drilling Block

**Borrow from: Tom Hopkins + Jordan Belfort**

**ADOPT — Named Closes + Role Play (Hopkins)**
Tom Hopkins names every close. Names make closes memorable. Create MAS-specific named closes:

1. **The Dated Close** — "If you don't move by [specific date], here's what happens to your options..."
2. **The Math Close** — "Let me show you what this costs you per month vs. renting..."
3. **The Regret Close** — "Of all the clients who hesitated, here's what they said 6 months later..."
4. **The Legacy Close** — "This isn't just a property decision. This is about what you leave your children."
5. **The Permission Close** — "Would it be okay if I showed you exactly why most people in your situation make this move?"

Format: Edmund demonstrates each close live. Then pairs role-play. Then Edmund debriefs the room.
Time: 25 min per close, 5 closes = ~2 hours with debrief. This is the highest-ROI block in the 3 days.

**ADOPT — Tonality Drills (Belfort)**
This is often the missing piece in Singapore property training. 30-minute tonality workshop:

- Certainty tone drill: "This property is going to move fast" (say it flat, then with conviction)
- Scarcity tone drill: "I can't guarantee this price will hold" (say it generic, then with genuine concern)
- Empathy tone drill: "I understand why you're hesitant" (clinical vs. warm)

Pairs drill each other. Game: can your partner guess what tone you're using? 

*Singapore adaptation: tone drills feel slightly awkward in Singapore culture initially. Frame it as "professional communication skills" not "persuasion tactics." Same content, different label.*

---

### 11:00am — Objection Handling Marathon

**Borrow from: Jordan Belfort + Tom Hopkins**

**ADOPT — The Looping Technique (Belfort)**
Belfort's most powerful tool: never fight an objection. Instead, "loop" — acknowledge, return to certainty, re-close.

Pattern:
1. Hear objection ("I need to think about it")
2. Acknowledge without fighting ("That makes sense, I understand")
3. Return to certainty ("Let me ask you — when you imagine having made this decision 6 months from now, what does that feel like?")
4. Re-close ("Based on what you've told me, does this feel like the right move?")

MAS Singapore top objections:
- "I need to consult my family first"
- "I'll wait for the market to drop"
- "The stamp duty is too much"
- "I'm not sure this agent knows my estate well"
- "Let me check other agents first"

Workshop: 45 min drilling these specific 5 objections using the Loop technique.

## Afternoon Block: "The Conversation Continues" (2:00pm–6:00pm)

### 2:00pm — The Grand Slam Listing/Buyer Presentation

**Borrow from: Alex Hormozi + Russell Brunson**

**ADOPT — Building the Grand Slam Offer (Hormozi → Applied to Agent Presentations)**
Just as Hormozi teaches businesses to build irresistible offers, agents need to build irresistible listing/buyer presentations.

MAS Grand Slam Listing Presentation Workshop:

**STEP 1 — Dream Outcome**
"What does your seller actually want?" (Not to sell the unit — to maximise price, minimise hassle, move on with their life)

**STEP 2 — Perceived Likelihood**
What proof do you show? (Past sales data in the same block, testimonials from same estate, professional marketing samples)

**STEP 3 — Reduce Time Delay**
"Here's my proven timeline — average 28 days to offer for units I represent in this area."

**STEP 4 — Reduce Effort**
"Here's what I handle for you completely, so you don't need to do anything but sign."

**STEP 5 — Stack it**
Professional photography, 3D tour, Facebook/Instagram campaign, PropertyGuru feature, SMS blast to buyer list, open house management, offer coordination.

"Stack" these visually on a printed sheet in the Millionaire Map Workbook.

### 3:30pm — The Perfect Advisory Conversation (based on Brunson's Perfect Webinar)

**ADAPT — Perfect Webinar Formula → Perfect Advisory Conversation (Brunson)**
Brunson's Perfect Webinar: Big Domino → 3 Secrets → The Stack → Close

MAS Perfect Advisory Conversation:
1. **The One Thing** — "The reason most people make the wrong property decision is [X]. Once you understand this, everything else becomes clear."
2. **3 Myths Busted** — 3 common beliefs that hold buyers/sellers back (each is a mini-teaching moment)
   - Myth: "Wait for prices to drop" → "Here's what actually happens to people who wait..."
   - Myth: "All agents are the same" → "Here's the 5 questions that reveal the difference..."
   - Myth: "DIY saves money" → "Here's the true cost of not having representation..."
3. **The Advisory Stack** — Show everything the agent does that DIY or average agents don't
4. **The Ask** — "Based on what I've shared, would you like to work together?"

Role play: agents practice the Perfect Advisory Conversation on each other. Edmund observes and coaches.

# THE PRE-EVENT FUNNEL (How to Fill the 3-Day Workshop)

**Borrow from: Sabri Suby + Russell Brunson + Dan Lok**

## Phase 1: The HVCO (Sabri Suby)
Create a High Value Content Offer — NOT a generic brochure. Example:

**"The Singapore Property Agent's GCI Acceleration Playbook: How 47 Singapore Agents Added $50K–$200K to Their Annual GCI Using the MAS Framework"**

- 30–50 pages, properly designed, printed quality
- Offered for $10 (physical) or free (digital) via Facebook/Instagram ads targeting SG property agents
- Landing page: headline + 5 bullet points + lead capture form

## Phase 2: The Magic Lantern (Suby → 5-Email Sequence)
Email 1 (Day 1): "Why 90% of Singapore agents plateau at $100K GCI" — pure education
Email 2 (Day 3): "The one positioning shift that moved Agent A from $80K to $267K" — case study
Email 3 (Day 5): "The 3 conversations your current clients are having without you" — problem deepener
Email 4 (Day 7): "What your Dated Million date says about your current strategy" — personalised teaser
Email 5 (Day 9): "We're opening registration for the MAS 3-Day Workshop" — the offer

## Phase 3: The Webinar (Brunson Perfect Webinar)
Before each quarterly workshop, run a free 90-min webinar:
- Title: "How to Double Your GCI in 18 Months Without Doubling Your Hours"
- Format: Edmund teaches 3 frameworks from the 3-day workshop
- Close: "If this is valuable in 90 minutes, imagine 3 full days. Here's how to register."
- Target: 200 registrants → 80 attend live → 15–20 register for workshop

## Phase 4: Application Page (Dan Lok)
Not a checkout page. An application page.
- "Apply for the MAS 3-Day Workshop"
- 8 questions about their current GCI, their goals, their market
- Edmund's team reviews and approves
- Approved applicants receive a call to confirm

This filters:
- Serious from curious
- Qualified from unqualified
- Creates investment before they've paid anything
