# SECTION 1 — THE MICRO-BOOK (what it is + full outline/draft)

**What it is:** a short (20–30 page / ~30-min read) free digital book that is the TOP of the agent funnel. Its jobs: (1) attract the right agents, (2) pre-frame the MAS method so the preview/3-day feel inevitable, (3) capture the lead + start the GHL nurture, (4) act as a retargeting pool. It sells the *idea*, not the workshop. (Sabri "Sell Like Crazy" + Henry micro-book play.)

**Working title (pick one):**
- **"The $1M Agent Math: Why Most Singapore Agents Will Never Hit Their Number — and the System That Changes the Date"**
- "Agent to Advisor: The Operating System for a Million-Dollar Property Practice in Singapore"
- "The Disqualified Agent: Why Working Harder Is the Trap"

**Cover promise:** read this and you'll know the exact year you hit $1M GCI on your current numbers — and the 3 levers that move the date closer.

### Structure (chapters)
1. **The Uncomfortable Math** — most agents are busy, not building. Introduce *The Dated Million*: on your current numbers, when do you actually cross $1M cumulative GCI? (For most: 2034 → never.) The hook that makes them feel the gap.
2. **Why Effort Isn't the Lever** — the feast-or-famine trap; working harder caps you. The system is the lever, not hustle. (Reframe: their problem isn't laziness.)
3. **Agent vs Advisor** — the identity shift. Agents sell units; advisors architect outcomes and get referred, repeat, premium. The whole positioning play.
4. **The Three Pillars (the Flywheel)** — Positioning → Conversations → Systems, with AI as the hub. Teach it at a level that's genuinely useful but leaves the *installation* to the workshop. (Hormozi generosity: give the what, the room gives the how.)
5. **Your Offer Is Weak** — most agents compete on rebates; nothing differentiates them. Diagnose the offer as the bottleneck. (Sabri.)
6. **The AI Reckoning** — agents who don't systemise won't survive the next cycle. Urgency without doom.
7. **What a Million-Dollar Practice Actually Looks Like** — the Anatomy (lite): structure, numbers, time. The destination.
8. **Your Next Move** — soft CTA to the $97 1-day preview ("see your own Millionaire Math live and watch the date move").

### Lead-gen mechanics
- Landing page: title + cover promise + email/mobile capture (PDPA consent line + privacy link).
- Instant delivery → GHL nurture sequence → preview invite.
- Also gates a retargeting audience for the video ads below.
