## PART A — THE JOB OF THIS DAY (design intent — do not lose this)

A preview is **not** a compressed 3-Day. Its job:

1. **Deliver ONE complete, real win** so they *feel* the system works and believe *"technique is transferable."*
2. **Make the full install (the 3-Day, S$7,000) the obvious next step** — sold structurally, not pushily.

**Two outcomes by 6pm:**
- Every agent leaves with **their Corner claimed + their #1 leak named + one AI-built asset in hand** — proof the machine is real.
- Enough of them **apply/deposit** for the 3-Day to fill the next intake.

**The rule of the room:** Give a genuine win; open loops the 3-Day closes. Value so good they'd leave happy even if they don't buy — structured so the smart ones can't *not* buy. **Seed throughout; one direct ask at the end (the Bridge Close).**

### What they GET today (give freely)
- **The Business X-Ray** → their Leakage Map + the "I'm a Market Taker" aha (WIN #1)
- **The Corner You Own** → their owned market + one-liner (WIN #2)
- **The Attract Engine — live taste** → one AI-built angle + hook/ad in their voice, posted or ready (WIN #3 + the "wow")

The Signature Consult · the Authority Deck · the full Attract Engine · the GHL Growth Engine · the 24/7 follow-up bot · the Market Maker AI Team · the live Category Launch Hour · the 90-Day Climb. **Name these as the install that's still to come — never hand them over.**
