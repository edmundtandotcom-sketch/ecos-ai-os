## Purpose

Protect the accuracy, privacy and persuasive value of proof.

## Proof levels

- **Verified public proof:** source, identity, permission, numbers and context confirmed.
- **Verified internal proof:** facts confirmed; permission/public-use status incomplete.
- **Candidate proof:** requires verification, consent or clearer context.
- **Illustrative example:** not a real case; must be labelled.

## Required checks

Before public use, confirm identity, actual outcome, dates/baseline, supporting numbers, REI contribution versus market effects, consent, current accuracy and required caveats.
