## 1. THE BUSINESS IN ONE PARAGRAPH
Dan Henry (GetClients.com) sells **client-acquisition systems for coaches/agencies**, built on two engines: a **micro-book / lead-magnet front end** and a **webinar (Webinar Profits) selling machine**, ascending into a monthly university (Get Clients University) bundled with software (Deal Engine = done-for-you funnels; Artemo = AI copy tool). The doctrine: a lead magnet + webinar does the selling at scale, and software + monthly community provides continuity. Education front, software + recurring community back.

## 3. KEY MECHANICS
1. **Micro-book as lead magnet** — short authority asset that converts up to ~300% better than a bare opt-in.
2. **Webinar as the core selling engine** — one asset sells at scale, repeatably.
3. **Software bundled into continuity** — Deal Engine (DFY funnels) + Artemo (AI) deepen retention.
4. **Monthly "Growth Call"** — one new technique per month keeps the subscription alive.
5. **Done-for-you templates** — reduce implementation friction (same lever as Peng Joon snapshots).
