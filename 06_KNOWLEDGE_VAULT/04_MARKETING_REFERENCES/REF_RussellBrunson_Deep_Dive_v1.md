## 1. THE BUSINESS IN ONE PARAGRAPH
Russell Brunson sells **software (ClickFunnels) financed by education**. Books and challenges are loss-leaders/tripwires that funnel users into the SaaS, which is the real recurring business; the annual **Funnel Hacking Live** is the cultural cathedral that creates belonging, status (2-Comma Club awards) and mass upsell. The machine: free/cheap book → challenge (One Funnel Away / Your First Funnel) → ClickFunnels subscription → events + masterminds. Education is customer-acquisition for software, and community + ritual is the retention moat.

## 3. KEY MECHANICS
1. **Education as customer acquisition for software** — the course isn't the product; the subscription is.
2. **Challenge format** — time-boxed daily sprints that onboard users into the tool with momentum.
3. **Status awards (2-Comma Club)** — manufactured milestones that create aspirational proof and stage moments.
4. **Event as cathedral** — FHL builds identity/tribe; the room sells the next rung at scale.
5. **Free-book tripwire** — self-liquidating front end builds a massive buyer list.

## 4. WHAT MAS ADOPTS / ADAPTS / REJECTS (Singapore RE context)
| Mechanic | Verdict | How it lands in MAS |
|---|---|---|
| Challenge format to onboard | **ADOPT** | A free/low 3–5 day "challenge" (e.g. build your advisory pipeline) as the front-end that feeds the 3-day. |
| Status awards / milestones | **ADOPT** | A MAS milestone (e.g. "First Advisory Win," "First $1M GCI year mapped") awarded on stage at the event. |
| Education → tool pull-through | **ADAPT** | Pull-through to the MAS Snapshot / GHL system rather than a SaaS Edmund owns; take affiliate margin. |
| Free-book tripwire | **ADAPT** | A short authority book / "micro-book" as the lead magnet (also a Dan Henry mechanic). |
| Mass-event hype + awards theatre | **REJECT (the scale of it)** | Keep intimate and advisory; one tasteful milestone, not a stadium. |

## 5. REPOSITION INTO THE 3-DAY WORKSHOP

- Create **one on-stage milestone moment** (the "Dated Million" recalculation already in v2 is perfect for this).
