## WHAT CHANGED FROM V1 TO V2

The v1 doc (June 2026) captured Peng Joon's core machine accurately: one framework, infinite wrappers, software (SalesProcess.com / GHL white-label) as the real product, and the TO DONE promise. 

What has deepened or shifted in 2025–2026:

1. **The AI layer is no longer an add-on — it IS the product.** His 2026 framing is "Build Your AI Empire." AI tools (content generation, automated follow-up, AI avatar, AI script writers) are now integrated into the SalesProcess platform and positioned as the reason the system works without the owner's constant presence.

2. **Content Stacking is his dominant marketing play.** One piece of source content (a presentation, a livestream, a workshop recording) is AI-repurposed into 10+ derivative assets across platforms. He teaches this as a scalable content engine any business can run — and demonstrates it with his own output.

3. **The "AI Employee" positioning.** He now explicitly frames the GHL AI tools as having hired employees who never sleep, never quit, and don't need salaries. This is a clear, vivid benefit statement that MAS can adapt for the agent's AI Advisor Team.

4. **Consolidation of the product stack.** The v1 doc noted multiple product rungs. By 2026, he has consolidated: the main entry is the AI-Powered Sales Process System ($997/yr) which bundles software + training + community; the Breakthrough Blueprint LIVE is the annual/semi-annual flagship; Dragon 100 / high-end mastermind remains the apex.

5. **The "Install Don't Build" doctrine is more developed.** By 2026, he has pre-built entire campaign sets — not just snapshot funnels but pre-written email sequences, pre-set AI prompts, pre-configured follow-up triggers. Participants arrive at events with their accounts pre-loaded. Zero blank-screen friction.

## 3. THE NEW MECHANICS (2025–2026 updates)

### 3.1 Content Stacking — the AI content flywheel
One source asset (a 60-minute workshop recording) is AI-repurposed into:
- 10 short-form clips (TikTok/Instagram Reels/YouTube Shorts)
- 5 "carousels" (LinkedIn / IG carousel posts)
- 3 email sequences (from the transcript)
- 1 blog post (AI-summarised)
- 1 podcast episode (audio extracted)
- AI-generated hooks, captions, and CTAs for each

The framework is taught as a **scalable content engine** any solopreneur can run. Peng Joon himself uses it to maintain a prolific presence across 5+ platforms without a large team. This is the "AI employee that never sleeps" in practice.

**For MAS:** Every agent leaves Day 3 with a content stacking system set up. One advisory insight → 10 pieces of content → distributed automatically.

### 3.2 AI Avatar / AI Employee positioning
In 2026, he explicitly teaches building an **AI avatar** (text + video AI clone of yourself) that:
- Answers FAQs via an AI chatbot on your website
- Responds to leads via AI SMS/WhatsApp before a human takes over
- Sends AI-personalised follow-up messages at scale

The framing: "You now have an employee that works 24/7, never takes a day off, and costs less than a part-time hire."

**For MAS:** This IS the AI Advisor Team concept already in the blueprint. The v2 update is to make the AI avatar set-up a Day 3 deliverable — agents leave with their own AI follow-up agent installed and active.

### 3.3 "Install Don't Build" — expanded
In 2025–2026 events, he doesn't just install snapshot funnels — he installs pre-written AI prompt libraries, pre-configured sequences for 6+ scenarios (new lead, viewing booked, offer declined, client won), and pre-set content stacking workflows. The entire back-end is done; participants spend event time on the last 20% (personalisation, offer specifics, niche-specific language).

**For MAS:** The MAS Snapshot must now include:
- Pre-configured AI follow-up sequences for Singapore RE contexts (HDB upgrade conversation, decoupling enquiry, investment review)
- Pre-written AI content prompts for the agent's advisory niche
- Pre-set database segmentation (by client stage, property type, intent)

### 3.4 AI Business Review (the monthly call evolution)
The continuity tier now includes a monthly "AI Business Review" call where he:
- Demonstrates new AI tools added to the platform
- Shows a new content stacking template
- Reviews a student's AI-implemented funnel live (social proof + education)

The call format keeps the $997/yr subscription alive without delivering live coaching hours (scales efficiently).

**For MAS:** The monthly continuity call evolves to include: one new AI prompt/tool + one student implementation review + one new Singapore market advisory play. Stays relevant; renews itself.

## 5. THE SCALE LESSON — UPDATED FOR 2026

The 2026 update to Peng Joon's scale thesis: **AI collapses the marginal cost of content and follow-up to near zero.** The business that would have required a 5-person team to run at scale in 2022 runs with a 1-person operator + AI tools in 2026. 

For MAS, this reframes the pitch entirely:
- **Old frame:** "You need systems to scale." (abstract)
- **New frame:** "You have an AI advisor team installed by the end of Day 3 — it follows up with every lead, sends market updates to your database, and prepares every client for their next move, while you focus on advisory conversations." (tangible, installed, immediate)

The magic is not that AI replaces the agent. It's that **AI makes one agent operate like a team of five** — specifically in the administrative and follow-up work that currently eats agent time without generating income.
