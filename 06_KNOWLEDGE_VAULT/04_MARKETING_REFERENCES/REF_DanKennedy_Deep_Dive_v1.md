## 3. KEY MECHANICS

1. **Message-Market-Media match** — the right message to the right market through the right medium. Misalignment on any one of the three means zero results regardless of effort. Most agents fail on Message (generic) or Market (everyone) rather than Media.
2. **Magnetic Marketing** — don't chase prospects; attract the right ones. Design positioning and content so ideal clients self-select and come to you. Repelling the wrong clients is not loss — it is efficiency.
3. **Build the herd** — the most important asset is the proprietary list (email, database, "tribe"). Renting attention (advertising) always costs more than owning attention (the list). Every campaign should grow the list.
4. **Direct response over image advertising** — every piece of marketing must have a specific call to action and a measurable result. "Brand awareness" without a response mechanism is money wasted.
5. **The "Most Incredible Free Gift Ever"** — the irresistible front-end lead magnet. Make the free thing so good that not taking it feels foolish. The free gift self-liquidates the acquisition cost.
6. **Price as positioning / premium client filter** — raise prices to repel price-sensitive clients and attract clients who value expertise. The premium price signals that something different is available.
7. **Packaging expertise** — package knowledge as systems, tools, and frameworks (not just your time). An agent who packages their methodology into a "system" commands premium over one who sells effort.
