# SPOUSE_GAP — follow-up
Version: v0.1 · Status: DRAFT · Date: 2026-07-11 · Supersedes: none
Sources: Risk Assurance v2.0 §9 (Spouse Not Aligned); Decision Psychology v2.1 RB-07; Diagnosis Call §5 (decision-maker rule)

## Gap definition
A material decision-maker is absent or unaligned (RB-07) — the biggest late-stage stall. Do not keep convincing the present partner. `[T3]`

| Field | Value |
|---|---|
| Objective | Get both decision-makers in the same conversation seeing the same numbers |
| Assets | Joint-session offer (Zoom/coffee); one-page shared summary; the values-level framing |
| Channel | WhatsApp/email to the present partner offering a joint session; do NOT lobby via proxy |
| Timing | Offer within 48h; hold for the joint session, no solo re-convincing in between |
| Owner | Consult advisor |
| Exit condition | Joint session booked → re-book, OR PREPARE (subject-to-spouse) |
