## Purpose
Defines the standard post-consult recap sent to the client and logged to CRM. A written recap is a field-tested winner (RB-08 comprehension) and the anchor for every gap follow-up. `[T3]`

## Recap format (client-facing, plain language)
1. **Where you stand today** — current position (plain, no internal labels)
2. **What we're really deciding** — the real decision named
3. **The paths we compared** — 2–3 pathways + trade-offs
4. **Recommended next move** — the sequence, not a single property
5. **What to avoid** — the avoid list
6. **Your next step** — one concrete, **dated** action + owner
7. **What we watch for** — the follow-up trigger

## Rules
- Plain public language only (no "RB-xx", "Ascent Stage", "RAM"). `[T5]`
- Zero pressure; "if the review says wait, we wait with a plan." `[T5]`
- No PII beyond the client's own copy; the CRM-logged version is anonymised in any OS artefact. `[HARD RULE 5]`
- Send within 24–48h of the consult.
