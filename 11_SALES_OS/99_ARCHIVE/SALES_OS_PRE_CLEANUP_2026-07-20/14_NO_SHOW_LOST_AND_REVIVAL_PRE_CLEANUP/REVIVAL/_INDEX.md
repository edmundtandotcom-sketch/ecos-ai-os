# REVIVAL — routing
Version: v0.1 · Status: DRAFT · Date: 2026-07-11 · Supersedes: none
Sources: Risk Assurance v2.0 §10 (lifetime review loop); CRM SOP 04 (nurture/reactivation); branch 13 (gap follow-up)

## Purpose
Re-engages no-shows, lost, and long-dormant leads at a *relevant* future trigger — never by generic re-blasting. The relationship does not end after one transaction or one lost consult. `[T5]`

## Revival triggers (point to Risk Assurance §10)
MOP / TOP / SSD · loan repricing · project TOP · rental change · child milestone · spouse income change · asset appreciation · market window · family milestone · 3–5 year portfolio review. `[T5]`

## Play
| Step | Move |
|---|---|
| At trigger | Value-first touch tied to *their* trigger (not "still interested?") |
| If engaged | Re-open the specific gap that stalled them (branch 13) |
| If ready | Re-enter consultation flow at the right phase (branch 07) |

## Rules
Every revival is trigger-driven and logged with a next Ascent trigger. Zero pressure; banned openers apply. `[T5]`
