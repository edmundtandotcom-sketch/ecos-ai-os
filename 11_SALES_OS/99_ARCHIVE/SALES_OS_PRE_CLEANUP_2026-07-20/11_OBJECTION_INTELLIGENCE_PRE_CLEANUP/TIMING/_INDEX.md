## Field rule
Validate first → **dated, sourced historical cycle data** → the client's *personal cost-of-waiting number* (loan-capacity decay, lost borrowing capacity). "Wait for cooling" is usually a request for external permission. Where family crash-trauma is present, treat as Level 4, not a debating point. Never deliver the "positioning beats prediction" reframe without the number behind it. `[T3]`

## When to stop pushing
If timing fear is genuine intergenerational trauma → respect it, reframe safety, don't argue the market. `[T3]`
