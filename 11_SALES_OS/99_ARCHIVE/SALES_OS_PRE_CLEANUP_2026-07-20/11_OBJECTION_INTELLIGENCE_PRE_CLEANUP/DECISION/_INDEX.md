## Category
Decision-stage stalls: paralysis / "need to think" / "no rush", and sell-high-buy-higher hesitation — the objections that appear when everything is understood but the client still won't commit. `[T3]`

## Routes to
- Belief blocks: **RB-02** (Need More Certainty), **RB-03** (Comfort > Growth), **RB-04** (Sell High Buy Higher).
- Sequences: Belief-Shift v2.0 §2, §4, §6; Closing the Loop §15.

## Field rule
The deeper lever is external conviction + a **forcing event/deadline**, not more research. Name any stated contradiction diplomatically ("you just agreed waiting costs you — what would need to be true to not wait?"). **Lock a concrete, dated next step at every close** — its absence is a top-5 loser. For RB-04, separate "hold for shelter" from "next property is where growth is" using the client's own numbers. `[T3]`

## When to stop pushing
When the correct answer is "not yet": convert to a NURTURE close with a readiness plan + dated review (branch 12/13), not more pressure. `[T5]`
