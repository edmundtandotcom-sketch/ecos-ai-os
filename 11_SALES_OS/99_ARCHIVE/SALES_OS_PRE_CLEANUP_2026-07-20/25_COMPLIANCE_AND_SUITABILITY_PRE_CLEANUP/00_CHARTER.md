## Purpose

Would govern regulatory and suitability guardrails on sales activity: CEA compliance, PDPA handling of client data, and suitability checks before a recommendation is made.

## When To Use

When any sales process touches client PII, a referral incentive, or a recommendation that needs a documented suitability basis.

## What Exists Elsewhere Already

- Decision 051 already set the PDPA baseline for this OS: consultation transcripts may be analysed for the intelligence pilot with anonymisation; raw transcripts never copied into the OS; outputs strip client identifiers. This is the only compliance ruling currently on record for Sales OS specifically.
- Identity/regulatory framing (REI is an advisory practice; PropNex is the regulatory wrapper, not the identity): `00_AI_OPERATING_SYSTEM\CLAUDE.md` §10
- No CEA-specific compliance document or suitability-check framework exists in the workspace today — genuine gap beyond the PDPA ruling above.

## What Triggers Build-Out

`20_REFERRALS_AND_ADVOCACY` proposes an incentive structure (needs compliance sign-off before shipping), or Edmund flags a specific CEA/PDPA requirement that needs codifying.
