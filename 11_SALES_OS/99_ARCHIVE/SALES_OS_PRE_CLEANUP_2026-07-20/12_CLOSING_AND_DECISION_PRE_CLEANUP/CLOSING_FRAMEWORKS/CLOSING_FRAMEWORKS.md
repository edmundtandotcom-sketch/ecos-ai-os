## 1. The Rule Above All Closes
There are exactly **4 sanctioned outcomes**: **PROCEED · PREPARE · NURTURE · NO FIT**. Every close technique below is a *means to reach one of these four honestly* — never a device to manufacture a decision. **Artificial pressure, scarcity, and takeaway/urgency tactics are banned.** Close by clarity, not by pressure. Every close ends on a concrete, dated next step. `[T5; T3 for the dated-step rule]`

## 2. The 8 Close Types → 4 Sanctioned Outcomes
The mandate names 8 close *types*. They are conversational techniques; they resolve into the 4 outcomes. `[T5]`

| Close type | Purpose | When to use | When NOT to use | Structure | Risk if misused | Resolves to |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Diagnostic** | Surface the real decision through diagnosis, not persuasion | Client unclear what they're deciding | Diagnosis incomplete / no numbers | Replay diagnosis → name the real decision → ask "did I capture this right?" | Becomes a leading question | PROCEED / PREPARE |
| **Alignment** | Confirm shared understanding before moving | Both decision-makers present, roadmap agreed | A decision-maker absent | Restate position + recommended pathway → confirm agreement | False consensus papering over a silent spouse | PROCEED |
| **Summary** | Recap the roadmap → the one next action | End of a complete session | Anything unresolved on the client's live concern | Current→Target→Risk→Recommended→Next action | Glosses an unaddressed objection | PROCEED |
| **Conditional** | Commit *subject to* a named condition | A genuine dependency exists (financing/spouse/sale) | No real condition — don't invent one | "If [condition] holds by [date], we proceed; if not, we [fallback]" | Fake conditions as soft pressure | PREPARE |
| **Choice** | Choose between genuinely valid pathways | ≥2 real, suitable options exist | Only one option is actually suitable (false choice) | Present valid options + trade-offs → client chooses the move (not *whether*) | Illusion-of-choice manipulation — **banned** | PROCEED |
| **Action** | Lock a clean next step | Client is ready and the step is suitable | Client not yet confident | Name the step, owner, and date | Rushing past unresolved fear | PROCEED |
| **Timeline** | Convert "not now" into a dated readiness plan | Direction valid, timing not ready | Client is actually ready now | Target amount + savings/trigger + review date (RAM SAYE) | Vague "keep in touch" | NURTURE / PREPARE |
| **No-sale** | Recommend *not* transacting | No feasible/safe path; poor fit | A safe path exists | State why no move is safest + the safer action | Over-serving a no-fit client | NO FIT |

## 3. Ethics Boundary (per close, mandate §12)
- Every close must be **reversible in spirit**: the client could walk away and still feel well-advised. `[T5]`
- **Choice close** is only ethical when every option offered is genuinely suitable — otherwise it is manipulation and is banned. `[T5]`
- **Conditional close** requires a *real* dependency; inventing conditions to soft-pressure is banned. `[T5]`
- **No-sale close** is a first-class sanctioned outcome, not a failure — "the right next action is not always buy". `[T5, Risk Assurance §12]`
