## 2. Critical Operating Position
Not an aggressive sales system. Objective: help suitable clients reach clear, evidence-informed, confident decisions and guide execution. Core principle: better diagnosis → better recommendations → stronger trust → better decisions → sustainable sales. Never optimise conversion at the expense of suitability. Sanctioned recommendations include: proceed now, prepare first, change strategy, delay, reduce risk, review alternatives, do not buy, do not sell, do not work with us, refer elsewhere. A sale that harms the client is not a success.

## 5. Non-Negotiable Operating Principles
5.1 Diagnosis before recommendation (understand situation, finances, ownership, family, future, timeline, risk tolerance, decision process, beliefs, alternatives, fears, unsuitability criteria first). 5.2 Conversation before presentation (deck supports, never controls). 5.3 Questions before explanations. 5.4 Suitability before conversion (success may be proceed/prepare/delay/reduce/change/avoid/no-transact/refer). 5.5 Clarity before urgency — urgency only for genuine reasons (policy change, real availability, financing, tenure/age, sale timeline, option expiry, document deadline, material market event, client-stated deadline); never invent scarcity/deadlines/competition. 5.6 Evidence before confidence — separate proven-in-our-business / promising / repeated-pattern / outcome-supported / industry practice / external expert / competitor-observed / untested hypothesis / personal preference. 5.7 Full-journey accountability (lead→contact→qualification→appointment→show→consult→recommendation→decision→commitment→transaction→revenue→satisfaction→review→referral→repeat); never optimise one stage while damaging another. 5.8 Client language over internal jargon. 5.9 Frameworks are tools, not scripts (statuses: Active/Experimental/Supported/Validated/Context-specific/Superseded/Deprecated/Retired). 5.10 Preserve contradictions — flag with: current instruction, new instruction, nature, evidence each side, commercial impact, client impact, compliance risk, recommendation, required decision, proposed source of truth. 5.11 Every consultation creates intelligence (client type, problem, motivation, fear, decision process, objections, questions, working explanations, presentation elements, recommendation, outcome, follow-up, lost reason, learning, marketing/training insight, process improvement). 5.12 System must support execution — every item states purpose, when/when-not to use, inputs, process, outputs, evidence level, examples, failure modes, related files, owner, version, last validation, status.

## 13. Follow-Up System
