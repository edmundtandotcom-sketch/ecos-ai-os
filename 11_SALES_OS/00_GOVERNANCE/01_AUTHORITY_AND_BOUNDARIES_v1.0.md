# Sales Authority and Boundaries
Version: v1.0
Status: APPROVED
Date: 2026-07-20

Sales owns:
- qualification and lead grading;
- booking, confirmation and show-up;
- objections, closing and outcome capture;
- follow-up, nurture, revival and referrals;
- pipeline stage meaning and movement criteria.

Client Advisory owns:
- diagnosis and discovery method;
- Strategy Session delivery;
- RAM, ProDeck and Blueprint;
- recommendation logic and advisory quality.

Operations owns:
- GHL field/stage configuration;
- workflows, automation and notifications;
- dashboards, tracking and technical QA.

Marketing owns:
- acquisition funnel/campaign strategy and the Marketing-to-Sales handoff.

Named contact and conversation evidence remains private. When a sales interaction turns into advice or diagnosis, follow Client Advisory authority.
