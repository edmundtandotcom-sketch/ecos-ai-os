# Closing and Outcome Codes
Version: v1.0
Status: APPROVED
Date: 2026-07-20

Closing converts an informed decision into a clear next action.

Required close:
- restate the agreed outcome and scope;
- confirm approved terms and conditions;
- ask for the decision without false pressure;
- capture `Proceed`, `Prepare`, `Nurture` or `No Fit`;
- record reason, owner, next action and date;
- hand off implementation/advisory work.

No outcome may be inferred from silence. `Won` and `Lost` are pipeline states; they must have a reason and date. Operations implements controlled codes and QA after Sales approval.
