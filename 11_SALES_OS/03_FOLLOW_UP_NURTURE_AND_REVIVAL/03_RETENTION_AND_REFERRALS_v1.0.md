# Retention and Referrals
Version: v1.0
Status: APPROVED GOVERNANCE
Date: 2026-07-20

Retention and referral requests begin only after the relevant delivery owner confirms the appropriate milestone.

Sales owns:
- commercial check-in and next-step progression;
- consented referral invitation;
- referral source capture and follow-up.

Client Advisory owns advisory-delivery quality and client outcomes. Operations implements reminders/tracking. Marketing may use approved, consented proof only through the Brand/Proof and Asset Library boundaries.

Never convert private client evidence into public proof without explicit approval and documented usage rights.
