# No-Show, Lost and Revival
Version: v1.0
Status: APPROVED
Date: 2026-07-20

## No-show

Confirm status, offer a bounded reschedule path and stop according to consent/rules. Do not silently mark a no-show as unqualified.

## Lost

Capture a controlled reason and whether future contact is permitted. `Lost` must not hide unresolved operational failure.

## Revival

Revival requires a defensible reason: changed timing, new relevant offer/context, requested follow-up date or verified new signal. Set a clear window, cap attempts and honor opt-out. Measure revival separately from new acquisition.
