# Follow-Up System
Version: v1.0
Status: APPROVED GOVERNANCE
Date: 2026-07-20

Every follow-up plan must define:
- current stage/outcome and reason;
- objective of the next contact;
- useful context or value;
- channel, consent and timing;
- owner;
- stop conditions;
- next review date.

Follow-up must be specific to the person’s stated situation, without storing that detail in general AI OS files. Stop on opt-out, wrong contact, no-fit or fulfilled objective. Avoid repeated generic chasing. Operations may automate delivery only when the Sales rule, consent and exception path are explicit.
