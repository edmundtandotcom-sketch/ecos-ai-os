# Marketing-to-Sales Handoff
Version: v1.0
Status: APPROVED
Date: 2026-07-20

A handoff is ready when it includes:
- source campaign and offer;
- requested next step;
- consent/channel;
- verified contact details in the live private system;
- qualification signals and explicit unknowns;
- assigned owner and response target;
- attribution fields required for reporting.

Marketing owns the acquisition promise and context. Sales owns response, qualification and progression. Operations implements routing and tracking.

If campaign messaging promises an advisory outcome that Sales cannot deliver, stop and reconcile with Client Advisory before launch.
