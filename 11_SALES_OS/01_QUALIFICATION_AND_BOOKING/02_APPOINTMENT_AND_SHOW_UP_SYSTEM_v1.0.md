# Appointment and Show-Up System
Version: v1.0
Status: APPROVED
Date: 2026-07-20

The system covers booking through attendance:
1. confirm the correct next step and owner;
2. capture consent, time zone and preferred channel;
3. send accurate confirmation and preparation requirements;
4. use bounded reminders with clear reschedule/cancel options;
5. record show, no-show, cancel or reschedule;
6. trigger the appropriate follow-up path.

Sales owns message intent and progression rules. Operations owns GHL implementation. Client Advisory owns the session agenda and diagnostic content. Avoid pressure, false scarcity and repeated contact beyond consent.
