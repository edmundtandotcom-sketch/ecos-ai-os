# Lead Qualification and Grading
Version: v1.0
Status: APPROVED GOVERNANCE
Date: 2026-07-20

Keep A–D lead grade separate from pipeline stage.

A qualification specification must define observable criteria for:
- relevance/fit;
- intent and urgency;
- ability to take the next step;
- responsiveness;
- exclusions and compliance constraints.

Record unknowns as unknown; do not invent financial or personal facts. Do not treat marketing engagement as readiness. Qualification controls sales priority and next action, not the advisory recommendation.

Operations implements fields/automation only after Sales approves the definitions. Validate the live GHL field values before use.
