# 01_PRODECK_CONSULT_DELIVERY_SYSTEM_v2.0
Status: ACTIVE SOURCE OF TRUTH
Folder: 02_CLIENT_OS / 04_PRODECK
Purpose: Define the live consult-delivery system for Client Edition.

## 1. Core Definition
ProDeck is the session control system.

It turns Client OS intelligence into a clear advisory conversation that the client can understand and act on.

The advisor is not presenting a property. The advisor is presenting:
- the client's real position;
- the market context relevant to that position;
- the risk controls;
- the next 2-3 moves;
- the decision path.

## 2. Session Control Rule
Do not let the client drag the session into project-first discussion too early.

If the client asks, "Which project should I buy?" the advisor should redirect:

"Before I can answer that properly, we need to know what this property is supposed to do for your next 3-10 years. Otherwise we may end up choosing a project before we know the job it needs to perform."

## 3. Pre-Session Gates
Before the full ProDeck session, confirm:

| Gate | Required? | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Both decision-makers present | Strongly preferred | Prevents post-session spouse veto |
| Basic numbers available | Required for meaningful advice | No numbers = no real strategy |
| Current property / ownership facts known | Required | Ownership affects ABSD, loan, exit, and name strategy |
| Timeline known | Required | MOP, lease decay, family event, school, retirement, cash event |
| Advisory fit confirmed | Required | Not every lead needs a full strategy session |

If the numbers are missing, run diagnosis and assign homework. Do not pretend to give a final strategy.

## 4. The 5 ProDeck Anchors
These are not slide titles. They are the five anchors the advisor must land during the session.

### Anchor 1: Position Clarity
The client must see where they really are.

Use:
- current property position;
- current equity;
- cash / CPF / loanability;
- ownership structure;
- timeline;
- Ascent Stage;
- decision pressure.

Client outcome:
"I now understand my real starting point."

### Anchor 2: Market Reward Clarity
The client must see what the market is currently rewarding and what their current position is missing.

Use only 2-3 relevant market signals. Do not give a market lecture.

Client outcome:
"I now understand why my current setup may not be enough."

### Anchor 3: Risk Assurance
The client must see that risk is being managed, not ignored.

Use RAM selectively:
- cash-flow safety;
- worst-case holding power;
- tenure structure;
- CPF / cash preservation;
- exit liquidity;
- ownership / ABSD exposure.

Client outcome:
"This is not reckless. There is a safety frame."

### Anchor 4: Next 2-3 Moves
The client must see a sequenced roadmap, not a random recommendation.

A move can be:
- hold and monitor;
- sell and rebuild;
- upgrade;
- decouple review;
- new launch vs resale decision;
- HDB / EC exit review;
- family buy-with-child review;
- portfolio restructure;
- do nothing for now because the numbers do not support action.

Client outcome:
"I can see the path, not just the property."

### Anchor 5: Decision Confidence and Next Action
The client must leave knowing what decision is actually needed next.

Do not close with pressure. Close with the next clarity gap.

Use:
"Based on what we covered, what is the one thing you still need to be clearer on before we decide the next step?"

Client outcome:
"I know the next action and why it matters."

## 5. Recommended 60-Minute Session Flow
| Phase | Time | Purpose | Advisor output |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Control the room | 0-5 min | Set frame and permission | Session agenda agreed |
| 2. Review real position | 5-20 min | Confirm numbers, ownership, timeline | Real Position Snapshot |
| 3. Evaluate market rewards | 20-32 min | Show relevant opportunity and inaction cost | Market Reward Lens |
| 4. Risk assurance | 32-42 min | Address fear and financial safety | Risk Assurance Board |
| 5. Identify next 2-3 moves | 42-55 min | Present roadmap and trade-offs | Next Move Roadmap |
| 6. Close and handoff | 55-60 min | Confirm next action | Follow-up or execution path |

For complex cases, split into Diagnosis Call + Strategy Session. Do not rush a complex household into a fake one-hour answer.

## 6. Advisory Language Rules
Use plain language first. Use internal labels only in notes.

Say:
- "your current position" instead of "A1 First Climb diagnosis";
- "your next move" instead of "Strategic Intent execution";
- "risk controls" instead of "RAM module";
- "what this unlocks next" instead of "redeployment pathway".

## 7. What ProDeck Must Never Do
ProDeck must not:
- become a generic sales script;
- push a project before diagnosis;
- promise returns;
- hide risk;
- use outdated stamp duty / policy tables without verification;
- treat all HDB / EC households as upgrade-ready;
- treat decoupling as the strategy itself;
- create a deck that sounds impressive but does not tell the client what to do next.

## 8. Session Output Standard
Every proper ProDeck session should produce one of these outputs:

1. Not ready - missing numbers / spouse / timing.
2. Hold for now - monitor and revisit.
3. Explore - needs deeper property or financing analysis.
4. Execute next step - viewing, valuation, banker, legal, sale preparation, or project shortlist.
5. Nurture - not urgent but clear follow-up trigger.

No session should end with vague "think about it" unless the advisor knows exactly what they are thinking about.
