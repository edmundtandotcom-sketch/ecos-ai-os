Status: ACTIVE SOURCE OF TRUTH

Purpose: This file merges the old CRM Client Intelligence Architecture and Contact Management Follow-Up Rules into one lean operating file.

## 1. Core Principle

**One Person -> One Record -> One Journey -> Lifetime Relationship**

CRM is not a contact database. CRM is an intelligence database.

Every useful contact record should help the team understand:

- who they are;
- what they own;
- what stage they are in;
- what triggered them;
- what is blocking them;
- what should happen next;
- when the next Ascent trigger may appear.

## 2. System of Record

| System | Role | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| GoHighLevel | Primary CRM and relationship movement system | One contact, one opportunity, one truth |
| Google Contacts | Secondary relationship directory | Do not use as CRM replacement |
| Google Drive | Document and file storage | One client, one folder when qualified |
| Client OS | Advisory and diagnosis source | Do not store live CRM data here |
| Operations & Tracking | Dashboards, SOPs, ad performance, lead quality | Measures what is running and what worked |

## 3. Required Client Intelligence Fields

| Field Group | Required Fields | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Identity | full name, mobile, email, spouse, children if relevant | Know who the relationship belongs to |
| Source | lead source, campaign, ad angle, referral, content consumed | Know what created attention |
| Entry Door | campaign door, organic, referral, event, consult | Know what promise opened the conversation |
| Ascent Stage | A0 Activation, A1 First Climb, A2 Compound (former A3/A4 RETIRED per Decision 063 — family/multi-name situations sit inside A1/A2) | Know journey position |
| Property Context | HDB, EC, condo, landed, investment, current home, years held | Know real-world position without creating a second ladder |
| Strategic Intent | hold, sell, buy again, upgrade, decouple, family legacy, next 3 moves | Know intended move |
| Buyer Profile | condo owner, DINK, decoupling planner, qualified HDB/EC upgrade-window, family planner, ready mover | Know language and psychology |
| Belief Block | why they are stuck or delaying | Know what must shift |
| Trigger Moment | valuation, project interest, spouse discussion, MOP/TOP/SSD, child milestone, market movement | Know why now |
| Readiness / Tier | Tier 1, Tier 2, Tier 3, Excluded | Know attention priority |
| Advisory Output | REI notes, recommended path, next 2-3 moves, avoid list, next action | Turn diagnosis into movement |
| Follow-Up | last summary, next follow-up date, next trigger, owner | Prevent memory-based follow-up |

## 4. Pipeline Rule

Pipeline = movement. Fields = intelligence.

Do not use pipeline stages to classify people.

| Pipeline Stage | Purpose |
|---|---|
| New Lead | New enquiry received and needs first contact |
| Attempting Contact | Outreach in progress |
| Diagnosis Call Booked | Initial diagnosis scheduled |
| Strategy Session Booked | Qualified consult/session booked |
| Implementation Opportunity | Recommendation given and decision pending |
| Nurture | Not now, but still relevant |
| Client / Past Client / Advocate | Relationship continues after execution |

## 5. Tiering Rule

| Tier | Signals | Action |
|---|---|---|
| Tier 1 | clear trigger, serious timing, suitable numbers, spouse/family discussion, project or next-move pressure | high-touch, book/prepare consult quickly |
| Tier 2 | clear intent but belief block, spouse hesitation, timing uncertainty or missing info | active nurture and belief-specific follow-up |
| Tier 3 | low urgency, early research, future trigger not active | light-touch authority nurture |
| Excluded | poor fit, no feasible path, values mismatch, no MAS relevance | do not over-serve |

## 6. HDB / EC Upgrade-Window Rule

HDB/EC is a qualified segment only when there is a real strategic next-move issue.

Valid signals:

- MOP/TOP/SSD or timing window is relevant;
- upgrade intent is realistic;
- equity/cash/CPF/loan path is meaningful;
- ownership names and family structure matter;
- they are serious about planning, not just browsing;
- there is a possible path into a private-property or portfolio strategy.

Do not treat every HDB/EC owner as a MAS-fit lead.

## 7. Follow-Up Philosophy

Follow-up is not chasing.

Follow-up means staying relevant to the client’s next Ascent trigger.

Every follow-up should answer one of these questions:

1. What changed?
2. Why does this matter now?
3. What should they reconsider?
4. Which next move or next risk is becoming clearer?
5. What action should they take next?

## 8. Follow-Up by Ascent Stage

| Ascent Stage | Follow-Up Focus | Example Angle |
|---|---|---|
| A1 First Climb | turn appreciation, name structure, or upgrade window into a clear next move | “Your property has moved. Your plan should be reviewed too.” |
| A2 Compound | monitor exit thesis, rental, redeployment and second-cycle timing | “The asset has performed. Is the next move still the same?” |

> **RETIRED (Decision 063, 2026-07-11):** the former A3/A4 follow-up rows are removed — the Ascent Model is A0–A2. Family angles (child eligibility, parent capital, family alignment) and portfolio angles (optimisation, protection, estate implications) are followed up inside standard A1/A2 engagements.

## 9. Required Updates After Each Interaction

| Interaction | Must Update |
|---|---|
| New enquiry | source, campaign, first concern, possible stage, owner, next action |
| First conversation | trigger, intent, possible profile, belief block, readiness |
| Diagnosis call | real position, Ascent Stage, strategic intent, key risk, next action |
| Strategy session | REI output, ProDeck notes, recommendation, objections, follow-up |
| Client executes | transaction details, exit thesis, next review date, next Ascent trigger |
| Nurture begins | reason for nurture, belief block, next trigger, cadence |
| Past client review | current result, exit/redeploy opportunity, family/legacy possibility |

## 10. AI Usage Rule

AI may classify and summarise based only on known facts.

AI must not invent client facts.

If data is missing, AI should say what is missing and make a provisional classification only.

## 11. Contact Completeness Test

A useful client record should let the advisor answer within 60 seconds:

- Who are they?
- What do they own?
- What stage are they in?
- What triggered them?
- What do they want?
- What is blocking them?
- How ready are they?
- What should happen next?
- When should we follow up?

If not, the record is incomplete.
