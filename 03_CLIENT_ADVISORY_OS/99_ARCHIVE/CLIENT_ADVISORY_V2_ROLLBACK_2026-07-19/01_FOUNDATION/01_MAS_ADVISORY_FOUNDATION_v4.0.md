## 1. What MAS Is

MAS means Millionaire Advisory System.

MAS is the Client Edition advisory operating system for helping suitable Singapore property owners and families make smarter next-move property decisions.

The core belief:

**We do not start with property. We start with position.**

Property is the vehicle. Future flexibility, net-worth growth, retirement safety, and family options are the outcome.

## 2. What MAS Is Not

MAS is not:

- a project-selling framework;
- a generic HDB-upgrader funnel;
- a buy-anything property hype system;
- a CPF technical lecture;
- a random content idea bank;
- a one-time transaction workflow.

MAS exists to help the right clients understand their position, identify their next 2-3 moves, and execute with exit-first discipline.

## 4. One-Level Ascent Decision

Use only **Ascent Stage** as the main level system.

Do not run two active ladders:

- Journey Stage
- Property Position

Property Position is still useful, but it is a context field only.

Example:

| Main Stage | Context Field |
|---|---|
| A1 First Climb | Condo owner with appreciated property |
| A1 First Climb | Qualified HDB/EC upgrade-window household |
| A1 First Climb | Couple considering decoupling/free-name strategy |
| A1/A2 (family situation) | Parent exploring child-name property structure — handled inside a standard A1/A2 engagement (the former A3 Legacy stage is RETIRED per Decision 063) |

## 5. Current Ascent Stages

| Stage | Meaning | What We Diagnose |
|---|---|---|
| A1 First Climb | Current property position should unlock the next strategic move | equity, names, spouse alignment, upgrade path, decoupling, hold/sell/buy-again choice |
| A2 Compound | Client has made or is near a strategic move and needs compounding discipline | exit thesis, redeployment, rental, market cycle, second-cycle timing |

> **RETIRED (Decision 063, 2026-07-11):** the former A3 Legacy and A4 Bespoke Portfolio / Mash-Up stages are removed — the Ascent Model is A0–A2 only. Family structure, child eligibility, parent capital, multi-name and multi-asset situations are diagnosed inside standard A1/A2 engagements without a dedicated level.

A0 Activation is not kept as a main stage. It is an awareness moment under A1.

## 6. First Climb Clarification

A1 First Climb includes the common thought:

**“My property went up. Now what?”**

This is not a separate stage. It is the recognition moment inside First Climb.

First Climb has two common moments:

| Moment | What They Think | What It Means |
|---|---|---|
| Recognition | “My condo/property went up. Now what?” | They realise gain exists but do not know what it should unlock |
| Movement | “Should I hold, sell, decouple, upgrade, or buy again?” | They are ready to map the next structured move |

## 7. Official REI Method

REI is the diagnostic method inside MAS.

| REI Step | Question | Output |
|---|---|---|
| R - Review Real Position | Where is the client really standing now? | current property, equity, loan, names, family, capital, timeline, constraints |
| E - Evaluate Market Rewards | What does the market reward now and later? | opportunity, risk, exit buyer logic, asset fit, timing |
| I - Identify Next 2-3 Moves | What sequence should they take next? | roadmap, next move, avoid list, exit-first logic, follow-up trigger |

REI is not marketing language. It is the internal diagnostic backbone.

## 8. ProDeck Role

ProDeck is the consult and presentation delivery tool.

REI diagnoses. ProDeck presents.

A proper ProDeck output should show:

- current position;
- Ascent Stage;
- risks and constraints;
- market pathway;
- exit-first logic;
- recommended next 2-3 moves;
- what to avoid;
- decision and execution path.

| Family Legacy — **RETIRED door (Decision 058) + retired stage (Decision 063)** | formerly A3 Legacy | Historical reference only — family/child-name situations now run inside standard A1/A2 engagements |
| Next 3 Moves | A1-A2 | Turn current position into a clear roadmap |

## 10. HDB / EC Guardrail

HDB/EC content must not be broad mass-market content unless deliberately approved.

Valid HDB/EC upgrade segment means:

- real upgrade or next-move window;
- meaningful equity/cash/CPF or realistic loan path;
- ownership/name/timing structure matters;
- household is serious and planning-oriented;
- move can reasonably connect to MAS advisory value.

Invalid broad targeting:

- any HDB owner;
- any EC owner;
- low-intent bargain hunting;
- no feasible path;
- purely “bigger home” lifestyle-only audience without strategy fit.

## 11. Advisory Rule

Every serious Client Edition output should answer:

1. What stage is this client in?
2. What position are they really in?
3. What is the belief block or decision gap?
4. What does the market reward or punish?
5. What are the next 2-3 moves?
6. What is the next action?

If an idea cannot support these questions, it should not become a core MAS framework.
