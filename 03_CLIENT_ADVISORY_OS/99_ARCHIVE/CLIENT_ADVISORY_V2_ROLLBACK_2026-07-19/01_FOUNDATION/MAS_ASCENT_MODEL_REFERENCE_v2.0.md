## 1. Core Decision

Use one official level system only:

# Ascent Stage

Ascent Stage is the client journey spine.

Property Position is supporting diagnosis context, not a second ladder.

## 2. Why We Are Simplifying

Older documents mixed two ideas:

1. journey stage: Activation, First Climb, Compound, Legacy;
2. property position: no property, one property, two properties, three or more properties, generational transfer.

Both are useful, but using both as “levels” creates confusion.

The upgraded rule:

| Concept | Role |
|---|---|
| Ascent Stage | Main journey label |
| Property Position | Context field inside Client Intelligence |
| Campaign Pack | Marketing entry door |
| REI Method | Diagnosis method |
| ProDeck | Delivery tool |

## 3. Official Ascent Stages

| Stage | Name | Meaning | Common Campaign Fit |
|---|---|---|---|
| A0 | Activation | The client begins to realise their current property position may need review. This is mostly awareness and pre-qualification. | Idle Million, Your Condo Went Up, early awareness content |
| A1 | First Climb | The client is moving from one meaningful property position into a structured next move. This is the main Legacy Launch / Decoupling / Exit-First stage. | Legacy Launch, Decoupling, Exit-First, New Launch vs Resale, Before OTP |
| A2 | Compound | The client has made or is preparing for exit, redeployment, optimisation, or the second cycle of property wealth building. | Exit Review, Redeploy, Portfolio Optimisation, Next 3 Moves |
| A3 | Legacy | Family capital, adult children, names, inheritance, and multi-generational planning become central. | Family Legacy, Buy With Child, Family Asset Planning |
| A4 | Mash-Up | Two or more stages operate together in one family strategy. | Mash-Up, Parent Restructure + Child Entry, Multi-Name Strategy |

## 4. Legacy Launch Anchor

Legacy Launch should usually anchor on:

# A1 — First Climb

Reason:
Legacy Launch usually speaks to people who already own a meaningful property and are exploring a structural next move: hold, sell, buy again, decouple, restructure, or move into a stronger second-asset strategy.

A0 Activation may be used for cold awareness, but it should not be treated as the main Legacy Launch advisory stage.

## 5. Property Position Field

Property Position should be captured separately.

Examples:
- no property;
- first property buyer;
- HDB / EC owner with qualified upgrade window;
- one private condo;
- one landed / private property;
- two properties;
- three or more properties;
- adult child clean name available;
- name-maxed parent;
- family with multiple usable names;
- family capital ready but structure unclear.

Property Position helps us diagnose the client’s real position. It does not replace Ascent Stage.

## 7. HDB / EC Guardrail

HDB/EC does not mean every HDB or EC owner.

For MAS Client Edition, HDB/EC only becomes relevant when the household has a meaningful upgrade or next-move window.

Qualifying signals may include:
- MOP/TOP/eligibility timing is relevant and verified before advice;
- enough cash, CPF, equity, income, or family support to explore safely;
- ownership/name structure affects the move;
- the household is considering a real upgrade, restructure, or private-property pathway;
- they need advisory clarity, not generic project tips;
- they are willing to discuss risk, numbers, timeline, and trade-offs.

Weak-fit signals:
- only wants cheapest project;
- asks only for discount or “best unit”;
- no realistic financing or timeline;
- unwilling to discuss numbers;
- purely speculative mindset;
- wants generic HDB-upgrader content with no MAS pathway.

## 8. REI Method Application

Every serious advisory engagement should still use REI:

1. Review Real Position
   - property position, financing, ownership, family structure, constraints, current assumptions.
2. Evaluate Market Rewards
   - market pathway, exit audience, risk, timing, opportunity cost, asset suitability.
3. Identify Next 2–3 Moves
   - the next move, the move after that, what to avoid, and what future trigger to track.

Ascent Stage tells us where they are.
REI tells us how to diagnose and advise.

## 10. Public Language Rule

Internal language can be precise:
- A1 First Climb;
- Decoupling Planner;
- HDB/EC Upgrade Window;
- Family Legacy;
- Mash-Up.

Public language should usually be simpler:
- your current property;
- your next move;
- before you buy;
- before you free a name;
- before you sign;
- what your property position unlocks.

Do not overload consumers with internal labels unless the content is meant for a warmer, framework-aware audience.

## 11. Final Rule

Ascent Stage is the anchor.

Property Position is the context.

Campaign Pack is the marketing door.

REI Method is the diagnosis.

ProDeck is the delivery.

This keeps the system simple enough to use and strong enough to scale.
