# 03_CLIENT_DECISION_MEMORY_v2.0

Status: ACTIVE SOURCE OF TRUTH
Folder: 01_CLIENT_BRAIN
Purpose: This file stores approved Client Edition decisions that should guide future work.

## Decision Memory Rule

If a decision affects Client Edition positioning, folder structure, AI behaviour, segmentation, campaign logic, client intelligence, migration, or conversion direction, it should be recorded here.

Do not rely on chat history alone.

## Approved Decisions

### CD001 - MAS Governs Client Edition

Status: Approved

Client Edition must be governed by MAS Client OS and advisory thinking, not generic property-agent content.

Impact:

AI must cross-check Client Edition outputs against MAS-style diagnosis, risk, exit safety, and Next 2-3 Moves thinking.

### CD002 - Strategic Intent Is More Important Than Broad Market Type

Status: Approved

First-timer, existing owner, investor, HDB owner, condo owner, and upgrader are only broad sorting labels.

The more important question is whether the person fits a MAS-style pathway or strategic intent.

Impact:

Do not create generic content just because a broad market segment exists.

### CD003 - Client Brain Must Stay Small

Status: Approved

Client Brain is not the full operating system. It is the AI behaviour and decision-memory layer.

Impact:

Campaign files, ProDeck, Trigger Questions, creative prompts, raw ads, landing pages, and dashboards do not belong in Client Brain.

### CD004 - Client OS Holds Advisory Intelligence

Status: Approved

MAS, REI Method, ProDeck, Trigger Questions, Client Intelligence, conversation systems, readiness stages, and advisory frameworks belong in Client OS.

Impact:

Do not dump advisory frameworks into Client Brain or Content Marketing Engine.

### CD005 - Content Marketing Engine Holds Campaign Execution

Status: Approved

Campaign packs, marketing angles, creative concepts, scripts, image ads, landing pages, launch handoffs, and production assets belong in Content Marketing Engine.

Impact:

Legacy Launch, Family Legacy, Decoupling, DINK, HDB/EC Upgrade Window, OTP, Exit-First and related campaigns should be organised there.

### CD006 - Knowledge Vault Is Reusable Intelligence, Not Active Drafts

Status: Approved

Research, market intelligence, swipe files, buyer language, proof patterns, and winning creative learnings should live in Knowledge Vault.

Impact:

Do not place active campaign drafts there.

### CD007 - Operations & Tracking Holds Execution Measurement

Status: Approved

CRM, dashboards, SOPs, ad results, lead quality, workflow tracking, and reporting live in Operations & Tracking.

Impact:

Do not place strategy source files or client brain files inside tracking folders.

### CD008 - HDB/EC Upgrade Segment Is Qualified, Not Broad

Status: Approved

HDB/EC upgrade segment does not mean anyone who owns HDB/EC.

It means a qualified upgrade-window household with relevant timing, numbers, ownership/name structure, holding power, and advisory fit.

Impact:

Avoid broad HDB/EC content as core MAS content. Use HDB/EC only when it connects to a strategic next-move pathway.

### CD009 - Decoupling Can Be A Campaign Pack

Status: Approved

Older docs may have treated decoupling as absorbed into broader Legacy Launch logic. The upgraded direction allows Decoupling to be built as its own campaign pack.

Core framing:

"Decoupling is not the strategy. It only opens the next decision."

Impact:

Do not treat older “decoupling retired” decisions as current if contradicted by newer v2.0 direction.

### CD010 - Use Raw `.md` and `.docx` Files For Operating Docs

Status: Approved

For these folder-cleanup/source-of-truth docs, create raw `.md` and `.docx` files, not native Google Docs.

Impact:

When rebuilding source files, provide both formats and avoid leaving native Google Doc versions active unless explicitly requested.

### CD011 - Public Language Should Be Client-Safe

Status: Approved

Avoid public-facing language such as mindset, false belief, sure-profit, guaranteed return, countdown fear, or project hype unless there is a deliberate reason.

Prefer:

- outdated strategy;
- old calculation;
- position not reviewed;
- silent cost;
- exit safety;
- next move;
- future buyer demand;
- decision sequence.

Impact:

Marketing should feel advisory, not hype-driven.

### CD012 - Archive Before Delete

Status: Approved

Old files should be archived first, not deleted, unless Coach Edmund clearly approves deletion.

Impact:

During cleanup, move outdated files to 99_ARCHIVE after creating v2 replacements.

## Decision Update Rule

Whenever a new decision changes the system, recommend adding it here.

If the decision affects both Client Edition and Agent Edition, record it in the broader Property Decision Memory instead.
