# 03_TRIGGER_QUESTION_USAGE_AND_HANDOFF_RULES_v2.0

Status: ACTIVE SOURCE OF TRUTH  
Folder: 02_CLIENT_OS / 03_TRIGGER_QUESTION  
Purpose: Define when and how to use trigger questions, how to capture answers, and when to hand off material to Content Marketing, Knowledge Vault, or Operations.

## 1. Core Usage Rule

Use trigger questions to create clarity, not pressure.

A question is useful only if it helps one of these happen:

- the client explains their real situation;
- the belief block becomes visible;
- the decision-maker dynamic becomes clearer;
- the cost of staying still is understood;
- the next safe action becomes obvious.

## 2. Where Questions Fit

| Conversation moment | Question purpose |
|---|---|
| Lead enquiry | Understand why now and whether there is a real advisory issue |
| Diagnosis call | Discover position, trigger, belief block, readiness, and decision-makers |
| Strategy session | Help the client compare trade-offs and understand the recommended roadmap |
| Risk assurance | Make safety, buffer, and downside planning visible |
| Follow-up | Reopen the next Ascent trigger without chasing |
| Client review | Reassess if the original thesis still holds |

## 3. Do Not Over-Question

Bad use:

- asking every question in the bank;
- turning the consult into an interrogation;
- asking future-regret questions before trust is built;
- asking technical structure questions before current reality is known;
- using questions as disguised pressure;
- using marketing hooks inside private advisory conversations.

Better use:

- ask one question;
- pause;
- reflect back what the client said;
- ask the next narrower question;
- stop when the diagnosis is clear.

## 4. Diagnosis Call Usage

Before the call, prepare likely question sets based on source, campaign door, and known client position.

During the call, prioritise:

1. why now;
2. current property position;
3. desired future;
4. spouse / family alignment;
5. belief block;
6. readiness;
7. next action.

Do not recommend a project during diagnosis.

## 5. Strategy Session Usage

During the strategy session, use questions to help the client compare options.

Useful question types:

- “What does this pathway give you?”
- “What does this pathway cost you?”
- “What is the risk if this pathway fails?”
- “Which pathway protects the next move better?”
- “What does your spouse or family need to see before deciding?”

The goal is not to make the client agree with the advisor.

The goal is to help the client see why one pathway is structurally stronger or safer than another.

## 6. Risk Assurance Usage

Use safety questions before numbers become overwhelming.

Examples:

- What is the monthly figure that starts to feel uncomfortable?
- How many months of buffer would make this feel safe?
- If rental is lower than expected, what gap can you tolerate?
- If income changes, what protection layer matters most?
- Are you more worried about monthly cashflow, total debt, exit risk, or family stress?

Risk assurance questions should feed into RAM and ProDeck, not replace proper number work.

## 7. Follow-Up Usage

Follow-up is not chasing.

Use questions to reopen the next trigger:

- Has anything changed since we last reviewed this?
- Is the same concern still the main concern?
- Has the decision window moved closer?
- Has your property value, family situation, or timeline changed?
- Is this still something you want to solve later, or is it becoming more urgent?

## 8. CRM / Operations Capture

After any meaningful conversation, capture the answer in the correct system.

| Client answer reveals | Capture as |
|---|---|
| Why now | Trigger Moment |
| What they own | Property Position |
| Where they are in journey | Ascent Stage |
| What they want | Strategic Intent |
| Why they hesitate | Belief Block |
| How they decide | Buyer Profile / Decision Style |
| Who must agree | Decision-Maker Dynamic |
| How ready they are | Readiness / Tier |
| What should happen next | Next Action / Follow-Up Date |

Do not store live client data inside Client OS files.

## 9. Content Marketing Handoff

This folder can inspire marketing, but it does not store final marketing outputs.

When a question repeatedly reveals a market pattern, hand it off to Content Marketing Engine as an insight, not as raw copy.

Example handoff:

| Advisory question | Marketing insight |
|---|---|
| What does your property gain actually unlock? | Many owners have paper gains but no next-move plan. |
| Is decoupling the strategy or just the mechanism? | Decoupling education needs to expose mechanism-vs-strategy confusion. |
| Are you waiting for certainty or waiting for the decision to feel easier? | Market-timing content should challenge false certainty. |

## 10. Knowledge Vault Handoff

Move reusable evidence to Knowledge Vault when it is not an active operating rule.

Examples:

- repeated client language from transcripts;
- consult patterns;
- tested market examples;
- swipe references;
- buyer language patterns;
- proof stories.

## 11. Operations Handoff

Move measurable results to Operations & Tracking.

Examples:

- which trigger produced bookings;
- which question improved consult quality;
- which campaign angle produced high-intent leads;
- which objection caused drop-off;
- which follow-up question revived a lead.

## 12. Archive Rule

Archive these after rebuild:

- old v1 trigger question indexes;
- old README files;
- old audit notes;
- duplicate marketing trigger banks inside Client OS;
- old files that use outdated Ascent Level / Activation language as active rules.

Do not delete old files until the wider cleanup is completed and deletion is explicitly approved.

## 13. Final Usage Test

Before using a trigger question, ask:

1. Does this question match the client’s stage and context?
2. Does it reveal something useful?
3. Does it create safety or clarity?
4. Is it better asked privately than used publicly?
5. Will the answer update CRM, ProDeck, follow-up, or marketing insight?

If the answer is no, do not ask it.
