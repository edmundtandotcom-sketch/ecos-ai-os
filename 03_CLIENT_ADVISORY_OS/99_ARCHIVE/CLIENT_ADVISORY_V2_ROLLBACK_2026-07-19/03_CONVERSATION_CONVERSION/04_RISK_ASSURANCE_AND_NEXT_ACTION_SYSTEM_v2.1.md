## 1. Core Principle

Clients do not need perfect certainty.

They need enough clarity and safety to take the next correct step.

Risk assurance is not used to make the move sound easy.

It is used to show the client:

- what can go wrong;
- what has been checked;
- what buffers exist;
- what trade-offs matter;
- what not to do;
- what action is safe, unsafe, or premature.

## 2. When To Deploy Risk Assurance

Use risk assurance when the client shows concern about:

- monthly commitment;
- job or income disruption;
- two loans;
- interest rate movement;
- holding power;
- CPF and cash usage;
- spouse safety concern;
- downside scenarios;
- exit liquidity;
- “what if we are wrong?”;
- fear of overcommitting.

Do not deploy RAM too early. First diagnose the pathway. Then show the safety structure.

## 3. RAM Conversation Role

RAM means Risk Assessment Module.

Inside conversation, RAM is a confidence-building lens, not a calculator folder.

It helps the client move from:

“I am taking on more risk.”

to:

“I can see how the risk is being structured, buffered, and monitored.”

The detailed calculators, tables, ProDeck visuals, and financial presentation outputs belong in:

02_CLIENT_OS / 04_PRODECK

This file only governs when and how to use the risk conversation.

## 4. The Five RAM Lenses

### Lens 1 — 3Ps

Use when the client only sees the liability side of property.

The 3Ps:

- Passive Income: rental income may offset holding cost.
- Principal Income: mortgage reduction builds equity over time.
- Profit: capital appreciation may create upside on exit.

Advisor rule: do not exaggerate. Use this as a mental model, not a guarantee.

### Lens 2 — Safety Net

Use when the client fears income disruption or family pressure.

Safety layers may include:

- rental income offset;
- CPF OA servicing capacity where applicable;
- emergency cash reserve;
- lower monthly obligation through tenure planning;
- asset liquidity as last resort;
- spouse/family fallback plan.

Advisor rule: show survivability before upside.

### Lens 3 — Stretch Tenure

Use when shorter tenure creates unnecessary monthly pressure.

Reframe: flexibility today can create safety tomorrow.

Advisor rule: explain that tenure is a cashflow design decision, not a signal of weakness. Actual suitability depends on bank rules, age, TDSR, property tenure, and client objectives.

### Lens 4 — CPF / Cash Ratio

Use when the client defaults to using CPF without considering opportunity cost.

Advisor rule: do not say “never use CPF”. The correct question is what ratio protects retirement safety, liquidity, and property strategy.

### Lens 5 — Save-As-You-Earn / Readiness Plan

Use when the client is not ready yet but can become ready.

Turn “not now” into:

- target entry amount;
- savings timeline;
- capital gap;
- review date;
- future trigger;
- monthly action.

Advisor rule: make “not yet” specific, not vague.

## 5. Risk Assurance Questions

Use these questions when fear is present:

- What scenario worries you most?
- Is the fear monthly cashflow, job loss, rates, exit, spouse concern, or being wrong?
- What monthly commitment feels comfortable, stretched, and unacceptable?
- What buffer would make this feel safer?
- How many months of holding power do you want before feeling comfortable?
- If we had to exit, who is the future buyer?
- What would make this move premature?
- What should we avoid?
- What is the safest next step, even if today is not the buying decision?

## 6. The Decision Gate: Proceed / Prepare / Nurture / No Fit (Decision 073)

Every serious conversation should end in one of the four sanctioned closes (identical to Strategy Session §8 — one label set, ruled by Edmund 2026-07-12):

### Proceed

Use when:

- diagnosis is complete;
- decision-makers aligned;
- risk is understood;
- numbers are workable;
- next action is time-sensitive and suitable.

Action: proceed to implementation planning.

### Prepare

Use when:

- path is likely valid;
- but missing numbers, documents, bank checks, spouse alignment, or project data.

Action: collect missing information and book the next review.

### Nurture

Use when:

- client is relevant but not financially, emotionally, or timing-ready.

Action: create readiness plan and next trigger.

### No Fit

Use when:

- pathway is not feasible;
- client behaviour is poor fit;
- advisory value is low;
- risk is not acceptable.

Action: exit politely and avoid over-serving.

## 7. Next Action Menu

Possible next actions:

- Strategy Session booking;
- spouse / family joint session;
- document collection;
- IPA / bank check;
- property valuation review;
- ownership-name review;
- project shortlist preparation;
- ProDeck roadmap preparation;
- risk / RAM review;
- nurture sequence;
- 3-month / 6-month / 12-month review;
- no-fit closure.

Do not end a conversation with “keep in touch” only.

Every conversation needs a defined next action or a deliberate no-action decision.

After every meaningful interaction, update the CRM / client intelligence record with:

- latest summary;
- Ascent Stage;

- Trigger Moment;
- Buyer Segment;
- Decision-Maker Map;
- Belief Block;
- Risk Concern;
- Tier / Readiness;
- Advisory Output;
- Next Action;
- Next Follow-Up Date;
- Owner;
- Next Ascent Trigger.

CRM execution belongs in Operations & Tracking. This file defines what must be handed off.

## 9. Follow-Up By Belief Block

### Fear Of Timing

Send market-context review, not hype.

### Need More Certainty

Send process, checklist, or decision framework.

### Comfort Greater Than Growth

Send future-flexibility or opportunity-cost reflection.

### Fully Paid Equals Safe

Send safety-net / future-readiness framing.

### Spouse Not Aligned

Bring both decision-makers together; do not keep convincing one side.

### Trust Deficit

Send transparent diagnosis, what-to-avoid, and willingness to say no.

## 10. Lifetime Review Loop

A Client Edition relationship should not end after one transaction.

Set future review triggers:

- MOP / TOP / SSD;
- loan repricing;
- project TOP;
- rental change;
- child turns 21 / starts work;
- spouse income change;
- asset appreciation;
- market window;
- family milestone;
- exit thesis update;
- 3-5 year portfolio review.

Follow-up is not chasing.

Follow-up means staying relevant to the client’s next Ascent trigger.

## 11. Compliance And Safety Language

Do not promise profit.

Do not guarantee returns.

Do not say risk is removed.

Do not provide financial-planning, legal, tax, CPF, or loan advice outside proper professional boundaries.

Use wording such as:

- “subject to proper checks”;
- “based on your numbers”;
- “illustrative only”;
- “we need to verify with bank/legal/tax where relevant”;
- “the goal is to understand risk before action”;
- “this is not a guarantee.”

## 12. Working Rule

The right next action is not always “buy”.

The right next action is the next step that makes the client’s position clearer, safer, and more strategic.
