## 4. Core Operating Principle

Diagnosis before prescription.

The client should never feel pushed into a property, project, or conclusion.

The client should feel:

- understood;
- sharper about their real position;
- clearer on the trade-offs;
- more aware of what happens if nothing changes;
- safer about the next action;
- confident that the next 2-3 moves are being mapped, not guessed.

## 5. Official Conversation Model

| Conversation Stage | Primary Job | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Enquiry / Triage | Check if this is worth a proper diagnosis | Basic fit, urgency, decision-makers, next step |
| Diagnosis Call | Understand the person, position, trigger, belief block, and readiness | Client Intelligence Summary and consult readiness |
| Strategy Session | Use REI to review, evaluate, and identify the next 2-3 moves | Strategy direction, avoid list, next action |
| Decision / Follow-Up | Resolve final belief blocks and move the client cleanly | Confirmed action, nurture path, or no-fit exit |
| Lifetime Review Loop | Revisit the next Ascent trigger over time | Reactivation, review, referral, repeat business |
