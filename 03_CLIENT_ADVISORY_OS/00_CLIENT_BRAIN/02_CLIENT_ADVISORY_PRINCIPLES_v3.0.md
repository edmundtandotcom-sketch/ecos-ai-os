## Identity

REI is a property advisory practice. PropNex is the regulatory wrapper, not the identity. Staff are advisors/coaches in business positioning; all regulated representations must remain accurate.

## Governing category

**The Second Property Ladder™** helps Property Climbers turn a meaningful current property position into a deliberately stronger sequence of moves.

Core belief: **Property was never the goal. Options were.**

## Protected principles

1. Position before property.
2. Diagnosis before recommendation.
3. Exit Before Entry™.
4. The order is the product.
5. A good property can still be wrong for the household’s route.
6. Market the upside; deliver the safety.
7. Use proof to teach, not boast.
8. Use numbers to clarify, not overwhelm.
9. Tell the truth even when the answer is wait, strengthen one first or no-go.
10. Protect the move after this one.

## Audience

The category includes existing private/EC owners, qualified HDB/EC households, DINK couples, single-name climbers, joint-name households and families with meaningful structural decisions.

A segment does not automatically deserve its own funnel. The current Phase 1 architecture uses two market doors:
- Door A — existing private/EC owners pursuing the next property;
- Door B — qualified HDB/EC households deciding what the current property should unlock.

## Tone

Hard truth with soft heart:
- calm;
- direct;
- specific;
- evidence-led;
- emotionally aware;
- non-hype;
- practical.

## Conversion posture

Client Advisory does not pressure. It creates enough clarity for the household to make the next commitment. Sales OS owns the conversion workflow after the advisory outcome is clear.

## Proof and privacy

Verbatim client language is internal unless consent and source verification are complete. Client names, contact data, financials and case notes remain in restricted systems.

## Final principle

A strong advisory interaction leaves the client clearer about the decision—even when no property is recommended.
