## Rule

Segment = who they resemble.
Readiness = how prepared they are for the next commitment.

## Stages

| Stage | Name | State | Advisory job |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | Comfortably Stuck | Stable, unreviewed and not actively deciding | surface the decision gap without pressure |
| R2 | Actively Confused | Comparing options without a decision filter | clarify the route and missing evidence |
| R3 | Commitment-Pressured | Near a property/mechanism commitment | interrupt project-first momentum and test the route |
| R4 | Advisory-Ready | Accepts the need for structured help | prepare the correct advisory session and evidence |

## Movement

R1 → R2: the household sees that appreciation/comfort is not a plan.  
R2 → R3: a live option or decision deadline appears.  
R3 → R4: the household accepts that route and evidence must precede commitment.

## Boundary

Client Advisory records the readiness meaning. Marketing chooses message intensity. Sales chooses booking/follow-up actions. Operations implements fields and workflows.

## Final rule

Readiness changes the next commitment—not the truth of the diagnosis.
