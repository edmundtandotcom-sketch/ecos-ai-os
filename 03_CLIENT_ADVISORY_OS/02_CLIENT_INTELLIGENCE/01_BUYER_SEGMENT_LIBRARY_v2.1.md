## Rule

A segment is a diagnostic psychology profile. It is not automatically a campaign, funnel, ad set or offer.

## Segments

| Code | Segment | Core question | Primary market door |
|---|---|---|---|
| FCC | Existing-owner climber | My property went up—what should it unlock? | Door A or B by property position |
| DINK | Flexible-window couple | What should our most flexible years be building? | Door A |
| FHA | Home-attached owner | Can we honour the home and still review the asset? | Door A or B |
| DFP | Decoupling/free-name planner | What must the free name achieve next? | Door A |
| RM | Ready mover | Before signing, is this the right move for the route? | Door A or B |
| HECO-Q | Qualified HDB/EC household | What should our current HDB/EC unlock next? | Door B |
| FAP | Family asset planner | How should property support the family’s next chapter? | Door A or B; no separate Family Legacy door |

## Usage

For each case identify:
- actual property position;
- Ascent stage;
- dominant segment;
- readiness;
- strategic intent;
- route tension;
- belief/risk gap;
- decision-makers.

Do not force one household into one permanent label. Segment tags can change as the decision changes.

## Public rule

Use Property Climber language and the client’s real situation. Keep internal codes out of cold public copy.

## Final rule

Segments help the advisor understand the client. The route and evidence determine the advice.
