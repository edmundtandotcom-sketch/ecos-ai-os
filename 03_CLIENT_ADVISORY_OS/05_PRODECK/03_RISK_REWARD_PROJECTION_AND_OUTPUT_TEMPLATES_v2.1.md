## Projection rules

- conservative, base and upside cases;
- no guaranteed-return language;
- separate cash proceeds and CPF refund;
- include capital required, holding cost and exit cost;
- show assumptions and confidence;
- verify current rules/rates before use;
- use qualified professional checks where required;
- state conditions that change the recommendation.
