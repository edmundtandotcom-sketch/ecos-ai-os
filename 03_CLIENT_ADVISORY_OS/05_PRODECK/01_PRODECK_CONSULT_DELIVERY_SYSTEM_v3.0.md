## Definition

ProDeck is the session-control and visual-delivery system. It turns verified advisory intelligence into a clear, decision-ready conversation.

## Pre-session gates

- relevant decision-makers;
- sufficient position/financial information;
- clear session scope;
- known missing verification;
- advisory fit;
- no expectation of an immediate project shortlist before engagement.

## Six anchors

1. **Position clarity** — Position Map
2. **Direction clarity** — Direction Statement
3. **Route clarity** — Keep and Add / Sell and Rebuild / Strengthen One First
4. **Sequence clarity** — Move Sequence
5. **Exit and survivability clarity** — relevant research principles and RAM
6. **Decision clarity** — outcome and next commitment

## Delivery rules

- Start with the client’s position, not a project.
- Use only evidence relevant to the decision.
- Show trade-offs and rejected routes.
- Do not use unverified live policy/financing claims.
- Do not present projections as promises.
- Stop when the evidence is insufficient.
- End with Proceed / Prepare / Nurture / No Fit and one next commitment.

## Scope boundary

The session may show an initial Property Job and selection logic. Detailed market universe, ranked shortlist, unit/stack and price recommendation occur after formal engagement under the Selection Doctrine.

## Handoff

ProDeck produces the advisory summary. Sales owns conversion/follow-up. Operations owns the record/workflow.

## Final rule

A good deck makes the decision easier to understand—not the advisor harder to question.
