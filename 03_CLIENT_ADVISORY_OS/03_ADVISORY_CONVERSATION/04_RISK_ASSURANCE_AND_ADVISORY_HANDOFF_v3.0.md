## Purpose

Help the household understand the material downside, safeguards, trade-offs and conditions required for the next correct commitment.

## Deploy when

- monthly commitment or income disruption is a concern;
- ownership or financing structure is unclear;
- spouse/family safety concerns block progress;
- exit liquidity or holding period is uncertain;
- the household mistakes no-action for no-risk.

## Method

1. Name the exact risk.
2. Separate probability, impact and uncertainty.
3. Show what is known, assumed and unverified.
4. Apply only the relevant RAM lens.
5. State the accepted trade-off.
6. Define the condition that changes the recommendation.
7. Assign one advisory outcome.

## Outcome labels

| Outcome | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Proceed | Evidence supports moving to the next commitment |
| Prepare | Direction is plausible but required evidence/alignment is missing |
| Nurture | Relevant case, but timing/readiness is not active |
| No Fit | Route is unsafe, infeasible or outside advisory fit |

## Handoff record

- outcome and reason;
- material risk;
- accepted trade-off;
- missing evidence;
- professional verification needed;
- next commitment;
- owner and due date;
- review trigger.

Sales OS handles the follow-up process. Operations implements the CRM workflow.

## Final rule

Risk assurance does not remove risk. It makes the decision conditions explicit.
