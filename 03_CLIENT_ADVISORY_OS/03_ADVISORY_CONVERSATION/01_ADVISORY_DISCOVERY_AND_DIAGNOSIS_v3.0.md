## Purpose

Understand the household and decision well enough to determine whether a meaningful advisory review is possible and what evidence is required.

## Required discovery

- why now;
- current property and ownership position;
- household and decision-makers;
- desired future position;
- timeline and policy clocks;
- financial information available/missing;
- strategic intent;
- routes already considered;
- advice already received;
- dominant belief/risk concern;
- constraints and non-negotiables.

## Flow

1. Set the frame: understanding before views.
2. Discover the trigger.
3. Review the current position.
4. Clarify the desired future.
5. Map decision-makers.
6. Surface route assumptions and belief/risk gaps.
7. Identify missing evidence.
8. State the advisory question and next commitment required.

## Output

- Discovery Summary;
- Known / Unknown table;
- Strategic Intent;
- initial Ascent and readiness context;
- route hypothesis—not recommendation;
- required information;
- decision-maker participation;
- appropriate advisory next step.

## Stop rules

Do not deepen into personalised modelling when:
- material facts are missing;
- legal/tax/loan assumptions are unverified;
- required decision-makers are absent;
- the household wants only project tips;
- there is no feasible advisory question.

## Sales handoff

Client Advisory states the next commitment required. Sales OS owns booking, qualification workflow and follow-up.

## Final rule

Discovery succeeds when the real decision becomes clearer—not when the advisor gives the most information.
