## Purpose

The **Next Move Strategy Session™** turns verified household information into route clarity and the next defensible commitment. It is not a project presentation and does not provide the post-engagement ranked shortlist.

## Pre-session gate

Confirm:
- material decision-makers;
- known property/ownership facts;
- adequate financial inputs for the intended depth;
- strategic intent and timeline;
- main decision tension;
- missing verification;
- client understands the session scope.

## Session spine

### 1. Replay the diagnosis
Confirm the household’s situation, objective and tension.

### 2. Review Real Position
Position Map: equity, ownership, loan headroom, timeline, constraints and policy clocks.

### 3. Set the Direction
Direction Statement: the NAV/options outcome and what must remain possible.

### 4. Compare routes
- Keep and Add
- Sell and Rebuild
- Strengthen One First

Show why a route fits or fails; do not present equal options without judgment.

### 5. Sequence the Moves
Move Sequence: dependencies, timing and what must happen first.

### 6. Clarify exit and survivability
Use relevant Selection Doctrine and RAM principles. Detailed project research and shortlist work remain post-engagement.

### 7. State the advisory outcome
- Proceed
- Prepare
- Nurture
- No Fit

### 8. Define the next commitment
Data, decision-maker, professional check, formal engagement or implementation step.

## Public/backend boundary

The session may provide position assessment, route comparison, affordability/timeline view, initial Property Job and explanation of selection logic.

It does not give the full ranked shortlist, unit/stack recommendation or complete personalised selection research before the appropriate engagement.

## Final rule

The household leaves with one clearer direction and one next commitment—not a pile of projects.
