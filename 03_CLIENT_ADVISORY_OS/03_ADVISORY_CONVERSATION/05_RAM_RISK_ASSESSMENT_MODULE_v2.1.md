## Purpose

RAM is the internal financial-risk-assurance framework used when the route is understood but the household needs to see whether it is survivable.

It is not financial planning, bank approval, tax/legal advice or a guarantee.

## Five lenses

| Lens | Job |
|---|---|
| 3Ps | Explain the possible rent, principal-reduction and capital-value components without guaranteeing any of them |
| Safety Net | Size property commitments against income, vacancy, rate and household stress |
| Stretch Tenure | Compare tenure/cashflow variants where permitted and suitable |
| CPF/Cash Allocation | Examine liquidity, CPF opportunity cost and accrued-interest implications |
| SAYE / Readiness Plan | Turn “not yet” into a defined capital gap, action and review date |

## Safety Net baseline

Use the current approved Selection Doctrine starting baseline: at least 12 months of relevant property commitments after completion, adjusted upward for income instability, multiple properties, vacancy reliance, completion risk and household circumstances.

## Verification rule

Before client use, verify:
- current bank/LTV/TDSR/tenure treatment;
- interest-rate assumptions;
- CPF usage and accrued-interest treatment;
- tax/stamp-duty/legal implications;
- rental and vacancy evidence;
- case-specific affordability.

Use official sources and qualified professionals. Mark illustrative calculations clearly.

## Output

- risk being tested;
- assumptions;
- stress cases;
- buffer/survivability read;
- trade-off;
- condition that changes the recommendation;
- Proceed / Prepare / Nurture / No Fit.

## Final rule

RAM supports the recommendation only after diagnosis. It cannot rescue a route that fails the household’s plan.
