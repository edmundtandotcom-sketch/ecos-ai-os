## What MAS is

MAS — Millionaire Advisory System™ — is the internal client-advisory operating system.

The Second Property Ladder™ is the public category and product direction. MAS is the internal operating system that helps the advisor deliver it.

## Advisory belief

**We do not start with property. We start with position.**

The goal is not more properties for their own sake. The goal is stronger NAV, better sequencing and more future options.

## The 5 Moves

1. Map the Position → Position Map
2. Set the Direction → Direction Statement
3. Sequence the Moves → Move Sequence
4. Research the Exit First → Selection Research + Survivability Check
5. Execute & Repeat → Climb Cadence

## Three canonical routes

- Keep and Add
- Sell and Rebuild
- Strengthen One First

The correct route is diagnosed; it is not assumed.

## Advisory sequence

1. Discover the household and decision.
2. Verify position, ownership, numbers and constraints.
3. Diagnose the route and decision gaps.
4. Conduct the Next Move Strategy Session™.
5. Produce the appropriate Blueprint outputs and advisory outcome.
6. Hand the next commitment to Sales and implementation requirements to Operations.
7. Begin detailed selection only after the correct engagement boundary.

## Public vs internal

Public:
- Property Climbers;
- Second Property Ladder;
- The 5 Moves;

- route names;
- Readiness Check;
- Next Move Strategy Session;
- Blueprint.

Internal:
- MAS;
- Ascent;
- REI;
- ProDeck;
- RAM;
- belief-block codes.

## Final rule

No lower advisory tool may override the category, The 5 Moves, owner-approved names or the Selection Doctrine.
