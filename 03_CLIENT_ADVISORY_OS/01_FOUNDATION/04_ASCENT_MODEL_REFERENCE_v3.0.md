## Purpose

Ascent Stage describes where the household is in the advisory journey. It is not a property-count ladder and not the public category.

## Stages

| Stage | Meaning | Advisory job |
|---|---|---|
| A0 — Activation | The household recognises the current position may need review but has not entered full diagnosis | clarify why review matters and what information is needed |
| A1 — First Climb | The current property/ownership position must unlock the first deliberate next move | diagnose route, structure, timing and household fit |
| A2 — Compound | A deliberate move has been made or a stronger position exists; exit, redeployment and review matter | assess performance, exit thesis, redeployment and next cycle |

A3 and A4 are retired. Family, child-name, multi-name and complex portfolio situations run inside A1/A2 according to the actual decision.

## Separate concepts

- Property position = context.
- Buyer segment = psychology.
- Readiness = closeness to action.
- Route = Keep and Add / Sell and Rebuild / Strengthen One First.
- Advisory outcome = Proceed / Prepare / Nurture / No Fit.

## Usage

Use Ascent to tailor depth and timing—not to force-fit clients or create new funnels.

## Final rule

The Second Property Ladder™ is the public climb. Ascent is the internal journey context.
