# 01_ADVISORY_TRIGGER_QUESTION_BANK_v2.0

Status: ACTIVE SOURCE OF TRUTH
Folder: 02_CLIENT_OS / 03_TRIGGER_QUESTION
Purpose: Store reusable advisory questions for diagnosis calls, strategy sessions, and consult preparation.

## 1. Question Bank Rule
Use questions to create self-realisation, not interrogation. A good question should reveal position, expose the decision gap, surface the belief block, make the cost of inaction visible, or move the client toward the next safe action.

## 2. Core Opening Questions
- What made this important enough to discuss now?
- What changed recently that made you revisit your property position?
- What are you hoping the next move solves?
- What have you already considered?
- What advice have you received so far?
- What part still feels unclear or risky?
- Who else needs to be aligned before a decision can happen?
- What would make this conversation useful for you?

## 3. Ascent Stage Questions
### A1 First Climb
- Your property has moved. What do you believe that gain should unlock?
- Is the goal to stay comfortable, or to create stronger future options?
- Are you clear whether this gain should be held, protected, repositioned, or redeployed?
- Which options are you currently comparing?
- Are you making a property decision, or a future-position decision?

### A2 Compound
- What was the original thesis for this property?
- Has the asset done what it was supposed to do?
- If you exit now, what should the capital do next?
- If you hold, what is the next review trigger?

### Family / multi-name situations (inside A1/A2)
> The former A3 Legacy and A4 Bespoke Portfolio stages are **RETIRED (Decision 063, 2026-07-11)** — the Ascent Model is A0–A2. The questions below remain usable when family capital, children's names, or complex multi-asset structure surface **inside a standard A1/A2 engagement**; do not classify the client at a dedicated family/portfolio level.

- What role should property play in the family’s future?
- Who owns the asset now, and whose name matters next?
- What family conversation has not happened yet?
- What is the current structure trying to achieve?
- Which asset is doing its job, and which one is becoming inefficient?
- Where is the biggest exposure: cashflow, exit, tax, names, leverage, or family alignment?

## 4. Property Position Context Questions
### Appreciated Private Property / Condo Owner
- What is your property worth today versus what you paid?
- What does that gain actually change for your family?
- If you do nothing, what are you assuming will happen next?

### Qualified HDB / EC Upgrade-Window Household
- What is the real timing window: MOP, post-MOP, TOP, SSD, or planned exit?
- Are you reviewing because of lifestyle, asset growth, ownership structure, or all three?
- What financial capacity do you realistically have before the move becomes unsafe?

### Decoupling / Free-Name Structure
- What do you expect freeing the name to achieve?
- After the name is freed, what is the actual next move?
- Is decoupling the strategy, or just the mechanism?

### DINK / Golden Window
- What does this stage of life allow you to do before responsibilities increase?
- Are you using your flexibility intentionally, or letting it pass by?
- What kind of property decision would protect lifestyle freedom rather than trap it?

## 5. Belief Block Questions
| Belief Block | Question |
|---|---|
| Timing | What exactly are you waiting to see before you feel it is time? |
| Certainty | Is the missing piece more information, or a clearer process? |
| Comfort | Is today’s comfort quietly limiting tomorrow’s flexibility? |
| Fully paid | Is the goal to eliminate debt, or improve future options safely? |
| Spouse / family | What has your spouse or family not seen yet? |
| Complexity | Which part feels difficult: the structure, the numbers, or the unknowns? |

## 6. Readiness / Tier Questions
- Is this something you are exploring generally, or something you need to decide soon?
- What happens if this is not resolved within the next 3–6 months?
- Are all decision-makers involved yet?
- Are you willing to share the numbers needed for a real diagnosis?

## 7. Next-Action Questions
- What should be clarified before you decide?
- What is the safest next step from here?
- Should your spouse / family be part of the next conversation?
- If the review shows that no move is safe, are you open to hearing that too?
